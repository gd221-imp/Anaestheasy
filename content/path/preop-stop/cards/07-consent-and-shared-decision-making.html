• Explain what matters to this patient (Montgomery): material risks, alternatives, and doing nothing
  • Give risks in numbers where possible, using the RCoA patient information leaflets
  • BRAN helps: Benefits, Risks, Alternatives, what if we do Nothing?
  • Assess capacity if in doubt (Mental Capacity Act); act in best interests if lacking
  • Children: Gillick competence; otherwise consent from a person with parental responsibility
  • High-risk surgery: shared decision-making with the surgeon, patient and family, and a treatment escalation plan
  • Document the discussion, the plan and who you discussed it with
