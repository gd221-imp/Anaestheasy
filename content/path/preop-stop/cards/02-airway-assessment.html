Look for predictors of difficult mask ventilation and difficult intubation, and check previous anaesthetic charts.

  • History: previous difficult airway, OSA (STOP-BANG), head and neck surgery or radiotherapy, reflux
  • Mouth opening (inter-incisor gap) and dentition: loose, capped or missing teeth
  • Mallampati score
  • Jaw protrusion and thyromental distance
  • Neck movement (rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, C-spine surgery)
  • Difficult mask ventilation predictors: obesity, beard, older age, snoring, no teeth

If several predictors are present, plan an awake technique or a clear strategy for each step, and tell the senior on call.
