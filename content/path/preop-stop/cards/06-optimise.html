• Anaemia: FBC early; oral iron if surgery is 6 to 8 weeks away, IV iron if sooner
  • Diabetes: elective surgery is usually deferred if HbA1c is above 69 mmol/mol (8.5%)
  • Smoking: stop at least 4 weeks before for the most benefit; any stop helps
  • Hypertension: proceed if clinic BP is below 180/110 mmHg (Association of Anaesthetists and British Hypertension Society)
  • OSA: bring CPAP; plan post-op monitoring
  • Prehabilitation: exercise, nutrition and psychological support (ERAS)
  • Fasting: solids 6 hours; clear fluids up to 2 hours in adults (many units now allow sips until sent for); children 6-4-1
