Most medicines continue. Write an individual plan for those that don't, and follow local guidelines.

  • Anticoagulants: stop timing depends on the drug, renal function and bleeding risk; bridging only for high thrombotic risk
  • Antiplatelets: don't stop aspirin or dual antiplatelets after a recent stent without cardiology advice
  • ACE inhibitors and ARBs: often omitted on the day of surgery (local policy)
  • SGLT2 inhibitors: omit the day before and the day of surgery (euglycaemic DKA); check ketones
  • GLP-1 agonists: delayed gastric emptying; follow current UK guidance (consider gastric ultrasound or RSI)
  • Insulin: reduce long-acting basal insulin by about 20% the day before; variable rate insulin if more than one missed meal
  • Steroids: continue; extra cover for major surgery
  • Continue beta-blockers, statins, inhalers and antiepileptics
