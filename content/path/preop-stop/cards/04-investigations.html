Order tests that change management, not a routine panel. NICE NG45 guides this by ASA grade and size of surgery.

  • FBC: major surgery or expected blood loss; ASA 3 or above; known anaemia
  • U&E: renal disease, diabetes, diuretics, ACE inhibitors, major surgery
  • ECG: cardiovascular, renal or diabetic disease, or intermediate or major surgery in older patients
  • HbA1c in diabetes (within 3 months)
  • Clotting: anticoagulants, liver disease, bleeding history
  • Pregnancy test where relevant; sickle cell screen if at risk
  • Group and save or crossmatch per local blood ordering schedule
