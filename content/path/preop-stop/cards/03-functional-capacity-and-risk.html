Functional capacity

  • 1 MET = 3.5 ml O₂/kg/min (resting). Under 4 METs means higher risk.
  • About 4 METs: two flights of stairs without stopping, or gardening
  • Self-reported capacity is unreliable; a structured tool such as DASI is better

Risk tools

  • ASA grade I to V (E for emergency)
  • Revised Cardiac Risk Index: IHD, heart failure, cerebrovascular disease, insulin-treated diabetes, creatinine above 177 µmol/L, high-risk surgery
  • Individual risk calculators: NSQIP, SORT, P-POSSUM; NELA for emergency laparotomy

When to test further

  • CPET for major surgery or uncertain fitness: anaerobic threshold above about 11 ml/kg/min is reassuring
  • 6-minute walk (over 400 m reassuring) or incremental shuttle walk
  • Echo for a new murmur, suspected heart failure or valve disease, if it would change management
