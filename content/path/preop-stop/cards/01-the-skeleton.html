Every pre-op assessment follows the same order. Do it the same way each time and you won't miss things.

History

  • The operation: what, why, urgency, expected blood loss and length
  • Past medical history by system: cardiac, respiratory, endocrine (diabetes, thyroid), renal, liver, neurological, haematological
  • Anaesthetic history: previous anaesthetics, airway problems, PONV; family history of malignant hyperthermia or suxamethonium apnoea
  • Drugs and allergies (what the reaction was)
  • Social: smoking, alcohol, recreational drugs, who is at home
  • Functional capacity: stairs, walking distance, what stops them
  • Frailty and cognition in older patients (Clinical Frailty Scale)
  • Last oral intake

Examination

  • Airway (see the airway card)
  • Heart (murmurs, heart failure), chest, BMI
  • Back if neuraxial planned; veins

Investigations, risk, plan

  • Review what's been done and add what changes management
  • Estimate risk and agree a plan (see the risk card)
  • Explain, consent and document
