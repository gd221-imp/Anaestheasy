Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Hysterectomy or endometriosis surgery
  • Cardiorespiratory reserve for steep head-down
  • Glaucoma, raised ICP

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • BMI

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, group and save

Optimise

  • Oral paracetamol
  • Team knows how to undock the robot quickly in an emergency

Intra-op

Equipment

  • 18G cannula (or 20G awake and 16G asleep)
  • ETT; nerve stimulator
  • Anti-slip mattress, eye protection

Induction

  • Propofol and remifentanil TIVA
  • Rocuronium 1 mg/kg (lower dose or atracurium possible, with slower onset)
  • Dexamethasone 6.6 mg and ondansetron 4 mg

Maintenance

  • TIVA; patient must stay still while docked
  • Magnesium sulphate 2.5 g
  • Oxycodone 10 mg about 30–40 minutes before the end
  • Watch airway pressures and CO₂ in steep head-down

Emergence

  • Reverse with sugammadex (or confirm TOF ratio if atracurium)
  • Check for facial and airway oedema

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Bowel, bladder or ureteric injury

Delayed

  • Vault dehiscence
  • Infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Raised ICP and IOP, airway oedema in steep head-down
  • Pressure and nerve injuries

Delayed

  • Shoulder-tip pain
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Oxycodone 1–10 mg in recovery
  • Regular paracetamol and ibuprofen; dihydrocodeine or Oramorph as needed

Destination

  • Day case or overnight ward

Tips

  • Back-to-back 45–90 minute cases: keep turnover slick
