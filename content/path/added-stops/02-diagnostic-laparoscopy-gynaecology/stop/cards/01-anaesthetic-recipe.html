Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Indication (pelvic pain, endometriosis, infertility)
  • Pregnancy possible?
  • Day-case suitability

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • BMI

Investigations (Ix)

  • Pregnancy test
  • Others only if indicated

Optimise

  • Day-case criteria; PONV risk

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, 20G cannula
  • ETT (pneumoperitoneum and head-down)
  • Nerve stimulator

Induction

  • Propofol and remifentanil (TIVA), atracurium 10–20 mg
  • Paracetamol and ibuprofen beforehand

Maintenance

  • TIVA; head-down with pneumoperitoneum
  • Dexamethasone 6.6 mg; ondansetron
  • Oxycodone about 10 mg around 40 minutes before the end

Emergence

  • Check train-of-four and reverse if needed
  • Extubate awake

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bowel, bladder or vessel injury at port insertion
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Port-site hernia
  • Infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Bradycardia on insufflation
  • Raised airway pressures and CO₂

Delayed

  • Shoulder-tip pain
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol and ibuprofen
  • Port-site local anaesthetic
  • Dihydrocodeine as needed

Destination

  • Home same day

Tips

  • Short cases on busy lists: plan relaxant timing so the patient is reversible at the end
