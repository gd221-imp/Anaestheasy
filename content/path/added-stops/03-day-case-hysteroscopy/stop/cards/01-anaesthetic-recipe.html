Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Indication (bleeding, polyps, fibroids)
  • Day-case suitability

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway

Investigations (Ix)

  • Pregnancy test; Hb if heavy bleeding

Optimise

  • Paracetamol and ibuprofen before

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, 20G cannula, LMA
  • Lithotomy

Induction

  • Propofol TCI (e.g. Marsh) with LMA
  • Option: alfentanil 1 mg in 50 ml propofol (20 mcg/ml)

Maintenance

  • Propofol TCI, spontaneous ventilation
  • Dexamethasone 6.6 mg
  • Watch fluid deficit with distension media (fluid overload, hyponatraemia with hypotonic fluids)

Emergence

  • Lower legs together from lithotomy
  • Remove LMA

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Uterine perforation
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Fluid overload and hyponatraemia from distension media
  • Laryngospasm

Delayed

  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol and ibuprofen as needed

Destination

  • Home same day

Tips

  • High-turnover list: short-acting drugs
  • Remifentanil doesn't mix well in propofol syringes; alfentanil is commonly added and works well (check local policy and the TIVA guidelines)
