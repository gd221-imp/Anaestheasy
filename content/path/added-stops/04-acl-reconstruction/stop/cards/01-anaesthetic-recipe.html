Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Usually young and fit
  • Day-case suitability

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none

Optimise

  • Consent for the block
  • Paracetamol and ibuprofen before

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, 20G cannula
  • ETT (or LMA for shorter repairs)
  • Ultrasound for adductor canal block; tourniquet

Induction

  • Fentanyl about 200 mcg, propofol, relaxant if intubating

Maintenance

  • TIVA or volatile
  • Note tourniquet time
  • Dexamethasone and ondansetron

Emergence

  • Extubate awake or remove LMA

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding into the joint
  • Nerve or vessel injury

Delayed

  • Graft failure
  • Infection
  • Stiffness

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Tourniquet pain and hypertension

Delayed

  • VTE
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Adductor canal block: up to 20 ml of 0.25% levobupivacaine
  • Dilute local anaesthetic into the joint by the surgeon
  • Fentanyl and Oramorph in recovery
  • Regular paracetamol, NSAID and codeine to go home

Destination

  • Home same day

Tips

  • Adductor canal block keeps quadriceps strength better than a femoral block
