Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Trigeminal neuralgia: triggers, medication (carbamazepine)
  • Day-case suitability

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway

Investigations (Ix)

  • Sodium if on carbamazepine

Optimise

  • Explain any awake testing if the surgeon needs sensory feedback

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, 20G cannula, LMA
  • Image intensifier

Induction

  • Induce on the table, then rotate it
  • Propofol TIVA with LMA

Maintenance

  • Propofol TIVA; fentanyl about 200 mcg in total
  • Lighten if the surgeon needs the patient to respond during testing
  • Dexamethasone 6.6 mg and ondansetron 4 mg
  • Watch for bradycardia (trigeminocardiac reflex) during needle placement

Emergence

  • Remove LMA
  • Check facial sensation

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Facial numbness, corneal anaesthesia
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Recurrence
  • Anaesthesia dolorosa

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Bradycardia
  • Airway at a distance

Delayed

  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Regular paracetamol; codeine as needed

Destination

  • Home same day; eye protection if the cornea is numb

Tips

  • Short case with the airway away from you
