Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Arthritis or previous trauma; diabetes and neuropathy
  • Day case or overnight

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Neurovascular status of the foot

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none if fit; HbA1c in diabetes

Optimise

  • Consent for the popliteal block
  • Paracetamol before

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, 20G cannula, LMA
  • Ultrasound for popliteal sciatic (± saphenous) block; tourniquet

Induction

  • Propofol TCI with LMA
  • Antibiotics; dexamethasone 6.6 mg and ondansetron 4 mg

Maintenance

  • Propofol TCI
  • Fentanyl 100–200 mcg
  • Note tourniquet time

Emergence

  • Remove LMA awake or deep

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Non-union
  • Wound breakdown

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Malalignment

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • LA toxicity
  • Tourniquet pain

Delayed

  • Rebound pain when the block wears off
  • VTE

Analgesia

  • Popliteal sciatic block, e.g. up to 20 ml of 0.5% levobupivacaine; add saphenous block for the medial side
  • Paracetamol and codeine; plan for rebound pain

Destination

  • Day case or overnight; elevation; VTE prophylaxis (some use aspirin 150 mg daily)

Tips

  • Bone surgery hurts: start oral analgesia before the block wears off
