Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Trigeminal neuralgia or hemifacial spasm; current medication (carbamazepine)
  • Comorbidities

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Neck movement (park bench position)

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E (sodium on carbamazepine), group and save

Optimise

  • Plan positioning with the surgeon

Intra-op

Equipment

  • 18G cannula on the side of surgery (it faces up once positioned)
  • Armoured ETT, taped
  • Depth of anaesthesia monitoring away from the pins
  • Head pins; park bench position

Induction

  • Induce on the trolley, then turn and slide across
  • Propofol and remifentanil TIVA (or fentanyl instead of remifentanil to limit hypotension while you're positioning)
  • Rocuronium 0.5 mg/kg
  • Optional midazolam 2 mg once the cannula is in

Maintenance

  • Propofol and remifentanil TIVA
  • Fentanyl about 250 mcg in total
  • Watch for bradycardia with brainstem or nerve handling
  • Dexamethasone 6.6 mg and ondansetron 4 mg

Emergence

  • Smooth emergence: avoid coughing
  • Early neurological check

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Cerebellar or brainstem injury
  • Hearing loss (VIII)
  • CSF leak

Delayed

  • Facial numbness or weakness
  • Recurrence of pain

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Bradycardia and hypotension (trigeminocardiac reflex)
  • Pressure injury in park bench position

Delayed

  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Fentanyl 0–200 mcg in recovery
  • Regular paracetamol; oral morphine as needed
  • Scalp infiltration

Destination

  • Neurosurgical ward or HDU with neuro observations

Tips

  • 2 to 3 hours with the airway at a distance and the head in pins: plan lines and monitoring before positioning
