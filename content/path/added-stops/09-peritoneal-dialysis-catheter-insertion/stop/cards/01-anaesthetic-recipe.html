Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Cause of renal failure; current dialysis
  • Cardiac disease, diabetes

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Fluid status
  • Fistula arm (protect it)

Investigations (Ix)

  • Recent U&E (potassium)
  • FBC

Optimise

  • Correct potassium
  • Avoid nephrotoxic drugs

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, 18G cannula (lidocaine to skin), ETT

Induction

  • Propofol and remifentanil TIVA, atracurium

Maintenance

  • TIVA; dexamethasone 6.6 mg
  • Fentanyl about 100 mcg
  • Port-site local anaesthetic

Emergence

  • Reverse if needed
  • Venous gas for potassium at the end

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bowel or bladder injury
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Catheter malposition or leak
  • Peritonitis, exit-site infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hyperkalaemia
  • Hypotension

Delayed

  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; fentanyl 0–100 mcg in recovery
  • Avoid NSAIDs; avoid morphine accumulation

Destination

  • Usually a day case

Tips

  • Quick laparoscopic case; patients are often hyperkalaemic, so check electrolytes before and after
