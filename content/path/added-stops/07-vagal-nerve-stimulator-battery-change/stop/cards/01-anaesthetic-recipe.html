Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Seizure types, frequency, and whether they stop on their own
  • Antiepileptic drugs taken today

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none

Optimise

  • Continue antiepileptics
  • Seizure rescue plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, 20G cannula, LMA

Induction

  • Fentanyl about 100 mcg; propofol; LMA
  • Can usually stay on the trolley

Maintenance

  • Propofol and remifentanil TIVA
  • Surgeon infiltrates local anaesthetic

Emergence

  • Remove LMA
  • Check the device is switched back on before discharge

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding, infection
  • Lead damage

Delayed

  • Device failure

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Seizures
  • Laryngospasm

Delayed

  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol before
  • Fentanyl up to 100 mcg in recovery

Destination

  • Home same day

Tips

  • Short case: the battery sits in the chest wall, where an ICD would
  • Stimulation can cause hoarseness, cough or bradycardia
