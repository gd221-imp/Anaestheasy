Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Symptoms of obstruction: stridor, orthopnoea, position of comfort
  • ECMO standby if critical

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Stridor at rest, positional dyspnoea
  • SpO2 on air

Investigations (Ix)

  • CT for level and severity of narrowing

Optimise

  • Plan A to D agreed with the surgeon

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Rigid bronchoscope with ventilating side-port, jet ventilator
  • TIVA with depth monitoring
  • Fibreoptic scope, small ETTs

Induction

  • Gas or careful IV induction maintaining spontaneous ventilation until the airway is secured
  • Ventilate via the rigid scope or with jet ventilation

Maintenance

  • TIVA
  • Stent placement under vision

Emergence

  • Maintain spontaneous ventilation or bag-mask until full reversal
  • Recover sitting up

Post-op

Complications

  • Steroids for oedema
  • Recover sitting up with humidified oxygen
  • Watch for stent migration and bleeding

Surgical

Immediate

  • Airway bleeding
  • Airway perforation
  • Stent malposition

Delayed

  • Stent migration
  • Re-stenosis
  • Mucus plugging

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Complete obstruction at induction
  • Barotrauma with jet ventilation
  • Awareness with TIVA

Delayed

  • Post-op stridor

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; usually minimal pain

Destination

  • HDU or ICU
