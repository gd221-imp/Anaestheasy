1. Increase FiO2 to 1.0, alert the team, ask for help
  2. Bronchoscope to confirm tube position
  3. Clamp/auscultate/hand-bag to assess
  4. Consider bleeding - optimise cardiac output
  5. Recruitment manoeuvre to the ventilated lung / increase PEEP
  6. Recruit the non-ventilated lung, then CPAP of 1-2cmH2O (higher pressures, e.g. the 5-10cmH2O in older texts, over-inflate it and get in the surgeon's way), or O2 insufflation via a suction catheter
  7. Intermittent two-lung ventilation
  8. Clamp the pulmonary artery (if pneumonectomy) / surgically-facilitated lung collapse

In an emergency, go straight to steps 1, 7 and 4.
