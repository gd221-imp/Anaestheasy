Double Lumen Tube

+ most definitive, can ventilate either lung, allows suction/bronchoscopy/CPAP

  • larger and more rigid, difficult in a difficult airway, needs exchanging for ITU, prone to malpositioning

Sizing (left DLT): female <160cm 35Fr, >160cm 37Fr; male <170cm 39Fr, >170cm 41Fr (consider 32Fr/37Fr for very short patients)

Position confirmed clinically (auscultation/inflation of cuffs) and then by bronchoscopy
