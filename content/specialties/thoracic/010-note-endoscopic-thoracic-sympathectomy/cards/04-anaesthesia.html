Pre-op: consider intra-op wake-up test if operating for angina, wide-bore IV access, a trained assistant, full AAGBI monitoring, +/- arterial line

Intra-op: DLT gives the best surgical access but increases atelectasis/shunt/hypoxaemia risk with bilateral surgery; single lumen tube with intrathoracic CO2 insufflation is an alternative (often first choice); bronchial blocker is another option
