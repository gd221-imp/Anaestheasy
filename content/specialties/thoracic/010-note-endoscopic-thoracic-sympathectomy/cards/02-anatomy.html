Thoracolumbar sympathetic outflow

  • Preganglionic sympathetic neurons: lateral horn (T1-L3), motor neurons synapse in the paravertebral (sympathetic) ganglion chain via white rami communicantes
  • Preganglionic fibres to the adrenal medulla pass through the chain without synapsing (splanchnic nerves) and release ACh onto chromaffin cells, which act as modified postganglionic neurons
  • Other postganglionic neurons (mostly noradrenergic) travel via grey rami communicantes (rejoining around T1-L4 level) to their targets
  • Longer sympathetic pathways: head & neck T1-2, upper limb T2-5, thoracic viscera T1-4
