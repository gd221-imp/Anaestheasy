Surgical

Immediate

  • From insufflation: tension pneumothorax, surgical emphysema, raised blood pressure
  • From bleeding: conversion to open surgery, damage to major vessels

Delayed

  • Persistent pneumothorax
  • Horner's syndrome
  • Compensatory sweating elsewhere

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • From the DLT: malposition, hypoxia during OLV
  • From positioning: tube dislodgement, eye damage, brachial plexus injury

Delayed

  • Acute lung injury
  • DVT
