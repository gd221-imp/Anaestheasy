Endobronchial/bronchoscopic options for severe COPD (high-risk, non-surgical treatment):

  • Endobronchial valves - allow air out but not in; ineffective if there is collateral ventilation
  • Coils
  • Biological LVR / sealants
  • Thermal vapour ablation

LVRS = a palliative procedure in severe COPD, working by reducing dynamic hyperinflation and increasing elastic recoil of the remaining lung
