Post-op dyspnoea assessed via spirometry (FEV1) and DLCO (diffusion capacity, lung parenchyma)

Predicted post-op (ppo) value = pre-op value x (19 - number of segments resected) / 19

When compared to normal predicted: ppoFEV1 and ppoDLCO >40% is acceptable for surgery (the same threshold applies to pneumonectomy); <30-40% marks increased risk

(BTS guidance) if <40% -> further investigation:

  • >30% predicted: shuttle walk test (pass if >400m), 6-minute walk >400m, or stair climb (pass if >22m; 2 flights without stopping is the minimum)
  • <30% predicted: CPET - VO2max >20ml/kg/min few complications, <15 high risk, <10 very high risk

High risk = non-surgical treatment, or needs pre-op counselling regarding LTOT and dyspnoea

Medium risk = surgical treatment appropriate

Ultimately = MDT discussion and decision

Cancer staging -> CT/PET -> if positive lymph nodes -> EBUS/mediastinoscopy -> proceed to surgery if T<3 N<1 M0
