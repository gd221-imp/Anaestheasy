30-day mortality 5-10%

Contraindications: failed physiological testing, pulmonary hypertension, severe valvular disease, metastatic disease

Indications: trauma / infection / inflammatory disease / congenital disease / cancer

Optimise: smoking cessation, treat lung disease/infection, exercise, nutrition, treat anaemia, treat comorbidities

Complications: arrhythmias (30-50% after lung resection, 60-70% of them AF; ~60% after pneumonectomy, ~40% after lobectomy), pulmonary hypertension with right heart failure (a small remaining lung with a high cardiac output raises pulmonary vascular pressure), pulmonary oedema (higher risk after right pneumonectomy, >50% mortality), bronchopleural fistula, cardiac herniation, nerve damage (phrenic or recurrent laryngeal)

Note: a left-sided DLT is generally used for a right pneumonectomy; if a bronchial blocker is used it should be withdrawn before stapling
