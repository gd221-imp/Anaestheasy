• 2 bronchial arteries to the left lung, from the descending thoracic aorta
  • 1 bronchial artery to the right lung, from a right bronchial or intercostal artery
  • Venous drainage: mostly via the pulmonary veins; bronchial veins (around 15%) drain to the azygous vein (right side) or hemiazygous/intercostal veins (left side)

Causes: TB, trauma, iatrogenic, vasculitis, pulmonary-renal syndromes, bronchiectasis, cancer or abscess of the lung
