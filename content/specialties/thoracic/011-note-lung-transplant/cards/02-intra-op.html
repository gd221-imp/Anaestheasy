• Aim to limit donor lung ischaemic time to ~4h; longer times (often quoted >5.5-6h) increase the risk of primary graft dysfunction (PGD)
  • VA ECMO is increasingly used instead of CPB intra-operatively
  • Reperfusion: release the PA clamp slowly, increase FiO2, give an IV fluid bolus
  • Test the new lung with one-lung ventilation: PEEP 8-10, tidal volume 3-4ml/kg, target SpO2 >90%
  • Post-implantation: fluid restriction to reduce PGD risk
