Single lung transplant = idiopathic interstitial pneumonia / COPD; bilateral (most common) = suppurative disease (CF/bronchiectasis)

Selection: >50% mortality risk within 2 years from the underlying lung disease, and generally >80% probability of 5-year survival post-transplant

Pre-op: pulmonary rehab, optimise BMI, cardiology input, PFTs, V/Q scan, CT chest, TTE, PA catheter
