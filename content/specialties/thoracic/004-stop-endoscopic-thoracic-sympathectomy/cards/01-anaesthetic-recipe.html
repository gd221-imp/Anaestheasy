Intra-op

Equipment

  • Double-lumen tube or bronchial blocker, arterial line optional, defib pads

Induction

  • Often bilateral, so expect OLV on each side in turn

Maintenance

  • GA with OLV
  • CO₂ insufflation in chest can cause hypotension

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • From insufflation: tension pneumothorax, surgical emphysema, raised blood pressure
  • From bleeding: conversion to open surgery, damage to major vessels

Delayed

  • Persistent pneumothorax
  • Horner's syndrome
  • Compensatory sweating elsewhere

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • From the DLT: malposition, hypoxia during OLV
  • From positioning: tube dislodgement, eye damage, brachial plexus injury

Delayed

  • Acute lung injury
  • DVT

Analgesia

—

Destination

  • CXR for pneumothorax, simple analgesia, day case or overnight
