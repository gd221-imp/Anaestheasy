Primary (absence of lung disease, e.g. smokers/Marfan's) or secondary (with lung disease, e.g. asthma/COPD/cancer/fibrosis)

Iatrogenic: central line insertion, PPV, thoracic procedures - watch for a slow bleed into a thin chest drain

Traumatic: managed with lung-protective ventilation; secondary prevention is medical or surgical (stripping of the parietal pleura)

Pneumomediastinum: causes include GI rupture or alveolar rupture

Subcutaneous emphysema
