The innominate artery crosses the trachea around the 6th-9th tracheal rings; cuff pressure against the wall can cause necrosis, erosion, and haemoptysis

Risk factors: tall patient, low tracheostomy placement, steroid use, a rigid or high-riding tracheostomy

Treatment: hyperinflate the tracheal cuff for tamponade, insert an ETT beyond the fistula/into the good lung, surgical exploration and ligation
