Indications: cancer, tracheal stenosis, tracheobronchial strictures, tracheobronchomalacia

Pre-op: optimise comorbidities, imaging (CT), PFTs (flow-volume loops)

Intra-op: GA, +/- arterial line, TIVA; flexible bronchoscopy uses an LMA/ETT/THRIVE; rigid bronchoscopy uses jet ventilation

In subsequent procedures with a stent already in place: use an LMA first, then intubate under fibreoptic guidance if needed - avoid dislodging the stent
