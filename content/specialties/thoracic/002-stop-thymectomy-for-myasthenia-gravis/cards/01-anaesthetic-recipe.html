Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Neurology review: disease control, pyridostigmine, steroids, IVIg or plasma exchange before surgery

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Bulbar function (swallow, cough, voice)
  • Ptosis and fatigable weakness

Investigations (Ix)

  • CT for mediastinal mass and airway compression

Optimise

  • Bulbar and respiratory function (vital capacity)

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, arterial line
  • Quantitative neuromuscular monitoring
  • Sugammadex available

Induction

  • Avoid or minimise neuromuscular blockers (TIVA or volatile alone often enough)

Maintenance

  • if used, small doses titrated to monitoring
  • VATS needs one-lung ventilation
  • sternotomy for open
  • Continue anticholinesterase plan agreed with neurology

Emergence

  • Extubate only with full strength (train-of-four ratio above 0.9)
  • Sit up; monitor vital capacity

Post-op

Complications

  • Extubate when fully reversed and strong
  • Watch for myasthenic or cholinergic crisis
  • Restart pyridostigmine promptly

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Phrenic nerve injury
  • Pneumothorax

Delayed

  • Incomplete thymic resection
  • Wound infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Prolonged neuromuscular block
  • Respiratory failure needing ventilation

Delayed

  • Myasthenic crisis
  • Cholinergic crisis

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol
  • Regional techniques to limit opioids
  • Opioids cautiously (respiratory weakness)

Destination

  • HDU
