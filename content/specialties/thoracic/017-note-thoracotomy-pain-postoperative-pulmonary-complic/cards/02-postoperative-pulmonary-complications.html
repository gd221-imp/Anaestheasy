Risk increases with duration of surgery, degree of handling, and fluid shifts; reduced by lung-protective ventilation, goal-directed fluid therapy, and optimising the patient pre-op

  • Atelectasis
  • Pneumonia
  • Pneumothorax
  • Pleural effusion
  • Respiratory failure
  • Hypoventilation
  • Pulmonary oedema

Other Postoperative Complications

  • Cardiac arrhythmias (30-50% after lung resection, ~60% after pneumonectomy; mostly AF)
  • Cardiac herniation - high mortality
