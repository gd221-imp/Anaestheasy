1. Ipsilateral shoulder pain - due to irritation of the pleura/phrenic nerve; treat with NSAIDs/paracetamol, an intercostal block, withdrawing the chest drain slightly, or a phrenic nerve block
  2. Somatic pain - from the intercostal nerves, worse with muscle-involving surgical approaches
  3. Visceral pain - via the phrenic nerve and autonomic nervous system

Paravertebral block is generally first choice (requires intact pleura)

Chronic post-thoracotomy pain: at 6 months around 33% after thoracotomy and 25% after VATS have ongoing pain along the scar; linked to the severity of acute pain
