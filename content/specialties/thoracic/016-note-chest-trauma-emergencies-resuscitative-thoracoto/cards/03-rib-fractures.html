Big issues: hypoventilation, reduced gas exchange, altered chest wall mechanics

Paravertebral block typically covers around 6 rib levels (roughly T2-9 depending on placement)

Consider surgical fixation if: flail segment present, already undergoing thoracotomy for another reason, or refractory pain

Scoring systems for rib fracture risk/morbidity: EASTER score (age + (number of rib fractures x sides affected)), BATTLE score, and STUMBL score
