• Erosion of the tracheostomy tube (cuff or tip) through the anterior tracheal wall into the innominate (brachiocephalic) artery
  • Rare (under 1% of tracheostomies) but often fatal; most occur 1-6 weeks after tracheostomy (peak days 7-14)
  • Risk factors: low tracheostomy (below the 3rd-4th ring), high cuff pressures, tube movement, infection, steroids, radiotherapy, a high-riding innominate artery
  • Usually preceded by a sentinel bleed - any bright red bleeding from a tracheostomy after 48h needs urgent assessment
