1. Call for help (ENT/thoracic/vascular surgeons, senior anaesthetist), 100% O2, major haemorrhage protocol
  2. Hyperinflate the tracheostomy cuff to tamponade the bleeding point
  3. If bleeding continues: remove the tracheostomy tube, intubate orally with the cuff beyond the stoma, and apply digital compression - a finger through the stoma pressing the artery forwards against the back of the sternum (Utley manoeuvre)
  4. Protect the airway and the other lung; suction; resuscitate with blood products
  5. Definitive treatment in theatre: sternotomy and ligation/resection of the innominate artery, or endovascular stent as a bridge
