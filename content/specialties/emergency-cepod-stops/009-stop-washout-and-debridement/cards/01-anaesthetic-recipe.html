Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Antibiotic history
  • Trauma related?
  • Any infection control issues?

Examination (O/E)

  • Any other injuries?
  • Blood sugar if diabetic

Investigations (Ix)

  • Sepsis, previous anaesthetic charts, bloods

Optimise

  • Fasted: sip til send
  • Analgesia before surgery
  • Analgesic block

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access, LMA or ETT

Induction

  • Consider ketamine for analgesia
  • Consider regional anaesthesia if extended debridement

Maintenance

  • Repeat GA: check what worked before
  • Likely volatile

Emergence

  • Usual; can transfer on an i-gel to recovery if breathing spontaneously

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Incomplete debridement

Delayed

  • Ongoing infection needing re-look
  • Wound healing problems

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Haemodynamic instability in sepsis
  • Awareness or inadequate analgesia with repeated short anaesthetics

Delayed

  • Opioid tolerance with repeated procedures
  • Acute kidney injury

Analgesia

  • Plan analgesia for dressing changes, e.g. Oramorph before the change

Destination

  • ward or ICU depending on sepsis

Tips

  • Patients may be septic and come back for repeated trips to theatre.
  • Check the last anaesthetic chart for what worked.
  • Dressing changes and washouts are painful; plan analgesia beyond theatre.
