Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Incomplete debridement

Delayed

  • Ongoing infection needing re-look
  • Wound healing problems

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Haemodynamic instability in sepsis
  • Awareness or inadequate analgesia with repeated short anaesthetics

Delayed

  • Opioid tolerance with repeated procedures
  • Acute kidney injury

Analgesia

  • Plan analgesia for dressing changes

Destination

  • ward or ICU depending on sepsis

Tips

  • Patients may be septic and come back for repeated trips to theatre.
  • Check the last anaesthetic chart for what worked.
  • Dressing changes and washouts are painful; plan analgesia beyond theatre.
