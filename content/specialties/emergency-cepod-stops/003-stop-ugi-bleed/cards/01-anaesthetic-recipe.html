Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Haematemesis or melaena; previous bleeds; try to quantify blood loss
  • Syncope
  • Anticoagulants and antiplatelets: need reversal?
  • NSAID use?
  • Liver disease, varices, alcohol

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway and GCS
  • Haemodynamic status (shock index)
  • Signs of chronic liver disease

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E (raised urea), LFTs, clotting
  • VBG with lactate and Hb
  • Crossmatch; activate major haemorrhage protocol if needed
  • Blatchford and Rockall scores

Optimise

  • Resuscitation first, Glasgow-Blatchford score, major haemorrhage protocol, antibiotics, PPI
  • Terlipressin and antibiotics if variceal (local guidelines)
  • The medical team calls the on-call gastroenterologist, who mobilises the endoscopy team; they can take up to an hour to be ready in theatre

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Large-bore access ×2, blood, suction ×2 in case one becomes clotted
  • Consider a videolaryngoscope, balancing the risk of blood obscuring the camera

Induction

  • RSI with ETT if active bleeding or reduced GCS
  • Insert the mouthpiece for the OGD scope; cutting one side lets the ETT stay fairly central

Maintenance

  • TIVA
  • Arterial line; vasopressor ready
  • Blood products guided by gases and viscoelastic testing (TEG)
  • Warming
  • Keep an eye on the ETT: it can move as the operator moves the scope

Emergence

  • Suction the stomach and oropharynx
  • Extubate awake only if bleeding controlled and stable; otherwise ventilate to ICU

Post-op

Complications

  • watch for re-bleeding

Surgical

Immediate

  • Re-bleeding
  • Perforation at endoscopy

Delayed

  • Re-bleeding needing surgery or radiology
  • Ischaemia after embolisation

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration of blood at induction
  • Cardiovascular collapse at induction
  • Complications of massive transfusion

Delayed

  • Hepatic encephalopathy in chronic liver disease
  • Acute kidney injury

Analgesia

  • Usually minimal pain
  • Paracetamol (reduce dose in liver disease); avoid NSAIDs

Destination

  • HDU
