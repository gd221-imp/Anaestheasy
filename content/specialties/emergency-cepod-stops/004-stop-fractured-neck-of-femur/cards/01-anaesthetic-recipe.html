Hip fracture

Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Mechanism: why did they fall? (A–E causes): mechanical or cardiac-related
  • Comorbidities, frailty, cognition (AMT)
  • Anticoagulants and antiplatelets
  • Functional baseline and residence

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Murmur (aortic stenosis)
  • Chest, hydration and cognition
  • Back for spinal

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC (Hb above 90 g/L, or 100 g/L with IHD), U&E, clotting, group and save
  • ECG
  • CXR
  • Echo only if it would change management; don't delay surgery
  • Nottingham Hip Fracture Score

Optimise

  • Theatre within 36 hours
  • Cognition
  • Anticoagulants
  • Fascia iliaca block

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access, spinal or GA kit
  • Consider a vasopressor infusion from the start in patients with more cardiovascular comorbidity

Induction

  • Spinal (no sedation or light sedation) or GA

Maintenance

  • Avoid hypotension
  • Watch for BCIS when cementing: some increase FiO₂ and support blood pressure before cementing (see the BCIS note)

Emergence

  • Minimal sedation; no benzodiazepines
  • Extubate awake if GA
  • Check sensory level and haemodynamics in recovery

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Malposition of the fixation

Delayed

  • Dislocation
  • Infection
  • Failure of fixation

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Bone cement implantation syndrome
  • Hypotension under spinal or GA
  • Over-sedation

Delayed

  • Delirium
  • VTE
  • Pneumonia
  • Pressure sores
  • Acute kidney injury
  • 30-day mortality

Analgesia

  • Regular paracetamol, low-dose opioid, delirium screening, early mobilisation

Destination

  • Orthogeriatric ward; HDU if high risk
  • Delirium screening and early mobilisation

Tips

  • Aim to operate on the day of, or the day after, admission.
  • Spinal or general anaesthesia, plus a fascia iliaca block.
  • Avoid hypotension in older patients.
  • With cemented hemiarthroplasty, watch for bone cement implantation syndrome: hypoxia and hypotension as the cement goes in.
