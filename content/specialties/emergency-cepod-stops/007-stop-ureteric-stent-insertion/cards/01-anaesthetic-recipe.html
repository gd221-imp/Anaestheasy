Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Pain score now
  • Underlying conditions that predispose to kidney stones: hyperparathyroidism, gout and hyperuricaemia, renal tubular acidosis, cystinuria, inflammatory bowel disease or bowel resection, sarcoidosis, recurrent UTI (struvite stones), dehydration, obesity and diabetes

Examination (O/E)

  • Usual anaesthetic examination, plus examination for any underlying disease

Investigations (Ix)

  • Often an obstructed, infected kidney: sepsis, renal function, potassium, antibiotics

Optimise

  • Pain control before surgery
  • Keep fasted but sip til send
  • Pregnancy test before X-ray screening

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access, LMA, lead apron

Induction

  • Fentanyl, propofol and LMA in lithotomy

Maintenance

  • Support BP if septic
  • Volatile unless TIVA is indicated

Emergence

  • Can transfer on a T-piece if breathing spontaneously

Post-op

Complications

  • escalate if septic shock

Surgical

Immediate

  • Ureteric perforation
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Stent migration or encrustation
  • Stent symptoms (pain, frequency)
  • Urosepsis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension in septic shock
  • Lithotomy nerve injury

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury

Analgesia

  • Usual post-operative anaesthetic care

Destination

  • Ward with fluids and antibiotics
