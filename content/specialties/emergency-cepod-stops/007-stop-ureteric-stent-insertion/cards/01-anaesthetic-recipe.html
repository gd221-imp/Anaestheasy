Post-op

Complications

  • escalate if septic shock

Surgical

Immediate

  • Ureteric perforation
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Stent migration or encrustation
  • Stent symptoms (pain, frequency)
  • Urosepsis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension in septic shock
  • Lithotomy nerve injury

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury

Analgesia

—

Destination

  • Ward with fluids and antibiotics

Tips

  • Often an obstructed, infected kidney: the patient may be septic and need urgent source control.
  • Cystoscopy in lithotomy, usually under a laryngeal mask.
  • Check renal function and potassium, and that antibiotics have been given.
  • X-ray screening is used, so wear lead.
