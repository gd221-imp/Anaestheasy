Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Cerebral hypoperfusion in the beach-chair position
  • Bezold-Jarisch reflex (bradycardia and hypotension)
  • Phrenic nerve palsy from interscalene block
  • Venous air embolism

Delayed

  • Persistent nerve injury
  • Post-op pain when the block wears off
