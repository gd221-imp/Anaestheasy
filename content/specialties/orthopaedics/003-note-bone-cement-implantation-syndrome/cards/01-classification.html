Grade 1 = moderate hypoxaemia (SpO2 <94%) or hypotension (↓SBP >20%): mild hypoxia.

Grade 2 = severe hypoxaemia (SpO2 <88%) or hypotension (↓SBP >40%): severe.

Grade 3 = CVS collapse requiring CPR.

Pathophysiology: ↑PAP + V/Q mismatch → RV strain → ↓RV output → ↓LV compliance → ↓LV cardiac output.
