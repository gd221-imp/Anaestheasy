Avoids GA, cheap, reliable, rapid recovery, bloodless field, motor & sensory block within 15min.

Disadvantages: <60min duration (no post-op analgesia), LA toxicity risk; compartment syndrome, thromboembolism, cuff nerve injury.

Cuff time: keep the cuff inflated for at least 20-25 minutes after injection even if surgery is shorter (Miller >=25 min; UK practice often 20 min); deflate in stages and watch for LA toxicity after release.

Absolute contraindications: Raynaud's, Buerger's disease, AV fistula, LA allergy, patient refusal.
