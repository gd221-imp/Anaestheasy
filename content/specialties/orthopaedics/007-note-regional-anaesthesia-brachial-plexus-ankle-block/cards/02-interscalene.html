At root/trunk level. Risks: phrenic nerve block (near 100%), Horner's syndrome, recurrent laryngeal nerve block, C8-T1 often not blocked (ulnar-sparing). Good for shoulder surgery.

Supraclavicular: at trunk/division level. Risk of pneumothorax and phrenic nerve block. Suits arm, forearm and hand surgery; may spare the suprascapular nerve, so less reliable for shoulder surgery.

Infraclavicular: at cord level.

Axillary: at branch level. Requires multiple injections/punctures; good for forearm/hand surgery.
