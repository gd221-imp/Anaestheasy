TMJ involvement, C-spine ankylosis, cricoarytenoid involvement → pre-op airway assessment, assess disease severity; peri-op difficult airway equipment, awake fibreoptic intubation; post-op raised risk of airway obstruction from oedema.

B: apical fibrosis, costovertebral joint involvement → restrictive lung defect; kyphosis → post-op CPAP/NIV.

C: conduction disorder (Purkinje fibrosis), valve disease (aortic fibrosis), raised risk of IHD → pre-op ECHO, ECG, FBC/U&E/clotting/group & save.

D: peripheral nerve impingement → document neuro deficit; subluxation/# risking spinal cord damage/cauda equina syndrome → careful positioning, neuraxial difficult (calcified interspinous ligaments).

E: anterior uveitis → pre-op extra-articular features.

Spinal XR: normal → ossification of interspinous ligaments + bony bridge formation → "bamboo spine".
