Post-op delirium → increased risk with previous delirium/dementia, age >70, alcohol abuse, visual or hearing impairment, HTN, family history.

Management: minimise ABx, multimodal analgesia, avoid CO2/oxygen extremes, keep hydrated, normo-physiology.

N.B. DOLS (Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards): confined to a restricted space, lacks capacity, continuous supervision, not free to leave.
