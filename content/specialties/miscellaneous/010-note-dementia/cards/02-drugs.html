Anticholinesterases - donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine → NMBA/reversal interaction, ↑S/E of anticholinergics/DA agonists.

NMDA-R antagonist - memantine → risk of serotonin syndrome.

SSRI - citalopram → bleeding risk.

Herbal medication - ginkgo biloba → increased bleeding risk.

Antipsychotics - risperidone → prolonged QT/depression of major surgery risk.
