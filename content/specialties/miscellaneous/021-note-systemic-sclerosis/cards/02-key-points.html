Limited (skin distal to elbow/knee & organs) or Diffuse (proximal skin, elbow/knee & organs).

CP: calcinosis, Raynaud's, oesophageal dysmotility, sclerodactyly, telangiectasia (CREST).

B: pulmonary artery hypertension → cor pulmonale/ILD/aspiration pneumonia.

C: myositis/cardiac fibrosis - conduction defects/CAD.

E: scleroderma renal crisis (accelerated HTN & renal failure), microangiopathic haemolytic anaemia, anaemia of chronic disease, antiphospholipid syndrome association.
