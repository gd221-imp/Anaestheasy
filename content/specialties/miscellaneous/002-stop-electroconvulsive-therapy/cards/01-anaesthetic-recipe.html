Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Recent MI or stroke, raised ICP
  • Previous ECT anaesthetics
  • Medication: lithium, MAOIs

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway and dentition
  • Cardiovascular examination

Investigations (Ix)

  • ECG
  • U&E if on lithium

Optimise

  • Consent, cardiovascular risk
  • Can consider checking previous ECT charts for doses

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Bite block, monitoring

Induction

  • Propofol or etomidate, suxamethonium
  • Can consider alfentanil 250–500 mcg (blunts the sympathetic surge), propofol 0.75–2.5 mg/kg and suxamethonium 0.5–1 mg/kg
  • Can consider methohexital, which lowers the seizure threshold but is rarely used now (cost)

Maintenance

  • ventilate
  • Can consider hyperventilating before the stimulus

Emergence

  • Recover in lateral position until awake
  • Treat agitation

Post-op

Complications

  • Recover until awake

Surgical

Immediate

  • Prolonged seizure
  • Dental and tongue injury

Delayed

  • Memory loss and confusion
  • Headache

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Bradycardia then tachycardia and hypertension
  • Aspiration
  • Suxamethonium apnoea or myalgia

Delayed

  • Post-ictal agitation
  • Myalgia

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol for headache or myalgia

Destination

  • Back to the psychiatric ward once recovered
