Specialist MDT.

Alpha-blockade for at least 10-14 days (often longer) before surgery → then beta-blockade only once alpha-blockade is established / arrhythmia control / correct BM & electrolytes.

Phenoxybenzamine or doxazosin (α-blockers); metyrosine (blocks catecholamine synthesis); atenolol/metoprolol (selective β1) - start only after alpha-blockade is established (unopposed alpha stimulation otherwise) and continue up to surgery.

Optimise myocardial function → ECG/ECHO, restore blood volume → salt & fluid intake.
