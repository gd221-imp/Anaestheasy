Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • involve carers
  • Comorbidities: epilepsy, cardiac disease, reflux

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway (may only be possible after induction)
  • Cardiorespiratory examination if cooperation allows

Investigations (Ix)

  • Take bloods once asleep if needed
  • Antiepileptic levels if relevant

Optimise

  • Best interests decision and Mental Capacity Act assessment
  • Reasonable adjustments: quiet area, first on list, carers present

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring
  • nasal ETT
  • Throat pack
  • Premedication options

Induction

  • IV or gas induction

Maintenance

  • Premedication if needed (oral midazolam)
  • Use the opportunity for bloods or other procedures (combined care)
  • Throat pack documented

Emergence

  • Remove throat pack and suction under vision
  • Extubate awake, lateral

Post-op

Complications

  • Throat pack removed and checked
  • Recover with carers present

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Damage to adjacent teeth

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Dry socket

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Laryngospasm
  • Difficult airway
  • Aspiration

Delayed

  • Distress and challenging behaviour
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia

Destination

  • Day case unit with carers; overnight if comorbid or bleeding
