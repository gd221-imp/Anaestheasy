Single amino acid substitution (pseudocholinesterase deficiency).

4 alleles (usual, atypical/dibucaine-resistant, fluoride-resistant, silent). Genotypes: usual homozygote ~96% (normal dibucaine number 70-80); heterozygous atypical ~4% (Miller 1 in 480; DN 50-60, block prolonged by 50-100%); homozygous atypical ~1 in 2000-3200 (DN 20-30, block 4-8hrs → won't wean off ventilator); silent homozygotes very rare (~1 in 100,000).

2° causes: non-specific plasma esterase pathway.
