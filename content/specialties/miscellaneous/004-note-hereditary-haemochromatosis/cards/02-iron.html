O2 transport (Hb & myoglobin), catalyst (cytochromes/peroxidase enzymes).

Normal diet = 15mg of iron per day → 1-2mg absorbed in duodenum.

No mechanism for excretion, therefore controlled by absorption.

↑Iron = ↑hepcidin → inhibits ferroportin on basolateral membrane of enterocyte → ↓absorption.

↓Iron = ↓hepcidin → ↑absorption.

In plasma: Fe2+ → Fe3+ via transferrin → bone marrow, excess stored as ferritin.

Affected organs: liver (cirrhosis), heart (cardiomyopathy), pancreas (DM), skin/joints, gonads/thyroid/pituitary (reduced function).

Dx: ↑ferritin, ↑transferrin saturation.

Pre-op: ?HF/NYHA, ECHO, recent cardiology letter, discuss with haematology.

Bloods: Hb, glucose, HbA1c, TFTs, LFTs, iron studies. ?recent venesection.
