Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Symptoms: headache, sweating, palpitations
  • Duration of alpha and beta blockade
  • Associated syndromes (MEN 2)

Examination (O/E)

  • Postural hypotension (adequate alpha blockade)
  • Nasal stuffiness
  • Cardiac examination

Investigations (Ix)

  • Metanephrines, imaging
  • Echo (catecholamine cardiomyopathy)
  • FBC (haematocrit falls with blockade), U&E, glucose

Optimise

  • Alpha-blockade first (phenoxybenzamine), then beta-blockade

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line before induction, central line, vasodilators and vasopressors

Induction

  • Smooth, deep induction with opioid; avoid triggers (e.g. ketamine, ephedrine)
  • Arterial line before induction

Maintenance

  • GA
  • control hypertension during tumour handling
  • expect hypotension after removal

Emergence

  • Extubate if stable
  • Glucose monitoring

Post-op

Complications

  • watch hypoglycaemia

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Adrenal vein ligation causing sudden hypotension

Delayed

  • Persistent hypertension
  • Adrenal insufficiency (bilateral)

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypertensive crisis on induction or tumour handling
  • Arrhythmias
  • Hypotension after tumour removal

Delayed

  • Hypoglycaemia
  • ICU admission

Analgesia

  • Epidural or PCA
  • Paracetamol

Destination

  • HDU
