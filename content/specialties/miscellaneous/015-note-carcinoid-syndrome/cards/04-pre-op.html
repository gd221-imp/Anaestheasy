Specialist centre, octreotide → somatostatin analogue → reduces vasoactive hormone release. 100μg SC TDS 2/52 or IV infusion 50-100μg/hr.

ECG & ECHO → assess right heart & valves; treatment of bronchodilators, treat HF, correct electrolytes, and PFTs.

INTRA-OP: IABP, avoid histamine release drugs, severe hypotension = 10-20 micrograms octreotide IV, severe hypertension = esmolol/labetalol.

POST-OP: epidural, multimodal analgesia, ICU, wean octreotide over 7-10days, avoid histamine-releasing drugs.

N.B. avoid metaraminol → risk of carcinoid crisis.
