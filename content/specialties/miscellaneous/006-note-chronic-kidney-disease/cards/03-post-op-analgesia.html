1. avoid NSAIDs. 2) avoid opioid morphine (↓RBF, ↑bleeding, accumulation of morphine-6-glucuronide). 3) avoid tramadol (~30% excreted unchanged; its active metabolite O-desmethyltramadol is renally cleared and accumulates - seizure risk). 4) care with renal drugs.

(↓BP = ↓RBF - cannot give fluids freely.)
