Progressive supranuclear palsy/multisystem atrophy/corticobasal degeneration)

= loss of dopaminergic neurons in the pars compacta of the substantia nigra.

Bradykinesia / muscular rigidity / resting tremor (assoc. with dementia & autonomic dysfunction in some subtypes).

Anaesthetic considerations:

A: upper airway dysfunction → failure to clear secretions/fixed flexed C-spine.

B: restrictive pulmonary deficit.
