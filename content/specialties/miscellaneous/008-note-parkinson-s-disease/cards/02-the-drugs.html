DA precursor (L-DOPA), DA decarboxylase inhibitor (carbidopa), DA agonists (pramipexole/ropinirole/apomorphine/bromocriptine), anti-AChE (rivastigmine), MAO-B inhibitors (selegiline), COMT inhibitors (entacapone), glutamate antagonist (amantadine).
