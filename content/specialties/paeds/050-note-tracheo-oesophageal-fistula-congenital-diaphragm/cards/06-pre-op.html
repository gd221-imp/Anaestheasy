Optimise - intubation and ventilation ± nitric oxide, NGT, assess for associated conditions, echo.

Intra-op: open or thoracoscopic repair (↑cardiovascular instability, ↓SaO2 with thoracoscopic); difficult ventilation, invasive arterial BP monitoring, lung protective ventilation (peak inspiratory pressure <25cmH2O), avoid N2O, avoid bag-mask ventilation (reduces further gastric/bowel inflation).

Post-op: ventilation for 24hrs, thoracic epidural, risk of pulmonary hypertensive crisis from acidosis/hypoxia - treatment: FiO2 1.0, fluid balance, prostacyclin, IV sildenafil, nitric oxide, ECMO, prostaglandin E (maintains PDA, supporting right ventricle by allowing right-to-left shunting) - watch for ↑peak inspiratory pressure, ↓PaO2/↑PaCO2, ↓BP, ↑lactate.
