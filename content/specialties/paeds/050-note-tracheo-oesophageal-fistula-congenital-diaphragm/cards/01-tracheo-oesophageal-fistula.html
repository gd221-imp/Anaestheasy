Definition: defective embryological development of the trachea and oesophagus - possible defective sonic hedgehog gene.

Associated syndromes: DiGeorge, Treacher-Collins, Trisomy 18/13/21, VACTERL association, (Fanconi) CHARGE syndrome - up to ~50% have an associated anomaly.

Classification (Gross): Type C (III) = most common, proximal oesophageal atresia with distal fistula; other types (a) proximal, (b) distal, (c) proximal+distal; Type E (H-type fistula, no atresia).

Clinical presentation: antenatal - polyhydramnios/absent stomach bubble/large oesophageal pouch; postnatal - excess salivation, choking/coughing on feeding, cyanosis on feeding, respiratory distress, failure to pass an NG tube.

Investigations: CXR with a coiled NG tube in the upper pouch; also assess whole abdomen for ?duodenal atresia.

Management: surgery within 24hrs of delivery due to risk of aspiration = ligation of the fistula and anastomosis repair (open or thoracoscopic).
