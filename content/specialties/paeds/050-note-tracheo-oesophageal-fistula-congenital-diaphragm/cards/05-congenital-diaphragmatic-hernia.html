NOT a surgical emergency. 30% mortality due to lung hypoplasia, abnormal pulmonary vasculature, pulmonary hypertension.

Classification: A - small, B - <50% of the chest wall without diaphragmatic tissue, C - >50% chest wall, D - complete absence of hemidiaphragm.

Diagnosis: antenatal (USS, ± fetal intervention), postnatal - tachypnoea, cyanosis → CXR, bowel loops seen in the chest in >85% (left-sided more common).
