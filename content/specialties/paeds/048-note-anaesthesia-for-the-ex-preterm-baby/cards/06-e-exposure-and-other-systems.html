Gastrointestinal

  • Significant reflux from ↓oesophageal motility, ↓gastric emptying and an underdeveloped lower oesophageal sphincter → risk of aspiration

Renal

  • ↓eGFR
  • Immature renal tubular function

Haematological

  • Blood volume ~90 ml/kg
  • HbF 80% at birth
  • Vitamin K: risk of haemorrhagic disease of the newborn (vitamin K deficiency bleeding); check vitamin K has been given

Pharmacological

  • ↑Vd for hydrophilic drugs
  • ↓Plasma protein binding, therefore ↓protein-bound drug fraction
  • Immature liver enzymes/pathways

Skin

  • Thin skin

Anaesthetic implications

  • Limit fluid fasting
  • Replace maintenance and insensible losses
  • Monitor Hb

Fluid management (term babies, NICE NG29)

  • Day 1: 50–60 ml/kg/day 10% glucose
  • Day 2: 70–80 ml/kg/day 10% glucose with 0.45% NaCl and K⁺ 1–2 mmol/kg/day
  • Day 3: 80–100 ml/kg/day 10% glucose with 0.45% NaCl and K⁺ 1–2 mmol/kg/day
  • Day 4: 100–120 ml/kg/day 10% glucose with 0.45% NaCl and K⁺ 1–2 mmol/kg/day
  • Days 5–28: 120–150 ml/kg/day

NICE NG29: IV fluid therapy in children and young people ↗

Preterm babies: discuss with NICU.
