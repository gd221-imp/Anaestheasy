Anatomical

  • Large head, prominent occiput
  • Short neck
  • Floppy U-shaped epiglottis
  • Short, compliant trachea

Ex-preterm specific

  • Previous prolonged intubation/ventilation may cause laryngotracheomalacia or subglottic stenosis
  • Increased upper airway soft tissue compliance = increased risk of obstruction

Anaesthetic implications

  • Avoid flexion/hyperextension during face mask ventilation
  • Use a straight blade
  • ETT sizes: 2.5 mm <2 kg, 3 mm 2–4 kg, 3.5 mm at term
