Neurological

  • ↑Permeability of the blood-brain barrier
  • Immature CNS
  • ↑Risk of intraventricular haemorrhage from structural abnormality and immature autoregulation
  • ↓Cerebral autoregulation, ↑cerebral blood flow

Temperature

  • ↓Temperature regulation
  • ↑BSA:volume ratio
  • No shivering
  • ↓Insulation
  • ↓Brown fat (↓non-shivering thermogenesis)

Glucose

  • ↓Glycogen stores plus pre-op fasting = risk of hypoglycaemia

Anaesthetic implications

  • Adjust warming strategy to weight
  • Monitor temperature and keep a thermoneutral environment
  • Monitor blood glucose
  • Early return to feeding
