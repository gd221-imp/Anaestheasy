Anatomical

  • Diaphragmatic breathing
  • Immature alveoli
  • ↑Chest wall compliance
  • ↓Type 1 fibres in the diaphragm

Physiological

  • ↓FRC, and FRC less than closing capacity
  • ↑Airway resistance

Ex-preterm specific

  • Disrupted vasculogenesis = ↓compliance and ↑V/Q mismatch
  • ↓Glycogen stores
  • ↓Lung elasticity
  • Bronchopulmonary dysplasia from barotrauma, high FiO₂ and underdevelopment

Control of ventilation

  • ↓Central respiratory control
  • Caution with apnoea risk factors: hypoxia, anaemia, hypothermia, hypoglycaemia, sepsis, opioids, stress

Therefore:

  • Can we wait until >60 weeks PCA?
  • Inpatient bed
  • Check Hb
  • Minimise opioids
  • Post-op care: SpO₂ and apnoea monitoring

Anaesthetic implications

  • Intubation and ventilation for all but the very shortest of procedures
  • PEEP
  • Lung protective ventilation
  • Minimise dead space in the circuit
  • Apnoea monitoring if <60/40 weeks post-conceptional age
  • Abdominal distension easily splints the diaphragm causing hyper-reactivity
  • Decompress the stomach with a suction catheter or NGT
  • Be alert to ETT kinking, blockage with secretions or migration
