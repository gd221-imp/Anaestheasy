Physiological

  • Relatively fixed stroke volume, so cardiac output is heart-rate dependent
  • Pulmonary vascular resistance drops at birth and continues to fall
  • Relatively higher Hb

Ex-preterm specific

  • ↑Incidence of congenital cardiac defects
  • Delayed PDA closure (right-to-left shunting risk)
  • Difficult cannulation

Anaesthetic implications

  • Avoid factors that increase PVR (hypoxia, hypercapnia, acidosis)
  • Maintain preload and afterload
  • Consider pre- and post-ductal SpO₂ monitoring
