Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Gestation and birth weight
  • Associated anomalies

Examination (O/E)

  • Bowel condition and perfusion
  • Hydration and temperature

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E, glucose, blood gas
  • Group and save

Optimise

  • Fluids and heat loss, cover bowel

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Warming, ETT, arterial line

Induction

  • RSI

Maintenance

  • staged closure if high abdominal pressure

Emergence

  • Stay ventilated if closure tight or high airway pressures

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Abdominal compartment syndrome from tight closure
  • Bowel ischaemia

Delayed

  • Short gut and prolonged TPN
  • Wound or silo infection
  • Adhesive obstruction

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypothermia and fluid loss
  • Raised airway pressures on closure

Delayed

  • Prolonged ventilation
  • Line sepsis

Analgesia

  • Morphine infusion
  • Paracetamol (neonatal dose)
  • Epidural in some units

Destination

  • NICU, ventilation
