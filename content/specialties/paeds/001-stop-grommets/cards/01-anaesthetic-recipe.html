Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • URTI check, day case suitability

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Chest (URTI)
  • Weight

Investigations (Ix)

  • None

Optimise

  • Postpone if febrile or chesty
  • Premedication or distraction for anxiety

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Facemask or LMA
  • cannula equipment ready

Induction

  • Gas induction

Maintenance

  • keep deep enough to avoid laryngospasm

Emergence

  • Recover in the lateral position
  • Watch for emergence agitation

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding from the ear canal

Delayed

  • Otorrhoea
  • Grommet blockage or extrusion
  • Persistent tympanic membrane perforation

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Laryngospasm, particularly with a light plane on a facemask
  • Airway events with a recent URTI

Delayed

  • Emergence agitation after sevoflurane

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia

Destination

  • home same day
