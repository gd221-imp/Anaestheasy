Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Get a tissue diagnosis under local if possible

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway and stridor
  • Positional symptoms
  • SVC obstruction signs

Investigations (Ix)

  • Symptoms on lying flat, imaging (CT), echo

Optimise

  • Discuss diagnosis under local, or steroids or radiotherapy first
  • MDT plan including rigid bronchoscopy and ECMO or bypass if needed
  • Induce in the position of comfort

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line before induction, rigid bronchoscope and cardiac bypass access considered

Induction

  • Change position if airway or circulation collapses

Maintenance

  • Maintain spontaneous ventilation
  • avoid relaxants

Emergence

  • Extubate awake, sitting up
  • Watch for obstruction in recovery

Post-op

Complications

  • Close monitoring
  • airway can obstruct after the procedure

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding from biopsy site
  • Pneumothorax

Delayed

  • Wound infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Complete airway obstruction on induction or loss of spontaneous ventilation
  • Cardiovascular collapse from great vessel or cardiac compression
  • SVC obstruction

Delayed

  • Tumour lysis syndrome
  • Post-op respiratory compromise

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol
  • Local infiltration

Destination

  • HDU or PICU
