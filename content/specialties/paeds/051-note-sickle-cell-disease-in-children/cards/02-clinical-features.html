Anaemia, ↑bilirubin, bone marrow hyperplasia, ↓capillary blood flow → stroke, vaso-occlusive ulcers, pulmonary infarct, priapism, avascular necrosis.

Diagnosis: blood film/sickledex/Hb electrophoresis.

Triggers for sickling: ↓PaO2, ↓pH, ↓temperature, dehydration, infection, limb tourniquet.

Deoxygenated HbS polymerises and precipitates in the RBC → distortion and rigidity → ↑viscosity, ↓blood flow, thrombosis, organ infarction. Normal RBC lifespan 90-120 days; sickle RBC 10-20 days.
