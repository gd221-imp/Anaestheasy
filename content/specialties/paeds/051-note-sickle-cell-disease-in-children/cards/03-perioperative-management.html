• Pre-op: FBC, blood film, reticulocyte count
  • Sickle cell crisis: analgesia, oxygen, rehydration, exchange transfusion (aim HbS <30%); before surgery, prophylactic simple transfusion to Hb ~100g/L is recommended for all but short, minor procedures (TAPS trial) - discuss with haematology
  • Intra-op: minimise heat loss, FiO2 to correct ↓SaO2, IV fluids to avoid dehydration
  • Post-op: oxygen for 24hrs, consider HDU, good analgesia (may have opioid tolerance)
