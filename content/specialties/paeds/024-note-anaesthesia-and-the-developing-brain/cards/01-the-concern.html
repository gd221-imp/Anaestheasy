• Anaesthetic agents can damage developing neurons.
  • Animal studies: mice and rats exposed to sevoflurane (and other agents) showed damaged brain tissue (neuronal apoptosis) and poorer performance on learning and memory tasks. Similar effects are seen across species, up to primates.
  • Vulnerability is greatest during the period of rapid synapse formation (around postnatal day 7 in rodents).
  • Proposed mechanisms include apoptosis, oxidative stress, neuroinflammation and altered synapse formation.
  • These results are hard to apply directly to human infants.
