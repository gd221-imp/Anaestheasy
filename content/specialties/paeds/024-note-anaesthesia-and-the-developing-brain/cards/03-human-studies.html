GAS trial

  • Randomised trial of infants under 60 weeks post-menstrual age (born after 26 weeks) having inguinal hernia repair: about half had awake regional (spinal/caudal) and half had sevoflurane GA, under 1 hour.
  • Included the highest-risk group: young infants and ex-preterm babies.
  • No difference in neurodevelopment at 2 years (Lancet 2016), and no difference in IQ at 5 years (Lancet 2019).

PANDA study

  • Sibling-matched study (JAMA 2016): children who had a single GA for hernia repair before age 3, compared with a sibling who had not.
  • IQ and neuropsychological testing at 8–15 years showed no significant difference.

MASK study

  • Mayo Clinic, single centre (Anesthesiology 2018): children exposed before age 3, tested at 8–12 or 15–20 years.
  • Single exposure: no difference in IQ.
  • Multiple exposures: small reductions in processing speed and fine motor skills, and more parent-reported behaviour and learning problems.
