APRICOT

  • European study of children having anaesthesia, with each centre collecting data over a 2-week period.
  • Severe critical incident rate of about 1 in 20 anaesthetics.
  • In-hospital mortality of about 1 in 1,000, a figure that has barely changed for decades.
  • Critical incidents were most common in the youngest children.

NECTARINE

  • Focused on neonates and infants.
  • Critical incidents needing intervention in about 35%, higher in neonates.
  • These babies are only operated on when they have to be: often ASA 3–4, having major and usually emergency surgery.
