Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Duration of vomiting
  • Weight loss
  • Birth history

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Hydration (fontanelle, capillary refill)
  • Weight

Investigations (Ix)

  • Medical, not surgical, emergency: correct fluids and electrolytes first (chloride above 100, bicarbonate below 28)

Optimise

  • Correct fluids and electrolytes over 24 to 48 hours
  • Aspirate the NG tube before induction

Intra-op

Equipment

  • NG tube (aspirate before induction), ETT, warming

Induction

  • RSI or modified RSI

Maintenance

  • short case, often laparoscopic

Emergence

  • Extubate awake
  • Avoid long-acting opioids

Post-op

Complications

  • apnoea monitoring

Surgical

Immediate

  • Mucosal perforation
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Incomplete myotomy with persistent vomiting
  • Wound infection or dehiscence

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration of gastric contents at induction
  • Hypothermia
  • Laryngospasm

Delayed

  • Post-op apnoea (persisting alkalosis, opioids)
  • Hypoglycaemia

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, local infiltration

Destination

  • Ward with apnoea monitoring
