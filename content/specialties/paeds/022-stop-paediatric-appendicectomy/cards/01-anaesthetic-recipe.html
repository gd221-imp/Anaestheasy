Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • peritonitis
  • vomiting

Examination (O/E)

  • Sepsis and fluid status

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E, CRP
  • Group and save if complicated
  • Blood glucose

Optimise

  • Fasting
  • Analgesia and antibiotics given

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access, cuffed ETT
  • Warming
  • Paediatric RSI drugs and doses checked

Induction

  • RSI or modified RSI
  • Port-site local anaesthetic or TAP blocks

Maintenance

  • Fluid bolus 10 ml/kg if needed
  • maintenance fluids

Emergence

  • Extubate awake, lateral
  • Aspirate stomach before extubation

Post-op

Complications

  • Antiemetic
  • Watch for sepsis and collections

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Bowel injury

Delayed

  • Intra-abdominal collection
  • Wound infection
  • Ileus

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration at induction
  • Hypotension in sepsis

Delayed

  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID, opioid as needed

Destination

  • Paediatric surgical ward
