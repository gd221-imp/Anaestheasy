• Definition: involuntary sustained closure of the vocal cords due to reflex glottic closure, usually triggered by airway stimulation under light anaesthesia (secretions, blood, surgical stimulus, extubation)
  • Risk factors: URTI (recent or current), passive smoking exposure, airway surgery, light plane of anaesthesia during stimulation
  • Management: 100% O2, remove stimulus, CPAP with tight-fitting mask/jaw thrust, deepen anaesthesia (IV propofol if access available), if severe/persisting - suxamethonium (IV or IM) ± atropine for bradycardia, be prepared to intubate
  • Complications: hypoxia, negative pressure pulmonary oedema, aspiration, bradycardia/cardiac arrest if unrecognised
