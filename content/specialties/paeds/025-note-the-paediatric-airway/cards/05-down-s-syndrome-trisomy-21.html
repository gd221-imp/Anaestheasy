• Airway: macroglossia, midface hypoplasia, short neck, subglottic stenosis, large tonsils/adenoids → OSA common
  • C-spine: atlanto-axial instability (avoid neck extension/hyperflexion during laryngoscopy)
  • Cardiac: ~50% have CHD (AVSD most common, also VSD/ASD/PDA/ToF) - needs pre-op echo if not recently done
  • Other: hypothyroidism, duodenal atresia, epilepsy, ↑risk of post-op airway obstruction and bradycardia with induction
