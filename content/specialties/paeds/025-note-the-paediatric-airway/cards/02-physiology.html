• More reactive airways - higher risk of laryngospasm and bronchospasm.
  • Reduced FRC (further reduced under anaesthesia) with higher oxygen consumption (around 6–8 ml/kg/min vs 3–4 in adults), so children desaturate quickly.
  • Young infants are preferential nasal breathers.
  • Horizontal ribs and a diaphragm-dependent breathing pattern; they tire easily.
  • Resistance rises steeply with small reductions in airway radius, so a little oedema causes a lot of obstruction.
