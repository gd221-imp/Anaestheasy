• Beckwith-Wiedemann: macroglossia, macrosomia, omphalocele, neonatal hypoglycaemia
  • Pierre-Robin sequence: micrognathia, glossoptosis, cleft palate → difficult FMV and laryngoscopy, tongue falls back
  • Treacher-Collins: mandibular/malar hypoplasia, choanal atresia, ear abnormalities - progressively harder airway with age
  • Goldenhar syndrome: hemifacial microsomia, vertebral anomalies, cardiac defects
  • Apert syndrome: craniosynostosis, midface hypoplasia, syndactyly
  • Mucopolysaccharidoses (e.g. Hurler's): macroglossia, thickened tissues, stiff joints, progressively difficult airway with age, restrictive lung disease
