Senior help, difficult airway trolley available, plan for spontaneous ventilation/gas induction, have a clear back-up plan, anticipate a smaller than expected airway for age.

First attempt is the best attempt. Airway trauma from repeated attempts causes considerable swelling in the small paediatric airway, making both intubation and air passage progressively harder. Optimise position, equipment and operator before the first attempt.

Follow the APA/DAS paediatric difficult airway guidelines, which cover difficult mask ventilation, unanticipated difficult intubation, and can't intubate, can't oxygenate.
