Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Indication (phimosis, balanitis, religious)
  • Birth history and URTI
  • Bleeding history

Examination (O/E)

  • Weight, day case suitability, consent for the block

Investigations (Ix)

  • None

Optimise

  • Topical anaesthetic cream if planning a cannula

Intra-op

Equipment

  • LMA, caudal or penile block kit (no adrenaline in a penile block)

Induction

  • Gas or IV induction, then caudal or penile block before surgery

Maintenance

  • Volatile, spontaneous ventilation

Emergence

  • Remove LMA deep or awake
  • Recover in the lateral position

Post-op

Complications

  • check passing urine if a caudal was used

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Meatal stenosis
  • Poor cosmetic result

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Laryngospasm
  • Block complications: haematoma, failed block; with a caudal, dural puncture or local anaesthetic toxicity

Delayed

  • Urinary retention or leg weakness after a caudal
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol and ibuprofen

Destination

  • Home same day
