• Before admission: leaflets, books and videos; clinical psychologists where needed.
  • Play therapy with visual aids.
  • Distraction: bubbles, toys and screens.
  • Engage the child in the anaesthetic: blowing up the balloon, helping build the anaesthetic circuit.
  • Adjust the environment: lighting, music, and keeping staff in the room to a minimum.
  • Actively involve parents and carers.
  • Communication aids: hospital "passports" and Makaton, especially for neurodiverse children.
  • Relaxation techniques such as breathing exercises.
