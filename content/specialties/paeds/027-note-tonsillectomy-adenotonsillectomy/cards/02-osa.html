Obstructive sleep-disordered breathing → upper airway obstruction or pharyngeal collapse → snoring, ↑respiratory effort; most severe form = OSA.

Diagnosis: loud snoring, daytime somnolence, poor school performance, history of GORD/prematurity; overnight pulse oximetry or overnight polysomnography (adenotonsillar hypertrophy peaks age 3-10yrs).

Apnoea-hypopnoea index (children): normal <1 event/hr, mild 1-5, moderate 5-10, severe >10 (adult cut-offs are <5, 5-15, 15-30, >30).
