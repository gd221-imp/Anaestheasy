• Pre-op: paracetamol 20mg/kg, ibuprofen 10mg/kg, ± midazolam 0.5mg/kg; check anaesthetic machine, prepare drugs and equipment
  • Intra-op: full AAGBI monitoring, trained assistant; induction - IV or inhalational, 1mcg/kg fentanyl, 2-3mg/kg propofol, flexible LMA (pros: easy to site, protects from nasal airway/blood, smooth emergence, no paralysis, reduced airway trauma; cons: less secure, less surgical access); maintenance - sevoflurane to MAC 1, 0.15mg/kg dexamethasone
  • Post-op: analgesia - paracetamol + ibuprofen ± rescue morphine 0.1mg/kg; destination - ward

Key intra-op moments: Boyle-Davis gag application - check chest rise/ventilation pressures; at end of surgery, ensure no ongoing clot at tonsillar bed.
