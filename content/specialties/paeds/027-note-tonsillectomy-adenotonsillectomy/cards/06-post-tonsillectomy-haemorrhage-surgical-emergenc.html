• Primary <24hr; secondary up to day 28 (peak day 5-10) - often due to detachment of eschar at tonsillar bed
  • Risk factors: recent haemorrhage/haematemesis, frightened child, full stomach, shock (hypovolaemia), potential difficult airway, recent GA → possible residual anaesthetic effect
  • Pre-op: IV access, review previous chart/post-op notes, assess hypovolaemia (CRT, HR, skin colour, BP, AVPU, Hb, lactate, symptoms); 20ml/kg crystalloid → cross-matched blood if needed, TXA 15mg/kg
  • Intra-op: head-down and lateral positioning, maintain IV access, watch for irritability; team - consultant anaesthetist and senior ENT surgeon present; modified rapid sequence induction vs inhalational, IV access secured, sevoflurane, OGT emptied before induction
  • Post-op: head-down/lateral recovery position, surgical re-exploration for bleeding.
