Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Stabilise first
  • surgery is not urgent

Examination (O/E)

  • Pre- and post-ductal saturations
  • Chest
  • Cardiac assessment

Investigations (Ix)

  • Echo for pulmonary hypertension and cardiac lesions
  • Blood gas
  • Chest X-ray

Optimise

  • Stabilise on NICU first
  • Gentle ventilation, permissive hypercapnia
  • Treat pulmonary hypertension (nitric oxide)

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, gentle ventilation

Induction

  • Usually already intubated
  • Opioid-based anaesthesia

Maintenance

  • Avoid high pressures
  • treat pulmonary hypertension

Emergence

  • Stay ventilated to NICU

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Recurrence of hernia

Delayed

  • Chylothorax
  • Adhesive obstruction
  • Chest wall deformity

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Pulmonary hypertensive crisis
  • Pneumothorax on the contralateral side from barotrauma
  • Hypoxia and hypercapnia (especially thoracoscopic)

Delayed

  • Prolonged ventilation
  • Chronic lung disease

Analgesia

  • Morphine or fentanyl infusion
  • Paracetamol (neonatal dose)

Destination

  • NICU
