Examination (O/E)

Investigations (Ix)

  • Time-critical: aim for theatre within hours of onset to save the testis

Optimise

  • do not delay for fasting
  • Fasting status, last oral intake, vomiting
  • analgesia given
  • Consent with parent and child (Gillick competence in older boys)
  • bilateral fixation discussed

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access
  • Age-appropriate airway: LMA if fasted, cuffed ETT if not
  • Atropine or glycopyrronium drawn up

Induction

  • RSI or modified RSI if not fasted
  • otherwise IV or gas induction with LMA
  • Local anaesthetic infiltration by the surgeon or ilioinguinal block

Maintenance

  • Deepen before cord traction (vagal response, laryngospasm)
  • paracetamol and NSAID

Post-op

Complications

  • Antiemetic
  • follow-up if orchidectomy

Surgical

Immediate

  • Non-viable testis needing orchidectomy
  • Bleeding and scrotal haematoma

Delayed

  • Testicular atrophy
  • Wound infection
  • Subfertility

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration at induction (not fasted)
  • Bradycardia and laryngospasm with cord traction

Delayed

  • Post-op nausea and vomiting
  • Emergence delirium

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia with oral opioid as needed

Destination

  • Usually home the same or next day
