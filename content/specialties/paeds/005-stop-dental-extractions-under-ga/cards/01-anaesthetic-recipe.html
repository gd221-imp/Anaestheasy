Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Usually a day-case list: learning disability, autism, ADHD and cerebral palsy are common
  • Cardiac history: endocarditis risk in congenital heart disease

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway; loose teeth
  • Cardiac murmur
  • Cooperation (may only be possible after induction)

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none

Optimise

  • plan reasonable adjustments with carers
  • Fasting, premedication plan, URTI check

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring
  • nasal or oral ETT, or LMA shared with the dentist
  • Throat pack
  • Cannula equipment ready after induction

Induction

  • Gas or IV induction depending on the child

Maintenance

  • consider premedication
  • Agree airway plan with the dentist
  • throat pack in and documented
  • PONV prophylaxis
  • local anaesthetic infiltration by the dentist

Emergence

  • Remove the throat pack and check it
  • Suction blood under direct vision
  • Extubate awake or deep in the lateral position

Post-op

Complications

  • Throat pack removed and checked
  • watch for bleeding into the airway

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding from extraction sockets
  • Dental trauma to remaining teeth

Delayed

  • Socket infection
  • Persistent pain

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Laryngospasm, especially with blood in the airway
  • Retained throat pack
  • Airway obstruction shared with the operator

Delayed

  • Post-op nausea and vomiting
  • Emergence delirium

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia

Destination

  • Recovery in lateral position
  • home same day
