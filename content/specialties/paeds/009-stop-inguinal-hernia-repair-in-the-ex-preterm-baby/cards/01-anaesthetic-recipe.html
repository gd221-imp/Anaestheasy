Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Ex-premature apnoea risk

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Weight
  • Chest and respiratory support

Investigations (Ix)

  • Hb (anaemia increases apnoea risk)

Optimise

  • Discuss timing (apnoea risk until 60 weeks PMA)
  • Caffeine per local practice
  • Neonatal bed booked

Intra-op

Equipment

  • LMA, caudal

Induction

  • Gas induction, caudal or spinal in ex-prems

Maintenance

  • Awake spinal or caudal without sedation where possible
  • Or GA with minimal opioid
  • Keep warm

Emergence

  • Avoid opioids; extubate awake

Post-op

Complications

  • Apnoea monitoring if ex-prem under 60 weeks PMA

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Injury to the vas or testicular vessels

Delayed

  • Recurrence
  • Testicular atrophy
  • Wound infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Laryngospasm and desaturation
  • Hypothermia
  • High or failed spinal/caudal block

Delayed

  • Post-op apnoea (risk until 60 weeks post-conceptional age)
  • Bradycardia

Analgesia

  • Caudal or spinal
  • Paracetamol

Destination

  • Overnight with apnoea monitoring if under 60 weeks PMA
