• Organism: Neisseria meningitidis (gram-negative diplococcus)
  • Features: fever, non-blanching purpuric rash, ↓consciousness, signs of shock (tachycardia, prolonged CRT, hypotension - late sign), meningism
  • Management: early recognition, IV/IO access, broad spectrum antibiotics without delay (follow local guidelines; e.g. IV ceftriaxone), fluid resuscitation, escalate early to PICU, inotropes if fluid-refractory shock, public health notification and contact prophylaxis
