Management of a concerning finding in theatre with the patient anaesthetised: do not photograph, do not prolong the GA unnecessarily.

  1. Check hospital notes/records
  2. Inform the duty consultant and the on-call paediatrician (safeguarding lead), who is asked to attend; inform the surgeons and the theatre team; identify the "named person for child protection"
  3. Document findings and actions
  4. Paediatrician and anaesthetist discuss with parents post-op with full explanation; if remaining concern → involve social care/police/consider whether safe to go home

Child protection training levels (intercollegiate guidance): Level 1 - all staff, Level 2 - clinical staff with occasional contact with children, Level 3 - clinicians who work with children (including anaesthetists who anaesthetise children), Level 4 - named professionals, Level 5 - designated professionals.

Categories of abuse: physical, emotional, sexual, neglect, (fabricated/induced illness).

Legislation: Children Act 1989 and 2004 (amended by the Children and Social Work Act 2017); statutory guidance is Working Together to Safeguard Children.
