Definition: a continuum of drug-induced depression of consciousness.

ASA classification: minimal (normal responsiveness, ABC unaffected, no fasting required) → moderate (responds to tactile/verbal stimulus e.g. "hello", ABC may be affected, some fasting) → deep (repeated painful stimulus/"jaw thrust" required, ABC needs intervention, fasting required - risk of loss of verbal contact).

Contraindications: A - risk of aspiration, B - respiratory failure, D - raised ICP (poorly controlled epilepsy).

Challenges in paediatric sedation: cooperation with sedation, consent from carer, difficult IV access.
