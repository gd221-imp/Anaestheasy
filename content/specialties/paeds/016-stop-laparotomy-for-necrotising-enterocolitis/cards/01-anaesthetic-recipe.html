Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Septic, coagulopathic neonate

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Perfusion, abdominal distension
  • Bleeding

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, platelets, clotting
  • Blood gas, lactate, glucose
  • Crossmatch

Optimise

  • Resuscitate with fluids and blood products
  • Antibiotics
  • Consider operating on the NICU

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, blood products, warming

Induction

  • RSI, inotropes

Maintenance

  • Opioid-based anaesthesia; volatile if tolerated
  • Inotropes and blood products
  • Warming

Emergence

  • Stay ventilated to NICU

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bowel perforation and contamination
  • Haemorrhage, including liver capsule bleeding

Delayed

  • Short gut syndrome
  • Strictures
  • Stoma complications

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Cardiovascular instability from sepsis
  • Coagulopathy and thrombocytopenia
  • Hypothermia

Delayed

  • Prolonged ventilation
  • Intraventricular haemorrhage

Analgesia

  • Morphine or fentanyl infusion
  • Paracetamol (neonatal dose)

Destination

  • NICU
