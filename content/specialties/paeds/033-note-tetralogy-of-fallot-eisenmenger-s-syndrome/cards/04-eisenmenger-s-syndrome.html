Long-standing left-to-right shunt (uncorrected VSD/ASD/PDA) → chronically ↑pulmonary blood flow → pulmonary vascular remodelling → irreversible pulmonary hypertension → shunt reversal (right-to-left) → cyanosis. High risk for anaesthesia/pregnancy - avoid factors that increase PVR or decrease SVR, avoid air bubbles in IV lines (paradoxical embolism).
