Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • What was swallowed and when
  • Drooling, dysphagia, stridor
  • Last oral intake

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway and signs of tracheal compression
  • Neck tenderness, surgical emphysema

Investigations (Ix)

  • Chest X-ray (lateral view helps)

Optimise

  • What and when (button batteries are urgent), fasting

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT, suction

Induction

  • RSI with ETT

Maintenance

  • Volatile or TIVA
  • Keep the airway secure during endoscopy

Emergence

  • Extubate awake, lateral

Post-op

Complications

  • Watch for oesophageal injury

Surgical

Immediate

  • Oesophageal perforation or mucosal injury
  • Button battery burns and erosion into the aorta or trachea

Delayed

  • Oesophageal stricture
  • Tracheo-oesophageal fistula
  • Mediastinitis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration at induction
  • Airway compression from the foreign body

Delayed

  • Post-op stridor

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol

Destination

  • Ward; HDU if oesophageal injury or button battery
