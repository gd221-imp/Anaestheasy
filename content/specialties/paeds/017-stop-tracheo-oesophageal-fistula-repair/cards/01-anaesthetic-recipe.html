Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Gestation and associated anomalies (VACTERL)
  • Respiratory distress, feeding

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Cardiac murmur
  • Chest

Investigations (Ix)

  • Replogle tube, cardiac echo

Optimise

  • Replogle tube on suction
  • Head up; avoid bag-mask ventilation
  • Echo to check arch side and cardiac lesions

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT positioned beyond fistula, arterial line

Induction

  • Avoid IPPV before intubation where possible

Maintenance

  • Keep the ETT tip beyond the fistula (check with bronchoscope if possible)
  • Gentle ventilation; watch gastric distension
  • Arterial line

Emergence

  • Usually stay ventilated to NICU

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Anastomotic leak
  • Injury to the trachea or recurrent laryngeal nerve

Delayed

  • Oesophageal stricture
  • Recurrent fistula
  • Tracheomalacia
  • GORD

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Gastric distension from ventilation through the fistula
  • ETT malposition into the fistula
  • Hypoxia during surgical retraction

Delayed

  • Prolonged ventilation
  • Post-op chest infection

Analgesia

  • Morphine infusion
  • Paracetamol (neonatal dose)

Destination

  • NICU
