Multimodal analgesia (paracetamol 7.5mg/kg + fentanyl + LA); apnoea alarm, consider HDU; aim for normoglycaemia, feed 6hrs post-op with clear fluids (continue IV fluids until feeding established); maintain normothermia; watch for laryngospasm/bronchospasm.

Surgical approach: open (longitudinal incision through the pylorus) vs laparoscopic (↓pain, ↓length of stay, ↓time to feed - usually at specialist centres).
