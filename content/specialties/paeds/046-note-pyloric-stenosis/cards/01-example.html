4-week-old male, projectile vomiting and weight loss.

Definition: gastric outlet obstruction due to hypertrophy of the pyloric smooth muscle - medical emergency, not surgical.

Risk factors: white ethnicity, male, first-born, Caesarean section, autumn/spring birth, younger maternal age, maternal Fbx/smoking.

Clinical presentation: projectile vomiting, dehydration, palpable abdominal "olive" in RUQ, onset typically 4-6 weeks of age.

Investigations: USS confirms pyloric hypertrophy.
