Hypokalaemic, hypochloraemic metabolic alkalosis (bicarbonate not reabsorbed in the small intestine):

  • GI loss: ↓H2O/HCl/H+/Na+
  • Intracellular movement: H+ out and K+ in to buffer the alkalosis
  • Renal loss: ↓circulating volume → ↑RAAS → Na+ retained in exchange for H+ - initially HCO3- excreted to buffer alkalosis, but as hypokalaemia worsens H+ is preferentially exchanged for Na+ (over K+) producing a paradoxically acidic urine
  • Respiratory compensation: ↓RR and ↑CO2
