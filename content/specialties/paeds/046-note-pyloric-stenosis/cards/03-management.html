1. Fluids: 20ml/kg IV crystalloid bolus as needed, then maintenance ~150ml/kg/day of 0.45% NaCl + 5% dextrose + 20mmol/L KCl; replace enteral losses with 0.9% NaCl + 20mmol/L KCl (up to 1L)
  2. Correct the metabolic alkalosis (low H+ in CSF causes reduced central respiratory drive → risk of apnoea if not corrected pre-operatively)
  3. Reduce aspiration risk - "4 quadrant aspiration" via NG tube

Proceed to theatre when: euvolaemic (2-3 wet nappies, urine output 1-2ml/kg/hr), Cl- >95mmol/L, pH <7.5, base excess <6mmol/L, HCO3- <30mmol/L, K+ normal
