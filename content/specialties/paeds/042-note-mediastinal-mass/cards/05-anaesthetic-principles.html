• Pre-op: CT/MRI for airway/SVC compression, echo, consider pre-op size reduction (increased dose steroid, radiotherapy)
  • Intra-op (using E-T-I-M-E framework): antisialogue; gas induction (lateral or head-up position); maintain spontaneous ventilation, maintain pharyngeal muscle tone; awake extubation
  • Post-op: airway compromise - Help! ABCDEFG, CPAP, reposition (lateral/prone/"favourite" position), PPV with PEEP, one-lung ventilation, flexible/rigid bronchoscopy. CVS compromise - ensure filling/reposition, reduce/stop GA, sternotomy and elevation of mass if needed
  • Consider whether GA can be avoided altogether: is imaging clear enough for surgical plan? Can LA/regional be used instead?
