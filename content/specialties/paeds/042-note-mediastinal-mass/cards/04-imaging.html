CT is investigation of choice. Red flags on imaging: tracheal or bronchial diameter (or cross-sectional area) <50% of normal - unsafe for GA regardless of symptoms - plus cardinal/bronchial compression, great vessel compression, pericardial effusion. CXR to screen for mediastinal lymphadenopathy.

Causes: acute lymphoblastic leukaemia, vascular malformations, germ cell tumours, lymphoma (non-Hodgkin's - more common in children, Hodgkin's).
