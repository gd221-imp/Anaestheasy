Rapid tumour breakdown → rapid release of intracellular substances → ↑K+, ↑phosphate, ↓Ca2+ (phosphate binds calcium), ↑uric acid, ↑urea → AKI and arrhythmias. Prevention: IV fluids and allopurinol (xanthine oxidase inhibitor); rasburicase (breaks down uric acid) for high risk or established TLS. Treatment: correct electrolyte abnormalities, IV fluids, renal replacement therapy if needed.
