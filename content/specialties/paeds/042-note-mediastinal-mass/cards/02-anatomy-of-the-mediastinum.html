• Superior mediastinum: bounded by thoracic inlet superiorly and a plane to T4 inferiorly; contains the aortic arch and its branches, brachiocephalic veins and upper SVC, oesophagus, trachea, CN X, phrenic nerves, thoracic duct, thymus remnant and lymph nodes (the descending aorta and azygos vein are in the posterior mediastinum); posteriorly relates to the vertebral column
  • Anterior mediastinum: connective tissue, thymus, lymph nodes; between anterior pericardium and sternum
  • Middle mediastinum: heart, major vessels, phrenic nerve
