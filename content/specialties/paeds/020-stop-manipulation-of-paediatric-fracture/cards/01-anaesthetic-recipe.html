Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • safeguarding check of mechanism

Examination (O/E)

  • Neurovascular status of the limb

Investigations (Ix)

  • X-ray
  • Usually no bloods

Optimise

  • Fasting status (often recently eaten after injury)
  • Analgesia given

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, LMA
  • Image intensifier

Induction

  • GA with LMA (RSI if not fasted and urgent)
  • regional block as an option for older children

Maintenance

  • Short case

Emergence

  • Remove LMA awake or deep
  • Recover in the lateral position

Post-op

Complications

  • Neurovascular observations

Surgical

Immediate

  • Neurovascular injury
  • Failed reduction

Delayed

  • Compartment syndrome
  • Malunion

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration (not fasted)
  • Laryngospasm

Delayed

  • PONV
  • Emergence delirium

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia, elevation

Destination

  • Day case if appropriate
