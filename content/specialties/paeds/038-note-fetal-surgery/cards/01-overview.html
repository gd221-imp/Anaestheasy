Performed for select severe congenital anomalies (e.g. myelomeningocele repair, congenital diaphragmatic hernia with severe lung hypoplasia, twin-twin transfusion syndrome, sacrococcygeal teratoma) - requires maternal and fetal anaesthesia, deep uterine relaxation (high-dose volatile), risk of preterm labour, maternal haemorrhage, and fetal cardiac depression from anaesthetic agents crossing the placenta.
