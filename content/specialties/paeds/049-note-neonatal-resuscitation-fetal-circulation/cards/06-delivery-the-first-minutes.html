1. First breaths expand the lungs and raise alveolar oxygen. PVR falls sharply and pulmonary blood flow rises many times over.
  2. Cord clamping removes the low-resistance placenta, so SVR rises.
  3. Pressures reverse: left atrial pressure now exceeds right atrial pressure, pushing the flap of the foramen ovale closed (functional closure within minutes).
  4. Flow through the ductus arteriosus reverses to left-to-right as aortic pressure exceeds pulmonary pressure.
