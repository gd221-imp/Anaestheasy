In the first days and weeks the pulmonary vessels are still muscular and reactive, and the shunts can reopen.

  • Triggers that raise PVR: hypoxia, hypercapnia, acidosis, hypothermia, sepsis and pain.
  • Result: right-to-left shunting through the foramen ovale and ductus, a return towards fetal circulation.
  • Persistent pulmonary hypertension of the newborn (PPHN): suspect when pre-ductal (right hand) SpO₂ is clearly higher than post-ductal (foot) SpO₂.
