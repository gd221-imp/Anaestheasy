• PVR keeps falling as the muscular layer of the pulmonary arterioles thins, reaching near-adult levels by around 6–8 weeks.
  • As PVR falls, left-to-right shunts (e.g. a VSD) increase, so heart failure from these lesions often appears at around 4–6 weeks.
  • HbF is gradually replaced by HbA over the first months, and haemoglobin falls towards its physiological low point (around 8–12 weeks in term babies).
  • Duct-dependent lesions may present as the ductus closes in the first days to weeks: collapse or cyanosis needs prostaglandin to reopen it.
