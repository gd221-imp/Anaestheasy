• Avoid the triggers: keep the baby warm, well oxygenated and normocapnic, and treat acidosis and pain.
  • Monitor pre- and post-ductal saturations when shunting is a concern.
  • Duct-dependent lesions: keep the ductus open with a prostaglandin infusion until surgery.
  • A persistent PDA may be closed medically (e.g. ibuprofen, indometacin or paracetamol) or surgically.
  • Ex-premature babies and those with lung disease have a more reactive pulmonary circulation for longer.
