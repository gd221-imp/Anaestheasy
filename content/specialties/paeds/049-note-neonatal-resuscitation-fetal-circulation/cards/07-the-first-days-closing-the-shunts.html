• Ductus arteriosus: constricts as PaO₂ rises and prostaglandin E₂ falls (placental source lost, more metabolised by the lungs). Functional closure usually within 24–72 hours; anatomical closure by fibrosis over 2–3 weeks, leaving the ligamentum arteriosum.
  • Ductus venosus: closes over the first week, leaving the ligamentum venosum.
  • Foramen ovale: functionally closed from birth; anatomical fusion takes months and stays probe-patent in at least 15% (commonly quoted ~25%) of adults.
  • Umbilical vessels: the umbilical vein becomes the ligamentum teres; the umbilical arteries become the medial umbilical ligaments.
