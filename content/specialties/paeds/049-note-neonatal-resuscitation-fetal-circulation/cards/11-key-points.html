Fontan circulation (a palliative surgical arrangement, e.g. for single-ventricle physiology): separates the systemic and pulmonary circulations; blood flow to the lungs becomes passive (no sub-pulmonary ventricle) therefore relies on low pulmonary vascular resistance, adequate central venous pressure, sinus rhythm, and avoidance of positive-pressure intrathoracic pressure increases. Complications: protein-losing enteropathy, plastic bronchitis, Fontan-associated liver disease, arrhythmias, venous thromboembolism.
