Examination (O/E)

—

Investigations (Ix)

  • Why GA or sedation: age, anxiety, learning disability, long scan
  • MRI safety checklist: implants, metal, pacing devices
  • Fasting, airway assessment, plan for transfer within the MRI unit

Optimise

—

Intra-op

Equipment

  • MR-conditional monitoring, infusion pumps and anaesthetic machine
  • Long breathing circuit and extension lines
  • Ear protection
  • warming

Induction

  • Induce in the anaesthetic room outside the magnet
  • LMA or ETT with volatile, or TIVA
  • sedation (e.g. dexmedetomidine or chloral hydrate) for selected children

Maintenance

  • Remote monitoring from the control room

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Be ready for delayed safeguarding questions if imaging is for suspected NAI

Surgical

Immediate

  • Projectile injury from ferromagnetic objects
  • Burns from monitoring leads or implants

Delayed

  • Missed findings needing repeat scan

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Limited access to the child during the scan
  • Airway obstruction or apnoea under sedation
  • Hypothermia

Delayed

  • Failed sedation and repeat attempts
  • Emergence delirium

Analgesia

—

Destination

  • Recover in a dedicated area with full monitoring
  • Discharge criteria as for day case
