• Gower's sign = "walking up the legs" at age 4-6yrs = hip and thigh muscle weakness (proximal) with wasting
  • Waddling gait → wheelchair-bound by teens
  • Pseudohypertrophy of the calves
  • Muscle spasm/contractures/scoliosis
  • Restrictive lung defect and dilated cardiomyopathy are the major causes of death in the 20s/30s
  • X-linked recessive, males only, incidence ~1:3500

Becker Muscular Dystrophy: milder than DMD, onset ~11yrs, death in 40s/50s, X-linked recessive, incidence ~1:30,000.
