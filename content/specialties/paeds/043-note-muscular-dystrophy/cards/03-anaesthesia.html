• Pre-op: pre-op assessment and MDT discussion
  • Intra-op: avoid suxamethonium (risk of hyperkalaemia and rhabdomyolysis); caution with non-depolarising muscle relaxants (prolonged effect); avoid volatile agents (risk of rhabdomyolysis) - TIVA preferred without NMBD where possible
  • Post-op: cardiac monitoring (risk of arrhythmia), HDU/PICU level care

N.B. The myopathies strongly associated with malignant hyperthermia (RYR1-related) are central core disease, multiminicore disease, King-Denborough syndrome, and Native American myopathy.
