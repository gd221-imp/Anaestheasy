• Multimodal analgesia: paracetamol/NSAID/opioid + regional anaesthesia - options: caudal block, penile block, topical lidocaine gel, frenulum infiltration of LA
  • Penile block: dorsal nerve of penis = S2-4, passes under inferior ramus of pubis then into Buck's fascia. Needle inserted at base of penis either side of the suspensory ligament, advance 3-5mm until "click" as Buck's fascia is breached, inject 1-2ml 0.5% bupivacaine. Complications: incomplete block, pain during injection, bleeding/haematoma. Do NOT use adrenaline (penile artery is an end artery).
  • Assessing pain in children is difficult: unable to communicate pain, pain complicated by anxiety, additional distress from unfamiliar environment
  • Physiological indicators: HR/BP/RR. Behavioural indicators: irritability, unusual posture, reluctance to move, unusual quietness, whimpering, restlessness
  • Structured pain scales: FACES, Wong-Baker, FLACC (0-10 score)
  • Ensure paediatric-trained staff and parent/carer views involved (neonates: NIPS and N-PASS scales).
