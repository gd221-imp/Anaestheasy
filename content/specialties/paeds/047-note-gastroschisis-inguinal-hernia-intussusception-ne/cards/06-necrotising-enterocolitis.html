A form of enterocolitis in premature infants, seen after introduction of enteral feeding - high morbidity and mortality.

Example: 28-week gestation male, on NICU for 10 days, abdominal distension and appears uncomfortable.

Diagnosis: clinical + AXR (± free air) + Doppler ultrasound (mesenteric flow).

Classification - Bell's stages: 1 (suspected, mild), 2 (confirmed, additional GI signs, +/- lethargy/apnoea), 3 (advanced, severe signs, +DIC).

Risk factors: prematurity (↑risk with very low birth weight and extreme prematurity), umbilical catheterisation, cyanotic heart disease, immature immune system, formula feeding.

Management: medical (for stages 1/2) - bowel rest, decompression, broad spectrum antibiotics (follow local guidelines), supportive organ care. Surgical (stage 3) - laparotomy, resection ± stoma, high surgical mortality (~20%).
