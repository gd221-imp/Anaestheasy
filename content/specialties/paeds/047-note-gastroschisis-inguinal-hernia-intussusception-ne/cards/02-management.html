Primary surgical closure, staged surgical closure, or formation of a "silastic pouch" in theatre/NICU (cover with cling film to prevent heat/fluid loss and protect from damage/entry point for infection).

Pre-op specifics: fluid resuscitation (large third-space/evaporative losses), inspect bowel for rupture/ischaemia, bowel decompression with NGT, temperature regulation.

Post-op: most return to NICU ventilated, an epidural may allow extubation within 2 days, watch for progress if there are other associated abnormalities; monitor for respiratory compromise/raised intra-abdominal pressure - may need return to theatre.
