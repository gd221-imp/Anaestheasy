Total fluid requirement = fasting deficit + other losses + maintenance + evaporative losses + blood loss.

  • Fasting deficit: hours fasted × the child's hourly maintenance rate (4-2-1 rule), to replace. For example, a 23 kg child needs 63 ml for every hour fasted
  • Other losses: estimated (e.g. vomiting, diarrhoea, NG losses)
  • Maintenance: 4-2-1 rule: 4 ml/kg/hr for the first 10 kg, 2 ml/kg/hr for the next 10 kg, 1 ml/kg/hr for each kg above 20 kg
  • Evaporative losses: 5–10 ml/kg/hr
  • Blood loss
