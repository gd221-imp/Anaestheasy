• Most healthy children need none.
  • Haemoglobin: ex-premature babies, risk of anaemia, or major surgery.
  • Haemoglobinopathies (sickle cell, thalassaemia) if at risk and status unknown:
    • Full blood count and haemoglobin electrophoresis (HPLC) for a definitive answer.
    • Sickledex (sickle solubility test) is a quick screen, but gives false negatives under 3 months because of high fetal haemoglobin.
  • Echo and cardiology input for a pathological murmur.
  • Group and save if significant blood loss is expected.
