BINDS: the paediatric extras

  • Birth history:
    • Gestation and mode of delivery
    • NICU or SCBU admission
    • Feeding
    • Respiratory support
    • If intubated and ventilated: failure to thrive, chronic lung disease, subglottic stenosis
  • Immunisations: up to date? any recent live vaccines
  • Nutrition: feeding, growth and weight centile, anaemia
  • Development: milestones, developmental delay or neurodiversity
    • Gross motor skills are the key: delay can be the first sign of an undiagnosed myopathy such as Duchenne muscular dystrophy.
    • These children are at risk of anaesthesia-induced rhabdomyolysis (AIR) with volatile agents and suxamethonium, so use TIVA for any confirmed or suspected risk of AIR.
  • Social: who has parental responsibility, home circumstances, safeguarding concerns

Then the usual anaesthetic history

  • Medical history: syndromes, cardiac or respiratory disease, neuromuscular conditions, developmental needs or neurodiversity.
  • Airway and breathing: recent URTI, cough or wheeze, snoring or witnessed apnoeas (OSA).
  • Previous anaesthetics: problems, emergence agitation, PONV, how induction went.
  • Family history: malignant hyperthermia, suxamethonium apnoea, bleeding disorders.
  • Drugs and allergies, including latex.
  • Loose teeth, and any safeguarding concerns.
