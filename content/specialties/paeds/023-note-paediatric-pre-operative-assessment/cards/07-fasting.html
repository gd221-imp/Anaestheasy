Prolonged fasting is a problem in children: it causes low blood sugar and dehydration, as well as distress and irritability. This is why the guidelines on clear fluids were updated.

  • Solids, formula milk and other milk: 6 hours
  • Breast milk: 4 hours
  • Clear fluids: encouraged up to 1 hour before induction, to a maximum of 3 ml/kg.

Clear fluids such as water empty from the stomach within about 30 minutes, so they add little to aspiration risk.
