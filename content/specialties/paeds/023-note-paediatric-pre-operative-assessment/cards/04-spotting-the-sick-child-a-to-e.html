• A, Airway: stridor, snoring, drooling, signs of obstruction.
  • B, Breathing: raised respiratory rate, recession, grunting, nasal flaring, head bobbing, wheeze, low SpO₂.
  • C, Circulation: tachycardia, capillary refill over 2 seconds, pale, mottled or cold peripheries, fewer wet nappies; hypotension is a late sign.
  • D, Disability: AVPU, lethargy or floppiness, irritability, poor feeding, bulging fontanelle; check glucose.
  • E, Exposure: temperature, rash (especially non-blanching), dehydration.

A child who looks unwell should prompt you to delay elective surgery and escalate.
