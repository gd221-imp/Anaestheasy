Definition: no respiratory effort for more than 20 seconds, or cessation of breathing for more than 10 seconds with bradycardia or desaturation.

  • Central: increased risk under 60 weeks post-conceptional age (PCA); made worse by anaemia; caffeine reduces it.
  • Obstructive (OSA): 70–90% due to enlarged adenoids and tonsils; these children have increased sensitivity to opioids.
  • Mixed: features of both.
