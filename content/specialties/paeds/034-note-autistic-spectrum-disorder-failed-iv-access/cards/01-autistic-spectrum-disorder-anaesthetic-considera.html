• Pre-op: detailed history from parents/carers of triggers, communication needs, sensory sensitivities, routines
  • Minimise unfamiliar environment/staff changes, consider first on list, allow parent/carer present throughout
  • Consider premedication (oral/intranasal) if likely to be highly distressed by cannulation
  • Use of visual aids/social stories pre-op if used at home
  • Avoid unnecessary examination/handling pre-induction if distressing
  • Post-op: quiet recovery environment, minimise sensory stimulation, clear analgesia plan as pain may be communicated atypically
