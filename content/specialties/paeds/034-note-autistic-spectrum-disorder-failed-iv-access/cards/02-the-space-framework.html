A simple framework for supporting autistic children (and adults) through healthcare. Ask parents what works for their child, and use the hospital passport if they have one.

  • Sensory needs: noise, light, touch and smells; a quiet room or side room, dimmed lights, minimal staff, the child's own comforter or headphones.
  • Predictability: explain what will happen and when; first on the list or a set time; minimise waiting; use pictures or social stories; avoid last-minute changes.
  • Acceptance: accept different behaviour (stimming, avoiding eye contact); do not force interaction; be flexible about the induction plan.
  • Communication: clear, literal language; check the child's preferred way to communicate (e.g. Makaton, pictures); give time to process.
  • Empathy: recognise how stressful hospital can be for the child and family; involve parents as the experts on their child.
