Sites to try

  • Hand: dorsal veins, and the ventral (volar) surface of the hand and wrist.
  • Foot: the great saphenous vein, which runs just in front of the medial malleolus; dorsal foot veins.

Recommendations

  • Optimise: warmth, dependent position, vein visualisation aids (transillumination/USS)
  • Topical vasodilation (GTN patch) or local anaesthetic cream
  • Ultrasound-guided cannulation
  • Intraosseous access if emergency and access still fails
  • Consider inhalational induction to facilitate access once anaesthetised
  • Senior help early if repeated attempts failing
