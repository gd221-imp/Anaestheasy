Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Associated syndromes (Pierre Robin, Treacher Collins, 22q11)
  • cardiac lesions
  • URTI check

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway assessment
  • feeding and weight

Investigations (Ix)

—

Optimise

—

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Oral RAE tube, throat pack
  • Difficult airway equipment
  • Boyle-Davis gag (watch for ETT compression)

Induction

  • Gas or IV induction
  • secure the tube well
  • Infraorbital nerve blocks for lip

Maintenance

  • palate infiltration by surgeon
  • Remifentanil or careful opioid

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Throat pack out
  • nasopharyngeal airway or tongue stitch after palate repair

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Wound dehiscence

Delayed

  • Fistula
  • Velopharyngeal insufficiency
  • Speech problems

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • ETT kinking or dislodgement with the gag
  • Difficult intubation

Delayed

  • Post-op airway obstruction
  • Desaturation and OSA

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID, careful opioid

Destination

  • HDU-level monitoring for airway obstruction
