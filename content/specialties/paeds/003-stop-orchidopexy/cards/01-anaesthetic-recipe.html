Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Birth history and prematurity
  • URTI
  • Previous anaesthetics

Examination (O/E)

  • Weight, day case suitability

Investigations (Ix)

  • None

Optimise

  • Fasting (6-4-1)
  • Topical anaesthetic cream; premedication if anxious

Intra-op

Equipment

  • LMA, caudal or ilioinguinal block kit
  • atropine drawn up

Induction

  • Gas or IV induction, then block

Maintenance

  • deepen before cord traction

Emergence

  • Remove LMA deep or awake
  • Recover in the lateral position

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Injury to the vas or testicular vessels

Delayed

  • Testicular atrophy
  • Re-ascent of the testis
  • Wound infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Laryngospasm or bradycardia with cord traction

Delayed

  • Urinary retention or leg weakness after a caudal
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia

Destination

  • home same day
