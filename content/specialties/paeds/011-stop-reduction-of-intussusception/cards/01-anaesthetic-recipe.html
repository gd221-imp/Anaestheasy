Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Duration of symptoms
  • Failed radiological reduction?
  • Signs of sepsis

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Hydration and perfusion
  • Abdominal distension

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E, group and save
  • Blood gas

Optimise

  • Fluids, sepsis

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT, NG tube

Induction

  • RSI

Maintenance

  • Volatile or TIVA
  • Fluids for losses; antibiotics
  • Warming

Emergence

  • Extubate awake if stable
  • Aspirate the NG tube

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bowel perforation during reduction
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Recurrence
  • Bowel resection if not reducible
  • Adhesions

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration at induction (obstructed bowel)
  • Hypovolaemia and sepsis

Delayed

  • Post-op ileus
  • Fluid and electrolyte imbalance

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, morphine infusion or NCA
  • Wound infiltration or rectus sheath blocks

Destination

  • Ward or HDU
