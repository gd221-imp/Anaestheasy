Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Stone history, previous procedures
  • Urinary infection
  • Comorbidities

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway (prone)
  • Chest

Investigations (Ix)

  • Renal function, infection, positioning plan

Optimise

  • Treat urinary infection before surgery
  • Stop anticoagulants per plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT, arterial line if comorbid, prone positioning kit

Induction

  • careful prone positioning

Maintenance

  • GA with ETT
  • Watch for pneumothorax and bleeding

Emergence

  • Turn supine as a team
  • Extubate awake; check chest for pneumothorax

Post-op

Complications

  • watch for sepsis

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Pleural injury (pneumothorax or hydrothorax)
  • Bowel injury

Delayed

  • Infection and urosepsis
  • Urinary leak
  • Residual stones

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Prone positioning issues
  • Fluid absorption and hypothermia from irrigation

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia, opioid PRN

Destination

  • Urology ward; HDU if septic or bled
