Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Primary graft non-function or delayed graft function
  • Renal artery or renal vein thrombosis
  • Urine leak from the ureteric anastomosis

Delayed

  • Ureteric stenosis or obstruction
  • Lymphocele
  • Wound infection or other infection
  • Graft rejection
  • Recurrence of the primary disease
