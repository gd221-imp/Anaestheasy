Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Cardiorespiratory reserve
  • Smoking
  • Previous chemotherapy
  • Renal function

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Cardiorespiratory examination
  • Back for epidural

Investigations (Ix)

  • Prehabilitation, renal function

Optimise

  • Prehabilitation and nutrition
  • Anaemia correction
  • Smoking cessation

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, cardiac output monitor, epidural or rectus sheath catheters

Induction

  • robotic cases need steep head-down
  • Can consider a small cannula awake and a large one asleep; arterial line once asleep
  • Can consider tying or taping the tube loosely to allow for facial swelling
  • Can consider a throat pack, as gastric contents can pool in steep head-down

Maintenance

  • GA
  • Can consider propofol and remifentanil TIVA with relaxant (the robot is rigid) and a metaraminol infusion
  • Can consider keeping fluids dry: about 2 L for the case
  • Can consider morphine 0.1 mg/kg; magnesium; dexamethasone 6.6 mg and ondansetron 4 mg

Emergence

  • Check for airway and facial oedema after head-down
  • Extubate awake

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Major blood loss
  • Rectal or ureteric injury

Delayed

  • Ileus
  • Urinary leak
  • Ureteric stricture
  • Infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Steep head-down tilt: raised ICP, IOP and airway oedema (robotic)
  • Hypothermia

Delayed

  • VTE
  • Acute kidney injury
  • Chest infection

Analgesia

  • Epidural or rectus sheath catheters
  • Paracetamol
  • PCA if needed
  • Can consider a spinal with diamorphine (mainly adds intra-op hypotension), or modified-release oral opioid instead of PCA
  • Can consider laxatives; early eating and drinking

Destination

  • HDU, ERAS
