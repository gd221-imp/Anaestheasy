Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, long lines and extensions (arms tucked)
  • Arterial line if comorbid
  • Eye protection, anti-slip mattress

Induction

—

Maintenance

  • GA with relaxation
  • restrictive fluids until the anastomosis (reduces facial and airway oedema)
  • Manage high airway pressures and hypercapnia
  • Keep the patient still: robot docked

Post-op

Complications

  • Check for airway and facial oedema before extubation
  • Catheter care

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Rectal injury
  • Anastomotic leak

Delayed

  • Incontinence
  • Erectile dysfunction
  • Lymphocele

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Raised ICP and IOP in steep head-down
  • Airway and facial oedema
  • Hypercapnia and surgical emphysema

Delayed

  • Corneal abrasion
  • Pressure and nerve injuries
  • VTE
