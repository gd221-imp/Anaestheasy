Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Cardiorespiratory reserve for steep head-down and pneumoperitoneum
  • Glaucoma or raised ICP risk
  • Long procedure: pressure care

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Cardiorespiratory examination
  • Eyes (glaucoma)

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E, group and save
  • ECG

Optimise

  • Discuss tolerance of steep head-down with cardiorespiratory disease

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, long lines and extensions (arms tucked)
  • Arterial line if comorbid
  • Eye protection, anti-slip mattress

Induction

  • IV induction, relaxant, ETT secured well
  • Lines and extensions placed before arms are tucked
  • Eyes protected

Maintenance

  • GA with relaxation
  • restrictive fluids until the anastomosis (reduces facial and airway oedema)
  • Manage high airway pressures and hypercapnia
  • Keep the patient still: robot docked

Emergence

  • Level the table; cuff-leak test if facial oedema
  • Extubate awake, sitting up

Post-op

Complications

  • Check for airway and facial oedema before extubation
  • Catheter care

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Rectal injury
  • Anastomotic leak

Delayed

  • Incontinence
  • Erectile dysfunction
  • Lymphocele

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Raised ICP and IOP in steep head-down
  • Airway and facial oedema
  • Hypercapnia and surgical emphysema

Delayed

  • Corneal abrasion
  • Pressure and nerve injuries
  • VTE

Analgesia

  • Multimodal analgesia

Destination

  • Urology ward, often next-day discharge
