Hypertension, hypotension (late), arrhythmias.

Resp effects: dyspnoea, ↓SpO2, pulmonary oedema.

Neuro effects: ↑ICP, cerebral oedema, water intoxication - headache/confusion/reduced consciousness/visual disturbance/seizures.

Glycine toxicity: visual disturbance/transient blindness (glycine is an inhibitory retinal neurotransmitter), NMDA potentiation → encephalopathy/seizures; glycine is deaminated to ammonia - CNS depression once ammonia >100µmol/L; nausea/vomiting within 1hr.

The ideal irrigation fluid: non-conductive, non-haemolytic, transparent.
