Microlaryngoscopy tube (MLT) vs Tubeless techniques for shared-airway laryngeal surgery:

MLT:

  • Airway protection: yes
  • Cons: reduced access to the posterior 1/3 of the larynx; tube diameter limits "surgical" access

Tubeless (e.g. jet ventilation/apnoeic technique):

  • HFNO - best surgical access
  • Cons: no airway protection, cannot monitor end-tidal CO2, requires TIVA
