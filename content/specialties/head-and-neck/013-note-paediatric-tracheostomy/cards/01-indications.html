Long-term respiratory support (chronic lung disease, chronic neuromuscular disorders, reduced respiratory drive, tracheobronchomalacia); clearance of secretions; airway patency (bulbar palsy); upper airway obstruction (craniofacial abnormalities, head and neck lesions, laryngotracheomalacia, subglottic stenosis); trauma (burns, caustic alkali ingestion); perioperative (failed extubation, major head and neck surgery).

Differences from adult tracheostomy: open procedure, no inner tube, usually uncuffed.
