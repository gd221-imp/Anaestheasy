Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Pneumothorax
  • Oesophageal injury
  • Damage to the recurrent laryngeal nerve

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Granuloma formation
  • Erosion of nearby vessels
  • Suprastomal collapse
  • Tracheal stenosis
  • Tracheo-oesophageal fistula

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Loss of the airway

Delayed

  • Accidental decannulation
  • Obstruction with secretions
