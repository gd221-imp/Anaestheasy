Investigations (Ix)

Optimise

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Microlaryngoscopy tube (5.0–6.0), or jet ventilation equipment, or HFNO for tubeless
  • TIVA
  • Rescue airway kit

Induction

—

Maintenance

  • TIVA with remifentanil
  • relaxation for surgical access
  • Shared airway: agree who controls it
  • Laser precautions if laser used

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Dexamethasone if oedema expected
  • Recover sitting up
  • Watch for stridor

Surgical

Immediate

  • Airway bleeding
  • Vocal cord injury

Delayed

  • Airway oedema
  • Voice change

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypoxia and hypercapnia
  • Laryngospasm
  • Airway fire

Delayed
