N.B. Trismus = painful restriction in mouth opening due to spasm of the muscles of mastication. Mild = 20-30mm mouth opening, moderate = 10-20mm, severe = <10mm.

Causes: intra-articular (mandible fracture, TMJ dislocation, rheumatoid arthritis), extra-articular (fracture of the neck of the condyle, oedema/trismus of infection, tetanus, malignant hyperthermia).
