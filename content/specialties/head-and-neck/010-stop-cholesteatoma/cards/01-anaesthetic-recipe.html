Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • PONV risk

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Facial nerve function documented

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none for fit patients

Optimise

  • PONV risk score; plan multimodal prophylaxis

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT or LMA, nerve monitoring

Induction

  • TCI propofol and remifentanil
  • Short-acting relaxant only, for facial nerve monitoring

Maintenance

  • TIVA
  • avoid nitrous oxide
  • controlled hypotension

Emergence

  • Smooth emergence; avoid coughing and straining

Post-op

Complications

  • PONV prophylaxis

Surgical

Immediate

  • Facial nerve injury
  • Dural exposure or CSF leak

Delayed

  • Recurrence
  • Hearing loss
  • Vertigo

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Bleeding from hypertension
  • Nitrous oxide effects on the middle ear

Delayed

  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID
  • Local infiltration
  • Opioid PRN

Destination

  • Day case or overnight ward
