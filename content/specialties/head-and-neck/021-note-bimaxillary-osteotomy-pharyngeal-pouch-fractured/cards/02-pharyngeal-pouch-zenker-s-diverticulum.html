Outpouching of pharyngeal mucosa through Killian's dehiscence (between thyropharyngeus and cricopharyngeus of inferior constrictor). Presents with dysphagia, regurgitation of undigested food, halitosis, aspiration risk. Anaesthetic considerations: high aspiration risk - consider RSI, empty the pouch preoperatively if possible (recent meal/food may sit in the pouch for a prolonged period), gentle instrumentation to avoid perforation.
