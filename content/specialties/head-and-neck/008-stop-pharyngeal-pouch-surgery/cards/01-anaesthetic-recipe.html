Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Aspiration risk

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Chest (aspiration)

Investigations (Ix)

  • Barium swallow

Optimise

  • Fluids only for 48 hours before; empty the pouch if possible
  • Head up

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT, suction

Induction

  • RSI

Maintenance

  • Volatile or TIVA
  • Shared airway with the endoscope

Emergence

  • Extubate awake, sitting up

Post-op

Complications

  • Watch for perforation

Surgical

Immediate

  • Oesophageal or pouch perforation
  • Recurrent laryngeal nerve injury
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Mediastinitis
  • Fistula
  • Recurrence

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration of pouch contents at induction

Delayed

  • Aspiration pneumonia

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; opioid PRN

Destination

  • Ward; nil by mouth until reviewed
