Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Trismus, airway

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway: trismus (may be pain-related and improve after induction)
  • Loose teeth and bleeding

Investigations (Ix)

  • Imaging (OPG, CT)
  • FBC, group and save

Optimise

  • Agree the airway route with the surgeon
  • Antibiotics

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Nasal ETT, fibreoptic scope

Induction

  • Nasal intubation; awake fibreoptic if trismus is mechanical
  • Throat pack

Maintenance

  • Volatile or TIVA
  • PONV prophylaxis

Emergence

  • Awake fibreoptic or gas induction

Post-op

Complications

  • Extubation plan

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Nerve injury (inferior alveolar)

Delayed

  • Malocclusion
  • Non-union
  • Infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Difficult intubation from trismus or swelling
  • Aspiration of blood or teeth

Delayed

  • Airway obstruction with intermaxillary fixation (wire cutters at the bedside)
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID
  • Opioid PRN

Destination

  • Ward; wire or elastic cutters at the bedside
