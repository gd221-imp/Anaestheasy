• Use the lowest FiO2 tolerated (ideally <30%, avoid N2O as it also supports combustion)
  • Use a laser-resistant/laser-safe ETT (double-cuffed, filled with saline ± dye to indicate cuff rupture) or consider tubeless techniques (jet ventilation, apnoeic oxygenation) to remove the ETT from the field entirely
  • Protect the eyes (patient and all staff) with appropriate laser safety goggles specific to the wavelength used
  • Have saline available immediately for airway fire, plus a fire management protocol (stop ventilation, remove the ETT, extinguish with saline, re-establish airway, assess for thermal injury/administer steroids, bronchoscopy to assess damage)
  • Damp swabs around the surgical field, minimise other flammable materials in the field
