Red flags: drooling, reduced air entry/volume without treatment, systemically unwell.

Causes: foreign body aspiration, epiglottitis, croup, laryngitis, anaphylaxis, neck abscess, vocal cord palsy.

Temporising measures: HFNO (oxygenation and PEEP), nebulised adrenaline 1-5ml of 1:1000 (vasoconstricts mucosa), steroids (reduce oedema over hours), heliox (↓density → laminar flow, reduced Reynolds number).
