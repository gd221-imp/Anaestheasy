• Le Fort I: transverse fracture through the maxilla
  • Le Fort II: pyramidal fracture
  • Le Fort III: horizontal fracture (craniofacial disjunction - face separated from the skull base)
  • Le Fort IV: Le Fort III plus involvement of the frontal bone

Le Fort II/III are relative contraindications to nasal intubation due to risk of dural tear and CSF leak (unless removal needed for post-op bleeding/airway compromise - use with caution and limited cuts/scissors nearby).
