Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Mechanism and other injuries (head, C-spine)
  • Last oral intake
  • Bleeding

Examination (O/E)

  • C-spine, airway assessment, bleeding

Investigations (Ix)

  • CT face and C-spine
  • FBC, group and save

Optimise

  • Agree airway route with the surgeon (nasal, submental or tracheostomy)
  • Avoid nasal intubation with base of skull or Le Fort II/III fractures

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Nasal or submental intubation kit, fibreoptic scope

Induction

  • Awake fibreoptic if difficult; otherwise IV induction with videolaryngoscope
  • Suction ready; throat pack

Maintenance

  • Agree airway with surgeon

Emergence

  • awake fibreoptic if difficult

Post-op

Complications

  • Extubation plan, wire cutters if jaw wired

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding into the airway
  • Malunion

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Nerve injury (infraorbital, inferior alveolar)
  • Malocclusion

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Difficult intubation and failed airway
  • Aspiration of blood
  • Inadvertent intracranial nasal tube in base of skull fracture

Delayed

  • Airway obstruction after extubation
  • Need for tracheostomy

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID if appropriate
  • Opioid PRN
  • Local by surgeon

Destination

  • Ward or HDU; ICU if airway at risk
