Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Indication (obstruction, prolonged ventilation, surgery)
  • Previous neck surgery or radiotherapy
  • Ease of previous intubation

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway assessment, plan for oxygenation

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, clotting
  • Imaging of the airway or neck if a lesion

Optimise

  • Plan if the airway is obstructed (awake fibreoptic or awake tracheostomy under local)
  • Correct coagulopathy

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT, airway equipment, capnography

Induction

  • IV induction if airway secure; awake technique if obstructed
  • Relaxant once the airway is secure

Maintenance

  • GA
  • FiO₂ 1.0
  • withdraw ETT as tracheostomy placed

Emergence

  • Confirm tracheostomy position with capnography
  • Wake on the tracheostomy; secure ties

Post-op

Complications

  • Bedhead signs, tracheostomy emergency algorithm

Surgical

Immediate

  • Haemorrhage
  • Pneumothorax
  • Surgical emphysema
  • Damage to nearby vessels or structures
  • Failure

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Ulceration
  • Granuloma
  • Tracheo-innominate fistula
  • Stenosis
  • Tracheomalacia
  • Dysphonia
  • Cosmetic and psychological effects

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Misplacement
  • Cuff herniation
  • Aspiration
  • Air embolism

Delayed

  • Blockage
  • Displacement

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; local infiltration
  • Opioid PRN

Destination

  • Ward with tracheostomy-competent staff, or HDU/ICU
