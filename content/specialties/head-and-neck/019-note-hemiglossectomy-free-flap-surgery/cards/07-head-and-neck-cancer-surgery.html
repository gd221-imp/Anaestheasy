Additional anaesthetic considerations (in addition to general shared-airway care): increased bleeding risk with neck dissection - wide-bore IV access; full AAGBI monitoring plus invasive arterial blood pressure and cardiac output monitoring, 15-20° head-up positioning, remifentanil infusion for controlled hypotension, long operating time (↑duration and bleeding risk); ?refeeding syndrome risk, ?alcohol withdrawal risk.

Previous radiotherapy to the neck: fibrosis of the tissues of the neck → difficult facemask ventilation, laryngoscopy, and surgical dissection.
