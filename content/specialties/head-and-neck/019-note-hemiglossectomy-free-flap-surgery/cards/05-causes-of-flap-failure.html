Colour change, capillary refill time/turgor change, temperature change, bleeding on pinprick, abnormal Doppler signal.

Anaesthetic causes: excess fluid administration, hypercoagulable state.

Surgical causes: 1) insufficient arterial supply (kinking, thrombosis, vasospasm), 2) insufficient venous drainage (kinking, haematoma, compression, poor flap positioning causing oedema → venous congestion), 3) reperfusion injury (↑warm ischaemia time → ↑inflammatory mediators → microvascular failure), 4) infection.
