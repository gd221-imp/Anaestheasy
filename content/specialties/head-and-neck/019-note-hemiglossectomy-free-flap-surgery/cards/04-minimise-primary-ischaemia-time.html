Hagen-Poiseuille equation governs flow through the anastomosed vessel: Q = πΔPr⁴ / 8ηL - therefore maintain MAP to ensure adequate filling and vessel diameter, minimise CVP (not too much - use cardiac output monitoring), maintain normothermia (skin-core temperature difference <1.5°C), avoid alkalosis, avoid excessive vasopressor use (use CO monitoring/stroke volume variation instead), ensure adequate pain control (catecholamines → vasoconstriction), maintain normal haematocrit 0.3-0.35 for normal blood flow.

Indications for free flap reconstruction: reconstructive surgery after trauma, cancer (post-mastectomy or head & neck cancer).
