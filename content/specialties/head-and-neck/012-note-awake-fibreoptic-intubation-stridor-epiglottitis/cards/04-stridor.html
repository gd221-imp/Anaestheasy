A harsh, high-pitched sound occurring due to upper airway obstruction. Inspiratory → laryngeal obstruction; expiratory → tracheobronchial obstruction; biphasic → subglottic/glottic pathology.

Differential diagnosis: croup, epiglottitis, foreign body, trauma, acute tonsillitis, retropharyngeal abscess, angio-oedema, smoke inhalation, diphtheria.
