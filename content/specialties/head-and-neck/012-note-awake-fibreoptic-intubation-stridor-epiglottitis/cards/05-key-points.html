Epiglottitis (H.influenzae/strep pneumoniae/strep pyogenes/staph aureus):

Presentation: rapid deterioration, absence of cough, sore throat, fever, odynophagia, muffled voice; O/E - audible inspiratory stridor, irritability, pyrexia, leaning forward and drooling.

Management: call for help - consultant anaesthetist and consultant ENT surgeon, plus a trained assistant; avoid distressing the child; inhalational induction (takes longer to achieve depth) → laryngoscopy and IV access; PICU, blood cultures, throat swab, IV fluids, humidified oxygen, airway toilet, broad-spectrum antibiotics; extubate 2-3 days later once oedema has settled, leak present around the ETT, afebrile - flexible nasendoscopy to check.
