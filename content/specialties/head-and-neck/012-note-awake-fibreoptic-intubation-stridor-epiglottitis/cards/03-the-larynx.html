Protects trachea during swallowing, allows coughing, allows speech, allows straining, physiological PEEP. Extends from root of tongue to the cricoid cartilage (C3→C6); squamous epithelium above the vocal cords and columnar below.

Arterial supply: paired superior laryngeal artery (from external carotid) and paired inferior laryngeal artery (from thyrocervical trunk); paired veins accompany.

Innervation: extrinsic muscles move the larynx up during swallowing and return it to the resting position (suprahyoids - CN V3, VII and XII/C1; infrahyoids - ansa cervicalis C1-3); intrinsic muscles - abduction (posterior cricoarytenoid), adduction (lateral cricoarytenoid, transverse arytenoid), slacken the vocal cords (thyroarytenoid, vocalis) - all via the recurrent laryngeal nerve (branch of vagus) except cricothyroid (only tensor, superior laryngeal nerve).

Sensation: superior laryngeal nerve - glottis and above; recurrent laryngeal nerve - subglottis.

N.B. RLN palsy: partial (abductors affected more than adductors) vs complete (vocal cord in a semi-abducted position).
