Surgical

Immediate

  • Recurrent laryngeal nerve injury: unilateral causes a hoarse voice; bilateral causes stridor or airway obstruction and may need emergency re-intubation or tracheostomy
  • Wound haematoma, which can rapidly compress the airway: keep suture cutters or a staple remover at the bedside
  • Pneumothorax (if a retrosternal goitre needs sternotomy)

Delayed

  • Hypocalcaemia from parathyroid injury or removal

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Thyroid storm
  • Airway obstruction at extubation

Delayed

  • Post-op nausea and vomiting
