Iodine trapped by the thyroid follicular cells → oxidised and organified onto tyrosine residues on thyroglobulin (via thyroid peroxidase) → forms MIT/DIT → coupling of MIT+DIT or DIT+DIT forms T3/T4 → stored in colloid → released into circulation bound to thyroxine-binding globulin, controlled by the hypothalamic-pituitary-thyroid axis (TRH → TSH → T3/T4, with negative feedback).
