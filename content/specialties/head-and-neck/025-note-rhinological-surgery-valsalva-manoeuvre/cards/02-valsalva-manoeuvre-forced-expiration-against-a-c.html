• Phase I: transient rise in BP as intrathoracic pressure rises and blood is forced from the pulmonary circulation into the left heart
  • Phase II: BP falls as venous return is impaired by raised intrathoracic pressure - reflex tachycardia and increased peripheral resistance occur (baroreceptor reflex)
  • Phase III: brief further fall in BP at release of the strain as intrathoracic pressure suddenly drops
  • Phase IV: overshoot rise in BP above baseline (increased venous return meeting an already vasoconstricted circulation) with reflex bradycardia
