Functional endoscopic sinus surgery, septoplasty): shared airway, often performed with the patient in a head-up position to reduce venous congestion/bleeding; throat pack to prevent blood trickling into the stomach/aspiration; controlled/deliberate hypotension frequently requested by the surgeon to improve the surgical field in a highly vascular area; local vasoconstrictor application (e.g. topical adrenaline/cocaine solution) - watch for systemic absorption causing hypertension/arrhythmia; TIVA often favoured to facilitate controlled hypotension and reduce bleeding/improve surgical view compared with volatile agents; PONV prophylaxis important (blood in the stomach from swallowed blood increases nausea risk); smooth emergence to avoid coughing/straining and disrupting surgical repair, remove throat pack and check for further bleeding before extubation.
