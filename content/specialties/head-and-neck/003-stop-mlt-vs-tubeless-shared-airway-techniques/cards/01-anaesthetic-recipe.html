Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Symptoms of obstruction: stridor, voice change
  • Previous airway procedures
  • Cardiorespiratory reserve

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway: mouth opening, neck extension, teeth
  • Stridor

Investigations (Ix)

  • Nasendoscopy findings
  • CT if a lesion

Optimise

  • Airway obstruction, plan with surgeon

Intra-op

Equipment

  • MLT tube or high-flow nasal oxygen, TIVA

Induction

  • tubeless with high-flow oxygen if suitable

Maintenance

  • TIVA with remifentanil

Emergence

  • Rapid emergence (remifentanil)
  • Return of airway reflexes before leaving theatre

Post-op

Complications

  • Sit up, monitor for airway oedema

Surgical

Immediate

  • Airway bleeding
  • Vocal cord injury

Delayed

  • Airway oedema
  • Voice change

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypoxia and hypercapnia
  • Airway fire with laser
  • Laryngospasm

Delayed

  • Post-op stridor

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; usually minimal pain

Destination

  • Day case or ward; HDU if airway concerns
