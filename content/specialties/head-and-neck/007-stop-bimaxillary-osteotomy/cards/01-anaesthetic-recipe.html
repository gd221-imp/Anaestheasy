Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Previous orthodontics, OSA
  • Bleeding history

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Nasal patency

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, group and save

Optimise

  • Airway plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Nasal ETT, arterial line

Induction

  • IV induction, nasal ETT (vasoconstrictor to nose)
  • Throat pack
  • Secure the tube well

Maintenance

  • Hypotensive anaesthesia

Emergence

  • Remove the throat pack; suction
  • Extubate fully awake; wire or elastic cutters at the bedside

Post-op

Complications

  • Wire cutters at bedside

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding into the airway
  • Nerve injury (inferior alveolar)

Delayed

  • Malocclusion
  • Infection
  • Relapse

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Nasal tube damage or displacement
  • Blood loss needing transfusion (rare)

Delayed

  • Airway obstruction with swelling or wired jaws (wire cutters at the bedside)
  • PONV with fixed jaws

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID
  • Opioid PRN
  • Dexamethasone for swelling

Destination

  • Ward; HDU if intermaxillary fixation and airway concern
