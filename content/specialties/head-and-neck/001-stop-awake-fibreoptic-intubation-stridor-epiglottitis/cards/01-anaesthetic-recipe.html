Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Explain to the patient
  • antisialagogue (glycopyrronium)

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway: mouth opening, neck movement, nasal patency
  • Stridor and work of breathing
  • Previous airway charts

Investigations (Ix)

  • Nasendoscopy findings
  • CT of the airway if a lesion

Optimise

  • Explain the procedure and consent
  • Antisialagogue (glycopyrronium) 30 to 60 minutes before
  • Plan B and front-of-neck access agreed

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Fibreoptic scope, topical lidocaine, high-flow nasal oxygen
  • Remifentanil TCI

Induction

  • Topicalise airway, light sedation
  • Pass scope then tube
  • confirm with capnography before induction

Maintenance

  • Once the tube is confirmed: induce and maintain as for the surgery

Emergence

  • Extubation plan per DAS guidelines (awake, exchange catheter if high risk)
  • Nil by mouth for 2 hours after topicalisation

Post-op

Complications

  • Plan extubation

Surgical

Immediate

None specific.

Delayed

None specific.

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Over-sedation with airway obstruction or apnoea
  • LA toxicity from topicalisation
  • Laryngospasm or complete obstruction in stridor
  • Bleeding and failed intubation

Delayed

  • Airway trauma and sore throat
  • Aspiration after topicalisation

Analgesia

  • Per the surgery; paracetamol for sore throat

Destination

  • Per the surgery; HDU if airway risk
