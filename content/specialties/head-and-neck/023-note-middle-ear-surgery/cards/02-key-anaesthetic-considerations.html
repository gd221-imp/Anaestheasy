• N2O avoided or discontinued well before middle ear closure - it diffuses into the closed middle ear space faster than nitrogen diffuses out, causing a rise in middle ear pressure which can displace grafts/prostheses or cause post-op pain
  • Facial nerve monitoring often used during mastoid/acoustic neuroma surgery - therefore avoid or minimise long-acting muscle relaxants intraoperatively so nerve monitoring remains reliable
  • Controlled hypotension often requested by the surgeon to improve the surgical field in a very vascular, confined operative area
  • PONV is common and particularly undesirable (risk to graft/reconstruction from straining/vomiting) - use multimodal antiemetic prophylaxis
  • Positioning: head turned away from the operative side, care with pressure areas and ETT security as the head is often draped and inaccessible during the case
  • Local infiltration with adrenaline-containing solution is common - watch for arrhythmia.
