Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Duration, trismus, dysphagia, voice change
  • Diabetes
  • Fasting status

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway: trismus, floor-of-mouth swelling, stridor
  • Signs of sepsis

Investigations (Ix)

  • Trismus, airway swelling, imaging

Optimise

  • Antibiotics and steroids
  • Senior anaesthetist and ENT surgeon; front-of-neck access ready
  • Agree airway plan before induction

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Fibreoptic scope, videolaryngoscope, front-of-neck access kit

Induction

  • Awake fibreoptic or awake tracheostomy if severe obstruction
  • Gas induction only if obstruction mild
  • Trismus may not relax after induction

Maintenance

  • Volatile or TIVA
  • Antibiotics

Emergence

  • Awake fibreoptic intubation or gas induction depending on obstruction

Post-op

Complications

  • May need to stay intubated

Surgical

Immediate

  • Rupture of the abscess into the airway
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Spread of infection (Ludwig angina, mediastinitis)
  • Recurrence

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Loss of the airway at induction
  • Trismus limiting laryngoscopy
  • Aspiration of pus

Delayed

  • Post-extubation airway obstruction from oedema
  • Sepsis

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID if appropriate
  • Opioid PRN

Destination

  • ICU
