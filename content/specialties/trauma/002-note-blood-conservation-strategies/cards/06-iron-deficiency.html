Causes: reduced intake (vegan/anorexia), malabsorption, chronic blood loss (GI/menstrual), increased demand (pregnancy).

AAGBI guidance: FBC for all surgery with EBL >500mL; treat anaemia early (>130 or when known >2wks away).

Ferritin <30 or with CRP <100 (or unknown <30) = iron deficient.

PO iron 40-60mg/day if time (6-8/52 away).

IV iron if surgery <6/52 away.
