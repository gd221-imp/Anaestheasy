Electrothermal burn - heat dissipated when current flow meets resistance, therefore burns at contact points with the ground.

Electrical arcs - no direct contact (lightning) → temperature up to 2500°C → deep thermal burns.

Flame - ignition of clothes.

Flash - heat from nearby air → not from current through the body.

Extent of injury determined by: voltage, current (AC>DC), resistance, duration of contact, pathway of current.

Threshold effects:

1mA - tingling.

5mA - pain without tetany.

10mA - burns.

15mA - tonic muscle contraction.

25mA - tetany of respiratory muscles.

50mA - respiratory arrest, severe pain.

75-100mA (macroshock) - VF.
