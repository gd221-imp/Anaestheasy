6 P's: pain, paraesthesia, paralysis, paraplegia, priapism, ptosis.

Usually C-spine → increased risk with children as relatively larger head vs column, shallow facet joints, increased ligamentous laxity, weaker neck muscles, increased head:body ratio.

Rx: primary survey → MILS (manual in-line stabilisation), X-ray/CT c-spine as guided by rules → CT → MRI (if neurology present).
