1° - direct tissue damage from the shockwave.

2° - shrapnel injury.

3° - injury from displacement of body (blast wind throwing the casualty).

4° - other types (burns).

5° (quinary) - hyperinflammatory state from additives in the device (bacteria, radiation, chemicals).

Long-term psychological effects are usually considered separately.

Considerations: type of blast, distance from blast, enclosed space (increases severity).
