Pre-hospital: minimise volume of blood loss - direct pressure, tourniquet, pelvic binder, splint long bones.

Damage control resuscitation: remove wet clothing, vasopressors, active warming, roll patient with log-roll to assess thirds/back.

Damage control resuscitation → 1) permissive hypotension (MAP 40-50) - volume of clot (SBP 80-100). 2) early haemostasis. 3) damage control - limit surgical intervention to control haemorrhage → definitive surgery.

CRASH-2: TXA reduces mortality with traumatic haemorrhage when given within 3hrs of injury.

CRASH-3: TXA within 3hrs reduces head injury death in mild-moderate isolated TBI.
