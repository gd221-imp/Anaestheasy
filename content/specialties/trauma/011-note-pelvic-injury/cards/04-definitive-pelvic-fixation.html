PRE-OP → other traumatic injury, position, urological injury, 4-6 units crossmatch, cell salvage, coagulation & electrolytes.

INTRA-OP → arterial line/large-bore IV access, 2x wide-bore IV, reinforced ETT, extra staff for positioning, TXA/give infusion, serial ABG & TEG, VTE risk assessment.

POST-OP → multimodal analgesia, avoid NSAIDs, epidural, HDU, assess for VTE and continue mechanical & pharmacological VTE prophylaxis when appropriate.
