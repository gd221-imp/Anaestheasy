A/B: exacerbation of COPD, pneumonia - caution with neuraxial if respiratory compromise.

C: arrhythmia/MI/valvular/structural disease - IABP/cardiac output monitoring; avoid neuraxial if reduced LV outflow (e.g. severe AS).

D: stroke, peripheral neuropathy, dementia, Parkinson's disease - difficult positioning for neuraxial.

E: DM - consider awake technique to allow neuro monitoring; polypharmacy - judicious drug use. Infection - avoid neuraxial if sepsis. Difficult positioning if MSK disease.
