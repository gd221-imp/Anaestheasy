Medical assessment, frailty assessment, cognitive assessment, analgesia (multimodal + fascia iliaca block), consent issues. Surgery within 36hrs.

NICE guidance: regular (not PRN) opioids on prescription to allow OT/mobilisation; avoid NSAIDs.

Risk vs benefit in the very co-morbid: Hb >90g/L (>100g/L if IHD); Association of Anaesthetists guidance on anticoagulant timescales.
