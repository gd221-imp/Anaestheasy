Cochrane review 2016 - spinal vs GA (spinal only superior for VTE risk). Since then REGAIN (2021) and RAGA (2022) found no difference in ambulation, mortality or delirium between spinal and GA.

Aim bupivacaine dose <10mg. Titrate to effect/level checked (EEG & SV monitoring can help).

Benefits of good anaesthetic technique: better analgesia, facilitates OT/ITU, reduced time to rehabilitation. Care if anticoagulated (neuraxial).

Avoid hypotension. Reduce risk of POCD - avoid excess opioids, care with anticholinergics, avoid benzodiazepines. Consider regional/nerve blocks (fascia iliaca).
