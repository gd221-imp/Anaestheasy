Definition: muscle cell necrosis leading to leakage of intracellular contents (K+, PO4 3-, CK, myoglobin, urate).

CK >1000IU/L or urinary [myoglobin] positive.

Treatment: supportive (CO2/IVF) / specific (treat cause; sodium bicarbonate to raise urine pH >6.5 keeps myoglobin soluble and reduces tubular precipitation - but evidence is weak: bicarbonate/mannitol did not reduce AKI in >2000 trauma patients, so aggressive IV fluids are the key treatment).
