Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Existing EVD

Examination (O/E)

  • WFNS grade, GCS, focal deficit

Investigations (Ix)

  • Sodium, ECG changes and troponin (neurogenic stunning)

Optimise

  • nimodipine started
  • anticoagulation plan with neuroradiology

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line before induction
  • Remote-site set-up in the angiography suite
  • long lines
  • Heparin and protamine available

Induction

  • Smooth induction avoiding hypertension (rupture) and hypotension (ischaemia)

Maintenance

  • Normocapnia, normothermia
  • heparin as directed
  • Be ready for intraprocedural rupture: protamine, BP control, EVD drainage

Emergence

  • Smooth emergence: avoid coughing and hypertension
  • Lie flat for groin haemostasis

Post-op

Complications

  • Extubate if good grade
  • Watch for vasospasm days 3 to 14
  • avoid hyponatraemia and hypovolaemia

Surgical

Immediate

  • Aneurysm rupture during coiling
  • Thromboembolic stroke

Delayed

  • Vasospasm and delayed cerebral ischaemia
  • Hydrocephalus
  • Groin haematoma

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypertensive surge at induction or laryngoscopy
  • Contrast reaction

Delayed

  • Hyponatraemia (SIADH or cerebral salt wasting)
  • Neurogenic pulmonary oedema

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, codeine or small opioid doses

Destination

  • neuro critical care if poor grade
