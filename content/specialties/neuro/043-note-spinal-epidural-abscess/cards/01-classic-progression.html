Back pain, followed by fever, followed by neurological symptoms - radicular pain, then sensory deficit and motor weakness, then bladder/bowel dysfunction, and ultimately paralysis if untreated

Risk factors: diabetes, alcohol excess, IV drug use, systemic infection, immunocompromise, spinal abnormality, recent spinal intervention or trauma

Microbiology: around 80-90% Staphylococcus aureus; also streptococcus or gram-negative/anaerobic organisms

Investigations: FBC, CRP, blood cultures, MRI spine with contrast - this should be done urgently, as neurological deficit is not reversible once established

Treatment: empirical antibiotics (follow local guidelines; e.g. a cephalosporin and vancomycin) until culture results are back, plus neurosurgical decompression and washout
