Declare an emergency, get help, ask the surgeon to stop stimulating; keep the head fixed in the Mayfield pins (removing them risks scalp laceration and loss of head control - protect the airway and neck instead); irrigate the brain surface with ice-cold saline (watch for scalp laceration or C-spine injury from sudden movement); ABCDEF and give oxygen; small doses of propofol (10-30mg), midazolam (2-5mg), or thiopentone (25-30mg) can terminate the seizure - all of these suppress the ECoG signal to some degree, so are used judiciously if cortical mapping is still needed
