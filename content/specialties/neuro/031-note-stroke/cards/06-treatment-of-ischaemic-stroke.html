IV thrombolysis (alteplase, a tissue plasminogen activator): within 4.5hrs of onset; less effective for large artery occlusion (e.g. MCA); target BP <185/110 otherwise there's an increased risk of haemorrhagic transformation

Mechanical thrombectomy: interventional neuroradiology via femoral puncture for endovascular clot retrieval; within 24hrs of onset; better outcomes for anterior circulation strokes; used when thrombolysis is contraindicated (e.g. recent surgery); supported as first-line by the MR CLEAN trial
