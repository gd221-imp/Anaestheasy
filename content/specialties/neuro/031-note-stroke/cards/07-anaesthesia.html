The principle is 'time is brain': maintain CPP and avoid secondary brain injury/reperfusion injury

Be alert to malignant MCA syndrome, haemorrhagic transformation, and whether decompressive craniectomy may be needed (more often considered under 60yrs, with a significant deficit and cerebral oedema)

  1. Conscious sedation: no time delay, more stable cardiovascular parameters, allows clinical neuro monitoring, but airway is unprotected and there's a risk of patient movement
  2. General anaesthesia: causes some time delay, can cause more labile cardiovascular parameters, but provides a protected airway and an immobile patient
