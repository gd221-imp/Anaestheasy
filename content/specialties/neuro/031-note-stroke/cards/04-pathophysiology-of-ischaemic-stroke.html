Cessation of blood flow causes ischaemic infarction and neuronal damage, releasing excitatory glutamate, driving sodium/calcium influx, activating proteolytic enzymes, and causing apoptosis; this is compounded by reduced oxygen delivery and a neuroinflammatory response causing cerebral oedema and inflammatory cell uptake

  • The infarct core is generally unsalvageable; the surrounding penumbra is potentially salvageable if perfusion is restored promptly
