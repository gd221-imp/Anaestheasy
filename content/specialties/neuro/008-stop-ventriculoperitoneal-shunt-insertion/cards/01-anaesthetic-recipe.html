Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Cause of hydrocephalus
  • previous shunts

Examination (O/E)

  • Signs of raised ICP: headache, vomiting, reduced GCS, bradycardia

Investigations (Ix)

  • CT or MRI
  • U&E, FBC, group and save

Optimise

  • Fasting may be impossible if vomiting: plan RSI

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring
  • Warming
  • head ring
  • Can consider a forced-air warmer positioned as for an open abdomen

Induction

  • Smooth induction avoiding rises in ICP

Maintenance

  • avoid hypercapnia
  • Head turned
  • tunnelling is stimulating, so deepen beforehand
  • Can consider morphine about 7 mg
  • Can consider volatile with fentanyl boluses, about 350 mcg in total

Emergence

  • Smooth emergence
  • Neuro observations in recovery

Post-op

Complications

  • Neuro observations

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding along the ventricular catheter
  • Visceral injury from the peritoneal catheter

Delayed

  • Shunt blockage
  • Shunt infection
  • Over-drainage and subdural haematoma

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Raised ICP at induction
  • Aspiration if vomiting

Delayed

  • PONV
  • Seizures

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia plus small opioid doses
  • Watch for shunt blockage or infection
  • Can consider Oramorph as needed

Destination

  • Neurosurgical ward
