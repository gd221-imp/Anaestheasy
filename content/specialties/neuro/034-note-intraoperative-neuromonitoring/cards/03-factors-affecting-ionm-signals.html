Increased signal: raised temperature, ketamine

Decreased signal: reduced temperature, reduced CO2, reduced O2, volatile agents, dexmedetomidine, neuromuscular blockade (affects MEPs), opioids, nitrous oxide

  • An anaesthetic cause of change tends to be global, while a surgical cause tends to be focal - TIVA is generally best to minimise anaesthetic interference

Other drugs with less predictable effects: thiopentone, benzodiazepines, alpha-2 agonists (mainly via MAP effects)

A bite block is a useful precaution during MEP stimulation to prevent tongue/lip injury

Note: the risk of spinal cord injury during scoliosis surgery has been reduced from around 5% to 0.5% with the use of IONM
