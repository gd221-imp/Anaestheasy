Myotonia (incomplete muscle relaxation after contraction), distal muscle weakness, muscle wasting

Cardiac conduction defects and cardiomyopathy; restrictive lung disease and OSA

Intellectual impairment; bulbar weakness leading to aspiration risk; peripheral insulin resistance, testicular atrophy, gallstones/constipation from delayed gastric emptying, and type 2 diabetes

Frontal balding, weakness/wasting of facial muscles leading to bilateral ptosis, and cataracts

Triggers for a myotonic episode: suxamethonium (generalised myotonic contracture that can make ventilation and intubation impossible - avoid), surgery/diathermy, neostigmine, pain, low temperature/shivering, stimulation - always check train-of-four carefully
