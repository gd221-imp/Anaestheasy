Avoid premedication, MDT planning

Intra-op: arterial line, external pacing available, regional technique preferred over general anaesthesia where possible, RSI if GA needed, increased sensitivity to sedative agents, monitor blood glucose, use muscle relaxants cautiously (anticipate possible bulbar palsy)

Post-op: consider HDU, only discharge from recovery once able to cough effectively and maintain saturations on room air, multimodal analgesia used carefully around benzodiazepines/opioids

Myotonic crisis treatment: remove the trigger, and consider a class I antiarrhythmic (a sodium channel blocker such as procainamide or phenytoin)
