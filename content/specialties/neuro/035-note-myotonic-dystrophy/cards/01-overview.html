A rare multisystem neuromuscular disorder

Prevalence around 1 in 8000 (older texts quote lower figures, e.g. 1:25,000); autosomal dominant with anticipation. DM1 = CTG repeat in the DMPK gene (chromosome 19q13.3); DM2 = CNBP gene (chromosome 3). Myotonia reflects reduced muscle chloride channel conductance
