A - increased lip/tongue/jaw size, thickened pharyngeal tissue, laryngeal stenosis, increased space between teeth -> difficult bag-mask ventilation and airway, risk of nasopharyngeal bleeding

B - obstructive sleep apnoea from soft tissue swelling -> risk of post-op respiratory failure, may not tolerate CPAP well post-op

C - hypertension, LVH, cardiomyopathy, valve regurgitation, large hands can make cannulation tricky -> consider pre-op echo +/- arterial line

D - raised ICP (from a compressed 3rd ventricle), spinal cord compression, peripheral neuropathy, bitemporal hemianopia -> careful positioning, TIVA

E - type 2 diabetes, hyperlipidaemia, osteoarthritis, renal dysfunction, obstructive sleep apnoea, headache -> check blood glucose, careful padding, dose drugs carefully, consider diabetes insipidus and need for post-op cortisol replacement

Diagnosis: IGF-1 level, or growth hormone level following a 75g oral glucose tolerance test
