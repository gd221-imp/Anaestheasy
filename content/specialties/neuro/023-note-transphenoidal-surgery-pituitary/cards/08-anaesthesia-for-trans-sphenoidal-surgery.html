Using an operating microscope, aim for a relatively hypotensive anaesthetic, arterial line, local anaesthetic topicalisation, avoid excessive stimulation, remifentanil TCI, supine with head tilt, balance adequate IV filling against the risk of venous air embolism

Airway: wire-reinforced ETT, protect the eyes, an NG tube is often placed early, watch for a bleeding throat pack; ensure good IV access; the anaesthetist may need to assist the surgical team with positioning for image guidance; favour shorter-acting, reversible agents

Avoid raised ICP post-op: aim for smooth emergence, adequate reversal of neuromuscular block, and antiemetics
