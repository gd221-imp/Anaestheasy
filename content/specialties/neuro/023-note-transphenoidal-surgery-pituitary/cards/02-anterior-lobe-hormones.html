ACTH (cortisol and aldosterone release from the adrenal gland), GH (anabolism, lipolysis, reduced glucose utilisation), FSH & LH (spermatogenesis, egg production, oestrogen secretion), TSH (thyroid hormone release), prolactin (lactation)

Posterior lobe hormones: ADH (reduces free water excretion), oxytocin (myometrial contraction, let-down reflex)

Classification of pituitary tumours:

  • Hormone-secreting (over 60%): prolactinoma (commonest), GH-secreting (acromegaly), ACTH-secreting (Cushing's disease)
  • Non-secreting: non-functioning pituitary adenoma (commonest non-secreting tumour); other sellar masses include craniopharyngioma and meningioma
