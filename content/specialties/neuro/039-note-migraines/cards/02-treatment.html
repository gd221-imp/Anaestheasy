Aspirin, paracetamol, NSAIDs, metoclopramide/domperidone (antiemetics), triptans

If more than around 4-5 headache days per month, consider preventive treatment: propranolol, valproate, or topiramate

Watch for medication overuse headache with use on more than around 8 days per month

Further options for refractory cases: greater occipital nerve block, botulinum toxin, or occipital nerve stimulation
