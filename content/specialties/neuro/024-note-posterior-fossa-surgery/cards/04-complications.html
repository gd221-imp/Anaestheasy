Surgical

Immediate

  • Direct injury to brainstem nuclei causing bulbar dysfunction (dysphagia, impaired airway reflexes)
  • Haematoma

Delayed

  • Hydrocephalus

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Venous air embolism

Delayed

  • Airway and tongue oedema (macroglossia) after prolonged surgery
  • Vomiting, as surgery is close to the vomiting centre
