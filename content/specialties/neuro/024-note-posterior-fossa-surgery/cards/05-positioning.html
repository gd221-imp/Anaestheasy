Park bench position: halfway between lateral and prone, with the head tilted around 30 degrees down and the lower arm supported below the table, with a pillow

Lateral position risks: radial, common peroneal, saphenous, and brachial plexus nerve injury; lower ear folding; eye injury; malalignment

Prone position: essentially supine with the head turned

Sitting position: absolute contraindications - a ventriculo-atrial shunt or right-to-left cardiac shunt; relative contraindications - PFO, uncontrolled hypertension, extremes of age, severe autonomic neuropathy

  • Complications of the sitting position: ETT displacement, venous air embolism, macroglossia/tongue oedema, hypotension, coma/brainstem dysfunction, sciatic/femoral nerve injury, and compartment syndrome risk
