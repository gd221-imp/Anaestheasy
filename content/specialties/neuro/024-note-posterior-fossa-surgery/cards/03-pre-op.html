Assess for raised ICP, cerebellar signs (DANISH - dysdiadochokinesia, ataxia, nystagmus, intention tremor, slurred/staccato speech, hypotonia), and bulbar weakness

Intra-op: full AAGBI monitoring, reinforced ETT, arterial line +/- CVC, remifentanil/TIVA, aim for smooth extubation - remifentanil helps blunt the laryngoscopy response and reduce gag/vomiting response; monitor facial nerve function; be aware of bradycardia risk at induction and aspiration risk

Post-op: HDU, good analgesia, regular antiemetics, watch for post-op hypertension
