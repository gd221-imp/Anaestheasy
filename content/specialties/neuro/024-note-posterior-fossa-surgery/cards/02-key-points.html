The posterior fossa is a rigid compartment where a small volume change causes a large pressure change, risking brainstem compression

Boundaries: occipital bone posteroinferiorly, temporal bone anterolaterally, sphenoid bone anteromedially, tentorium cerebelli forms the roof

Contains: venous sinuses, brainstem, cerebellum, aqueduct of Sylvius, vertebral and basilar arteries, cranial nerves VII-XII
