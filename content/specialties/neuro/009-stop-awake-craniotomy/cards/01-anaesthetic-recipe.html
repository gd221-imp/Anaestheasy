Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Seizure history and triggers
  • Anxiety, ability to lie still and cooperate
  • OSA, GORD, chronic cough

Examination (O/E)

  • Patient selection and rapport, airway assessment, anxiety

Investigations (Ix)

  • MRI and functional mapping
  • FBC, U&E, group and save
  • Antiepileptic levels if relevant

Optimise

  • Meet the patient beforehand; rehearse the testing
  • Continue antiepileptics and steroids
  • Comfortable positioning with a clear view of the face

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Scalp block, TIVA (propofol and remifentanil) or dexmedetomidine
  • LMA ready, cold saline for seizures

Induction

  • Asleep phase: TIVA with LMA
  • Or sedation (propofol, remifentanil or dexmedetomidine)
  • Scalp block of 7 nerves before pins

Maintenance

  • Light sedation or none during mapping
  • Cold saline ready for seizures on the cortex
  • Re-deepen and re-insert LMA for closure if asleep-awake-asleep

Emergence

  • Asleep-awake-asleep or awake throughout with sedation
  • Patient awake and comfortable for mapping

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Seizures during cortical mapping
  • Bleeding
  • Brain swelling

Delayed

  • New neurological deficit
  • Infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Airway obstruction or apnoea from over-sedation
  • Agitation or loss of cooperation
  • Conversion to GA

Delayed

  • PONV
  • Psychological distress

Analgesia

  • Neuro observations, simple analgesia

Destination

  • Neuro HDU or ward
