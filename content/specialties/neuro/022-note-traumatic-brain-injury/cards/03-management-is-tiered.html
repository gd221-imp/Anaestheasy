Tier 1: head-up tilt around 30 degrees, RASS -5 (deeply sedated), PaCO2 4.5-5kPa, PaO2 >10kPa, ICP monitoring, CPP target 60-70mmHg, tight glucose control, temperature control, avoid tight ETT ties (to avoid impeding venous drainage)

Tier 2: if ICP remains >22mmHg for more than 5 mins despite tier 1 measures, repeat CT to exclude a surgical cause, then consider optimising PaCO2 to 4-4.5kPa, 20% mannitol 0.5g/kg, or 5% hypertonic saline 3ml/kg targeting a sodium of around 150-155mmol/L

Tier 3: if tier 2 fails, options include repeat CT versus a large decompressive craniectomy versus a barbiturate coma with EEG monitoring, targeting around 50% burst suppression
