Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Indication: Parkinson's disease, essential tremor, dystonia
  • Disease severity off medication; swallowing and speech
  • Cognitive state and ability to cooperate when awake

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway assessment (frame may limit access)
  • Tremor, rigidity, autonomic dysfunction (postural hypotension)

Investigations (Ix)

  • MRI for targeting; CT with stereotactic frame
  • Bloods, ECG; clotting (bleeding risk)

Optimise

  • Plan Parkinson's medication: often withheld before surgery for testing, restart as soon as possible
  • Agree awake or asleep technique with the neurosurgeon and neurophysiologist
  • Avoid dopamine antagonists (metoclopramide, prochlorperazine, haloperidol)

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Stereotactic frame key and frame-compatible airway equipment
  • Videolaryngoscope and supraglottic airway
  • Arterial line

Induction

  • Awake: scalp block and minimal sedation (e.g. remifentanil or dexmedetomidine) to allow microelectrode recording and clinical testing
  • Asleep: GA with secured airway when awake testing not needed
  • Frame fixation: local anaesthetic; airway access plan with frame on

Maintenance

  • Avoid propofol and benzodiazepines during microelectrode recording (suppress signals)
  • Tight BP control (systolic below about 140 mmHg) to reduce haemorrhage risk
  • Patient comfort: positioning, urinary catheter or bladder plan, warmth

Emergence

  • GA for internal pulse generator implantation (chest) after lead placement
  • Smooth emergence avoiding coughing and hypertension

Post-op

Complications

  • Intracranial haemorrhage
  • Seizures
  • Venous air embolism (awake, head up)
  • Airway obstruction with sedation

Surgical

Immediate

  • Intracranial haemorrhage
  • Lead misplacement

Delayed

  • Infection of the leads or generator
  • Hardware failure
  • Stimulation side effects (speech, mood)

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Airway obstruction with frame on
  • Hypertension
  • Seizures
  • Venous air embolism

Delayed

  • Parkinsonian crisis if medication delayed
  • Post-op confusion

Analgesia

  • Scalp block; paracetamol; small opioid doses

Destination

  • Neurosurgical ward or HDU; restart Parkinson's medication promptly
  • Post-op CT to confirm lead position
