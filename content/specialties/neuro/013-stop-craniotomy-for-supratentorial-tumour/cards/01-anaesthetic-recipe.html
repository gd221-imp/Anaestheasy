Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Symptoms of raised ICP, focal deficit, seizures
  • steroids and antiepileptics
  • Tumour size, site and vascularity

Examination (O/E)

—

Investigations (Ix)

—

Optimise

  • Plan for neuromonitoring or awake surgery if eloquent area

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, large-bore access
  • Head pins: local or remifentanil before pinning
  • Mannitol or hypertonic saline available

Induction

  • Smooth induction avoiding rises in ICP

Maintenance

  • TIVA or volatile below 1 MAC
  • Normocapnia, normothermia, normoglycaemia
  • head-up, neck not obstructed
  • Brain relaxation if tight: osmotherapy, mild hyperventilation briefly, deepen

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Smooth emergence without coughing or hypertension
  • Neuro observations
  • early assessment
  • PONV prophylaxis

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding and haematoma
  • Brain swelling
  • Venous air embolism

Delayed

  • New neurological deficit
  • Seizures
  • Infection
  • CSF leak

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Raised ICP at induction or pinning
  • Hypertension at emergence

Delayed

  • Delayed emergence
  • PONV
  • Sodium disturbance

Analgesia

  • analgesia (scalp block, paracetamol, codeine or small morphine doses)
