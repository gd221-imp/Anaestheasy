Pre-op: assess for a restrictive lung deficit (spirometry), right ventricular function (echo), and comorbidities (e.g. cardiomyopathy in Duchenne muscular dystrophy)

Intra-op: TIVA, SSEPs and MEPs monitoring, arterial line, urinary catheter, prone position, reinforced ETT, anticipate significant blood loss - have tranexamic acid/cell salvage/blood products available

Post-op: extubate for a neurological exam where possible, HDU, analgesia that still allows early mobilisation - options include PCA, regular NSAIDs/paracetamol, opioid PCA, or a surgically placed epidural
