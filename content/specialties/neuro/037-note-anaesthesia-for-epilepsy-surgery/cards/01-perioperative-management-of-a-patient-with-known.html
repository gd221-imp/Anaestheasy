Pre-op: review medication, seizure type and frequency, and driving status

Intra-op: induction with thiopentone/propofol/ketamine, maintenance with a volatile agent generally acceptable; some avoid etomidate/alfentanil-heavy techniques depending on the case; at emergence, be mindful of drugs that can lower seizure threshold (e.g. some opioids, xanthines, or dopamine antagonists) and modify the pre-op plan accordingly
