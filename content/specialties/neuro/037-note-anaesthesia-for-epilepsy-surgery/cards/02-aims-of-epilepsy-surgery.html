To resect the epileptogenic focus, disconnection surgery, or neuromodulation

Workup: clinical assessment plus EEG and MRI/CT; the commonest procedure is an anterior temporal lobectomy

Additional investigations may include: high-resolution MRI, FDG-PET, SPECT, neuropsychological assessment, EEG plus fMRI, and the Wada test (to lateralise language/memory function)
