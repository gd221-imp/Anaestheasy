Compensatory stage (under 60 mins): raised CMRO2 causing raised cerebral blood flow and ICP; limb movement raises lactate; raised catecholamine release causes raised HR/cardiac output, hypertension, and raised CVP

Physiological decompensation (over 60 mins): rhabdomyolysis, acidosis, hyperkalaemia; neurons begin to die, triggering inflammation and cerebral oedema, further raising ICP; cerebral autoregulation starts to fail, leading to cerebral hypoperfusion, hypoxia, and hypoglycaemia
