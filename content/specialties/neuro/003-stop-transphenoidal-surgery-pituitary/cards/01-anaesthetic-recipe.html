Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Visual symptoms
  • Endocrine symptoms (acromegaly, Cushing's)
  • OSA

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway (acromegaly: difficult)
  • Visual fields

Investigations (Ix)

  • Pituitary hormones, cortisol
  • U&E and glucose
  • Visual field testing

Optimise

  • Endocrine work-up: acromegaly (difficult airway), Cushing's, steroid cover

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT (south-facing or armoured), throat pack, arterial line

Induction

  • Plan for a difficult airway in acromegaly
  • TIVA, remifentanil
  • Throat pack

Maintenance

  • GA, often TIVA
  • steroid cover as needed
  • nasal vasoconstrictor
  • Watch for diabetes insipidus

Emergence

  • Remove the throat pack
  • Smooth, awake extubation; avoid bag-mask ventilation (CSF leak)

Post-op

Complications

  • Nasal packs, so mouth breathing: extubate awake
  • fluid balance and sodium checks

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding from the cavernous sinus or carotid artery
  • CSF leak

Delayed

  • Diabetes insipidus
  • SIADH
  • Hypopituitarism
  • Visual disturbance
  • Meningitis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Difficult airway in acromegaly
  • Hypertension from cocaine or adrenaline nasal preparations

Delayed

  • Airway obstruction with nasal packs
  • Hyponatraemia

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; opioid PRN

Destination

  • Neurosurgical ward or HDU; fluid balance and sodium
