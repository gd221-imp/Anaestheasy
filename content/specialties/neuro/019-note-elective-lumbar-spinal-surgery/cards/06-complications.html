Surgical

Immediate

  • Dural tear and CSF leak
  • Epidural haematoma: new or worsening leg weakness or cauda equina symptoms need urgent review
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Wound infection or discitis
  • Persistent CSF leak (positional headache, pseudomeningocele)
  • Recurrent disc prolapse or persistent pain

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Tube displacement or kinking while prone
  • Haemodynamic instability on turning
  • Eye injury, including postoperative visual loss
  • Peripheral nerve and pressure injuries from positioning

Delayed

  • Post-op nausea and vomiting
  • Pressure sores
  • VTE
  • Delirium in older patients
  • Urinary retention
