Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Symptoms of raised ICP, seizures
  • Antiplatelets and anticoagulants
  • Ability to cooperate if done awake

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway (access limited once the frame or clamp is on)
  • GCS and focal neurology

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, clotting, group and save
  • Imaging for planning

Optimise

  • Lesion site, frame or frameless, anticoagulants

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, ETT or LMA

Induction

  • Smooth IV induction, remifentanil
  • Local or remifentanil before pins or frame
  • Secure the airway well: access is limited
  • Can consider where to induce for ergonomics: on the trolley then turn, on the table then turn, or head away from the machine

Maintenance

  • GA, often TIVA
  • Can consider giving dexamethasone only after the samples are taken and confirmed
  • Can consider a pause while the sample is checked

Emergence

  • smooth emergence
  • Can consider allowing time for hair washing at the end

Post-op

Complications

  • Neuro observations
  • watch for bleeding

Surgical

Immediate

  • Intracranial haemorrhage
  • Seizures

Delayed

  • New neurological deficit
  • Infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypertension at pin or frame application
  • Raised ICP

Delayed

  • Delayed emergence
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, codeine or small opioid doses
  • Scalp infiltration
  • Can consider Oramorph as needed

Destination

  • Neurosurgical ward with neuro observations
