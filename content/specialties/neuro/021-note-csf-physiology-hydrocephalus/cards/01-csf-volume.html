Around 150ml (10% of intracranial volume)

Around 500ml/day produced by ependymal cells of the choroid plexus in the lateral ventricles

Absorbed via arachnoid granulations into the cranial venous sinuses

Circulation pathway: foramen of Monro -> 3rd ventricle -> cerebral aqueduct -> 4th ventricle -> foramina of Luschka/Magendie -> subarachnoid space -> around the spinal cord
