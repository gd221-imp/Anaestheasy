Extraventricular drain (EVD): used for acute hydrocephalus, infected shunt removal, or raised ICP with normal-sized ventricles

  • One end sits in the frontal horn of a lateral ventricle via a burr hole, the other end is tunnelled under the scalp and connected to a collection system
  • Zeroed at the level of the foramen of Monro (approximately the external auditory meatus if supine)
  • Drainage is level-dependent, based on ventricle size and desired opening pressure
  • Complications - insertion: seizures, haemorrhage, sub-optimal placement; post-op: displacement, infection, failure to control ICP, excessive drainage (>20ml/hr), blockage, or benign intracranial hypertension
