Pre-op: check for focal neurological deficit, indication, and signs of raised ICP; take care with positioning and be cautious with upper limb regional techniques for tunnelled lines (avoid if a ventriculo-atrial shunt is present)

Intra-op: ETT, antibiotics, keep the neck clear of the chest for the tunnelling part of the procedure, remifentanil TCI helps blunt the stimulating tunnelling step (consider IPPV if a VP shunt)

Post-op: watch the wound/drain site, complications include shunt blockage (usually proximal), shunt fracture, and shunt infection
