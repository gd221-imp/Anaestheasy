A condition of excess CSF in the brain's ventricles, causing raised ICP

Classification:

  • Communicating: free flow of CSF from the lateral ventricles to the basal cisterns is disrupted downstream, e.g. arachnoid villi blocked after SAH
  • Non-communicating ('obstructive'): CSF flow is blocked at some point within the ventricular system, e.g. congenital aqueduct stenosis
  • Normal pressure hydrocephalus: reduced cognition, gait disturbance, and urinary incontinence (Hakim's triad)
