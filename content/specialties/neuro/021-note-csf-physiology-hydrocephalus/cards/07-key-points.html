Benign intracranial hypertension (idiopathic intracranial hypertension): raised ICP with no other identifiable cause - headache worse in the morning or lying flat, may have visual disturbance and nausea, more common in females, obesity, and pregnancy; papilloedema; LP opening pressure >25cmH2O is both diagnostic and can be therapeutic

Treatment: acetazolamide, weight loss, and surgery (optic nerve sheath decompression or shunt surgery) for refractory cases
