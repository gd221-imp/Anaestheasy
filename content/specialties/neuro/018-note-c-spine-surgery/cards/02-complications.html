Surgical

Immediate

  • Injury to the spinal cord, nerve root, vertebral artery, oesophagus, trachea, carotid artery, recurrent laryngeal nerve, CN XII or sympathetic chain

Delayed

  • Neck haematoma causing airway compromise
  • Dysphagia

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • A: accidental extubation, airway or tongue oedema
  • B: reduced lung expansion if the abdomen is not accommodated
  • C: reduced venous return, loss of IV access
  • D: abnormal neck position reducing CPP and venous drainage

Delayed

  • Peripheral neuropathy
  • Central retinal artery occlusion or ischaemic optic neuropathy
  • Acid reflux
  • Pressure injury
