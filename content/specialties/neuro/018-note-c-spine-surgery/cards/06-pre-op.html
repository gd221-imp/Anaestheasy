Assess for pre-existing neuropathy

INTRA-OP: consider an arterial line if pre-existing neuropathy, videolaryngoscope and bougie for laryngoscopy to minimise neck movement, reinforced ETT or tube taped to one side, TIVA, head up around 20 degrees, sandbag between the shoulder blades to extend the neck, eyes taped and padded. Airway access is more difficult intra-op as the cervical vertebrae are now fixed.

POST-OP: watch for post-op haematoma, vocal cord palsy (surgical retraction of the recurrent laryngeal nerve against the ETT cuff), need for re-intubation, and carotid/vertebral artery/oesophageal/tracheal/thoracic duct injury. Airway obstruction is a risk after any neck surgery.

'Wrong level spinal surgery': two clinicians must independently identify the needle or K-wire position on X-ray before proceeding
