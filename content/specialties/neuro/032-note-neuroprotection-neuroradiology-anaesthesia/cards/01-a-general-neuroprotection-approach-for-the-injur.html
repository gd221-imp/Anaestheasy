A: consider ventilatory support if GCS <8 or motor response <4, poor gas exchange, loss of laryngeal reflexes, or an irregular respiratory pattern

B: target PaO2 >13kPa, PaCO2 4.5-5kPa, PEEP <12cmH2O, peak inspiratory pressure <30, lung-protective ventilation +/- paralysis; use RSI with manual in-line stabilisation if C-spine injury is a concern, with adequate pre-oxygenation

C: target MAP >80mmHg, using IV crystalloid or blood and vasopressors as needed; don't delay surgery or transfer just to place an arterial line; a rapid-acting opioid (e.g. alfentanil) helps obtund the laryngoscopy response; aim for a normal EtCO2 around 4-4.5kPa

D: give adequate sedation and analgesia to reduce CMRO2, treat seizures promptly, keep blood glucose around 6-10mmol/L, maintain normothermia, avoid tachycardia; 20% mannitol 0.5g/kg or 3% hypertonic saline 2ml/kg can be used for raised ICP

E: head-up 30-45 degrees, avoid tight ETT ties
