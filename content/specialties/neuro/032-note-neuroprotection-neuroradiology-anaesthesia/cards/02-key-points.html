Neurointerventional radiology principles (aneurysm coiling, stroke thrombectomy, AVM embolisation, cranial/vertebral stenting):

  • Target SBP 100-160mmHg at induction
  • Reinforced ETT or a south-facing RAE tube, arterial line, large-bore IV access, urinary catheter
  • Neuromuscular blockade bolus for immobility and to prevent movement/apnoea during runs
  • Heparin around 70-100 units/kg to target an ACT of 2-3x baseline
  • Radiation safety: 0.5mm lead apron and thyroid shield as standard
