  • Cranial diabetes insipidus: usually 12-48h post-op; suspect with polyuria (e.g. >250ml/hr or >3ml/kg/hr for 2 or more hours) of hypo-osmolar urine (specific gravity <1.005) with a rising serum sodium/osmolality; treat with desmopressin (DDAVP) IV/SC - BNF 1-4mcg daily, many neurosurgical protocols start lower (e.g. 0.4-1mcg) and titrate to urine output and sodium - and replace fluid/increase oral intake
