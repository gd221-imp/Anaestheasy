• Specific: IVIG, or plasmapheresis (typically around 4-5 exchanges of 3L plasma over 4-5 days) - watch for IVIG side effects such as nausea, fever, headache, aseptic meningism, encephalopathy, or anaphylaxis
  • General: VTE prophylaxis, analgesia/sedation, physiotherapy, early tracheostomy if prolonged ventilation likely, treat autonomic instability, early nutrition
