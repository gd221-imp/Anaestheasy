• Bloods: raised ESR/CRP, tests to rule out other causes (LFTs/serology), stool culture, ABG, CT head (to exclude other causes)
  • LP: raised protein
  • MRI spine: selective anterior nerve root enhancement with gadolinium
  • Nerve conduction studies: a demyelinating pattern (reduced conduction velocity) or an axonal pattern (reduced action potential size)
  • Antiganglioside antibodies (positive in around 50%): anti-GM1/anti-GD3 with classic GBS; anti-GQ1b with Miller Fisher syndrome (ataxia, areflexia, ophthalmoplegia)
