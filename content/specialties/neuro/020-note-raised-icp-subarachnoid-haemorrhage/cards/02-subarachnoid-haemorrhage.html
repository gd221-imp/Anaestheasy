Bleeding between the pia and arachnoid mater

Mortality around 50%; incidence around 10/100,000

Causes: aneurysmal SAH (85%), cerebral AVM (10%), traumatic SAH (5%)

Risk factors: ADPCKD, Ehlers-Danlos type IV, family history, Marfan's, hereditary haemorrhagic telangiectasia, hypertension, AVM, smoking, cocaine use, an existing large aneurysm

Diagnosis: CT head, followed by LP after 12hrs (looking for xanthochromia, a breakdown product of red blood cells) if CT is negative but suspicion remains
