• Early (within around 3 days): rebleeding, acute hydrocephalus (around 20%), acute cerebral oedema, cerebral salt wasting syndrome
  • Late (vasospasm starts around day 3, peaks days 5-7 and wanes over 2-4 weeks; angiographic in ~70%, symptomatic in 30-40%): delayed cerebral ischaemia = clinical or radiological signs of cerebral ischaemia after SAH, often related to cerebral vasospasm
