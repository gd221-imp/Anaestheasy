Fisher grade (based on CT findings - amount/pattern of blood) alongside WFNS grade or Hunt & Hess grade (based on clinical status - roughly: grade I is GCS 15 with no deficit, through to grade IV/V being GCS 12 down to under 7 with or without motor deficit)

Aneurysm location: most at a bifurcation - ACOM (40%), MCA (34%), PCOM (20%)

Risk factors for rupture: hypertension, smoking, size >7mm, seizures
