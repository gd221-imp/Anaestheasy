An angiographic phenomenon of narrowing of large/medium intracranial arteries after SAH (usually affecting the anterior circulation)

  • Contributes to new speech deficit, new motor deficit, or reduced GCS
  • Risk factors: smoking, diabetes, hypertension
  • Treatment: nimodipine, maintaining euvolaemia, maintaining electrolytes, and interventional options (angioplasty/intra-arterial vasodilators) if needed
