A microcatheter fed into the cerebral circulation via a femoral artery catheter deploys platinum coils into the aneurysm sac

+ Less invasive, quicker, better for posterior circulation aneurysms

  • Not always possible for wide-necked, irregular, or small aneurysms; complications include access site bleeding/infection, pseudoaneurysm, coil failure or aneurysm rupture

Neurosurgical clipping

+ Treats all aneurysm types, lower recurrence rate

  • More invasive craniotomy, longer procedure
