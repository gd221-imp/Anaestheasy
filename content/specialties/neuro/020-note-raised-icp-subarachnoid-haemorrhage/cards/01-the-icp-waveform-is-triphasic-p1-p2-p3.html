P1 relates to arterial pulsation (percussion wave), P2 to brain compliance (tidal wave), P3 to venous/aortic valve closure (dicrotic wave)

Pathological waves: A waves (plateau waves) last 5-10 mins and indicate critically reduced compliance; B waves occur around every 0.5-2 mins; C waves occur around 4-8 per minute (Traube-Hering waves)

Normal ICP: adult 10-15mmHg, child 5-15mmHg, infant 3-4mmHg
