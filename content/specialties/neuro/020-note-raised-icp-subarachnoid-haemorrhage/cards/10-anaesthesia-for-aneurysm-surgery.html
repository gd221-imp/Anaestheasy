Pre-op: usual assessment plus treating hypertension when supine if needed, IV access, group and save/coagulation/FBC; prepare for potential transfer and remote-site anaesthesia, and radiation safety if endovascular

Intra-op: arterial line monitoring plus temperature monitoring, urinary catheter, extension sets on lines for remote access, anticipate difficult airway access; maintain CPP 60-70mmHg, keep the patient immobile, aim for smooth extubation, use simple analgesia

Temporary clipping: keep to a maximum of around 7 mins due to ischaemia/dissection risk; used to control bleeding or allow clip readjustment or manage sudden decompression

Adenosine can be used as a bolus (around 0.3-0.6mg/kg) to briefly arrest the circulation for clip placement - contraindicated in asthma, coronary artery disease, or conduction abnormalities

If rupture occurs intra-operatively: propofol bolus (aiming to drop MAP by around 10-20%), communicate with the surgical team, and maintain CPP while reducing CMRO2
