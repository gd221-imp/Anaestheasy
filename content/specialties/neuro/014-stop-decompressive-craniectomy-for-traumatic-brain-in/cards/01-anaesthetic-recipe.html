Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • associated injuries
  • C-spine
  • Already intubated and ventilated? ICP monitoring
  • Coagulopathy

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway and C-spine
  • GCS, pupils
  • Other injuries (trauma survey)

Investigations (Ix)

  • GCS, pupils, CT
  • blood available

Optimise

  • Don't delay surgery
  • Correct coagulopathy
  • Osmotherapy if herniation signs

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, CVC if time
  • Hypertonic saline or mannitol
  • Blood products

Induction

  • If not intubated: RSI with in-line stabilisation, cardiovascularly stable (ketamine acceptable)
  • Vasopressor ready

Maintenance

  • Maintain CPP 60–70 mmHg
  • MAP target with vasopressors
  • PaCO2 4.5–5.0 kPa, SpO2 above 94%, normoglycaemia
  • Avoid hyperthermia
  • treat raised ICP

Emergence

  • Stay sedated and ventilated to neuro ICU

Post-op

Complications

  • Sodium and osmolality monitoring
  • Seizure prophylaxis per local policy

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Contusion expansion
  • Brain herniation through the defect

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Hydrocephalus
  • Subdural hygroma
  • Syndrome of the trephined

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension or hypoxia (secondary brain injury)
  • Coagulopathy

Delayed

  • Ventilator-associated pneumonia
  • Sodium disorders

Analgesia

  • Sedation and opioid infusion
  • Paracetamol

Destination

  • Neuro ICU with ICP monitoring
