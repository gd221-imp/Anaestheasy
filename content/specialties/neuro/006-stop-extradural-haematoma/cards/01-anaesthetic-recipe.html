Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Time-critical

Examination (O/E)

  • GCS, pupils, C-spine

Investigations (Ix)

—

Optimise

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line (do not delay surgery), ETT, mannitol or hypertonic saline

Induction

  • RSI with in-line stabilisation

Maintenance

  • avoid hypotension and hypoxia
  • Normocapnia (PaCO₂ 4.5–5 kPa), head up, avoid coughing

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Re-bleeding
  • Brain swelling

Delayed

  • Recurrence of haematoma
  • Infection
  • Seizures

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension or hypoxia worsening secondary brain injury
  • Raised ICP at intubation

Delayed

  • Need for ICU and ICP monitoring
  • Ventilator-associated pneumonia

Analgesia

—

Destination

  • ICU or neuro HDU depending on GCS
