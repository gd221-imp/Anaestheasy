Examination (O/E)

Investigations (Ix)

  • Respiratory function (FVC), cardiac involvement, neuromuscular cause, blood conservation

Optimise

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, central line, cell salvage, tranexamic acid
  • Neuromonitoring (SSEP and MEP)

Induction

  • TIVA, avoid relaxant after intubation for MEPs
  • Careful prone positioning: eyes, pressure points, abdomen free

Maintenance

  • Maintain MAP for cord perfusion

Emergence

  • wake-up test if signals lost

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Major blood loss
  • Spinal cord injury (loss of neuromonitoring signals)
  • Dural tear

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Implant failure
  • Pseudarthrosis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Accidental extubation in the prone position
  • Venous air embolism
  • Eye injury and post-op visual loss
  • Pressure injuries

Delayed

  • Respiratory complications
  • Post-op pain and opioid side effects

Analgesia

—

Destination

  • HDU or ICU, PCA or intrathecal diamorphine
