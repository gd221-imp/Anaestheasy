Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • WFNS grade
  • vasospasm risk

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • GCS and focal deficit

Investigations (Ix)

  • Sodium, cardiac changes (neurogenic stunning)

Optimise

  • nimodipine
  • Plan for temporary clipping and rupture

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line before induction, CVC
  • Blood available
  • adenosine if requested
  • Neuromonitoring

Induction

  • Smooth induction with an opioid (e.g. remifentanil) to blunt laryngoscopy
  • Arterial line before induction
  • Avoid hypertension (rupture) and hypotension (ischaemia)

Maintenance

  • Avoid hypertension at laryngoscopy and pinning
  • avoid hypotension
  • Brain relaxation
  • BP augmentation during temporary clipping
  • Be ready for intraoperative rupture

Emergence

  • Smooth emergence; extubate if good grade
  • Ventilate to ICU if poor grade or complicated

Post-op

Complications

  • vasospasm surveillance
  • Avoid hyponatraemia and hypovolaemia
  • Smooth emergence

Surgical

Immediate

  • Aneurysm rupture
  • Ischaemia from temporary clips

Delayed

  • Vasospasm and delayed cerebral ischaemia
  • Hydrocephalus
  • Seizures

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypertensive surge at laryngoscopy
  • Hypotension during rupture

Delayed

  • Hyponatraemia
  • Neurogenic pulmonary oedema

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, codeine or small opioid doses
  • Scalp block

Destination

  • Neuro critical care
