Insufficient transmission at the NMJ, causing severe respiratory and bulbar weakness (unable to support the head, weak gag/cough)

Cholinergic crisis = excess ACh at the NMJ from overtreatment/inadequate AChE activity, causing a SLUDGE picture (salivation, lacrimation, urination, defecation, GI upset, emesis)

Treatment of cholinergic crisis: atropine (acts at muscarinic receptors, not nicotinic receptors at the NMJ, so doesn't reverse the muscle weakness itself); other causes of a similar picture include anticholinesterase overdose, organophosphate or nerve agent exposure, and some poisonous mushrooms
