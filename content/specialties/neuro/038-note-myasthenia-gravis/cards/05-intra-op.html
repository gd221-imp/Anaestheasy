Avoid sedative premedication; continue peri-op steroid cover; continue anticholinesterase treatment; use neuromuscular monitoring; consider regional/local techniques where possible; if GA needed - propofol induction, intubating conditions can often be achieved by deepening with a volatile agent, if suxamethonium is used a higher dose may be needed and effect can be prolonged, non-depolarising agents are used at reduced dose (patients are markedly more sensitive) and titrated against train-of-four; spontaneous ventilation with pressure support is preferred where possible to avoid muscle relaxants; if positive pressure ventilation is needed, avoid long-acting muscle relaxants
