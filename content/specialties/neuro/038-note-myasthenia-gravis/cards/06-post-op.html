Aim to extubate, consider HDU

Risk factors for needing post-op ventilation: disease duration over 6 years, chronic respiratory disease (e.g. COPD), pyridostigmine over 750mg/day, vital capacity under 2-2.9L, bulbar or respiratory symptoms, a previous myasthenic crisis, intra-op blood loss over 1L, high anti-AChR antibody titres (over 100nmol/L), and a history of vocal cord or lung surgery
