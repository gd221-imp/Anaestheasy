• Paediatric sevoflurane: delta + alpha + theta activity
  • Propofol: slow-delta (0.1-4Hz) + alpha (8-12Hz) activity, with coherent frontal alpha
  • Ketamine: high beta/low gamma activity (with theta), with gamma bursts
  • Dexmedetomidine: slow/delta activity with spindles (9-15Hz; a sleep-like pattern)
  • Ageing is generally associated with reduced alpha power
