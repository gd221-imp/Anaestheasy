Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Seizure type, frequency and triggers
  • Antiepileptic drugs
  • Neuropsychology and Wada results

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Baseline neurology

Investigations (Ix)

  • MRI, EEG
  • FBC, U&E, group and save

Optimise

  • Antiepileptic drugs continued, seizure plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, ETT, electrocorticography may need low volatile

Induction

  • Propofol or thiopentone
  • Avoid benzodiazepines (electrocorticography)
  • Expect resistance to non-depolarising relaxants

Maintenance

  • GA
  • avoid drugs that suppress seizure activity during mapping
  • Treat seizures promptly

Emergence

  • Smooth emergence; delayed waking is common

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Seizures during or after resection

Delayed

  • New neurological deficit (memory, visual fields)
  • Infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Drug interactions with antiepileptic drugs
  • Interference with electrocorticography

Delayed

  • Post-op seizures
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, codeine or small opioid doses
  • Scalp block

Destination

  • Neuro HDU, continue antiepileptics
