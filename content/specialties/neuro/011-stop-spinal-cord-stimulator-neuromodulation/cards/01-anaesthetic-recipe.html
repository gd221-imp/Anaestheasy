Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Pain history and current opioid use
  • Psychology assessment completed
  • Trial results

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway (prone)
  • Back and skin at implant sites

Investigations (Ix)

  • Clotting if on anticoagulants
  • Usually none otherwise

Optimise

  • Anticoagulants, infection risk

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, prone positioning kit

Induction

  • Local with sedation (patient reports paraesthesia during trial) or GA

Maintenance

  • Light sedation so the patient can report paraesthesia
  • Antibiotics before incision

Emergence

  • Turn supine carefully
  • Check neurology

Post-op

Complications

  • device checks

Surgical

Immediate

  • Dural puncture
  • Epidural haematoma
  • Nerve injury

Delayed

  • Lead migration
  • Infection
  • Device failure or battery problems

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Over-sedation so the patient cannot report paraesthesia
  • Prone positioning issues

Delayed

  • Post-dural puncture headache

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia

Destination

  • Day case or overnight ward
