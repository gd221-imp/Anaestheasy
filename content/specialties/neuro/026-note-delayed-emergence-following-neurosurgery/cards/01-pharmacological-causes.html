Benzodiazepines, opioids - can cause reduced respiratory drive, unstable circulation, or direct sedation; serotonin syndrome; central anticholinergic syndrome; residual neuromuscular block - consider plasma cholinesterase deficiency (acquired, e.g. liver failure or pregnancy, or genetic); magnesium, aminoglycosides, or lithium can unmask or worsen myasthenia gravis or Eaton-Lambert syndrome

Metabolic causes: hypoglycaemia, hyperglycaemia (osmotic effects/cerebral oedema), hyponatraemia, severe hypothyroid (myxoedema) coma

Neurological causes: raised ICP/thrombosis/infarction, pneumocephalus, seizures or a post-ictal state, non-convulsive status epilepticus

Other: a functional (psychogenic) cause
