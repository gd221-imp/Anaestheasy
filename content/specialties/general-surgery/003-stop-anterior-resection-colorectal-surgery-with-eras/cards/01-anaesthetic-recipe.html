Pre-op

History (Hx)

—

Examination (O/E)

—

Investigations (Ix)

  • CPET if high risk

Optimise

  • ERAS pathway: carbohydrate loading, no prolonged fasting, prehabilitation
  • Anaemia correction
  • Stoma and bowel preparation plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring
  • arterial line if comorbid
  • Cardiac output monitor for goal-directed fluids
  • Warming

Induction

  • steep head-down for laparoscopy
  • Analgesia: spinal with intrathecal diamorphine, or TAP or rectus sheath blocks

Maintenance

  • GA with protective ventilation
  • Goal-directed or restrictive fluids

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Early oral intake and mobilisation
  • Avoid NGT and drains where possible
  • VTE prophylaxis

Surgical

Immediate

  • Anastomotic leak
  • Bleeding
  • Ureteric injury

Delayed

  • Ileus
  • Wound infection
  • Stoma problems

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Raised airway pressure in steep head-down
  • Hypotension with spinal and pneumoperitoneum

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury
  • VTE

Analgesia

  • Multimodal opioid-sparing analgesia
