Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • BMI and weight history
  • OSA (STOP-BANG), CPAP use
  • Diabetes, hypertension, reflux
  • Exercise tolerance

Examination (O/E)

  • OSA screening, CPAP, weight-based doses

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E, HbA1c
  • ECG; echo if signs of heart failure or pulmonary hypertension
  • Sleep study if not done

Optimise

  • Bring CPAP on the day
  • Pre-op weight-loss diet (liver shrinkage)
  • VTE prophylaxis plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Ramped position, videolaryngoscope, arterial line if needed

Induction

  • Pre-oxygenate with head up
  • RSI if reflux
  • Lean body weight for induction, total for suxamethonium

Maintenance

  • PEEP

Emergence

  • Extubate fully awake, sitting up, fully reversed
  • CPAP in recovery if used at home

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Visceral injury on port insertion

Delayed

  • Anastomotic leak
  • Stricture
  • Internal hernia
  • Nutritional deficiencies

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Difficult mask ventilation and intubation
  • Rapid desaturation
  • Drug dosing errors with weight

Delayed

  • Post-op respiratory failure and OSA
  • VTE
  • Rhabdomyolysis

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID if appropriate
  • Port-site local anaesthetic
  • Minimise opioids (OSA)

Destination

  • CPAP if used at home, opioid-sparing analgesia, early mobilisation
