Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • <C>ABCDE
  • trauma team and consultant present
  • activate major haemorrhage protocol
  • Permissive hypotension until haemorrhage control (not in head injury)

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway and C-spine
  • Primary survey: chest, abdomen, pelvis, long bones
  • GCS

Investigations (Ix)

  • VBG with lactate and Hb
  • Viscoelastic testing
  • Crossmatch; don't delay surgery

Optimise

  • Tranexamic acid within 3 hours

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Large-bore access or rapid infuser, arterial line
  • Blood products 1:1 plasma to red cells
  • cell salvage
  • Warming of patient and fluids

Induction

  • Prep and drape before induction
  • reduced-dose induction (ketamine) and rocuronium

Maintenance

  • Damage control resuscitation: blood products guided by viscoelastic testing, calcium
  • Abbreviated surgery: stop bleeding and contamination, pack, temporary closure

Emergence

  • Stay ventilated to ICU

Post-op

Complications

  • Planned relook in 24 to 48 hours
  • Tertiary survey for missed injuries

Surgical

Immediate

  • Ongoing haemorrhage
  • Missed injuries

Delayed

  • Abdominal compartment syndrome
  • Intra-abdominal sepsis
  • Fistula

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Cardiovascular collapse at induction
  • Coagulopathy, hypothermia and acidosis
  • Hyperkalaemia and hypocalcaemia from massive transfusion

Delayed

  • TRALI and TACO
  • ARDS
  • Acute kidney injury and rhabdomyolysis

Analgesia

  • Opioid infusion with sedation
  • Paracetamol

Destination

  • ICU for correction of the lethal triad (hypothermia, acidosis, coagulopathy)
