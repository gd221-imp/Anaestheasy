Variceal bleed → endoscopic therapy (adrenaline/banding/cautery) / terlipressin / antibiotics (follow local guidelines).

→ Balloon tamponade (Sengstaken/Blakemore/Linton) - risk of aspiration, severe portal hypertension.

→ TIPSS → interventional radiology procedure for porto-systemic shunt in uncontrolled bleed with no PV thrombosis. Catheter through IJV → balloon dilated & stent. Can precipitate encephalopathy/liver failure/contrast nephropathy.

Non-variceal bleed → ulcers, tumours, vascular abnormalities.

→ PPI, H.pylori eradication → inject adrenaline/thrombin/fibrin or clip or cauterise.
