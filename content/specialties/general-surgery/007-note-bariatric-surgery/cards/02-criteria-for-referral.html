BMI >40, or BMI 35-40 + other significant disease that could be improved with weight loss.

→ weight loss not achieved despite all appropriate non-surgical techniques.

→ patient commits to long-term follow-up in a Tier 3 obesity service.

Common comorbidities: OSA, DM, HTN, GORD, asthma.

The procedures → restrictive: small gastric pouch → sleeve gastrectomy / gastric banding.

Malabsorptive: reduced length of gut & size of stomach → Roux-en-Y.
