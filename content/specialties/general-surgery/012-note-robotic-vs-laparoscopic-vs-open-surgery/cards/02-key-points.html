PRE-OP → history of glaucoma/intracranial pathology? Severe CVS/resp disease? On ERAS pathway?

INTRA-OP → careful access to airway, LPV, avoid high PaCO2, aware of vagally mediated bradycardia on pneumoperitoneum (peritoneal stretch); CO2 gas embolism (not air) is a risk at insufflation, NMBD or remain fairly still, NGT, padded shoulder rests to prevent sliding, multimodal analgesia, lead time prior to extubation.

POST-OP → early oral intake, early mobilisation, VTE prophylaxis.
