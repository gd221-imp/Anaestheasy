Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Aspiration risk: assume full stomach

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Drooling, inability to swallow saliva
  • Surgical emphysema (perforation)

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none for food bolus
  • Chest X-ray if perforation suspected

Optimise

  • Suction the oesophagus/pharynx before induction
  • Head up

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT, suction

Induction

  • RSI with cricoid

Maintenance

  • head up

Emergence

  • Extubate awake, sitting up

Post-op

Complications

  • Watch for oesophageal perforation

Surgical

Immediate

  • Oesophageal perforation
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Mediastinitis
  • Oesophageal stricture
  • Reflux after myotomy

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration of oesophageal contents at induction
  • Airway compromise from a dilated oesophagus

Delayed

  • Aspiration pneumonia

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; opioid PRN

Destination

  • Ward or day case; nil by mouth until reviewed if perforation possible
