Nissen's: cell salvage, TEG.

Splenectomy: wide-bore IV access, invasive monitoring, arterial line.

Liver transplant: a-line, 2x large-bore access, CO monitoring, catheter, full monitoring, care over/skin/ICU. Clamp physiology - mimic IVC clamped, CVP <5mmHg, TXA - withhold or titrate.

Laparotomy for cancer: neuroaxial - epidural if suitable, TIVA, short-acting agents.
