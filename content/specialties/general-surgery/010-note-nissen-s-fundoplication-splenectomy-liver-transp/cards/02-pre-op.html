Nissen's: standard work-up.

Splenectomy: recent CT or radiotherapy? Blood transfusion/blood products from RTC? Group & save, FBC → pancytopenia. Vaccination - pneumococcal/Hib/meningococcal/influenza per local guidance.

Liver transplant: pathology → HCC, ALD, NAFLD, hepatitis, AIH (autoimmune hepatitis), or metabolic/drug/paracetamol (acute liver failure); consider effect of chemotherapy. Child-Pugh & MELD scores. Difficult airway from encephalopathy? CT coagulopathy?

Laparotomy for cancer: crossmatch, FBC (chemotherapy → pancytopenia), CT/CPET/DSE, XETOH/smoking, dietician input, prehab/psych, biliary drainage if >250 bilirubin.
