Splenectomy: multimodal analgesia; watch for coagulopathy - blood products & TEG guided.

Liver transplant: early = bleeding, 1° allograft failure. Hepatic/portal vein thrombosis, biliary dysfunction, AKI, rejection. Late = infection, graft rejection, recurrent 1° disease, immunosuppressant complications. Specialist ward vs HDU.

Laparotomy for cancer: multimodal analgesia, avoidant of NSAIDs if coagulopathy, avoid excess opioid on renal referral, pain team referral.
