A motility disorder due to failure of relaxation of the lower oesophageal sphincter and loss of peristalsis in the oesophageal body, due to degeneration of the myenteric (Auerbach's) plexus.

Presentation: progressive dysphagia to solids and liquids, regurgitation of undigested food, weight loss, chest pain, recurrent aspiration/nocturnal cough.

Investigations: barium swallow ("bird's beak" sign), oesophageal manometry (gold standard), endoscopy to exclude malignancy (pseudoachalasia).

Anaesthetic considerations: significant aspiration risk due to a dilated oesophagus retaining food/fluid/secretions - consider prolonged pre-op fasting, clear fluid diet in the days before surgery, awake fibreoptic intubation or RSI with head-up positioning, may require oesophageal emptying/suction before induction.

Treatment options: pneumatic dilatation, Heller's cardiomyotomy (± fundoplication), peroral endoscopic myotomy (POEM), botulinum toxin injection (temporary, used in high-risk surgical patients).
