Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • IBD (UC or Crohn's)
  • Elective or emergency (toxic megacolon, perforation, failed medical therapy)
  • Steroid, immunosuppressant and biologic use
  • Nutrition and weight loss

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Fluid status and sepsis
  • Abdomen
  • Spine if neuraxial planned

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC (anaemia)
  • U&E, magnesium, albumin, CRP
  • Group and save
  • ECG
  • CT

Optimise

  • Steroid cover plan (hydrocortisone): Association of Anaesthetists peri-operative glucocorticoid guideline (2020) ↗
  • Correct anaemia and electrolytes
  • Nutrition (dietitian, TPN if malnourished)
  • Stoma site marked
  • VTE plan, since IBD carries a high VTE risk

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, arterial line, ± cardiac output monitor
  • Warming
  • Lithotomy supports

Induction

  • 20G cannula for induction, 16G once asleep
  • RSI if septic or obstructed
  • Hydrocortisone if on steroids
  • Follow local guidelines. Antibiotics (cefuroxime and metronidazole if not allergic)
  • Arterial line

Maintenance

  • Goal-directed fluids, vasopressors if septic
  • Lithotomy and head-down: pressure care and nerve protection
  • Warming, TXA, electrolyte management, PONV prophylaxis

Emergence

  • Full reversal
  • Rectus sheath catheters or epidural if not already in
  • Extubate awake

Post-op

Complications

  • Leak and pelvic sepsis
  • High-output stoma: dehydration and AKI
  • Adrenal crisis
  • Ileus
  • VTE
  • Lithotomy nerve injury

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Pelvic nerve injury
  • Ureteric injury

Delayed

  • Pouch or stoma complications (retraction, necrosis, high output)
  • Perineal wound breakdown
  • Pelvic sepsis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Adrenal crisis without steroid cover
  • Hypovolaemia
  • Lithotomy nerve injuries

Delayed

  • Dehydration and AKI from high-output stoma
  • VTE

Analgesia

  • Epidural or rectus sheath catheters
  • Consider ketamine or clonidine if opioid tolerant
  • Paracetamol, avoid NSAIDs early

Destination

  • HDU if emergency, septic or epidural; otherwise ERAS ward
  • Stoma output and fluid balance charts
  • Steroid weaning plan
  • Extended VTE prophylaxis
