Testing for brainstem death (UK AoMRC/FICM criteria): core temperature >34C; the apnoea test starts with PaCO2 >=6.0kPa and pH <7.40, and requires a PaCO2 rise of >=0.5kPa with no respiratory effort over 5 minutes; legal time of death is the completion of the first set of tests (two sets are performed). The US (AAN) thresholds differ (PaCO2 >=8.0kPa/60mmHg and a rise of 2.7kPa/20mmHg).
