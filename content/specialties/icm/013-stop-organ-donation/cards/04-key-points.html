Critical care management of the potential donor: active/passive temperature management, lung-protective ventilation, appropriate line placement (arterial and central) and positioning (30 degrees head up), active warming to normothermia. Fluid management with 5% dextrose or saline +/- insulin if glucose is raised, aiming for BM 4-10mmol/L. Consider continuing antibiotics and enteral feed, IV methylprednisolone, blood products for coagulopathy, desmopressin for diabetes insipidus, T3/T4 if needed, and vasopressin first-line for vasopressor support (NHSBT donor bundle - ADH replacement improves graft function), weaning catecholamines (high noradrenaline requirements predict poorer organ outcomes).

UK is now an 'opt-out' system, except for those under 18, those lacking capacity to opt out, and visitors/tourists.
