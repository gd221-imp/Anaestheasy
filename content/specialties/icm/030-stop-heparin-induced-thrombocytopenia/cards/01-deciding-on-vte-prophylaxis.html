Weigh thrombosis risk (active cancer, age >60, dehydration, thrombophilia, raised BMI, comorbidities, previous VTE, HRT/OCP use, pregnancy, ICU admission, surgery >90mins, immobility) against bleeding risk (active bleeding, an acquired bleeding disorder, anticoagulant use, acute stroke, platelets <75, BP >230/120)

Contraindications to prophylaxis: recent neurosurgery, spinal or eye surgery, LP within the last 24hrs, or another high-risk procedure
