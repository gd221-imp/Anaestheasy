Heparin increases antithrombin III activity roughly 1000-fold against factor Xa and thrombin, with onset within minutes.

LMWH is derived from heparin, a naturally occurring glycosaminoglycan produced and stored in the granules of mast cells and basophils.

Low molecular weight heparin (LMWH) is unfractionated heparin (UFH) fractionated into shorter polysaccharide chains - it still binds antithrombin III against factor Xa (with less anti-IIa effect); it doesn't need routine APTT monitoring when given subcutaneously for prophylaxis, needs caution in renal failure, and risks underdosing in obesity.
