CSF findings vary by cause (normal vs viral vs bacterial vs TB vs fungal): opening pressure tends to be normal or mildly raised in viral causes and more markedly raised in bacterial, TB, and fungal causes; protein is normal or mildly raised in viral infection and more markedly raised in bacterial/TB/fungal infection; white cell count is typically under 5 normally, under 100 (lymphocyte-predominant) in viral infection, over 1000 (neutrophil-predominant) in bacterial infection, and variably raised (lymphocyte-predominant) in TB/fungal infection; CSF:plasma glucose ratio is normal in viral infection but reduced in bacterial, and more markedly reduced in TB/fungal infection.

Opportunistic causes to consider in immunocompromise: CMV, EBV, Listeria monocytogenes, Treponema pallidum, Cryptococcus neoformans, Histoplasma capsulatum, Aspergillus, Toxoplasma gondii
