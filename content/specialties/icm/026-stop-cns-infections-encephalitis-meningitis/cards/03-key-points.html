Autoimmune encephalitis is the most common non-infective cause, with a similar presentation but a more subacute onset (under 3 months). Often paraneoplastic - anti-NMDAR encephalitis is associated with ovarian teratoma; anti-AMPAR encephalitis is associated with thymoma, ovarian teratoma, or breast/lung cancer.

Treatment: high-dose corticosteroids, IVIG, plasma exchange
