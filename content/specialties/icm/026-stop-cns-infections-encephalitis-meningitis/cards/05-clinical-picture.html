Cardiovascular - signs of sepsis, meningism (neck stiffness, photophobia), pyrexia, a purpuric rash; neurological - nuchal rigidity, photophobia, cranial nerve palsies, aphasia, hemiparesis, seizures, reduced GCS, and abnormal pupillary signs

All patients over 3 months with suspected non-meningococcal meningitis should receive IV dexamethasone alongside antibiotics (follow local guidelines for the antibiotic choice).
