Management: ABCDE, bloods (FBC/U&Es/CRP/clotting/cultures), CT brain, then LP if safe (no raised ICP on CT, no coagulopathy, not septic)

Empirical treatment (follow local guidelines): e.g. ceftriaxone 2g BD plus aciclovir 10mg/kg TDS

HSV-1 causes around 50% of cases; other causes include HSV-2, VZV, and a wide range of other organisms. HSV encephalitis classically causes temporal lobe oedema on imaging.
