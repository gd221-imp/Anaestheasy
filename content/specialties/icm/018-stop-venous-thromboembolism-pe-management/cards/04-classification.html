Massive - cardiovascular instability, a saddle embolus, or RV strain - consider thrombolysis (e.g. alteplase 50mg bolus plus 50mg over 30 mins, continuing CPR for 60-90 mins after thrombolysis if arrested (RCUK)). Absolute contraindications to thrombolysis: bleeding diathesis, major trauma/surgery/head injury, a CNS neoplasm, ischaemic stroke within 6 months, or any history of haemorrhagic stroke.

Sub-massive: RV strain on echo - treat with LMWH, consider CTPA, manage on ITU

Non-massive: LMWH for around 3 months
