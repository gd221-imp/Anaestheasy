ABCDE, resuscitation, antibiotics (follow local guidelines; e.g. piperacillin-tazobactam plus clindamycin), and urgent surgical exploration and debridement

Intraoperative findings: necrosis of the fascial plane, lack of resistance to blunt dissection, muscle involvement, lack of bleeding, and foul-smelling 'dishwater' pus

Adjuncts sometimes considered: IVIG (may help neutralise exotoxin) and hyperbaric oxygen therapy (proposed to improve neutrophil bactericidal activity, though evidence is limited)
