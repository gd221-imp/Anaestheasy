Suggests success if: RR <35/min, minute ventilation <10L/min, tidal volume >5ml/kg, and P/F ratio >26kPa

Suggests failure if: RR rises significantly, SpO2 <90%, HR >140, a significant rise or fall in blood pressure, sweating, agitation, or respiratory distress

If failing: gradually reduce pressure support over days and retrial; ongoing failure may prompt consideration of tracheostomy
