Neuromuscular: hypomagnesaemia, GBS, critical illness polyneuropathy/myopathy, electrolyte abnormalities

Respiratory: raised airway resistance (bronchospasm, secretions), reduced compliance (splinting, pneumothorax), or increased ventilatory requirement (hypermetabolism, PE, sepsis)

Cardiac: systolic or diastolic dysfunction, ischaemic heart disease

Central: blunted CO2 response (sedatives, damage to the respiratory centre) or blunted hypoxic response (e.g. in COPD)
