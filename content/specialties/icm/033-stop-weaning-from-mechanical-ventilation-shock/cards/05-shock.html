A life-threatening acute circulatory failure associated with inadequate oxygen delivery and/or utilisation by cells

Distributive: 'vasodilatory' - raised cardiac output with reduced SVR - e.g. sepsis, anaphylaxis, neurogenic shock

Cardiogenic: ventricular dysfunction (e.g. from acute MI) - reduced cardiac output - e.g. arrhythmia, valvular disease, LVOT obstruction

Hypovolaemic: reduced intravascular volume - reduced cardiac output - e.g. haemorrhage, vomiting, burns, third-spacing

Obstructive: obstructed flow through the great vessels - e.g. PE/VTE, valve lesion, tamponade, tension pneumothorax, aortocaval compression
