Noise: from equipment (CPAP/ventilator alarms) and background activity (staff, other patients) - typical ICU noise is 60-70dB, but a comfortable sleep environment needs under 40dB

Light: lack of natural light, and disruptive overnight ambient lighting

  1. Social isolation from family/friends, limited visiting time, and reduced deep/REM sleep (patients may be awake for 30-60% of typical 'sleep' time) - associated with delirium, immune dysfunction, and altered catecholamine levels
  2. Communication breakdown with staff, causing frustration and anxiety
  3. Loss of privacy and independence - inability to feed, wash, toilet, or reposition oneself
