Increased via raised BMR, shivering thermogenesis, non-shivering thermogenesis (brown fat), and normal skeletal muscle activity; decreased via sweating (sympathetically driven, cooling by the latent heat of vaporisation) and increased skin perfusion. Thermoreceptors are found in the skin, hypothalamus, spinal cord, and abdominal organs; the hypothalamus acts as the central integrator; effectors include sympathetic output to sweat glands, skin arterioles, the adrenal medulla, and motor pathways to skeletal muscle.
