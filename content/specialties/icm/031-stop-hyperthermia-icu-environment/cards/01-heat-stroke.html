Altered mental status, anhidrosis, and temperature >40.6C, plus tachycardia, hypotension, arrhythmias, delirium, lethargy, coma, and seizures; can cause AKI, hypokalaemia, deranged LFTs, DIC, and rhabdomyolysis.

Classical (non-exertional) heat stroke affects the elderly or very young; exertional heat stroke affects young, healthy people in a hot/humid climate.
