Breakdown of large numbers of tumour cells releases intracellular contents into the circulation, causing raised potassium, urea, phosphate, and nucleic acid breakdown products, with reduced calcium

Risk factors: acute leukaemias, high-grade lymphomas, chemotherapy

Diagnosis: 2 or more electrolyte abnormalities, plus one of AKI, arrhythmia, seizure, or cardiac arrest

Treatment: ABCDE, fluid resuscitation, treat hyperkalaemia, rasburicase (for established hyperuricaemia) or allopurinol (for prevention)
