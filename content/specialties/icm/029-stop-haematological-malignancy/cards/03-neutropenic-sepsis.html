Neutrophils <0.5x10^9/L with a source of infection or temperature >38C

Management: ABCDE, positive pressure/side room isolation and avoiding invasive procedures where possible, oral hygiene and chlorhexidine decontamination, blood cultures and source identification, broad-spectrum antibiotics with pseudomonas cover (follow local guidelines; e.g. piperacillin-tazobactam or meropenem), plus antifungal cover if septic shock persists
