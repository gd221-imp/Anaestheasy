Alkylating agents (prevent cell replication by cross-linking DNA) - e.g. cyclophosphamide, cisplatin, carboplatin

Anti-metabolites (disrupt nucleotide metabolism/DNA synthesis and repair) - e.g. methotrexate, 5-fluorouracil

Anti-microtubule agents (interfere with mitotic processes) - e.g. paclitaxel, vincristine

Cytotoxic antibiotics (damage DNA) - e.g. bleomycin
