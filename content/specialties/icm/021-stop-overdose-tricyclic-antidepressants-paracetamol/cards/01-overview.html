Tricyclic antidepressant overdose (e.g. amitriptyline, nortriptyline): TCAs have anti-muscarinic, anti-histaminergic, and anti-alpha-adrenergic effects, and inhibit noradrenaline/serotonin reuptake, and block sodium channels.

Overdose picture: cardiovascular - tachycardia, hypotension, widened QRS, ventricular arrhythmias; neurological - mydriasis, reduced GCS, tremor, seizures
