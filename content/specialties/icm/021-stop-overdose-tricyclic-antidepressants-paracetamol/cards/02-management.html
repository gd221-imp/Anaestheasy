Supportive - ventilatory support if GCS <8, IV crystalloid or blood, vasopressors as needed

Specific - activated charcoal within 1hr of ingestion, gastric lavage if the airway is secured; IV sodium bicarbonate if QRS >100ms (bolus then infusion, aiming pH 7.45-7.55) - alkalinising the blood increases protein binding of the drug and reduces cardiac/neuro toxicity; benzodiazepines for seizures (avoid phenytoin, which can also block sodium channels)
