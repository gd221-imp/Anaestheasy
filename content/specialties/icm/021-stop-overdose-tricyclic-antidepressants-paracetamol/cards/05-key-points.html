N-acetylcysteine (NAC) replenishes glutathione stores. It's most effective started within 8hrs, but can still have benefit up to 72hrs post-ingestion.

Approach: 1. Check the paracetamol level and compare against the treatment nomogram 2. Follow a weight-based dosing protocol (e.g. an initial infusion over 2hrs, then a longer infusion over several hours) - can be stopped early if INR <1.3, ALT <2x normal, paracetamol level <10mg/L, and there's no evidence of liver failure

Without NAC treatment: Stage 3 (48-96hrs) - jaundice, coagulopathy, encephalopathy; Stage 4 (>96hrs) - severe liver injury (~60%), death (~5%)

Hepatic encephalopathy grading: I - altered behaviour; II - lethargy, mood/confusion changes; III - somnolent; IV - coma
