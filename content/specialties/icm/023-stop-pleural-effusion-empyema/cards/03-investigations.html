CT chest, USS chest, then pleural tap - send for gram stain and culture, AFB/TB culture, cytology, glucose, and protein; protein >30g/L, pH <7.2, or raised LDH suggest an exudate

Light's criteria (an exudate if any of): pleural:serum protein ratio >0.5, pleural:serum LDH ratio >0.6, or pleural LDH >2/3 of the upper limit of normal serum LDH

(Normal pleural fluid pH is around 7.6-7.64)
