Absorption: reduced gastric emptying, raised gastric pH, and reduced perfusion reduce subcutaneous or IM drug absorption

Distribution: reduced volume of distribution for lipophilic drugs, but fluid extravasation raises the volume of distribution for hydrophilic drugs; reduced plasma protein and altered pH affect ionisation; reduced albumin reduces protein binding

Metabolism: vasopressor use tends to reduce hepatic blood flow and therefore hepatic clearance

Excretion: renal function is often reduced
