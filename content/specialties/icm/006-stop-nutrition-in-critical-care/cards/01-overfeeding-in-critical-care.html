Risk factors include extremes of size/age. Presents with increased gastric aspirate, vomiting/regurgitation, aspiration risk, diarrhoea, or malabsorption. Excess amino acids can cause uraemia and raised ammonia; excess lipid can cause fat overload syndrome; excess carbohydrate raises CO2 production (respiratory quotient up to 1.5), increases infection risk, impairs wound healing, and can cause glucose overload.

Nutritional requirement (rough guide) is often estimated relative to weight/height2 (kg/m2).
