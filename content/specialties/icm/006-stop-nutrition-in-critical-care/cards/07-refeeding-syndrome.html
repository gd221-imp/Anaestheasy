Within a few days of reintroducing feed after a period of starvation, falling phosphate, potassium, magnesium, and extracellular fluid volume can occur. During starvation insulin falls; on refeeding, glucose and insulin rise, driving glucose, magnesium, potassium, and phosphate into cells - plasma levels fall while sodium and water retention rise, risking left ventricular failure.

Treatment for those at risk: slow introduction of feeding with aggressive electrolyte correction.
