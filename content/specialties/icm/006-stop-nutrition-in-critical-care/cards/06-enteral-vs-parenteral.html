Parenteral (via CVC) avoids reliance on the GI tract with no interruption for feeding, but carries CVC infection/DVT risk and can cause LFT abnormalities. Enteral (NG/PEG/JEJ) is cheap, simpler, maintains gut blood flow and integrity, avoids a CVC, and reduces catabolism, but carries risk of VAP, sinusitis, and can be compromised by oesophageal/pharyngeal/gastric perforation, diarrhoea, or ileus.
