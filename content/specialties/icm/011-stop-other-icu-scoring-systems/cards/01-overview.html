APACHE II (Acute Physiology, Age, and Chronic Health Evaluation) - scored out of 71; >25 predicts around 50% mortality, >35 predicts around 85% mortality. Weakness: an admission score that doesn't account for any therapeutic or resuscitative efforts made.

P-POSSUM (Portsmouth Physiological and Operative Severity Score for the enUmeration of Mortality and morbidity) - weakness: needs intraoperative findings.

NELA (National Emergency Laparotomy Audit) - more accurate than P-POSSUM, but estimates mortality only.
