Medical: reduce intraluminal contents (NG tube, flatus tube, enemas, reduce enteral feed rate); improve abdominal wall compliance (optimise sedation/analgesia, remove tight dressings, consider neuromuscular blockade, reverse Trendelenburg positioning); organ support (fluid/HDU-ICU level care, cardiovascular support, RRT)

Surgical: imaging to exclude a drainable collection, or decompressive laparotomy (leaving the abdomen open with a temporary closure such as a Bogota bag) with a plan for definitive closure, typically within 8 days
