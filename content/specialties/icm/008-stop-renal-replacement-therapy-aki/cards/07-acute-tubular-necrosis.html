Damage to renal tubular epithelial cells from ischaemia or nephrotoxins (exogenous - contrast, gentamicin, cisplatin; or endogenous - myoglobin, haemoglobin, ethylene glycol). Vasoconstriction and inflammation reduce GFR; sloughed, dying tubular cells obstruct tubules, causing reduced urine output, AKI, uraemia, fluid overload, acidosis, and characteristic granular 'muddy brown' casts.

Treatment: supportive - remove/stop the trigger, manage fluid balance, correct abnormalities, and restore perfusion.
