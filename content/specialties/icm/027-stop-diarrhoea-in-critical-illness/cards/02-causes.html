Infectious: C.difficile, norovirus, rotavirus, and others

Non-infectious: enteral feeding (the most common cause), drugs (laxatives, antibiotics, magnesium), overflow diarrhoea, ischaemic colitis, exacerbation of IBD, malabsorption (e.g. short bowel) - contributing feed factors include too high a rate, hyperosmolar content, reduced fibre, or migration of the NG tube into the duodenum. Physiological stress and endocrine conditions can also contribute.
