• Indications for bilevel NIV: acute hypercapnic respiratory failure in COPD (pH below 7.35 and PaCO2 above 6.5 kPa despite optimal medical treatment), obesity hypoventilation, and neuromuscular or chest wall disease.
  • CPAP is used for cardiogenic pulmonary oedema and hypoxaemic respiratory failure.
  • Starting settings: IPAP about 15 cmH2O, titrated up to 20–30 cmH2O within the first hour; EPAP 3–5 cmH2O (higher in obesity). Target SpO2 88–92% in hypercapnic patients.
  • Recheck the blood gas within 1–2 hours. If it is not improving, escalate to intubation if appropriate.
  • Agree and document a ceiling of treatment before starting.
