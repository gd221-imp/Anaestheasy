Raised PT (reflects factors II, V, VII and X - all synthesised in the liver, as are IX and most others; IX is intrinsic pathway, so not in the PT), reduced platelets (splenomegaly and sequestration), anaemia (often from folate/B-vitamin deficiency), acute or chronic renal failure, and hepatorenal syndrome with secondary hyperaldosteronism can all occur - reduced SVR reduces renal perfusion, triggering renal artery vasoconstriction and renal dysfunction.
