Absolute - coma, hypoxaemia or hypercapnia despite treatment, cardiac arrest. Relative - failure to respond to treatment, exhaustion, drowsiness, confusion

  • Hypotension often follows induction from dehydration and raised intrathoracic pressure reducing venous return - have fluid and vasopressors/inotropes ready; ketamine is a good induction choice as it's also bronchodilating
  • Ventilate with around 6ml/kg tidal volume, reduced rate, reduced PEEP, plateau <35cmH2O, titrate FiO2, and accept permissive hypercapnia
