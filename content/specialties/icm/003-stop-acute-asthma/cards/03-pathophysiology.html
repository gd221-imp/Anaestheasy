Bronchospasm causes obstruction of small airways, so downstream alveoli don't empty, causing V/Q mismatch and hypoxia. Rising intrinsic PEEP must be overcome before air can be inspired, worsening the work of breathing; air trapping raises FRC and reduces diaphragmatic efficiency. Alveolar hypoventilation from respiratory fatigue raises PaCO2. Raised intrathoracic pressure reduces venous return, compounded by dehydration from tachypnoea and sweating.
