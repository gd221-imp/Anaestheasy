1. Treat the underlying cause
  2. Non-pharmacological: earplugs, night-time light and noise reduction, daytime light exposure, large clocks/signage, reassurance, cognitive stimulation, promoting mobilisation, removing invasive devices where possible, ensuring glasses/hearing aids are worn
  3. Pharmacological: antipsychotics such as haloperidol or olanzapine; dexmedetomidine (an alpha-2 agonist); melatonin; avoid benzodiazepines and ketamine where possible

Prevention: multimodal opioid-sparing analgesia, using depth-of-anaesthesia monitoring to titrate sedation, and avoiding excessive sedation

Triggers: hypoxaemia, hypercapnia, hypertension, pain, infection, electrolyte disturbance, hyper/hypoglycaemia, AKI, liver failure, inflammation, stress
