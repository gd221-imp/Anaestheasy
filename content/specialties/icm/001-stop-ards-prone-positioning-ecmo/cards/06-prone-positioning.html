Reduces mortality in severe ARDS by around 10%. Improves V/Q matching and oxygenation by relieving pressure of the heart/mediastinum on the posterior dependent lung and reducing the ventral-dorsal compression difference.

Complications: dislodgement of ETT/chest drain/lines, vomiting, facial oedema, raised intra-abdominal/intracranial pressure, pressure injuries, brachial plexus stretch.
