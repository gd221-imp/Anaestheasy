1. Acute - within 1 week of a known clinical insult
  2. Imaging - bilateral opacities on CXR or CT consistent with pulmonary oedema, or bilateral B-lines/consolidation on lung USS
  3. Respiratory distress not fully explained by cardiac failure or fluid overload
  4. Hypoxaemia by P/F ratio: mild 27-40kPa (200-300mmHg), moderate 13-26.7kPa (100-200mmHg), severe <13.3kPa (<100mmHg)

Oxygenation Index = MAP x FiO2 x 100/PaO2 (used in ventilated patients)
