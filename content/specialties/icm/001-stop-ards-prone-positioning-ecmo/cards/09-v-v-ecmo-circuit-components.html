Drainage and return cannulae (often a dual-lumen catheter), centrifugal pump, gas blender, oxygenator

Haematological consequences: anticoagulation with unfractionated heparin (target ACT ~180sec), plus ongoing product consumption from the circuit and heparin effect - regular RBC/FFP transfusion often needed, bleeding risk, fibrinogen/vWF depletion

CESAR Trial (2009): ECMO vs conventional ventilation - ECMO was associated with increased survival without disability at 6 months
