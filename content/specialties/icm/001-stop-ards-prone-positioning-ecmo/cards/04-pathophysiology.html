Inflammation causes endothelial dysfunction, progressing through exudative, proliferative, then fibrotic phases. Alveolar capillary leakage causes alveolar damage, raised TNFa/IL-6, neutrophil recruitment, and free radical/thromboxane formation. Ventilator-associated lung injury (reduced compliance from volutrauma/barotrauma/atelectrauma) compounds this, and fibrosis/remodelling can become irreversible.
