Can improve survival and neurological outcome (one study showed 100% survival if started within 30 mins of arrest, 25% within 75 mins). Needs femoral vessel access (often cut-down); complications include infection, limb ischaemia, thrombosis/bleeding, AKI, air embolism, haemolysis, oxygenator failure. Needs an ECMO centre, ideally started within 30 mins of arrest.
