V-A / V-V / A-V (uncommon)

Indications (broad): potentially reversible severe respiratory failure, no limitation to life-sustaining treatment, a Murray score >=3 or uncompensated hypercapnia. The Murray score combines P/F ratio, CXR quadrants involved, PEEP, and compliance.

Relative contraindications: age >65, intracranial bleed, contraindication to anticoagulation, >7 days of high peak pressure (>30cmH2O) or FiO2 >0.8 on ventilation, BMI >40
