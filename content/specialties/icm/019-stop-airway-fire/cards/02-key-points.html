Fire alarm response in ICU (RACE-type approach): RESCUE (remove staff/patients from immediate danger), ALERT (alert others and the fire warden), CONTAIN (turn off O2 outlets, close doors and bulkheads), EXTINGUISH (a small fire can be tackled with a fire blanket or extinguisher - water/dry powder for bedding, CO2 for electrical fires), RELOCATE (once extinguished, relocate patients/staff)
