The cannula (changed every 2-3 days) is attached by flexible tubing +/- a subcutaneous glucose sensor. Delay surgery until HbA1c is reasonably controlled where possible, involve the diabetes specialist nurse pre-op, put patients first on the list, change the cannula in the morning, ensure the patient is conscious and able to manage the pump continuously during surgery, and check BM hourly - stop the pump and switch to IV insulin if glucose rises above around 12, or if it falls below 4.

Pancreas endocrine function: islets of Langerhans - alpha cells produce glucagon, beta cells produce insulin, delta cells produce somatostatin.
