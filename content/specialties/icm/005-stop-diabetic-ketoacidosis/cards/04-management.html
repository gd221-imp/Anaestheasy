Fixed-rate IV insulin at 0.1u/kg/hr plus continuing any long-acting insulin; fluid with Hartmann's or 0.9% saline with potassium; add glucose (10% or 5% dextrose) once BM <14; VTE prophylaxis; consider infection; diabetes specialist nurse referral

Targets per hour: BM falling by 3mmol/L, HCO3- rising by 3mmol/L, ketones falling by 0.5mmol/L

Resolution: ketones <0.6mmol/L, HCO3- >15mmol/L, pH >=7.3

Complications of treatment: hypo/hyperkalaemia, hypoglycaemia, cerebral oedema, AKI, VTE
