Pro-insulin is synthesised in the RER, transported to the Golgi apparatus, and cleaved into insulin and C-peptide. Glucose enters cells via GLUT1/GLUT2 receptors, glycolysis produces ATP, and ATP-sensitive potassium channels alter the resting membrane potential, causing depolarisation, calcium channel opening, and insulin exocytosis.

Insulin encourages storage (increased glucose uptake via GLUT4, glycogenesis, fatty acid synthesis) and discourages breakdown (reduced lipolysis, glycogenolysis, gluconeogenesis, and proteolysis).
