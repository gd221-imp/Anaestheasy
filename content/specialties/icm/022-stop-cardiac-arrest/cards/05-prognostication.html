Factors associated with reduced survival: pre-arrest - older age, frailty, a high clinical frailty score, active cancer, renal disease; intra-arrest - unwitnessed arrest, a non-shockable rhythm, an out-of-hospital arrest

Neurological assessment at 72hrs - poor outcome is suggested by bilateral absent pupillary/corneal reflexes, myoclonus or prolonged seizures, loss of grey-white matter differentiation on CT/MRI, bilaterally absent N20 SSEP responses, or a suppressed/burst-suppression EEG
