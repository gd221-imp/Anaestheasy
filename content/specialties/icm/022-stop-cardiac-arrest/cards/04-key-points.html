Positive pressure ventilation raises both intrathoracic and intracranial pressure (reducing cerebral perfusion pressure) - there's ongoing interest in whether this affects neurological outcomes.

Post-ROSC key points: temperature control - prevent fever (keep <37.7C) for at least 72hrs (ERC 2021; the TTM trials showed no difference between 33C and 36C, so older advice of targeting 36C has been replaced), and delay formal neuroprognostication until at least 72hrs post-arrest.
