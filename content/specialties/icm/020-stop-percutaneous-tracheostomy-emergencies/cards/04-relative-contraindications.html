Anatomical (aberrant vessels over the trachea, abnormal tracheal/thyroid anatomy, morbid obesity, inability to extend the neck, a previous tracheostomy), respiratory (PEEP >10cmH2O, FiO2 >0.6), or other (local infection, coagulopathy)

Surgical vs percutaneous tracheostomy is favoured when it's part of another procedure, with significant obesity, cervical instability, or a challenging airway.
