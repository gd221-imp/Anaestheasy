Initial: declare a critical incident, call for help, get the difficult airway trolley/tracheostomy box, apply high-flow oxygen to both the tracheostomy and the mouth/nose, and assess breathing with capnography using a Mapleson C circuit - but don't bag through the tracheostomy yet.

Specific steps: 1. Remove the speaking valve, inner tube, and cap 2. Attempt to pass a suction catheter 3. Deflate the cuff and remove the tracheostomy tube, then assess or ventilate via the stoma 4. Use oral airway manoeuvres and cover the stoma with gauze 5. Attempt ventilation beyond the stoma, or intubate the stoma directly with a size 6.0 ETT/tracheostomy tube, advancing with fibreoptic guidance
