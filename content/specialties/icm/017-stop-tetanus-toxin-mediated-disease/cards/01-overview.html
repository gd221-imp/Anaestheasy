Incubation typically under 7 days, with symptom onset 3-21 days after a wound.

Clostridium tetani = a spore-forming anaerobic bacterium producing the tetanospasmin neurotoxin, which binds the presynaptic membrane at the NMJ and undergoes retrograde axonal transport to the spinal cord and brainstem, selectively inhibiting interneurons (blocking glycine/GABA release) and preventing normal reflex inhibition.
