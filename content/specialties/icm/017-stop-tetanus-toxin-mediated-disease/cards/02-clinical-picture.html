Muscle rigidity, muscle spasm, autonomic instability. Early features include trismus, dysphagia, and tremors, progressing to spasms ('risus sardonicus', opisthotonus), hyperpyrexia, and apnoea, then autonomic dysfunction over days (arrhythmias, sweating, labile blood pressure)

Differential: hypocalcaemia, choreoathetosis, epilepsy, meningoencephalitis, SAH, rabies
