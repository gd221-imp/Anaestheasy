ABCDE, with attention to laryngospasm and chin/masseter spasm risk

Supportive: ICU care, a dark and quiet room, ventilation if needed, VTE prophylaxis, treatment of rhabdomyolysis, sedation to reduce sympathetic drive (avoiding stimulation), and management of spasms
