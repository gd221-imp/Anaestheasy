Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Mechanism, time, enclosed space (inhalation, carbon monoxide)
  • Burn area and depth
  • Comorbidities

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway: facial or airway burns, stridor, soot
  • % body surface area (Lund-Browder)
  • Circumferential burns

Investigations (Ix)

  • ABG with carboxyhaemoglobin
  • FBC, U&E, CK
  • Crossmatch

Optimise

  • Burn area, fluids, airway

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Large-bore access, arterial line, warming, blood

Induction

  • avoid suxamethonium after 24 hours

Maintenance

  • GA
  • Warm theatre
  • blood loss

Emergence

  • Stay intubated if airway burns or large excision
  • Keep warm

Post-op

Complications

Burn wound sepsis, ARDS, pulmonary oedema.

Early → fluid over-resuscitation, DVT, gastric ulceration, AKI, ↓temperature, infective complications → VAP/corneal ulcer/UTI/invasive line infection/IE.

Late → pulmonary fibrosis, chronic pain, sleep disorder, mental health disorder (PTSD, depression, disfigurement effects), contractures.

Analgesia

  • Opioids (high requirements)
  • Ketamine
  • Paracetamol; regional where possible

Destination

  • ICU or burns unit
