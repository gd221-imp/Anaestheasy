Swelling of face, singed nasal hairs, wheeze, secretions, voice change, stridor, soot in sputum, resp distress, SpO2 <94% on air, ↑CO, burns to face.

How? Heat → burns of naso/oropharyngeal mucosa → oedema → airway obstruction.

Particulate matter deposition → soot settles in small airways → mechanical obstruction, atelectasis, ↓compliance.

Chemical irritants → inflammation of lung → alveolar oedema → V/Q mismatch.

CO toxicity → ↓DO2, shift oxyHb curve left, ↑flow O2 → FiO2 1.0 when high level; histotoxic hypoxia.

Hydrogen cyanide → binds cytochrome A3 oxidase in the electron transport chain in mitochondria → hydroxocobalamin 5g over 15min. (carbonaceous deposits, mucosal pallor).
