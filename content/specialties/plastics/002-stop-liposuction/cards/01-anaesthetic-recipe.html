Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • BMI and comorbidities
  • Tumescent solution: lidocaine dose limits
  • Day case or overnight

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • BMI

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC if large volume

Optimise

  • Volume of aspirate planned

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access
  • Warming

Induction

  • GA or sedation with tumescent local anaesthesia

Maintenance

  • Calculate lidocaine dose (tumescent limit up to 35–55 mg/kg, absorbed slowly)
  • Fluid replacement for large-volume liposuction

Emergence

  • Extubate awake
  • Monitor for LA toxicity

Post-op

Complications

  • Monitor for LA toxicity (peak 12 hours later)
  • Fluid balance
  • VTE prophylaxis

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Visceral perforation

Delayed

  • Seroma
  • Skin necrosis
  • Contour irregularity

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • LA toxicity
  • Fluid overload
  • Hypothermia

Delayed

  • Fat embolism
  • VTE

Analgesia

  • Tumescent local anaesthetic
  • Paracetamol, NSAID

Destination

  • Day case or overnight; overnight if large volume
