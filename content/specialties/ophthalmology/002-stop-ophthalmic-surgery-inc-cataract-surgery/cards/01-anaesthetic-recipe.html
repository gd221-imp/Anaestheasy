Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Ability to lie flat and still for 30 minutes
  • Anticoagulants
  • Cough, tremor, claustrophobia

Examination (O/E)

  • Axial length (long eyes need care with needle blocks)

Investigations (Ix)

  • INR if on warfarin (below 3.5)
  • Usually no other tests

Optimise

  • Anticoagulants, ability to lie flat

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Topical or sub-Tenon's block

Induction

  • sedation rarely needed

Maintenance

  • Local anaesthesia

Emergence

  • None under local; check comfort

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Posterior capsule rupture
  • Retrobulbar haemorrhage

Delayed

  • Endophthalmitis
  • Retinal detachment
  • Loss of vision

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Globe perforation from the block
  • Brainstem anaesthesia after retrobulbar block
  • Oculocardiac reflex

Delayed

  • Diplopia
  • Ptosis

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol

Destination

  • Home same day
