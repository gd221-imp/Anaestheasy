Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Full stomach versus risk of raised intraocular pressure

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Other injuries
  • Avoid pressure on the eye

Investigations (Ix)

  • CT orbit if foreign body

Optimise

  • Surgery soon (sight), but allow fasting if possible
  • Antiemetic; avoid crying or straining in children

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT, suction

Induction

  • RSI

Maintenance

  • avoid coughing and straining

Emergence

  • Smooth emergence; consider deep extubation if appropriate
  • Antiemetics

Post-op

Complications

  • Antiemetics

Surgical

Immediate

  • Extrusion of ocular contents
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Loss of vision
  • Endophthalmitis
  • Sympathetic ophthalmia

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Rise in intraocular pressure (coughing, straining, laryngoscopy, suxamethonium)
  • Aspiration (full stomach)
  • Oculocardiac reflex

Delayed

  • Coughing or vomiting at emergence

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; opioid PRN

Destination

  • Ophthalmic ward
