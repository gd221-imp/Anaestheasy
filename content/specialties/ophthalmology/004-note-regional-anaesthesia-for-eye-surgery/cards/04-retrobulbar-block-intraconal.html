Sharp needle. Eye in primary (neutral) gaze - the traditional Atkinson "up & in" position stretches the optic nerve into the needle path and is no longer recommended. First choice for vitrectomy. Risk of retrobulbar haemorrhage/globe injury, CN II injury/neuronal spread, ↑IOP.

Peribulbar block (extraconal): lower risk of globe perforation than retrobulbar but needs larger volume.
