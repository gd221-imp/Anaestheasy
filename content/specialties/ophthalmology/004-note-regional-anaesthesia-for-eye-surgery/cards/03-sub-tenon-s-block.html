Uses a blunt cannula. First choice for most eye surgery. ↓IOP.

Technique: 1) "look up & out" 2) topical anaesthesia 3) non-toothed forceps lift Tenon's fascia 5-10mm from limbus, small cut 4) blunt cannula advanced with vertical entry → aspirate & inject 3.5-5mL of half 2% lidocaine + half 0.5% bupivacaine.

Complications: chemosis/subconjunctival haemorrhage/globe perforation/neuronal spread/prolonged muscular palsy.

Watch for oculocardiac reflex.
