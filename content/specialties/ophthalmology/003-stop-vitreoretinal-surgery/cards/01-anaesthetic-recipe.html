Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Often diabetic with comorbidities
  • LA block or GA
  • ability to lie flat and still

Examination (O/E)

—

Investigations (Ix)

—

Optimise

  • Gas tamponade planned? (SF6, C3F8)

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring
  • Block equipment or LMA
  • Warning bracelet for intraocular gas

Induction

  • Peribulbar or sub-Tenon block, or GA

Maintenance

  • Avoid nitrous oxide if gas is to be injected (and for weeks after)
  • Oculocardiac reflex: atropine ready

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Posturing as instructed (face down)
  • PONV prophylaxis
  • Gas bubble warning: no flying, no N2O

Surgical

Immediate

  • Retinal detachment
  • Bleeding

Delayed

  • Raised IOP
  • Cataract
  • Endophthalmitis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Globe perforation from block
  • Oculocardiac reflex
  • Nitrous oxide expanding the gas bubble

Delayed

  • Loss of vision with later N2O exposure
  • PONV
