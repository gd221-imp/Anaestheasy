Relaxant: RSI with rocuronium 1.2mg/kg (sugammadex available) avoids the transient IOP rise from suxamethonium; suxamethonium remains acceptable if needed (e.g. difficult airway) - vitreous extrusion has not been shown in practice. Avoid mask pressure on the eye, hypercarbia and coughing.

Avoid N2O (expands intraocular gas bubbles), PONV → antiemetics, smooth extubation, avoid coughing/straining.

Rx of raised IOP: increase depth of GA, analgesia, PaCO2 4.5-5kPa, SpO2 >94%, avoid increased venous pressure, mannitol 0.5g/kg IV, acetazolamide 500mg IV.
