• Typical morphine PCA: 1mg bolus, 5-minute lockout, no background infusion in opioid-naive patients (background infusions increase respiratory depression)
  • Prescribe antiemetic and naloxone; hourly observations initially - sedation score, respiratory rate, SpO2, pain
  • Only the patient presses the button (no PCA by proxy)
  • Use oxycodone or fentanyl in renal impairment; consider opioid-tolerant patients separately (continue baseline opioid)
