• Lidocaine: e.g. 1.5mg/kg bolus then 1-2mg/kg/hr (ideal body weight) - opioid-sparing, may speed return of bowel function after abdominal surgery; needs monitoring for LAST, and not with regional LA blocks running
  • Ketamine: low-dose (e.g. 0.1-0.25mg/kg/hr) - opioid-sparing and anti-hyperalgesic, useful in opioid-tolerant patients; watch for psychotomimetic effects
