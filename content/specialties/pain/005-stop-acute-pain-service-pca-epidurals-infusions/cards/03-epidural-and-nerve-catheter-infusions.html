• Regular checks: block height and density (Bromage score), sedation, respiratory rate, BP, catheter site, pressure areas
  • Red flags: new or increasing motor block, especially after a period of recovery, or back pain - stop the infusion and review urgently; if not recovering within 4hrs, urgent MRI to exclude epidural haematoma or abscess
  • Timing of catheter removal must follow anticoagulant guidance
  • Hypotension: check for hypovolaemia/bleeding before blaming the epidural
