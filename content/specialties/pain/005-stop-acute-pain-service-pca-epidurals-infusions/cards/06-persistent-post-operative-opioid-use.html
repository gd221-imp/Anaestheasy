• A proportion of opioid-naive patients still take opioids months after surgery; risk factors include pre-op opioid use, depression/anxiety, high early opioid requirements and long discharge supplies
  • Prescribe short courses (e.g. up to 5-7 days) of immediate-release opioid at discharge, with a clear plan to stop; avoid modified-release opioids for acute pain
  • Communicate the weaning plan to the patient and GP (deprescribing)
