• Sedation precedes respiratory depression - monitor sedation score; a falling respiratory rate and SpO2 are late signs (especially on supplemental O2)
  • Risk factors: OSA, obesity, older age, renal impairment, opioid-naive patients, concurrent sedatives (benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids), background infusions, the first 24hrs
  • Treat with stimulation, oxygen, stop opioids, naloxone in small increments (e.g. 100mcg IV)
