Management of chronic non-specific low back pain: patient education, encourage continued activity/exercise, physiotherapy, weight loss if appropriate, psychological approaches (CBT) for chronic pain, analgesia per WHO ladder principles (though NICE now advises caution with long-term opioids for chronic non-specific back pain), consider interventional options (facet joint injections, radiofrequency denervation) in selected cases, surgery reserved for specific indications (significant neurological deficit, spinal instability, failed conservative management with correlating pathology on imaging).
