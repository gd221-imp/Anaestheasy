Red flags for serious spinal pathology ("cauda equina/malignancy/infection" screen): saddle anaesthesia, bladder/bowel dysfunction (retention or incontinence), bilateral leg weakness/sciatica, age <20 or >50 with new onset, history of malignancy, unexplained weight loss, fever/night sweats (infection), IV drug use, immunosuppression, significant trauma, thoracic pain, progressive neurological deficit, pain worse at night/at rest.
