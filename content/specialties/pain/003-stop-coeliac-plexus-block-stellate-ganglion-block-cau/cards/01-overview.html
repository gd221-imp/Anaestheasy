Targets the coeliac plexus (sympathetic plexus anterior to the aorta at approximately L1) to treat visceral pain from upper abdominal malignancy (classically pancreatic cancer) or chronic pancreatitis.

Technique: performed under CT or fluoroscopic guidance (posterior or anterior approach), local anaesthetic ± neurolytic agent (alcohol or phenol) injected around the plexus.

Complications: orthostatic hypotension (sympathetic block to the splanchnic vessels), diarrhoea (unopposed parasympathetic activity), retroperitoneal haemorrhage, injury to adjacent structures (kidney, aorta), rare risk of paraplegia (spinal artery injury/spasm).
