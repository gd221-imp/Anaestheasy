Blocks the cervicothoracic sympathetic ganglion (formed by fusion of the inferior cervical and first thoracic ganglia), located anterior to the neck of the first rib/C7 transverse process.

Indications: complex regional pain syndrome of the upper limb, sympathetically maintained pain, some vascular conditions (Raynaud's), refractory ventricular arrhythmia (rare use).

Signs of a successful block: Horner's syndrome (ptosis, miosis, anhidrosis, enophthalmos) on the ipsilateral side.

Complications: recurrent laryngeal nerve block (hoarseness), phrenic nerve block, intravascular injection (vertebral artery - can cause immediate seizure with very small volumes), pneumothorax, haematoma.
