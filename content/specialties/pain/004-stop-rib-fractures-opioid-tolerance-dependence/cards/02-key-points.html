Intravenous drug use (IVDU) and chronic opioid use - relevant definitions:

  • Tolerance: a reduced effect from the same dose of a drug over time, requiring escalating doses to achieve the same effect - a normal pharmacological phenomenon with regular opioid exposure
  • Dependence: a physiological state where abrupt cessation, or reversal, causes a withdrawal syndrome; can occur with appropriate therapeutic use and is not the same as addiction
  • Addiction: a psychological and behavioural pattern of compulsive drug use despite harm, characterised by craving, loss of control over use, and continued use despite negative consequences
