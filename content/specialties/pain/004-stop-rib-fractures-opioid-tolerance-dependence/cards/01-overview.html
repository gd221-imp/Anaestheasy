Pain management is central to preventing respiratory complications (splinting, atelectasis, retained secretions, pneumonia). Multimodal analgesia is key: paracetamol, NSAIDs where not contraindicated, opioids (with caution regarding sedation/respiratory depression), and consider regional techniques (thoracic epidural, paravertebral block, erector spinae plane block, serratus anterior plane block, intercostal nerve blocks) especially for multiple rib fractures/flail chest. Good analgesia facilitates deep breathing and effective coughing, reducing pneumonia risk - a key driver of morbidity/mortality in elderly patients with multiple rib fractures.
