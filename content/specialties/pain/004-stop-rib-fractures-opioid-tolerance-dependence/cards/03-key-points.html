Perioperative implications in patients with opioid tolerance/dependence/IVDU: baseline opioid requirement is higher (continue usual opioid/substitution therapy - e.g. methadone or buprenorphine - through the perioperative period where possible), standard "opioid-naive" dosing will be inadequate for acute pain, involve an acute pain team/addiction service early, use multimodal and regional techniques to reduce total opioid requirement, be alert to withdrawal symptoms (agitation, tachycardia, sweating, piloerection, diarrhoea) and to the possibility of undisclosed additional substance use.
