Pain that persists for >3 months after surgery, not explained by another cause, at the site of surgery.

Risk factors: severity of acute post-op pain (strongest predictor), pre-existing chronic pain, psychological factors (anxiety, depression, catastrophising), type of surgery (higher risk with amputation, thoracotomy, mastectomy, hernia repair), nerve injury during surgery, younger age, genetic factors, pre-op opioid use.

Prevention: good acute pain control, regional anaesthesia where appropriate, minimising nerve injury surgically, multimodal analgesia to reduce opioid requirement, psychological preparation/support for high-risk patients, early identification and treatment of neuropathic features postoperatively.
