Multimodal and often difficult to treat -

  • Pre-emptive/perioperative regional anaesthesia (epidural or peripheral nerve catheter) may reduce incidence
  • Pharmacological: gabapentinoids, tricyclic antidepressants, ketamine (NMDA antagonist - may help with central sensitisation), opioids (limited long-term benefit)
  • Non-pharmacological: mirror therapy, graded motor imagery, TENS, physiotherapy/desensitisation, psychological support (CBT)
  • Optimise prosthetic fit and treat any identifiable residual limb pathology (e.g. neuroma)
