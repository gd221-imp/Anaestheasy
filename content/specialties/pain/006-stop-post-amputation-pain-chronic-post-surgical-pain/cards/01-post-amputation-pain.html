• Residual limb pain (stump pain): pain localised to the residual limb, may be due to neuroma formation, poor prosthetic fit, infection, or ongoing surgical/wound-related pain
  • Phantom limb sensation: non-painful perception that the amputated limb is still present - very common, not itself pathological
  • Phantom limb pain: painful sensation perceived as arising from the amputated limb - thought to involve peripheral (neuroma), spinal (central sensitisation, dorsal horn changes) and cortical (cortical reorganisation/remapping) mechanisms
