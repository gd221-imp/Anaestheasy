WHO Analgesic Ladder (traditionally 3-step, now often used more flexibly):

  • Step 1: non-opioid (paracetamol ± NSAID) ± adjuvant
  • Step 2: weak opioid (e.g. codeine, tramadol) ± non-opioid ± adjuvant
  • Step 3: strong opioid (e.g. morphine, oxycodone) ± non-opioid ± adjuvant

Adjuvants: e.g. gabapentinoids/tricyclic antidepressants for neuropathic pain, bisphosphonates/denosumab for bone pain, corticosteroids for pain from oedema/nerve compression/raised ICP.
