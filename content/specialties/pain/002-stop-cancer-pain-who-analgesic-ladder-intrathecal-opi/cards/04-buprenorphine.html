A partial mu-opioid agonist with a high receptor affinity and a ceiling effect on respiratory depression (safer in overdose than full agonists); used in chronic pain (patches) and opioid substitution therapy; its high receptor affinity can make it difficult to displace with naloxone in overdose and can complicate the use of other opioids for acute pain/anaesthesia if not managed appropriately (may need higher doses of a full agonist, or temporary conversion, depending on the clinical context).
