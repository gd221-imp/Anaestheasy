• Pain history: site, onset, character, radiation, associated features, exacerbating/relieving factors, temporal pattern
  • Impact on function: sleep, mood, activities of daily living, work, relationships
  • Psychological factors: anxiety, depression, catastrophising, fear-avoidance behaviour, prior trauma
  • Social factors: occupation, litigation/compensation status, social support, financial impact
  • Use of validated tools: numerical rating scales, Brief Pain Inventory, DN4/PainDETECT for neuropathic features, hospital anxiety and depression scale (HADS)
  • Review of prior treatments and response (pharmacological, interventional, physical, psychological)
  • Examination: identify a specific pain generator/nerve distribution where possible, assess for signs of CRPS/neuropathic features.
