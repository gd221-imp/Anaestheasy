Early mobilisation and physiotherapy/occupational therapy are central; pain team referral; pharmacological options include gabapentinoids, tricyclic antidepressants, bisphosphonates (for bone pain component), and in some cases a short course of oral corticosteroids in the acute inflammatory phase; sympathetic blocks (e.g. stellate ganglion block for upper limb) may help diagnostically/therapeutically in selected patients; psychological support given the significant impact on function and mood; spinal cord stimulation may be considered for refractory cases.
