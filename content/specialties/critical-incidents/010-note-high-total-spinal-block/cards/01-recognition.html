• Causes: excessive intrathecal dose or spread, unrecognised intrathecal/subdural epidural catheter, epidural top-up after a spinal (CSE), large epidural volumes
  • Ascending signs: rapidly rising block, hypotension and bradycardia (sympathetic block above T4 → cardioaccelerator fibres), nausea, anxiety, tingling/weakness of hands and arms (C6-8), difficulty breathing or speaking/weak voice, falling SpO2
  • Total spinal: apnoea, loss of consciousness, fixed dilated pupils (brainstem/cranial nerve block), profound hypotension/cardiac arrest
