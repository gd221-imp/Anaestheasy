1. Call for help, stop any epidural infusion, 100% O2, left uterine displacement if pregnant
  2. Airway: support breathing; if inadequate ventilation or loss of airway reflexes, induce (reduced dose - consciousness may be lost) and intubate with RSI
  3. Circulation: IV fluids, vasopressors (phenylephrine/metaraminol, ephedrine, adrenaline if severe), atropine/glycopyrronium for bradycardia; CPR if arrest (deliver the baby within 5 minutes if pregnant and no ROSC)
  4. Ventilate and sedate until the block regresses (usually 1-2hrs); reassure and debrief - awareness is possible
