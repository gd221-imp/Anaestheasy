1. Call for help, stop/remove all possible triggers (including chlorhexidine), declare an emergency; maintain the airway with 100% O2; elevate legs
  2. Adrenaline: IV 50mcg boluses (0.5ml of 1:10,000) titrated to effect in adults, or IM 0.5mg if no IV access; start an infusion if repeated boluses are needed
  3. IV crystalloid 20ml/kg (repeat as needed); CPR if SBP <50mmHg or arrest
  4. Refractory: noradrenaline/vasopressin, glucagon if on beta-blockers; consider ECMO
  5. Second line after stabilisation: hydrocortisone 200mg IV; chlorphenamine 10mg IV (once stable)
  6. Bronchospasm: salbutamol, magnesium, consider ketamine/volatile
