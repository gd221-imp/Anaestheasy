• Incidence ~1 in 10,000 anaesthetics (NAP6); mortality ~4%
  • Commonest causes (NAP6): antibiotics ~47-53% (teicoplanin and co-amoxiclav highest), NMBAs ~33% (suxamethonium and rocuronium), chlorhexidine ~9%, patent blue dye ~5%; older French data put NMBAs first (~58%)
  • Presenting features: hypotension (most common, often the only sign), bronchospasm, low EtCO2, tachycardia (or bradycardia), rash/angioedema (often absent at first)
