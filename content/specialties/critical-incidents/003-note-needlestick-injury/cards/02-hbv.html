Acute infection = mild in adults, ~20% have symptoms (N&V, fever, myalgia, jaundice). Chronic infection in ~5% of immunocompetent adults (~90% of neonates infected perinatally) → risk of cirrhosis, HCC, fatty liver.

Seroconversion after a needlestick: HBV 6-30% (if non-immune), HCV 0.5-2% (75-85% of those infected become chronic), HIV ~0.3%.

Post-exposure: risk assessment, first aid (wash, encourage bleeding, do not suck), report incident, baseline & follow-up bloods, consider PEP (HIV) within 1-2hrs, HBIG/vaccination as indicated.
