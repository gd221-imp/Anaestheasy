1. Ensure full neuromuscular block; extend the neck; 100% O2 via the upper airway
  2. Laryngeal handshake to identify the cricothyroid membrane
  3. Palpable membrane: transverse stab incision through the membrane with a size 10 scalpel, blade facing caudally; rotate 90° (sharp edge towards the feet); slide a bougie along the blade into the trachea; railroad a lubricated size 6.0 cuffed tube; inflate the cuff, ventilate, confirm with capnography, secure
  4. Impalpable membrane: 8-10cm vertical midline skin incision, blunt dissection to identify the larynx, then proceed as above

Post-event: monitor for complications, document, complete an airway alert and inform the patient and GP.
