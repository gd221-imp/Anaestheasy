• Plan A - facemask ventilation and tracheal intubation: optimise head/neck position, preoxygenate (high-flow nasal O2 for apnoeic oxygenation), maximum 3 attempts (+1 by a more experienced colleague), videolaryngoscopy, bougie, external laryngeal manipulation, check neuromuscular block. Declare failed intubation
  • Plan B - maintain oxygenation with a SAD: second-generation SAD, maximum 3 attempts. If oxygenating: stop and think - wake the patient, intubate via the SAD with a fibrescope, proceed without intubation (rarely), or tracheostomy/cricothyroidotomy
  • Plan C - final attempt at facemask ventilation: two-person technique with adjuncts and full paralysis. If ventilation is possible, wake the patient (sugammadex for rocuronium)
  • Plan D - CICO: declare CICO and proceed to front-of-neck access
