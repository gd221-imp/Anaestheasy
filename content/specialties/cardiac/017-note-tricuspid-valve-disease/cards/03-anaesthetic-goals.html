• TR: keep preload adequate, keep HR normal to high-normal, avoid rises in PVR (hypoxia, hypercarbia, acidosis, high airway pressures, light anaesthesia), support RV contractility (inodilators such as milrinone/dobutamine), maintain SVR to protect RV coronary perfusion
  • TS: maintain preload and sinus rhythm, avoid tachycardia (fixed inflow)
  • Manage the RV as for RV failure: noradrenaline/vasopressin for perfusion pressure, inhaled pulmonary vasodilators if PVR is high
  • CVP is high and reflects TR rather than filling; PA catheter placement may be difficult
