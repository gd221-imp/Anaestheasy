• Tricuspid regurgitation (TR): usually functional/secondary - RV dilatation from left-sided valve disease, pulmonary hypertension or RV failure; primary causes include endocarditis (IV drug use), rheumatic disease, carcinoid, Ebstein's anomaly and pacing leads
  • Tricuspid stenosis (TS): rare; rheumatic (almost always with mitral disease) or carcinoid
  • Symptoms are of right heart failure: fatigue, peripheral oedema, ascites, hepatomegaly; giant v waves in the JVP (TR)
