ECG, FBC -> exercise testing; echo/angiogram, right heart catheter, PFTs, PA catheter, ABG, carotid imaging (evaluation of haemodynamic reserve)

  • Dobutamine stress echo: baseline echo, then IV dobutamine, looking for reversible wall motion abnormality
  • CPET: dynamic, non-invasive assessment at rest and during exercise, determines functional capacity
  • Cardiac MRI: assesses ischaemia and perfusion
  • Measures: expired gas analysis, pulmonary flow, 12-lead ECG, SpO2, NIBP
  • Calculates VO2, VCO2, RER (=VCO2/VO2) -> a 9-panel plot; may be stopped early or ECG changes noted
  • Key CPET values: peak VO2, ventilatory efficiency (VE/VCO2), anaerobic threshold (roughly 11ml O2/kg/min)
