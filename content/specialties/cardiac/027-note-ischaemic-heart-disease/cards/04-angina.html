• Stable: fixed atheromatous stenosis of a coronary artery; with increased oxygen demand, the artery cannot vasodilate to meet demand
  • Unstable: plaque rupture -> reduced coronary blood flow = an acute coronary syndrome
  • Vasospastic: epicardial artery vasospasm, may be triggered by cocaine
  • Risk factors: age, family history, smoking, hypertension, high cholesterol, diabetes, obesity, sedentary lifestyle
  • Classification: 1) angina only during intense/prolonged exertion 2) during ordinary activity 3) only comfortable at rest 4) symptoms at rest
