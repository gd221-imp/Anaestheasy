'usual anaesthetic history' plus chest pain characteristics, associated symptoms, hypertension, MI history, orthopnoea, syncope, palpitations, exercise tolerance threshold

  • 1 MET = 3.5ml O2/kg/min (in a 70kg adult, roughly 40 watts); <4 METs suggests increased cardiovascular and neurological complications
  • 3 METs = walking slowly; 4 METs = gardening/raking leaves; ~5 METs = climbing 1 flight of stairs; 8 METs = climbing stairs rapidly; 10 METs = strenuous exercise. ESC uses '2 flights without stopping' as a practical >4 MET marker
  • Self-reported METs are unreliable - a structured tool (DASI) is preferred

Examination: pallor, oedema, raised JVP, HR, BP, auscultate
