TOE - severity assessed as jet width as a percentage of LVOT diameter - mild <25%, severe >=65%; regurgitant fraction - severe >=50% (Miller quotes >60%); pressure half-time on CW Doppler - mild >500ms, severe <200ms

Indications for surgery: severe AR with symptoms, or with LVEF <50%, or significant enlargement of the aortic root

Anaesthetic management: 'fast and loose' / 'full, fast, forward' - maintain heart rate, reduce SVR, maintain contractility and sinus rhythm
