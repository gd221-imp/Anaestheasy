• Most common cause: rheumatic heart disease; often presents in AF
  • A fixed cardiac output state, so decompensates with tachycardia, AF, or increased circulating volume
  • Echo: normal valve area 4-5cm2; symptoms on exertion once <2.5cm2, usually at rest once <1.5cm2; severe <1cm2
  • Assess ventricular function and pulmonary artery pressure - may persist/worsen after surgery
  • Dyspnoea severity does not always correlate well with the degree of stenosis
