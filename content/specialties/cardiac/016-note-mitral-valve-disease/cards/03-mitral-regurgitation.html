• Acute causes: papillary muscle rupture (flail leaflet), LV dilatation, annular dilatation, ischaemia
  • Chronic causes: degenerative prolapse, endocarditis, rheumatic fever
  • Sequelae: pulmonary hypertension, pulmonary oedema, AF
  • Echo: mild - jet area <4cm2 (or <20% of LA area); severe - large central jet >40% of LA area (>10cm2), vena contracta >=0.7cm, regurgitant volume >=60ml, regurgitant fraction >=50%, EROA >=0.4cm2; also assess pulmonary artery pressure
