• Method of measuring cerebral tissue oxygenation
  • Forehead sensor emits light at 760nm and 850nm (Beer-Lambert law); usable range approx. 650-900nm
  • Does not need pulsatile flow
  • Take a pre-induction baseline (typically around 60-75%) and interpret changes against the patient's own baseline; a fall of >20% from baseline is significant
  • Interpreting a fall in value (= reduced CBF or reduced blood O2 content):
  1. Exclude artefact - reposition probe, check headband not too tight
  2. Optimise DO2 - Hb, MAP >65, FiO2, PaCO2 4.5-5.5kPa
  3. Optimise VO2 - depth of anaesthesia, exclude seizures
