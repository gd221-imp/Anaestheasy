Reduced systemic inflammation, reduced coagulopathy, reduced need for re-exploration, reduced ventilator/ITU time

Fewer aortic microemboli gives a theoretical reduction in POCD/stroke, but trials show POCD at similar rates after on-pump, off-pump, stenting and non-cardiac surgery, and no difference in stroke, MI, renal failure or death at 5 years

Advantages (Surg): reduced risk of aortic damage, reduced risk of air embolism
