Procedures that avoid a traditional midline sternotomy

  • MIDCAB - only for some coronaries (can be on or off bypass)
  • VATS - MIMVS / miniAVR / AF ablation

Advantages: reduced length of stay, reduced pain, improved cosmesis

Disadvantages: difficult perfusion, DVT risk, risk of conversion, cerebral hypoperfusion, extended paddle time, endovascular balloon migration
