• Major complication rate 2-4% (usually SVC injury), risk increases with dwell time, number of leads, age, anticoagulation, dual-coil ICD leads, and vascular injury at a previous extraction site
  • Indication: over 50% due to infection
  • Pre-op: device details and imaging (echo/CT), FBC, coag, group and save/crossmatch
  • Intra-op: hybrid operating theatre, MDT surgical bailout plan, cell salvage/blood products, REBOA of the SVC; general anaesthesia with ETT, arterial line, CVC, defib pads, TOE, femoral access for emergency cardiopulmonary bypass in case of tamponade
  • Post-op: extubate, wean, warm, if infected - prolonged antibiotics and delayed reimplantation; extraction can improve or worsen tricuspid regurgitation
