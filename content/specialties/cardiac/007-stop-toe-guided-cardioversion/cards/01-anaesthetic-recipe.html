Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Duration of AF
  • TOE to exclude left atrial appendage thrombus

Examination (O/E)

  • airway assessment

Investigations (Ix)

  • Electrolytes (potassium and magnesium)

Optimise

  • anticoagulation
  • Fasting

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access
  • Defibrillator with pads
  • Airway equipment

Induction

  • Sedation or GA with propofol (titrated) shared with the TOE probe

Maintenance

  • Synchronised shock(s)
  • Watch for apnoea and hypotension

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Recover until awake
  • Continue anticoagulation
  • ECG to confirm rhythm

Surgical

Immediate

  • Oesophageal injury from TOE

Delayed

  • Stroke from embolism

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Apnoea and airway obstruction
  • Hypotension
  • Arrhythmias after shock

Delayed

  • Skin burns from pads
