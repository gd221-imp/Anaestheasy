1. Revascularisation - PCI if <12hr onset or ongoing chest pain, in cardiogenic shock-AMI, or after out-of-hospital cardiac arrest
  2. IABP - inserted percutaneously into the femoral artery, positioned distal to the left subclavian artery (confirmed on XR or echo) and above the renal arteries
  • Inflation triggered by ECG or the arterial pressure waveform; anticoagulation not routinely required
  • Complications: reduced renal perfusion / haemolysis / dyssynchrony / limb ischaemia
  • Contraindicated in: aortic regurgitation or dissection / uncontrolled sepsis / bleeding diathesis / tachyarrhythmia / severe PVD
  • Vascular access complications: injury / infection / poor limb perfusion / compartment syndrome
  • Incorrect device placement can cause limb / cerebral / spinal cord / mesenteric / renal ischaemia
  • Other complications: dissection / tamponade / thromboembolism / sepsis / haemolysis / gas embolism from balloon rupture
  • Indications: acute HF with shock, MI with acute LVF or shock, bridge to other therapy (e.g. CABG), adjunct/prophylaxis for PCI
