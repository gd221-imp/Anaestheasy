• Keep the HR up - SV is fixed, so avoid bradycardia
  • Maintain preload - avoid falls in venous return (vasodilatation, high intrathoracic pressure)
  • Maintain SVR and contractility; avoid myocardial depression
  • Arterial line before induction; cardiovascularly stable induction (e.g. ketamine or etomidate with opioid); vasopressors ready
  • Spontaneous ventilation helps venous return; if IPPV, use low tidal volumes and PEEP
