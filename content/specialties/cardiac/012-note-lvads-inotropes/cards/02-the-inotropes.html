• Inodilators: milrinone / enoximone (PDE-III inhibitors), dobutamine (beta-1), levosimendan (calcium sensitiser)
  • Levosimendan increases the sensitivity of troponin C to calcium and opens ATP-sensitive potassium channels, improving coronary perfusion and reducing preload/afterload
  • Inotropes: adrenaline / dopamine (beta-1 and 2, increases myocardial demand)
