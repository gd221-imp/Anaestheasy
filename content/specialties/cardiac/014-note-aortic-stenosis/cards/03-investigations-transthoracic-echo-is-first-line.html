• Resting transthoracic echo (TTE) is the first-line test; TOE is used intra-operatively or when TTE windows are inadequate
  • Valve area: normal 3-4cm2, mild >1.5cm2, moderate 1-1.5cm2, severe <1cm2 (indexed AVA <0.6cm2/m2 is also severe; older texts call <0.6cm2 'critical')
  • Mean transaortic gradient: mild <20mmHg, moderate 20-40mmHg, severe >=40mmHg
  • Peak aortic flow velocity: normal <=2.5m/s, mild 2.6-2.9, moderate 3-4, severe >=4m/s
  • Echo also assesses LV size/LVEF/fractional shortening, any MR/AR, and post-stenotic dilatation of the aorta
  • ECG: LVH pattern with ST depression and T wave inversion, usually in the lateral leads (V4-6/I/aVL) suggesting ischaemia
  • Angiography or CT/MRI of the aortic root
