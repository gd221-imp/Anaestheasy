• NYHA classification: 1) no symptoms 2) symptoms on exertion 3) symptoms with minimal exertion 4) symptoms at rest
  • Anaesthetic approach - pre-op: assess for METs <4 (reduced functional capacity), other comorbidities, anaemia, LFTs, echo, CXR, optimise pharmacological therapy
  • Intra-op: local or regional technique if possible, maintain sinus rhythm/preload/contractility, avoid tachycardia and high SVR
  • Post-op: HDU, oxygen, ACEi reintroduction, careful fluid management
