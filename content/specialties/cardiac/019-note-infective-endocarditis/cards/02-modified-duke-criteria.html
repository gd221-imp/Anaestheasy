Diagnosis needs 2 major, or 1 major + 3 minor, or 5 minor

  • Major: 2 positive blood cultures or echo evidence
  • Minor: risk factor, fever >38C, emboli, immunological signs, microbiology not meeting major criteria

Management: MDT input, antibiotics for 4-6 weeks; surgery if embolic event with vegetation >10mm, ongoing severe valve dysfunction, or vegetation >15mm
