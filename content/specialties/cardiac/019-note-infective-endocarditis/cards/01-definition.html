A rare infection of the endocardium

Organisms: staph aureus or coagulase-negative staph, strep viridans/bovis, enterococci, HACEK group or fungi

Can be sub-acute: presents with night sweats, weight loss, fatigue, peripheral stigmata, malaise

Also: cardiogenic shock, septic emboli, splenomegaly, septic skin lesions

Risk factors: bicuspid aortic valve / MVP / rheumatic valve disease / prosthetic valve / ICD; IVDU, indwelling long lines, immunocompromise
