Venous drainage from RA/IVC -> venous reservoir -> suction pump -> oxygenator -> heat exchanger -> arterial filter (bubble trap) -> arterial cannula in the ascending aorta -> patient. The aortic cross-clamp is not part of the circuit: it sits on the ascending aorta between the arterial cannula and the aortic valve, isolating the heart. Cardioplegia is delivered via a separate cardioplegia pump.
