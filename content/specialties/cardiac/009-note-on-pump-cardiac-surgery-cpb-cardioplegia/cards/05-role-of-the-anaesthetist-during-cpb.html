• Anticoagulation: heparin 300-400u/kg (corrected body weight), target ACT >400-480s (normal 80-120s) or 3x baseline, TXA, recheck every 30 mins
  • Temperature: active or passive cooling, and normalised before coming off bypass
  • Anaesthesia: maintained via TCI or a volatile agent through the CPB circuit
  • Acid-base/electrolytes: normalised prior to coming off bypass
  • Coming off bypass: confirm rate and rhythm coordination, lungs re-expanded, heart progressively filled, heart rhythm (may require pacing)
  • Protamine reverses heparin; watch for adverse reactions - pulmonary vasoconstriction, anaphylactoid reaction, systemic hypotension, bleeding
