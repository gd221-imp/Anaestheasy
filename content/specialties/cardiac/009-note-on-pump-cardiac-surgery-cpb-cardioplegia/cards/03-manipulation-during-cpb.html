• Gas exchange: membrane oxygenation via the I:E gradient - increased FiO2 raises PaO2, increased flow lowers PaCO2
  • MAP: set by CPB flow rate (SVR can be altered pharmacologically)
  • Temperature: via conduction at the heat exchanger
  • Haematocrit: volume added at the reservoir or removed by ultrafiltration
  • Acid-base: 'pH-stat' corrects for temperature with a constant pH, so rising CO2 causes cerebral vasodilation; 'alpha-stat' does not correct for temperature, allowing alkalosis during cooling so autoregulation (and therefore CBF) is preserved
