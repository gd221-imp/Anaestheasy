• Simple: repaired (e.g. VSD) or unrepaired (small ASD); cardiology follow-up, non-specialist centre appropriate
  • Moderate: VSD with associated anomaly / moderate pulmonary stenosis / unrepaired PDA / repaired ToF / cardiac arrhythmia; discuss with specialist centre
  • Severe: adult cyanotic heart disease / single ventricle / corrected TGA / Ebstein's anomaly / double outlet ventricle; cardiology follow-up required, specialist centre

Other patient factors to consider: psychological factors, additional anatomy, antiarrhythmics/negative inotropy of general anaesthesia
