Counsel pre-conception and give individualised risk assessment:

  • Pulmonary hypertension: maternal mortality classically 30-50% (older series; recent series lower but still substantial) - mWHO IV, pregnancy not advised
  • Severe AS is a significant risk
  • Dilated aortic root in Marfan's is a significant risk
  • Cyanosis (SaO2 <85%) increases the risk of fetal loss
  • Heart failure with EF <40% is a significant risk
  • Management: specialist MDT, elective LSCS, low-dose regional technique, GA if needed, consider anticoagulation
  • Intra-op deterioration can occur from CVS instability -> reduced SVR (worsens right-to-left shunt) or hypoxia (Fontan circulation is very preload-dependent)
  • Arrhythmias can arise from scar tissue or chronic chamber enlargement -> AF or other arrhythmia
  • VTE risk is increased, particularly with a right-to-left shunt (Fontan physiology increases this risk further)
