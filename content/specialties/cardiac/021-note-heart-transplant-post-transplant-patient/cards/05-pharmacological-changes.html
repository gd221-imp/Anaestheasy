Atropine and glycopyrronium have no vagolytic effect; indirect-acting sympathomimetics have unpredictable effects; GTN has no reflex tachycardia; nitrates/ACE inhibitors have no reflex tachycardia; digoxin has an inotropic but not chronotropic effect; there is upregulation of receptors so adrenaline is more potent (start at a lower dose, e.g. 0.5mcg) and isoprenaline has increased potency
