• Aetiology of original cardiac failure; consider widespread atherosclerosis or other congenital abnormalities
  • Evidence of graft function: echo/ECG/exercise test/orthopnoea; endomyocardial biopsy if rejection suspected; cardiac catheter if concern for cardiac allograft vasculopathy
  • Check for a permanent pacemaker
  • Other organ dysfunction: renal (pre-transplant chronic kidney disease from drugs), liver dysfunction, lung disease
  • Bloods: FBC (bone marrow suppression), U&Es
  • Perioperative plan for transplant medications, and associated complications (steroid-induced adrenal suppression, diabetes, or ciclosporin-related hypertension/renal impairment)
  • Consider active infection - atypical organisms with immunosuppression: CMV, toxoplasmosis, PJP, legionella, candida albicans, HSV
