Elevated mean PAP >20mmHg at rest

  • Minimised by avoiding acidosis, hypoxia, and hypercarbia, and by using pulmonary vasodilators
  • Types: 1) pulmonary arterial hypertension (normal PCWP, mean PAP >20mmHg at rest) 2) with left heart failure 3) with lung disease 4) with chronic thromboembolic disease 5) multifactorial
  • Symptoms: dyspnoea, angina, syncope on exertion, right heart failure signs
  • Treatment (specialist centre): anticoagulation / diuretics / digoxin, ambulatory O2 / pulmonary vasodilators, escalating to transplant
  • WHO functional classification: 1) symptom-free 2) normal activity causes symptoms 3) activity greatly limited 4) symptoms at rest
