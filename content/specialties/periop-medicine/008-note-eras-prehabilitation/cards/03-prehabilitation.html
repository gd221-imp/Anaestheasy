Multimodal process of improving a patient's functional status pre-op to enable them to withstand upcoming stressors.

  1. Optimisation - nutrition → reduced infection, improved wound healing, reduced organ dysfunction, increased recovery, increased muscle bulk.
  2. Exercise programme - increased stroke volume, increased muscle mitochondria, increased pulmonary blood flow, increased muscle strength, increased capillary density in muscle.
  3. Carbohydrate preloading - reduced insulin resistance, reduced catabolism, reduced catabolism.
  4. Psychological intervention - procedural instruction, relaxation/support/cognitive intervention.

But... needs time & people, variable benefit, better for high than frail, must not delay surgery.
