Assess risk & fitness for surgery - patient selection, manage chronic disease, patient education, consent.

Optimisation - smoking cessation >4/52, anaemia correction, MRSA/MDRO screening, medication advice, premedication.

Reduce physiological effects of fasting - reduced pre-op fasting (carbohydrate drink up to 2hrs), goal-directed IV fluid therapy, staggered admission.

Minimally invasive techniques - reduced PONV, reduced drains, oblique/transverse wound, pre-warming.

Clear, structured post-op care - multimodal analgesia/early mobilisation/early nutrition (regional, avoid long-acting opioids).
