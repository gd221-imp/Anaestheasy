• Identify and optimise in pre-assessment: HbA1c within 3 months - elective surgery is usually deferred if HbA1c >69mmol/mol (8.5%) unless benefit outweighs risk; check complications (renal, cardiac, autonomic neuropathy - gastroparesis, silent ischaemia)
  • First on the list where possible; minimise starvation; individualised written plan for each drug
  • Short starvation (one missed meal): modify usual medication (e.g. reduce long-acting basal insulin by ~20% the day before; omit short-acting insulin with the missed meal)
  • Long starvation (more than one missed meal) or poorly controlled: variable rate IV insulin infusion (VRIII), continuing long-acting basal insulin at around 80% of the usual dose
