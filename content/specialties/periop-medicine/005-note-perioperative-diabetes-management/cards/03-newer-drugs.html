• SGLT2 inhibitors (-gliflozins): risk of euglycaemic DKA - omit the day before and the day of surgery (current UK guidance), check ketones peri-operatively, restart only when eating and drinking normally
  • GLP-1 receptor agonists (e.g. semaglutide): delay gastric emptying - risk of residual gastric contents and aspiration; follow current UK guidance (assess risk, consider gastric ultrasound/RSI; omission may be advised)
  • Metformin: usually continue if only one missed meal and eGFR normal; omit if contrast or AKI risk
  • Insulin pumps and DKA - see the DKA note
