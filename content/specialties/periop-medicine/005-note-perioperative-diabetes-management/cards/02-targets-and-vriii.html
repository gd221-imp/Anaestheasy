• CBG target 6-10mmol/L (4-12 acceptable); check hourly intra-op and in recovery
  • VRIII with a substrate fluid containing glucose and potassium (e.g. 0.45% saline + 5% glucose + 0.15% KCl), following the local sliding scale; check potassium
  • Hypoglycaemia (<4mmol/L): treat promptly (e.g. 10% glucose 150-200ml IV) and recheck after 15 mins
  • Convert back to the usual regimen when eating and drinking - give subcutaneous insulin with a meal and stop the VRIII 30-60 mins later
