Aims to determine functional capacity & aid risk prediction/resource allocation/identify co-morbid optimisation opportunities.

Cycle ergometer, rapid gas analysis, pneumotachograph, continuous ECG monitoring.

Derived variables: VO2, VCO2, RER → 9-panel plot.

VO2 max = maximum O2 consumption during test.

Anaerobic threshold: identified by the V-slope method (the inflection point when VCO2 is plotted against VO2) or the ventilatory equivalents method (VE/VO2 starts to rise while VE/VCO2 stays flat - the nadir of VE/VO2) = VO2 > DO2 → lactic acid produced → buffered by bicarbonate → increased CO2. Normal AT >11mL/kg/min.

VE/VCO2 = lung's ability to excrete CO2.
