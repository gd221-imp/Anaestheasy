History, examination, investigations.

Risk scores = simple but population-based, not individualised.

ASA grade: I normal, II mild systemic disease, III severe systemic disease, IV constant threat to life, V moribund.

Lee's Revised Cardiac Risk Index: original (Miller) major cardiac complication rates - 0 predictors 0.4%, 1 predictor 1.0%, 2 predictors 2.4%, >=3 predictors 5.4%. The 2017 Canadian (Duceppe) recalibration, which includes troponin-detected MI, gives 0 = 3.9%, 1 = 6%, 2 = 10.1%, >=3 = 15%.
