Acute MI/unstable angina, uncontrolled arrhythmia, syncope, active infective endocarditis, acute myocarditis, severe AS, uncontrolled heart failure/asthma/AAA.

1 MET (VO2) = 3.5mL O2/kg/min = resting.

3 MET = walking. 4 MET = gardening (~5 MET = 1 flight of stairs; 2 flights without stopping is the usual practical >4 MET marker and the minimum for lung resection). 8 MET = jogging/climbing stairs rapidly.
