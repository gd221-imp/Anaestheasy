Indications: 2:1/3° AV block, alternating bundle branch block, syncope with bifascicular block, symptomatic sinus node disease.

Capture = successful depolarisation of myocardium after pacing stimulus. Sensitivity = ability to detect intrinsic cardiac activity.

Pre-op: check for asynchronous pacing if significantly PPM-dependent, or diathermy above umbilicus.

Intra-op: bipolar diathermy > monopolar; if monopolar, apply diathermy plate 15cm away, avoid duration diathermy.

Pacemaker syndrome → loss of AV synchrony → ↓cardiac output → Rx = switch to DDD mode.
