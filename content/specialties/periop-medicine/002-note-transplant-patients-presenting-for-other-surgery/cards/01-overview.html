24yo male, previous heart transplant, presenting for other surgery.

Heart transplant → denervated heart: resting HR 90-110 (no vagal tone); no reflex response to laryngoscopy, hypotension or hypovolaemia (rate rises slowly via circulating catecholamines) - so preload-dependent: maintain filling. Indirect agents (atropine, glycopyrronium, ephedrine, neostigmine's chronotropic effect) are ineffective or unpredictable - use direct-acting drugs (adrenaline, isoprenaline) or pacing. Accelerated coronary vasculopathy causes silent ischaemia. Continue immunosuppression; strict asepsis.
