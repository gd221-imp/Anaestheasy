Minimum 12/12 dual antiplatelet therapy (DAPT) for ACS, and 6/12 for stable angina.

Elective non-cardiac surgery: ideally delay 6 months after elective PCI with a drug-eluting stent (12 months after ACS); bare-metal stent 30 days. Time-sensitive surgery: at least 1 month of DAPT (Miller suggests 3-6 months if the delay carries less risk), then continue aspirin alone - if emergent, bridge with tirofiban/cangrelor. Older texts advised avoiding surgery for 6 weeks after PCI.

Neurosurgery, intraocular, complex spinal, transbronchial procedures will want to stop aspirin too.

N.B. PRECISE-DAPT score: 10% of patients have increased bleeding risk after stopping DAPT.
