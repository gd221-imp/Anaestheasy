History - group A strep? current meds? usual & recent chemotherapy? any associated conditions? examination - ?pathogens/immunosuppressant, ?malnutrition.

Investigations: FBC, U&E, ECG, TTE, group & save.

INTRA-OP: antibiotic prophylaxis timed before incision (follow local guidelines); asepsis and normothermia; arterial line ± CVC if septic or haemodynamically unstable; trained assistant/consultant; RSI if indicated; careful positioning; steroid cover if on long-term steroids.

POST-OP: source control, review cultures and antibiotics (follow local guidelines), avoid NSAIDs if at risk of AKI, care with fluid balance, early mobilisation.
