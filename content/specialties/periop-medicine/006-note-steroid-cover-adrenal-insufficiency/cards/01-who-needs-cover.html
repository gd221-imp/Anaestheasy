• Patients with primary adrenal insufficiency (Addison's), secondary (pituitary) insufficiency, or on long-term glucocorticoids: prednisolone >=5mg/day (or equivalent) for more than 4 weeks, within the last 3 months
  • Also consider high-dose inhaled/topical steroids and those on drugs that interact (e.g. CYP3A4 inhibitors with inhaled fluticasone)
