Around half of twin deliveries are by LSCS, and many of the remainder convert to LSCS intrapartum

The delivery room is often not large enough for the full team needed (2 neonatal teams, 2 midwives, etc) - consider whether delivery in theatre or an HDU labour room is more appropriate

An epidural for labour provides good analgesic cover and can help avoid a GA if conversion to LSCS is needed

For LSCS: a spinal or an epidural top-up can be used, with awareness of increased risk of hypotension and hypoxaemia
