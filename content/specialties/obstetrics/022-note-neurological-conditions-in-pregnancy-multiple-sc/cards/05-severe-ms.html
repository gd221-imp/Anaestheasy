Caution with suxamethonium (hyperkalaemia risk) and increased sensitivity to non-depolarising neuromuscular blockers; autonomic dysfunction can cause hypotension on induction, so plan for careful fluid replacement and consider risk of bulbar dysfunction/aspiration. Epidural techniques may reduce some of these risks. Watch for reflux (can coexist with pre-eclampsia) and be mindful of increased risk of postoperative pulmonary complications.
