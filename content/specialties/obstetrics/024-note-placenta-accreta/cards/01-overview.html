= abnormal implantation of the placenta into the myometrium or beyond, without a normal decidual interface

FIGO classification: Grade 1 - placenta accreta; Grade 2 - placenta increta; Grade 3 - placenta percreta, subdivided by extent (a - to the uterine serosa, b - to the bladder, c - to more distal pelvic organs)

Diagnosis: ultrasound +/- MRI

Risk factors: previous LSCS, multiple pregnancy, endometrial surgery, IVF, maternal age >35, and prior uterine artery embolisation
