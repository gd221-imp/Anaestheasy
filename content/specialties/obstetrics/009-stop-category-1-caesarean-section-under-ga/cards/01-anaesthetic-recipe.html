Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Why GA: no time for neuraxial, failed block, contraindication, maternal choice
  • left uterine displacement

Examination (O/E)

  • Rapid airway assessment

Investigations (Ix)

  • Antacid (sodium citrate) and H2 antagonist

Optimise

  • announce the plan and failed intubation plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Ramped position, high-flow nasal oxygen for pre-oxygenation
  • Videolaryngoscope, smaller tube (6.0–7.0), second-generation supraglottic airway
  • Obstetric failed intubation algorithm on the wall

Induction

  • Pre-oxygenate to ETO2 above 0.9
  • RSI with propofol or thiopental and rocuronium or suxamethonium
  • Can consider an RSI with alfentanil 1–2 mg, propofol about 180 mg (second syringe ready) and rocuronium 1.2 mg/kg; videolaryngoscope
  • Can consider the category 1 sign-in and time-out with the abdomen prepared and draped before induction, so incision follows intubation straight away
  • Can consider thiopental and suxamethonium (used in most obstetric GAs in the DREAMY study), but use the drugs you know best

Maintenance

  • Volatile at adequate MAC (awareness risk)
  • depth monitoring
  • Oxytocin after delivery
  • opioid at induction (e.g. alfentanil/remifentanil) - tell the neonatal team; local practice here: only for pre-eclampsia/PIH, otherwise after cord clamping
  • Can consider morphine 10 mg after delivery; dexamethasone 6.6 mg and ondansetron 4 mg
  • Can consider sugammadex for reversal (likely needed)

Emergence

  • Extubate awake, head up or left lateral
  • Suction before extubation

Post-op

Complications

  • Extubate awake, head up, left lateral
  • Debrief
  • ask about awareness

Surgical

Immediate

  • Haemorrhage
  • Bladder injury

Delayed

  • Infection
  • VTE

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Failed intubation and CICO
  • Aspiration
  • Awareness
  • Neonatal depression from drugs

Delayed

  • Post-op pain without neuraxial opioid
  • Sore throat

Analgesia

  • TAP or quadratus lumborum blocks, or wound infiltration
  • multimodal analgesia
  • Can consider morphine 1–10 mg in recovery; paracetamol 1 g QDS, ibuprofen 400 mg QDS, dihydrocodeine 30 mg QDS, Oramorph as needed
  • Can consider lactulose, ondansetron and naloxone as needed

Destination

  • Obstetric HDU or labour ward recovery
  • Follow-up visit; ask about awareness
