Massive obstetric haemorrhage transfusion ratio (approximate): 4 units RBC : 4 units FFP : 2 units platelets, plus calcium, von Willebrand factor/cryoprecipitate, and TXA as guided by results

Defined as blood loss >1.5L, a haemoglobin fall >4g/dL, or an acute transfusion of more than 4 units RBC

The uteroplacental unit normally receives blood from the uterine and ovarian arteries, rising from under 5% of normal cardiac output to around 10% of cardiac output at term (roughly 700-900ml/min, about 80% of it to the intervillous space)
