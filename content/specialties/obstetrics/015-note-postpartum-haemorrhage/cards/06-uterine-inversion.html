A rare, life-threatening complication of the 3rd stage - complete inversion is when the fundus passes through the cervix

Management: 1. Resuscitation with blood products, and no further attempt to remove the placenta until the uterus is repositioned

  1. Replacement: manual reduction first; if unsuccessful, achieve cardiovascular stability then consider GA with uterine relaxants (e.g. magnesium sulphate, GTN, or a beta-2 agonist) to allow repositioning, followed by uterotonics once corrected
