Common peroneal nerve: compressed at the fibular head by lithotomy stirrups - causes foot drop and numbness over the lateral leg and dorsum of the foot (the 1st/2nd toe web space is its deep peroneal branch)

Femoral nerve (L2-4): affects contraction of the quadriceps

Lateral cutaneous nerve of thigh (meralgia paraesthetica, from L2-3): affected by hip flexion in lithotomy

Lumbosacral trunk (L4-5): can be compressed by the fetal head against the pelvic brim, causing unilateral foot drop (often with numbness over the lateral calf/dorsum of the foot)

Obturator nerve (L2-4): compressed by the fetal head or forceps - weak hip adduction and numbness over the medial thigh
