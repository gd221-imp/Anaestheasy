MDT input with HDU-level care on the delivery suite, and expedited delivery once the mother is stabilised

Specific: regional anaesthesia is usually contraindicated due to coagulopathy; fetal mortality is increased; liver and renal function can continue to worsen even after delivery, into the early postpartum period

Supportive: IV access, judicious IV fluids, IV glucose, TEG-guided correction of coagulopathy, and ventilatory support for encephalopathy/ARDS, plus RRT if needed
