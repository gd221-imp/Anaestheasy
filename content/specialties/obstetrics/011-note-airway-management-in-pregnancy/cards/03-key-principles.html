Maximise oxygenation at induction - head-up and ramped positioning, pre-oxygenate to an end-tidal FeO2 >=0.9, consider HFNO, and use bag-mask ventilation after neuromuscular blockade if needed (keeping peak inspiratory pressure under around 20cmH2O)

Consent should cover common and minor side effects as well as rare but devastating complications - the numerical risk of complications is well documented for regional but less so for GA

Proceed vs wake - factors to weigh include maternal condition, fetal compromise, the anaesthetist's experience, degree of obesity, degree of surgical complexity, aspiration risk, feasibility of an alternative anaesthetic technique or awake intubation, availability of a rescue airway device, and the degree of compromise to the maternal airway after bag-mask ventilation or rescue device attempts
