In theatre with left lateral tilt, pre-oxygenate (3 mins tidal breathing or 4-8 vital capacity breaths), thiopentone 4-6mg/kg and suxamethonium 1.5mg/kg, antibiotics (follow local guidelines; e.g. a cephalosporin plus metronidazole), cricoid pressure (around 20N), give an opioid at induction (e.g. alfentanil 10-20mcg/kg or remifentanil 1mcg/kg) to blunt the pressor response - current UK OAA/DAS practice; neonatal effects are mild and transient, so tell the neonatal team (omitting the opioid is itself an awareness/aspiration-bundle risk). Local practice here: opioid at induction only for pre-eclampsia/PIH, to blunt the hypertensive response to laryngoscopy - otherwise after cord clamping, volatile maintenance +/- nitrous oxide, and extubate awake

Post-op: paracetamol +/- NSAIDs, morphine +/- PCA, consider a TAP block, and TEDS plus VTE prophylaxis
