Order of loss as a block develops (motor preserved longest): B fibres (sympathetic) -> C fibres (cold) -> A-delta (pinprick/sharp pain) -> A-beta (light touch, the last sensory modality) -> A-alpha (motor, most resistant). So the block to cold extends higher than to pinprick, and pinprick higher than to touch

Symptoms of a high (ascending) block: difficulty breathing, nausea/vomiting, tingling or weakness in the hands/arms, feeling faint

Signs: irregular or tachycardic pulse, reduced respiratory rate, obtundation, a high block on re-testing, objective weakness
