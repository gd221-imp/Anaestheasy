The increased risk of accidental awareness during general anaesthesia (AAGA) in obstetrics (around 1 in 670) should be communicated as part of consent. Recommendations include ensuring an adequate induction dose, rapid onset and adequacy of the volatile agent, using nitrous oxide, and having an additional IV agent available if signs of inadequate anaesthesia appear; a failed regional technique is itself a risk factor for AAGA, so drug errors should be minimised.
