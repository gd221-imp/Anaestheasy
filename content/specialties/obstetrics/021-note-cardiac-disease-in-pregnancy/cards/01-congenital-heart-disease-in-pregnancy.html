Low-risk: mild pulmonary stenosis, PDA, MVP, a repaired PDA/VSD/ASD, or mild residual damage after repair - can be managed at a non-specialist centre

High-risk: a mechanical valve, a systemic right ventricle, Fontan circulation, unrepaired cyanotic heart disease, pulmonary hypertension, LVOT obstruction, or aortopathy - needs specialist centre input
