Avoid tachycardia: beta-blocker use, early epidural, avoid ephedrine/oxytocin boluses

Avoid raised pulmonary artery pressure: assisted (rather than prolonged maternal pushing) delivery, nitrous oxide use, keep the patient centrally positioned, avoid hypoxia/hypercapnia/acidosis

Avoid arrhythmia: avoid excessive local anaesthetic doses

Maintain euvolaemia: careful fluid replacement, avoid aortocaval compression

Manage afterload: a slow-onset epidural, or an alpha-agonist infusion if needed
