First choice is generally spontaneous labour with a vaginal delivery under low-dose regional anaesthesia, avoiding sudden preload/afterload changes

Consider: induction of labour, early venous access, telemetry/HDU monitoring, early epidural siting, avoiding hypovolaemia, cautious use of oxytocin (given slowly if used at all), and avoiding aortocaval compression

Uterotonic options: misoprostol 0.2-0.8mg (PV/PR/PO/SL), or oxytocin given as a slow 2 unit bolus over 10 mins followed by an infusion if needed
