Key points: poor tolerance of the increased blood volume and heart rate of pregnancy, and risk of atrial enlargement; a valve area under 1.5cm2 should prompt counselling against pregnancy

Triad of risk: maternal (pulmonary hypertension, right heart failure, VTE, AF) and fetal (premature birth, IUGR, fetal death)

Percutaneous mitral commissurotomy can be considered after 20 weeks if NYHA class III/IV and/or pulmonary artery pressure >50mmHg

The leading risk is pulmonary oedema at the time of delivery
