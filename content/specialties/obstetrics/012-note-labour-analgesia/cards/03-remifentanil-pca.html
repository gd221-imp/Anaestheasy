Bolus around 20-40mcg with a 2min lockout - onset around 1min, peak 2-3mins; crosses the placenta but is rapidly metabolised by fetal esterases

Contraindications: opioid allergy, parenteral opioid within the last 4hrs, gestation <36 weeks, intrauterine death, or severe cardiac/respiratory disease

Indications: patient preference, a structural abnormality of the lumbar spine, coagulopathy, thrombocytopenia, local sepsis, or an LA allergy/intolerance

Advantages: potent, no histamine release, rapid metabolism by esterases, no active metabolite, and a short context-sensitive half-time (around 3 mins)

Requirements: 1:1 midwife care, a dedicated IV line, oxygen via nasal cannula/facemask, only the patient can administer the dose, minimal neonatal respiratory depression, a clear guideline/patient leaflet and staff training, patient satisfaction auditing, and incident reporting
