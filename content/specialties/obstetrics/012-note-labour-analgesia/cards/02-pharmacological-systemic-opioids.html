Pethidine 1mg/kg IV or IM (can be given under midwife exemption) - delays gastric emptying, accumulates with an active metabolite (norpethidine, half-life 13-23hrs), maternal half-life 2.5-3hrs and up to 3x longer in the neonate

Morphine 2-5mg IV or 5-10mg IM - relatively poor analgesia in labour

Diamorphine 5-10mg IM - quicker onset than morphine, though still not great analgesia

Fentanyl 200mcg SC or 50mcg intranasal - around 800x more potent than pethidine, half-life 3-8hrs

Patient-controlled systemic opioid (pethidine/morphine/fentanyl) - onset/offset is often too slow, with sedation risk
