Epidural: local anaesthetics block fast voltage-gated sodium channels, and can increase the duration of the 1st and 2nd stages of labour; aim for a sensory block with minimal motor block

Bupivacaine causes dose-dependent motor block (lower concentrations reduce cardiotoxicity risk)

Patient-controlled epidural analgesia (PCEA) reduces motor block, increases satisfaction, and reduces total local anaesthetic used

Ropivacaine has reduced potency but an improved safety profile compared with bupivacaine

PDPH risk is around 1 in 100 with epidural technique

Lidocaine can develop tachyphylaxis with repeated dosing

Skin prep: typically 0.5% chlorhexidine in 70% alcohol
