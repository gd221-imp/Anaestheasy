Spinal (or CSE, 'to gain control'): uses a small volume of low-dose local anaesthetic

PDPH risk is around 1 in 300 (lower than epidural, given the smaller needle)

Intrathecal opioid options: morphine (lower lipid solubility, greater CSF spread, higher respiratory depression risk); diamorphine (higher lipid solubility, reduced CSF spread, shorter duration); fentanyl or sufentanil (intrathecally sufentanil 2-10mcg is roughly equivalent to fentanyl 25mcg, i.e. around 5-10x more potent; epidural mixes use fentanyl 1-3mcg/ml or sufentanil 0.1-0.5mcg/ml - systemically sufentanil is quoted as 5-10x fentanyl too, not 25x)
