Atosiban - a competitive oxytocin receptor antagonist

Beta-2 agonists (salbutamol, terbutaline, ritodrine) - side effects include tachycardia, agitation, tremor, and bronchodilation

Calcium channel blockers (e.g. nifedipine) - side effect of arteriolar vasodilation

GTN - nitric oxide donor causing uterine relaxation

Volatile anaesthetic agents also have a direct, dose-related uterine relaxant effect
