Liaise with the obstetric team - consider fetal monitoring needs, tocolysis, and antenatal steroids for fetal lung maturation if preterm delivery is a possibility

Avoid NSAIDs (risk of premature ductus arteriosus closure)

Use head-up tilt to reduce reflux risk and displace the uterus off the great vessels

RSI with cricoid pressure, adequate pre-oxygenation (around 3 mins)

Be aware that neostigmine can affect fetal heart rate, while sugammadex binds progesterone - both need consideration, though neither is an absolute contraindication

If laparoscopy is used: keep intra-abdominal pressure under around 12mmHg, control maternal EtCO2 closely, use an open (Hasson) technique to enter the abdomen, and minimise steep Trendelenburg positioning

If breastfeeding: avoid codeine, tramadol, and aspirin (Reye's syndrome and respiratory depression risk)
