Uteroplacental perfusion: can be reduced by a fall in maternal MAP under GA, or by aortocaval compression

Hyper/hypocarbia: failure to maintain adequate airway and ventilation risks fetal hypercarbia; conversely, overzealous hyperventilation can cause uterine artery constriction and fetal hypocarbia, and shifts the oxy-haemoglobin dissociation curve

Neurocognitive outcomes: some studies raise a small signal of concern (around 3-8% absolute risk difference in some analyses), possibly related to induced neuronal apoptosis - continuous CTG monitoring is often used where the fetus is viable
