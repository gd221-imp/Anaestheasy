Blood is close to body temperature, avoids the need for crossmatching, maintains normal 2,3-DPG levels, avoids allogeneic transfusion risks, and is generally good value

Disadvantages: risk of maternal Rh alloimmunisation (in obstetric use), loss of leucocytes/platelets/clotting factors, risk of a slower infusion rate and increased bradykinin effect causing hypotension, electrolyte disturbance, risk of venous air embolism, risk of bacterial contamination, need for staff training, and risk of fluid overload if used in excess
