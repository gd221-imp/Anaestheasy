= when fetal material enters the maternal bloodstream, causing sudden hypotension, hypoxaemia, DIC, arrhythmias, dyspnoea, fetal distress, and cardiac arrest

Risk factors: induction of labour, oxytocin use, LSCS/instrumented delivery are the most definite associations; increasing age (>35), male fetus, multiparity, placental abruption, and intrauterine fetal death are possible associations
