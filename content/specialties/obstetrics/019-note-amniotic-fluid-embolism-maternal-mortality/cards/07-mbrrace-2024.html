Maternal mortality around 13.6 per 100,000 pregnancies

Leading causes: 1. VTE 2. COVID 3. Cardiac disease 4. Mental health 5. Sepsis 6. Neurological 7. Other 8. Haemorrhage

Increased risk in Black and Asian women, in more deprived areas, in those over 35, overweight, and with multiple disadvantages

Recommendations included: ensuring access to VTE risk assessment and prescribing for all, reviewing weight-based anaphylaxis algorithms, having clear recommendations for imaging in pregnancy with cancer, and formal interpreter use with documentation of language needs
