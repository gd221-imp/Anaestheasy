Regional anaesthesia is often preferred but may be contraindicated by sepsis or abnormal clotting

Simple analgesia (paracetamol +/- NSAIDs) can be used even after fetal death, but caution is needed if coagulopathy develops

Opioids (IM/IV) - some concern about uteroplacental transfer, though this is less relevant once the fetus has died; PCA morphine/fentanyl can be used - remifentanil is used with more caution given less experience and reports of adverse events in this context
