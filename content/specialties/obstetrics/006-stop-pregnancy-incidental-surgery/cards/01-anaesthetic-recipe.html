Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Delay elective surgery
  • second trimester preferred if urgent

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway (more difficult in pregnancy)
  • Gestation; fetal heart rate

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E, group and save
  • Fetal heart rate before and after surgery

Optimise

  • Obstetric input, fetal monitoring plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Left lateral tilt after 20 weeks, antacid prophylaxis

Induction

  • RSI if GA after around 18 weeks

Maintenance

  • Regional where possible
  • Avoid hypotension and hypoxia

Emergence

  • Extubate awake, left lateral
  • Fetal heart rate check

Post-op

Complications

  • Fetal monitoring, VTE prophylaxis

Surgical

Immediate

  • Surgical complications of the underlying procedure
  • Uterine irritation

Delayed

  • Pre-term labour
  • Miscarriage

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Aspiration
  • Aortocaval compression and hypotension
  • Maternal hypoxia (reduced FRC)

Delayed

  • Fetal compromise
  • VTE

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, opioids (short courses)
  • Avoid NSAIDs, especially after 30 weeks
  • Regional techniques where possible

Destination

  • Ward with obstetric review; fetal monitoring plan
