Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • activate the obstetric haemorrhage protocol if bleeding
  • Existing epidural? Last oral intake

Examination (O/E)

  • observations and shock index

Investigations (Ix)

  • Estimate blood loss and look for ongoing bleeding
  • Group and crossmatch, FBC, coagulation

Optimise

  • aspiration prophylaxis

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Two large-bore cannulae, warmed fluids and blood
  • Uterotonics (oxytocin, ergometrine, carboprost) and GTN available for uterine relaxation
  • Cell salvage if available

Induction

  • Haemodynamically stable: top-up the epidural or give a spinal (T10 block enough)
  • Unstable or major bleeding: RSI GA

Maintenance

  • Oxytocin infusion after removal
  • GTN or volatile if the uterus needs relaxing

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Follow local guidelines. Antibiotics
  • Monitor bleeding, uterine tone and urine output
  • Thromboprophylaxis once bleeding is controlled
  • debrief

Surgical

Immediate

  • Postpartum haemorrhage
  • Uterine perforation or inversion
  • Retained products

Delayed

  • Endometritis
  • Secondary PPH

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension from spinal in a hypovolaemic woman
  • Aspiration under GA
  • Hypotension with GTN

Delayed

  • Complications of transfusion
  • VTE
