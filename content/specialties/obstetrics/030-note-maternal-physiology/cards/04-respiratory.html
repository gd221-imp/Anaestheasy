Increased abdominal diameter; minute ventilation rises by around 45-50% (tidal volume up ~40-45%, respiratory rate up only 0-15%); PaCO2 falls to around 4.3kPa with increased CO2 sensitivity; FRC falls by around 30% when supine; oxygen consumption rises by around 20-35% at term (about +40% in the first stage and +75% in the second stage of labour), meaning desaturation occurs faster during apnoea; the diaphragm is displaced upward.
