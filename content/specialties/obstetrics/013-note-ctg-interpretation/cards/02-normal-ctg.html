Baseline heart rate 110-160bpm, variability 5-25bpm (trough to peak within a 1 minute window)

Suspicious: 1 non-reassuring feature (with the rest reassuring)

Pathological: 1 or more abnormal features

Urgent intervention needed for: acute bradycardia, or a prolonged deceleration (>3 mins)

Acceleration = a rise of >15bpm for >=15 seconds - a sign of fetal wellbeing/activity
