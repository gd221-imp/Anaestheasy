Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Gestation (usually 12 to 24 weeks)
  • elective, or rescue cerclage with a dilated cervix

Examination (O/E)

  • Previous anaesthetics and back examination

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none; FBC if bleeding

Optimise

  • Fasting, aspiration prophylaxis from the second trimester

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access
  • Spinal kit
  • lithotomy with care for aortocaval compression in later gestation

Induction

  • Spinal with hyperbaric bupivacaine, low-dose opioid
  • block to around T10 (sacral cover essential)
  • GA if spinal contraindicated: RSI after about 18 weeks
  • Can consider a short-acting spinal: 2% hyperbaric prilocaine up to 2.5 ml

Maintenance

  • avoid deep volatile and coughing
  • Minimal uterine stimulation
  • tocolysis if the surgeons ask

Emergence

  • Lower legs slowly from lithotomy
  • Monitor for contractions and bleeding

Post-op

Complications

  • Monitor for contractions, bleeding or membrane rupture

Surgical

Immediate

  • Membrane rupture
  • Bleeding
  • Bladder injury

Delayed

  • Infection and chorioamnionitis
  • Pre-term labour or miscarriage
  • Cervical laceration at removal

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension after spinal
  • Aspiration under GA
  • Inadequate block

Delayed

  • Post dural puncture headache
  • Urinary retention

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia
  • Can consider avoiding NSAIDs

Destination

  • Usually home the same or next day
