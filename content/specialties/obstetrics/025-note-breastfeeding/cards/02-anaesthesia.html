Mothers can generally resume breastfeeding as soon as they're able to hold their baby

Use multimodal, opioid-sparing analgesia where possible (if morphine/diamorphine needed, plan around sleep/feeding times rather than routinely 'pump and dump'); provide support and an environment conducive to breastfeeding around the time of surgery

Most anaesthetic drugs have a short half-life and low bioavailability, so transfer to the infant via breast milk is generally minimal
