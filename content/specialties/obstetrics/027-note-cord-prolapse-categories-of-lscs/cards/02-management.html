Anaesthetic: GA, no sodium citrate, 3 mins pre-oxygenation (or via face mask while positioning), supine with left tilt, RSI with cricoid pressure

Obstetric (aiming for category 1 LSCS): wrap the protruding umbilical cord in a warm saline-soaked sterile towel to prevent cord vasospasm; knee-chest or head-down/exaggerated Trendelenburg positioning with manual elevation of the fetal head off the cord; alternatively, fill the bladder with around 500ml saline via a urinary catheter to elevate the presenting part off the cord; consider tocolytics to reduce contractions
