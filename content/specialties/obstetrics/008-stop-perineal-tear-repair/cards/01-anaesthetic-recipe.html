Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Degree of tear (third and fourth degree need theatre)
  • Is the labour epidural still working?

Examination (O/E)

  • Epidural block level and density
  • Haemodynamics and blood loss

Investigations (Ix)

  • blood loss

Optimise

  • Time since delivery, oral intake, aspiration prophylaxis

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access
  • Epidural top-up drugs or spinal kit
  • Lithotomy

Induction

  • Top up a working epidural or give a spinal with sacral cover (T10 is enough)

Maintenance

  • GA only if neuraxial not possible

Emergence

  • Lower legs slowly from lithotomy

Post-op

Complications

  • laxatives
  • Debrief and physiotherapy follow-up

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Haematoma

Delayed

  • Wound breakdown or infection
  • Anal incontinence
  • Dyspareunia

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension with spinal
  • Inadequate block

Delayed

  • Urinary retention
  • Post dural puncture headache

Analgesia

  • Regular paracetamol and NSAID if no contraindication
  • Bladder care (urinary retention after block)

Destination

  • Postnatal ward; bladder care
