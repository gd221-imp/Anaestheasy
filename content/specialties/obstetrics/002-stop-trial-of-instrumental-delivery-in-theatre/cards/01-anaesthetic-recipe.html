Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Indication and urgency: fetal compromise, delay in second stage
  • Check the existing epidural: block height, top-ups needed, how well it has worked

Examination (O/E)

—

Investigations (Ix)

  • CTG category

Optimise

  • Aspiration prophylaxis
  • plan for conversion to caesarean

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Full caesarean set-up and team
  • Epidural top-up drugs (e.g. 2% lidocaine with adrenaline and bicarbonate) or spinal kit
  • Neonatal team present

Induction

  • Extend a working epidural, or spinal (or CSE) if no epidural
  • aim for a dense block to T4 so conversion is immediate
  • Lithotomy
  • GA only if the block cannot be made adequate in time

Maintenance

  • left tilt
  • treat hypotension promptly
  • If the trial fails, proceed to caesarean without delay

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Uterotonics as needed
  • watch for PPH (higher after failed instrumental)
  • Debrief with the mother

Surgical

Immediate

  • Failed instrumental needing caesarean at full dilatation
  • Third- or fourth-degree perineal tear
  • Postpartum haemorrhage

Delayed

  • Pelvic floor dysfunction
  • Infection
  • Neonatal injury (cephalhaematoma, subgaleal bleed)

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Inadequate block when converting
  • High block or hypotension
  • Failed intubation and aspiration if GA needed

Delayed

  • Post dural puncture headache
  • Nerve injury from lithotomy or neuraxial

Analgesia

  • Analgesia via epidural or regular oral
