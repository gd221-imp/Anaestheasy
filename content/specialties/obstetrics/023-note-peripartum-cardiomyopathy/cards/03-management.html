ACE inhibitors are contraindicated in pregnancy (used postpartum), loop diuretics, beta-blockers, LMWH, +/- hydralazine if hypertensive

For vaginal delivery: arterial line monitoring, early epidural

At delivery, autotransfusion of around 500ml of blood from the contracting uterus, combined with relief of aortocaval compression, can precipitate acute decompensation of heart failure.
