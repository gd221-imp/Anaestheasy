PPH management in peripartum cardiomyopathy is complicated by the cardiovascular side effects of uterotonics - many women with PPH also have cardiovascular compromise:

Oxytocin can reduce SVR, causing reflex tachycardia and raised pulmonary vascular resistance from pulmonary vasospasm - give as a slow infusion rather than a rapid bolus

Carboprost markedly raises pulmonary vascular resistance

Ergometrine raises SVR and can cause coronary artery vasospasm

All of these can worsen cardiac status, so uterotonic choice needs careful consideration in this group.

Decisions around mechanical circulatory support need to balance the risks of anticoagulation against the timing and mode of delivery.
