Hypertension plus one of: proteinuria (urine protein:creatinine ratio >30mg/mmol, 2+ on dipstick, or albumin:creatinine ratio >8mg/mmol); uteroplacental dysfunction (fetal growth restriction, abnormal umbilical artery Doppler); or other organ dysfunction (creatinine >90micromol/L, ALT >70 IU/L, platelets <150, oliguria <500ml/24hr, or evidence of haemolysis)
