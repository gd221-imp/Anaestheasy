Managing eclampsia (seizures on a background of pre-eclampsia): magnesium sulphate 4g over 5 mins, then 1g/hr for 24hrs

For a repeat seizure: further 2g magnesium sulphate

Watch for magnesium toxicity: reduced reflexes, reduced respiratory rate, falling SpO2 - treat with calcium gluconate 10ml 10% over 10 mins

Magnesium is also started for severe pre-eclampsia if delivery is planned within 24hrs, and for fetal neuroprotection if gestation is under 34 weeks
