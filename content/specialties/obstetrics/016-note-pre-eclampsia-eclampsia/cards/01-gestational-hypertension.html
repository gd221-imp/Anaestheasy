BP >=140/90, newly developed after 20 weeks' gestation

Pre-eclampsia = abnormal placentation leading to hypertension and proteinuria, affecting around 7% of pregnancies

NICE 2023 diagnostic criteria: SBP >=140 (or >=160 for severe) or DBP >=90 (or >=110 for severe) on 2 occasions 4-6hrs apart, occurring after 20 weeks
