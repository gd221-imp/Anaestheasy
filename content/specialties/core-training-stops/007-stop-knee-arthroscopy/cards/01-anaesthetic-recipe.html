Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Day-case suitability

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none for day case

Optimise

  • Day-case criteria and escort

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, LMA, tourniquet

Induction

  • Propofol and LMA
  • Can consider fentanyl 150 mcg at induction and 100 mcg before skin; propofol TCI; dexamethasone 6.6 mg and ondansetron 4 mg

Maintenance

  • note tourniquet time

Emergence

  • Remove LMA awake or deep
  • Antiemetic

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding into the joint
  • Nerve injury

Delayed

  • Infection (septic arthritis)
  • Stiffness

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Laryngospasm with LMA
  • Tourniquet pain and hypertension

Delayed

  • VTE
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol and NSAID
  • Intra-articular local anaesthetic by surgeon
  • Opioid PRN
  • Can consider an adductor canal block for bigger knee procedures such as ACL reconstruction
  • Can consider codeine with paracetamol and an NSAID to take home

Destination

  • Intra-articular local, simple analgesia, home same day

Tips

  • A short day case under a laryngeal mask.
  • A tourniquet is often used; note the time it goes up.
  • Intra-articular local anaesthetic from the surgeon helps analgesia.
