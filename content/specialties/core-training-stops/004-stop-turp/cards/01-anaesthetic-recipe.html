Post-op

Complications

  • Check sodium if confused or nauseated

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Bladder or capsular perforation

Delayed

  • Urinary retention or incontinence
  • Urethral stricture
  • Retrograde ejaculation
  • Infection and urosepsis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • TURP syndrome (fluid overload, hyponatraemia, glycine toxicity)
  • High spinal
  • Hypothermia from irrigation

Delayed

  • Bacteraemia
  • Confusion in older patients

Tips

  • Often older men with comorbidities; spinal anaesthesia is common.
  • A spinal lets you spot TURP syndrome early: confusion, nausea and visual changes from absorbed irrigation fluid and low sodium.
  • Check sodium if the resection is long or the patient becomes unwell.
