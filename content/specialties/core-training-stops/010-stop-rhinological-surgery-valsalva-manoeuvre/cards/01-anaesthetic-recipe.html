Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Bleeding risk

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Nasal patency
  • OSA screening (STOP-BANG)

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none; clotting if bleeding history

Optimise

  • Stop antiplatelets if the surgeon asks
  • Plan for nasal packs (mouth breathing) in OSA

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT (south-facing), throat pack

Induction

  • TCI propofol and remifentanil
  • South-facing RAE tube and throat pack
  • Topical vasoconstrictor by surgeon: watch for hypertension and arrhythmia

Maintenance

  • TIVA
  • controlled hypotension

Emergence

  • Remove throat pack and suction under direct vision
  • Smooth emergence; avoid coughing and straining
  • Extubate awake, sitting up

Post-op

Complications

  • Nasal packs, so mouth breathing

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Orbital injury
  • CSF leak

Delayed

  • Synechiae
  • Infection
  • Septal perforation

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Blood in the airway and laryngospasm at extubation
  • Hypertension from vasoconstrictors

Delayed

  • Nasal obstruction with packs, worse in OSA
  • PONV from swallowed blood

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID if surgeon agrees
  • Opioid PRN

Destination

  • Day case or overnight ward; HDU monitoring if severe OSA with packs
