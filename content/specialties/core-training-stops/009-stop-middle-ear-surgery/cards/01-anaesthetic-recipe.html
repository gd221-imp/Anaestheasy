Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • PONV risk

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Neck rotation (head turned away)

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none for fit patients

Optimise

  • PONV risk score; plan multimodal prophylaxis

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT or LMA, nerve monitoring

Induction

  • TCI propofol and remifentanil
  • Short-acting relaxant only, or none, to keep facial nerve monitoring reliable
  • Secure the airway well: head draped and turned

Maintenance

  • TIVA
  • avoid nitrous oxide
  • controlled hypotension

Emergence

  • Smooth emergence, avoid coughing and straining (graft)
  • Consider deep LMA removal

Post-op

Complications

  • PONV prophylaxis

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding obscuring the surgical field
  • Facial nerve injury

Delayed

  • Hearing loss
  • Tinnitus
  • Vertigo

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Nitrous oxide expanding the middle ear and lifting the graft
  • Hypertension worsening bleeding

Delayed

  • PONV (very common)
  • Vertigo

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol, NSAID
  • Local infiltration by surgeon
  • Opioid PRN

Destination

  • Day case or overnight ward
