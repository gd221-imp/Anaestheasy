Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Tumour type and size
  • facial nerve function before surgery
  • Airway: usually straightforward
  • PONV risk

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Facial nerve function documented before surgery

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none for fit patients

Optimise

  • Agree the no-relaxant plan with the surgeon

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring
  • reinforced ETT or LMA
  • Facial nerve monitor

Induction

  • Avoid neuromuscular blockers after intubation (facial nerve monitoring)

Maintenance

  • remifentanil helps
  • Head-up, head turned
  • Controlled hypotension within safe limits for a clear field

Emergence

  • Smooth emergence; avoid coughing (haematoma)
  • Check facial nerve function in recovery

Post-op

Complications

  • PONV prophylaxis
  • Check facial nerve function

Surgical

Immediate

  • Facial nerve injury
  • Bleeding and haematoma

Delayed

  • Frey syndrome
  • Salivary fistula
  • Numbness of the ear lobe

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Patient movement without relaxant
  • Hypotension

Delayed

  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia

Destination

  • Ward overnight with drain
