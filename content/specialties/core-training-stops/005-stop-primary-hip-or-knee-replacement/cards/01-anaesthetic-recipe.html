History (Hx)

Investigations (Ix)

—

Optimise

  • anaemia (correct before surgery)
  • Anticoagulants and antiplatelets
  • Plan for early mobilisation

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access
  • Spinal kit
  • ultrasound for blocks
  • Tranexamic acid

Induction

  • Spinal ± light sedation, or GA
  • knee: adductor canal block and periarticular infiltration

Maintenance

  • Hip: consider PENG or fascia iliaca
  • Watch for bone cement implantation syndrome when cementing

—

Post-op

Complications

  • early mobilisation
  • VTE prophylaxis
  • Delirium prevention

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Nerve injury (sciatic, peroneal)
  • Fracture

Delayed

  • Dislocation
  • Infection
  • Loosening

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Bone cement implantation syndrome
  • Hypotension under spinal

Delayed

  • VTE
  • Urinary retention
  • Delirium
