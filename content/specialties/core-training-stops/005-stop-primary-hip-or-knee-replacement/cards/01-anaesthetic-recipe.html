Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Comorbidities and frailty

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Back for spinal
  • Murmur (aortic stenosis)

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC (treat anaemia), U&E, group and save
  • ECG

Optimise

  • anaemia (correct before surgery)
  • Anticoagulants and antiplatelets
  • Plan for early mobilisation

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access
  • Spinal kit
  • ultrasound for blocks
  • Tranexamic acid

Induction

  • Spinal ± light sedation, or GA
  • knee: adductor canal block and periarticular infiltration
  • Can consider a spinal of 2.5–2.8 ml 0.5% heavy bupivacaine without opioid, with propofol TCI sedation at about 0.5–1 mcg/ml if wanted
  • Can consider dexamethasone 6.6 mg; antibiotics; tranexamic acid

Maintenance

  • Hip: consider PENG or fascia iliaca
  • Watch for bone cement implantation syndrome when cementing
  • Can consider avoiding a tourniquet for knees: it doesn't reduce total blood loss and adds swelling
  • Can consider local infiltration into the joint (knee) or wound (hip)

Emergence

  • Check block and haemodynamics in recovery
  • Sit up when safe

Post-op

Complications

  • early mobilisation
  • VTE prophylaxis
  • Delirium prevention

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Nerve injury (sciatic, peroneal)
  • Fracture

Delayed

  • Dislocation
  • Infection
  • Loosening

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Bone cement implantation syndrome
  • Hypotension under spinal

Delayed

  • VTE
  • Urinary retention
  • Delirium

Analgesia

  • Multimodal analgesia
  • Can consider avoiding intrathecal opioid (retention, itch, respiratory depression), or low-dose intrathecal diamorphine
  • Can consider avoiding NSAIDs if on regular aspirin

Destination

  • Orthopaedic ward; enhanced recovery pathway
  • Can consider avoiding post-op IV fluids; eat, drink and mobilise early
