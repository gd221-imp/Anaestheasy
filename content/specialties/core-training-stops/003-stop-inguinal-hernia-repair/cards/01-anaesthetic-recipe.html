Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Day-case suitability
  • Chronic cough?
  • Constipation?

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Size and reducibility of the hernia: incarcerated or strangulated? This affects urgency

Investigations (Ix)

  • Usually none for fit day-case patients; again, follow the NICE pre-operative tests guidance

Optimise

  • Day-case criteria and escort
  • Treat chronic cough or constipation

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, LMA

Induction

  • Propofol, short-acting opioid, LMA
  • Ilioinguinal block, quadratus lumborum block or surgeon infiltration

Maintenance

  • Volatile or TIVA, spontaneous ventilation
  • Deepen for spermatic cord traction

Emergence

  • Remove the LMA awake, or transfer to recovery on an i-gel and T-piece
  • Antiemetic

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Injury to the vas or testicular vessels
  • Bowel or bladder injury (rare)

Delayed

  • Recurrence
  • Chronic groin pain
  • Wound infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Laryngospasm with LMA during surgical stimulation
  • LA toxicity from block plus infiltration

Delayed

  • Urinary retention
  • PONV

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia

Destination

  • home same day
