Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Indication (long-term ventilation, obstruction, secretions)
  • Birth history, syndromes
  • Current respiratory support

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway assessment, plan for oxygenation

Investigations (Ix)

  • Airway endoscopy findings
  • FBC, clotting

Optimise

  • Plan with ENT and PICU
  • Stay sutures and spare tubes ready

Intra-op

Equipment

  • ETT, airway equipment, capnography

Induction

  • Gas or IV induction, intubate
  • Often already intubated

Maintenance

  • GA
  • FiO₂ 1.0
  • withdraw ETT as tracheostomy placed

Emergence

  • Confirm tracheostomy with capnography
  • Secure ties; stay sutures labelled

Post-op

Complications

  • Bedhead signs, tracheostomy emergency algorithm

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Pneumothorax
  • Oesophageal injury
  • Damage to the recurrent laryngeal nerve

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Granuloma formation
  • Erosion of nearby vessels
  • Suprastomal collapse
  • Tracheal stenosis
  • Tracheo-oesophageal fistula

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Loss of the airway

Delayed

  • Accidental decannulation
  • Obstruction with secretions

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol
  • Opioid infusion if needed

Destination

  • PICU until first tube change
