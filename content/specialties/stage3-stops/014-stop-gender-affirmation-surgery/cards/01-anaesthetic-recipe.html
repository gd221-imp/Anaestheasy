Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Hormone therapy, VTE risk

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Back for neuraxial if planned

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E, group and save

Optimise

  • Hormone therapy plan per surgical team (VTE risk)
  • Use the patient's name and pronouns

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring

Induction

  • IV induction, ETT for long cases
  • Careful positioning and padding (lithotomy)

Maintenance

  • GA

Emergence

  • Extubate awake
  • Lower legs slowly from lithotomy

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Rectal or urethral injury

Delayed

  • Wound breakdown
  • Fistula
  • Urinary complications
  • VTE

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Prolonged positioning (lithotomy) injuries
  • Drug interactions with hormone therapy

Delayed

  • VTE (oestrogen therapy)
  • Psychological distress

Analgesia

  • Analgesia, VTE prophylaxis

Destination

  • Specialist ward
