Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Major trauma: associated injuries, tertiary survey

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Other injuries
  • Neurology of the legs (sciatic)

Investigations (Ix)

  • Blood loss and transfusion so far
  • coagulation

Optimise

  • VTE risk and timing of prophylaxis

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, large-bore access, cell salvage
  • Blood products available
  • Warming
  • prone or lateral positioning kit

Induction

  • GA with careful positioning
  • Analgesia: consider lumbar plexus or epidural if coagulation allows

Maintenance

  • Tranexamic acid
  • viscoelastic-guided transfusion

Emergence

  • Extubate if warm, stable and not coagulopathic

Post-op

Complications

  • VTE prophylaxis once bleeding controlled
  • Check for nerve injury

Surgical

Immediate

  • Major haemorrhage
  • Sciatic nerve injury
  • Vascular injury

Delayed

  • Infection
  • Heterotopic ossification
  • Post-traumatic arthritis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypothermia and coagulopathy
  • Positioning injuries

Delayed

  • VTE and PE
  • Fat embolism

Analgesia

  • Epidural or lumbar plexus catheter
  • PCA
  • Paracetamol

Destination

  • HDU
