Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Jaundice, weight loss, diabetes
  • Cardiorespiratory reserve
  • Biliary stent

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Nutritional state
  • Back for epidural

Investigations (Ix)

  • Nutrition, jaundice, coagulation

Optimise

  • Biliary drainage if bilirubin very high
  • Correct coagulopathy (vitamin K)
  • Prehabilitation and nutrition

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, central line, epidural

Induction

  • IV induction, relaxant
  • Epidural before induction
  • Arterial and central lines

Maintenance

  • GA
  • goal-directed fluids

Emergence

  • Extubate awake if warm and stable

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Major blood loss
  • Vascular injury (portal vein, SMV)

Delayed

  • Pancreatic fistula
  • Delayed gastric emptying
  • Bile leak
  • Post-pancreatectomy haemorrhage

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension with epidural and blood loss
  • Hyperglycaemia

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury
  • Chest infection
  • New-onset diabetes

Analgesia

  • Thoracic epidural
  • Paracetamol
  • PCA if epidural fails

Destination

  • HDU or ICU
