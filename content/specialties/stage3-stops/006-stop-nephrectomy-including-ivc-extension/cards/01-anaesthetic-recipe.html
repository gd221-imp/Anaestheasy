Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • cardiac surgery input if high IVC extension

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Cardiorespiratory examination
  • Back for epidural

Investigations (Ix)

  • Renal function and the remaining kidney
  • Tumour extension into the renal vein or IVC on imaging

Optimise

  • Plan for major bleeding

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, large-bore access, CVC
  • Cell salvage (if not contraindicated), blood available
  • TOE for high IVC thrombus

Induction

  • GA with lateral or supine positioning
  • Analgesia: epidural, or wound catheters
  • Can consider for smaller robotic cases, a spinal with 2 ml 0.5% heavy bupivacaine and 500 mcg diamorphine; 16G in the upper arm

Maintenance

  • IVC clamping causes hypotension (reduced venous return)
  • Can consider that hypotension is likely when the table is broken in the lateral position
  • Can consider magnesium 3 g; dexamethasone 6.6 mg

Emergence

  • Extubate awake if stable

Post-op

Complications

  • Renal function monitoring
  • Watch for tumour embolism and bleeding

Surgical

Immediate

  • Major haemorrhage
  • Tumour embolism
  • Pleural injury

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury in the remaining kidney
  • Infection
  • Ileus

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension with IVC clamping
  • Positioning injuries

Delayed

  • Chest infection
  • VTE

Analgesia

  • Epidural or wound catheters
  • Paracetamol
  • PCA
  • Can consider modified-release oral morphine twice daily with immediate-release as needed

Destination

  • HDU
