Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Dysphagia, weight loss, reflux
  • Neoadjuvant chemotherapy
  • Cardiorespiratory reserve, smoking, alcohol

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway (DLT)
  • Nutritional state
  • Back for epidural

Investigations (Ix)

  • Nutrition, CPET

Optimise

  • Prehabilitation and nutrition (feeding jejunostomy)
  • Smoking cessation
  • ICU bed

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, central line, DLT for thoracic phase, epidural

Induction

  • RSI (reflux, obstruction)
  • DLT for the thoracic phase
  • Epidural before induction

Maintenance

  • GA with OLV
  • avoid fluid overload

Emergence

  • Exchange DLT for single-lumen tube if staying ventilated
  • Extubate early if possible

Post-op

Complications

  • watch anastomotic leak

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Injury to the trachea or recurrent laryngeal nerve

Delayed

  • Anastomotic leak
  • Chylothorax
  • Conduit necrosis
  • Stricture

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypoxia during one-lung ventilation
  • Hypotension with epidural and fluid restriction
  • Arrhythmias

Delayed

  • Pneumonia and ARDS
  • Aspiration
  • Atrial fibrillation

Analgesia

  • Thoracic epidural or paravertebral catheter
  • Paracetamol
  • PCA

Destination

  • ICU
