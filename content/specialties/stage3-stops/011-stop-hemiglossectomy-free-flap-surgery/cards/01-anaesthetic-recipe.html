Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Smoking, alcohol
  • Previous radiotherapy
  • COPD, IHD, nutrition

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway: tumour, trismus, neck movement
  • Nutritional state

Investigations (Ix)

  • Nasendoscopy and imaging
  • FBC, U&E, LFTs, group and save

Optimise

  • Airway, nutrition, tracheostomy plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, cardiac output monitor, warming

Induction

  • Awake fibreoptic or awake tracheostomy if airway compromised
  • Otherwise IV induction with secured nasal or oral tube

Maintenance

  • GA
  • optimise flap perfusion (normothermia, normovolaemia, avoid vasoconstrictors)

Emergence

  • Usually tracheostomy; wake on it
  • Avoid coughing and hypertension (haematoma)

Post-op

Complications

  • flap observations

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding and neck haematoma
  • Flap vessel thrombosis

Delayed

  • Flap failure
  • Fistula
  • Wound infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Shared airway and tube displacement
  • Hypotension or vasoconstriction compromising flap perfusion
  • Hypothermia

Delayed

  • Airway obstruction (tracheostomy often needed)
  • Alcohol withdrawal and delirium

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol via NG tube
  • Opioid infusion or PCA

Destination

  • ICU or HDU
