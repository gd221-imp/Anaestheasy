Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • colorectal metastases after chemotherapy
  • nutrition

Examination (O/E)

—

Investigations (Ix)

  • Liver function and reserve
  • CPET
  • Plan for low CVP and blood availability

Optimise

—

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, CVC, large-bore access, cell salvage
  • Cardiac output monitoring
  • Warming

Induction

  • analgesia with wound catheters, intrathecal diamorphine or epidural

Maintenance

  • Low CVP technique during parenchymal transection (restrict fluids, head-up, GTN)
  • Pringle manoeuvre: watch for haemodynamic changes
  • Restore volume after resection

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Check lactate, glucose, coagulation and liver function
  • adjust drugs for reduced liver function

Surgical

Immediate

  • Major haemorrhage
  • Air embolism through hepatic veins
  • Bile leak

Delayed

  • Post-hepatectomy liver failure
  • Subphrenic collection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hypotension with low CVP
  • Coagulopathy affecting epidural removal

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury
  • Pleural effusion

Analgesia

  • Avoid NSAIDs early

Destination

  • HDU
