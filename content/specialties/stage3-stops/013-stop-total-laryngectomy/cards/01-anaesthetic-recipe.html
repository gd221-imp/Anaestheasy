Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Previous radiotherapy, tumour size, stridor
  • Smoking, alcohol, nutrition
  • COPD and IHD

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway and stridor
  • Neck (previous radiotherapy)

Investigations (Ix)

  • nasendoscopy and CT

Optimise

  • Airway plan: awake tracheostomy or careful induction

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, large-bore access
  • Awake tracheostomy kit or videolaryngoscope and small tubes
  • Laryngectomy tube and humidification

Induction

  • Awake tracheostomy under local, or careful induction with spontaneous ventilation
  • Front-of-neck plan agreed

Maintenance

  • GA with transfer of ventilation to the tracheostoma
  • Maintain flap and wound perfusion

Emergence

  • Secure the airway (often awake tracheostomy under local)

Post-op

Complications

  • stoma care (neck breather: oxygen via the stoma)
  • Alcohol withdrawal prophylaxis
  • Nutrition via NGT

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Carotid injury

Delayed

  • Pharyngocutaneous fistula
  • Wound infection
  • Hypocalcaemia (parathyroid)

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Loss of the airway at induction
  • Air embolism

Delayed

  • Stoma problems
  • Delirium and alcohol withdrawal
  • Chest infection

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol via NG tube
  • Opioid infusion or PCA

Destination

  • HDU
