Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Voice change, stridor
  • Previous laser procedures

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway: neck extension, mouth opening
  • Stridor

Investigations (Ix)

  • Nasendoscopy findings

Optimise

  • Airway obstruction, laser safety plan

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Laser-safe tube, wet swabs, low FiO₂

Induction

  • TIVA with relaxant
  • Laser-safe tube with saline-filled cuffs, or tubeless technique

Maintenance

  • TIVA
  • FiO₂ below 0.3
  • cuff filled with saline

Emergence

  • Rapid emergence; recover sitting up
  • Dexamethasone if oedema expected

Post-op

Complications

  • Airway fire protocol available

Surgical

Immediate

  • Airway bleeding
  • Vocal cord injury

Delayed

  • Airway oedema
  • Voice change
  • Laryngeal web or stenosis

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Airway fire
  • Eye injury from the laser
  • Hypoxia with the shared airway

Delayed

  • Post-op stridor

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; usually minimal pain

Destination

  • Day case or ward; HDU if stridor
