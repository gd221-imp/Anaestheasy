Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Symptoms of obstruction: stridor, orthopnoea
  • Previous stents
  • Cardiorespiratory reserve

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Stridor and position of comfort

Investigations (Ix)

  • Degree of obstruction, imaging

Optimise

  • Plan A to D with the surgeon
  • ECMO standby if critical

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Jet ventilator, rigid bronchoscope, TIVA

Induction

  • Gas or careful IV induction, maintaining spontaneous ventilation until the airway is secured
  • TIVA with relaxant once the rigid scope is in

Maintenance

  • TIVA
  • jet ventilation
  • watch for barotrauma

Emergence

  • Mask or LMA until fully reversed
  • Recover sitting up

Post-op

Complications

  • Sit up, oxygen

Surgical

Immediate

  • Airway bleeding
  • Airway perforation
  • Stent malposition

Delayed

  • Stent migration
  • Granulation tissue and re-stenosis
  • Mucus plugging

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Barotrauma, pneumothorax and surgical emphysema with jet ventilation
  • Hypoxia and hypercapnia
  • Awareness with TIVA

Delayed

  • Post-op stridor

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; usually minimal pain

Destination

  • HDU
