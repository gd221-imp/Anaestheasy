Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding (major in splenectomy and liver transplant)
  • Visceral injury
  • Pneumothorax in Nissen fundoplication

Delayed

  • Dysphagia and gas bloat after Nissen
  • Overwhelming post-splenectomy infection
  • Graft dysfunction, hepatic artery or portal vein thrombosis after liver transplant
  • Anastomotic leak after laparotomy

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Post-reperfusion syndrome in liver transplant
  • Coagulopathy
  • Hypothermia

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury
  • Post-op ileus
