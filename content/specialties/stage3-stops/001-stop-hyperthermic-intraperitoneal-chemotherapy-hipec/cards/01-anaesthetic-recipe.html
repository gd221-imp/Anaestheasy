Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Nutrition, cardiorespiratory fitness

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Nutritional state
  • Back for epidural

Investigations (Ix)

  • CPET
  • FBC, U&E, LFTs, clotting, crossmatch

Optimise

  • Prehabilitation and nutrition
  • Treat anaemia
  • ICU or HDU bed booked

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, central line, cardiac output monitor, thoracic epidural

Induction

  • IV induction, relaxant
  • Epidural before induction
  • Arterial and central lines

Maintenance

  • Large fluid shifts
  • active cooling during heated chemotherapy
  • Watch temperature, urine output and electrolytes

Emergence

  • Extubate if warm, stable and not bleeding; otherwise ventilate to ICU

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Major blood loss
  • Visceral injury

Delayed

  • Anastomotic leak
  • Intra-abdominal sepsis
  • Fistula

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Hyperthermia and fluid shifts during HIPEC
  • Coagulopathy
  • Electrolyte disturbance

Delayed

  • Chemotherapy toxicity (renal, bone marrow)
  • Prolonged ileus
  • ICU admission

Analgesia

  • Thoracic epidural
  • Paracetamol
  • PCA if epidural fails

Destination

  • ICU or HDU
