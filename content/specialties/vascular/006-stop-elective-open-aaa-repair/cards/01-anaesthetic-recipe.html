Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Cardiorespiratory comorbidities and exercise tolerance
  • Smoking
  • Renal function

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Cardiorespiratory examination
  • Back for epidural

Investigations (Ix)

  • Cardiorespiratory fitness (CPET), renal function
  • blood crossmatched

Optimise

  • Optimise medical therapy (statin, antiplatelet, BP)
  • Critical care bed

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, CVC, large-bore access, cell salvage
  • Thoracic epidural
  • Warming
  • vasoactive drugs

Induction

  • GA with epidural

Maintenance

  • anticipate cross-clamp hypertension (deepen, GTN)
  • Anticipate unclamping hypotension (fluids, vasopressors, release slowly)
  • Monitor urine output and temperature

Emergence

  • Extubate if warm, stable and not bleeding; otherwise ventilate to ICU

Post-op

Complications

  • Watch for bleeding, limb ischaemia and AKI

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Distal embolism and limb ischaemia

Delayed

  • Graft infection
  • Colonic ischaemia
  • Incisional hernia

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Cross-clamp hypertension and myocardial ischaemia
  • Unclamping hypotension and acidosis

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury
  • Myocardial infarction
  • Spinal cord ischaemia (rare)

Analgesia

  • Epidural analgesia

Destination

  • ICU or HDU
