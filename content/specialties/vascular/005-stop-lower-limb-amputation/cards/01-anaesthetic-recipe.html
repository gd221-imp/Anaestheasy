Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Often frail, diabetic, with vascular disease and CKD
  • sepsis from wet gangrene
  • Pain history and opioid use

Examination (O/E)

—

Investigations (Ix)

—

Optimise

  • plan for phantom limb pain
  • Anticoagulants and antiplatelets

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring
  • arterial line if unstable
  • Ultrasound for blocks
  • perineural catheter kit

Induction

  • Spinal or GA with sciatic and femoral blocks
  • Consider ketamine

Maintenance

  • surgeon-placed perineural catheter
  • continue regular analgesia

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • VTE prophylaxis and early rehabilitation

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Wound ischaemia

Delayed

  • Wound breakdown needing revision to a higher level
  • Infection

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Haemodynamic instability (sepsis, cardiac disease)
  • LA toxicity with large-volume blocks

Delayed

  • Phantom limb pain
  • Chronic post-surgical pain
  • Delirium

Analgesia

  • Perineural catheter infusion for several days
  • Pain team review
  • gabapentinoids if neuropathic pain
