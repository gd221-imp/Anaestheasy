Surgical

Immediate

  • Retroperitoneal or subarachnoid haemorrhage
  • Vessel dissection
  • Vascular access site complications

Delayed

  • Access site haematoma presenting later

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Anaphylaxis to contrast media
  • Angioedema
  • Airway compromise or aspiration under sedation

Delayed
