Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Permissive hypotension (talking patient, systolic around 70–90) until clamp
  • Activate major haemorrhage protocol, consultant presence

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Conscious level and haemodynamics
  • Pulsatile abdominal mass

Investigations (Ix)

  • FBC, U&E, clotting, crossmatch (don't delay)
  • VBG with lactate and Hb
  • CT only if stable enough

Optimise

  • Senior team, theatres and ICU alerted
  • 2 units of blood running or ready before induction
  • Consider endovascular repair under local

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Two large-bore lines, arterial line, rapid infuser, cell salvage, blood products

Induction

  • induce only when surgeon is ready to clamp
  • Cardiovascularly stable induction (ketamine or low-dose agents), RSI
  • Expect hypotension on induction and on unclamping

Maintenance

  • correct coagulopathy

Emergence

  • Prep and drape awake

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Massive haemorrhage
  • Distal embolism

Delayed

  • Abdominal compartment syndrome
  • Graft infection
  • Colonic ischaemia

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Cardiovascular collapse at induction
  • Coagulopathy, hypothermia and acidosis
  • Reperfusion injury on clamp release

Delayed

  • Acute kidney injury
  • Myocardial infarction
  • Multi-organ failure

Analgesia

  • Opioid infusion while ventilated
  • Paracetamol

Destination

  • ICU: watch renal failure, abdominal compartment syndrome and bowel ischaemia
