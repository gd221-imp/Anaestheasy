Surgical

Immediate

  • Failed deployment, which may need conversion to open repair
  • Aneurysm rupture
  • Aortic dissection
  • Distal embolisation

Delayed

  • Endoleak
  • Stent migration
  • Graft infection
  • Bowel, lower limb or spinal cord ischaemia

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Conversion to GA if converted to open repair
  • Contrast reaction

Delayed

  • AKI
