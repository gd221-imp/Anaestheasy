Optimise (often multiple) comorbidities, consider CPET for risk stratification, stop NSAIDs and ACE inhibitors pre-op, and consent covering cardiovascular events, conversion to open repair, and continued aneurysm risk

Intra-op: a trained assistant, full AAGBI monitoring plus arterial line, temperature monitoring, and a urinary catheter; large-bore IV access with long extension tubing (for remote access from the radiolucent table); IV heparin (around 5000 units) given after femoral artery exposure, targeting an ACT of 200-250 seconds +/- reversal; blood and rapid transfusion equipment should be available even if not routinely needed
