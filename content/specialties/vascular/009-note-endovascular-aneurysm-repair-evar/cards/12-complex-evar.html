For aneurysms involving visceral branches, or with an inadequate infrarenal landing zone, in patients unfit for open repair

Usually needs GA given the complexity and duration; cross-match 4-6 units of blood; avoid reversing heparin unless there's significant coagulopathy

Stent preparation can take 6-8 weeks ('chimney' techniques can serve as a bailout option); HDU/ICU care often needed post-op

Post-implantation syndrome: a mild, self-limiting immune response to the inserted graft material, causing pyrexia and raised white cell count/CRP - affects under 40% of patients, typically lasting 2-10 days
