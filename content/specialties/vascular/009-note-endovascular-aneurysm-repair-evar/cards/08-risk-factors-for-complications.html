Surgical: age >70, diabetes, AF, a previous EVAR within 6 months, AKI, delays in preparation, poor nutrition, and anxiety - consider diuretics if contrast can't be avoided within a short interval

Anaesthetic: risk of renal artery plaque embolisation; complex EVAR (fenestrated, chimney, or branched grafts) needs more IV contrast; maintaining adequate MAP and blood glucose control are important throughout
