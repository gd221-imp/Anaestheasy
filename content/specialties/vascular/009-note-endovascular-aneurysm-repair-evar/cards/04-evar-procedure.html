Performed by an interventional radiologist and/or vascular surgeon in a hybrid theatre on a radiolucent, tilting table

  1. Both groins and the entire abdomen are draped and prepared (in case conversion to open repair is needed)
  2. The vascular surgeon exposes the femoral arteries (often under local anaesthetic), and interventional radiology performs an angiogram of the aorta
  3. A stent graft is deployed across the aneurysm, excluding it from blood flow so the sac thromboses; care is taken to avoid twisting the graft limb into the common iliac artery

Anaesthetic technique: GA, regional, or local anaesthesia can all be used depending on complexity and patient factors
