Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Comorbidities: IHD, COPD, CKD, diabetes
  • Functional capacity
  • Previous contrast reactions

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway
  • Cardiorespiratory examination
  • Peripheral pulses

Investigations (Ix)

  • Renal function, contrast plan, cardiac risk

Optimise

  • Stop NSAIDs and ACE inhibitors before surgery
  • IV fluids for contrast nephropathy
  • Consent for conversion to open repair

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, large-bore access, radiation protection, crossmatch
  • Can consider small and large cannulae and the arterial line placed awake; processed EEG

Induction

  • Local with sedation, regional, or GA
  • watch for conversion to open
  • Can consider giving antibiotics slowly, as they can drop the BP

Maintenance

  • Avoid hypertension during deployment
  • Can consider a metaraminol infusion
  • Can consider heparin 5000 units when asked; start a 90-minute timer and give 2000 units more if not finished; tell the team once it has circulated (about 2 minutes)
  • Can consider not reversing the heparin (usual practice)

Emergence

  • Lie flat; check groins and distal pulses

Post-op

Complications

Surgical

Immediate

  • Failed deployment, which may need conversion to open repair
  • Aneurysm rupture
  • Aortic dissection
  • Distal embolisation

Delayed

  • Endoleak
  • Stent migration
  • Graft infection
  • Bowel, lower limb or spinal cord ischaemia

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Conversion to GA if converted to open repair
  • Contrast reaction

Delayed

  • AKI

Analgesia

  • Paracetamol; local to groins; opioid PRN

Destination

  • Ward or HDU, renal function, limb perfusion
  • Can consider limb observations; usually HDU
