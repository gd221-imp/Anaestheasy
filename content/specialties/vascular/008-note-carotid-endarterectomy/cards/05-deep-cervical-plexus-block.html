Targets C2-C4, along a line between the mastoid process and the transverse process of C6 (level of the cricoid cartilage); C2, C3, and C4 are located roughly 2, 3, and 4cm from the mastoid process respectively

Can be a single injection at C3, or 3 separate injections - the needle is directed posteriorly and caudally until the transverse process is contacted, injecting 3-4ml of local anaesthetic at each level, withdrawing 1-2mm before injecting
