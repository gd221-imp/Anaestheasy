= a vascular surgical procedure to remove atheromatous plaque from the carotid artery, reducing stroke risk

Indications: stroke/TIA with moderate (50-69%) or severe (>70%) carotid stenosis

Risk of further stroke is high after a TIA, so referral for carotid endarterectomy is generally recommended within 24hrs of the event, with surgery ideally within 2 weeks

Avoid/manage perioperative VTE with heparin (e.g. enoxaparin), and control BP carefully around the time of surgery

Complications of delay/waiting: carotid artery dissection or restenosis; post-op haematoma is a particular concern as it can cause airway compromise
