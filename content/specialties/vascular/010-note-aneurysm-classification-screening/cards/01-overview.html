Aortic aneurysm classification (Crawford, for thoracoabdominal aneurysms):

Type I: from below the left subclavian artery to above the renal arteries

Type II: from below the left subclavian artery to below the renal arteries (most extensive)

Type III: from the distal thoracic aorta to below the renal arteries

Type IV: confined to the abdominal aorta
