Ultrasound-based, with referral for CT with contrast if the aorta measures over 55mm in men or 50mm in women

In the UK, screening programmes generally only screen men

If a woman is found to have an aneurysm, she's considered at increased rupture risk; the same applies to smokers

Rupture risk: under 1% per year if the aneurysm is under 4.5cm, rising non-linearly (more steeply) once the diameter exceeds 5cm
