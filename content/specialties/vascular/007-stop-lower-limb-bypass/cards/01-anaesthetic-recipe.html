Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Diabetes, IHD, CKD
  • often critical limb ischaemia

Examination (O/E)

—

Investigations (Ix)

—

Optimise

  • Antiplatelets and anticoagulants (neuraxial timing)
  • Heparin plan with surgeon

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line
  • Spinal or epidural kit, or GA
  • Heparin and protamine

Induction

  • Neuraxial (if anticoagulation allows) or GA with blocks

Maintenance

  • Heparin before clamping
  • maintain BP for graft flow

Emergence

—

Post-op

Complications

  • Graft and limb checks
  • continue antiplatelets
  • Watch for bleeding and compartment syndrome

Surgical

Immediate

  • Bleeding
  • Graft thrombosis

Delayed

  • Graft infection
  • Wound breakdown
  • Lymphocele

Anaesthetic

Immediate

  • Neuraxial haematoma with anticoagulation
  • Haemodynamic instability

Delayed

  • Myocardial infarction
  • Acute kidney injury
  • Amputation if graft fails
