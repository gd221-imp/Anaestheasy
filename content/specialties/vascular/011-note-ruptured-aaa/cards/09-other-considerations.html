Maintaining distal perfusion during cross-clamping: options include partial left heart bypass (if the clamp is distal to the left subclavian artery, with partial reperfusion techniques used, e.g. over 200-300 seconds), a Gott shunt, or partial femoral-femoral bypass

If the repair involves the descending thoracic aorta: consider a double-lumen tube, right upper limb arterial line, left internal jugular CVC, and temperature monitoring in the nasopharynx (the left lung may need to be deflated, with the patient in a semi-lateral position)

If the repair involves the aortic arch: full cardiopulmonary bypass and deep hypothermic circulatory arrest are needed
