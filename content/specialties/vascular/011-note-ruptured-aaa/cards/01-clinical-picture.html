Severe pain in the back, abdomen, or flank, hypotension, tachycardia, syncope, and a palpable, pulsatile abdominal mass

Differential diagnosis: bowel perforation, ischaemia or obstruction, MI, pancreatitis, acute cholecystitis

Pathophysiology: degradation of elastin fibres and collagen disruption, related to an imbalance in matrix metalloproteinase activity

In-hospital mortality is around 40%; overall mortality (including pre-hospital deaths) is around 80%
