Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Indication (prolonged ventilation, weaning, airway protection)
  • Previous neck surgery or radiotherapy
  • Anticoagulation and platelets

Examination (O/E)

  • Neck anatomy (ultrasound for vessels)
  • Current ventilator settings and FiO2
  • Ease of original intubation

Investigations (Ix)

  • Indication, coagulation, neck anatomy, ventilation requirements

Optimise

  • Correct coagulopathy; hold anticoagulants
  • Fast enteral feed per protocol
  • Airway plan if the ETT is lost

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Bronchoscope, airway equipment, capnography

Induction

  • Withdraw ETT under bronchoscopy

Maintenance

  • GA with relaxant on ICU
  • FiO₂ 1.0
  • confirm placement by capnography

Emergence

  • Continue sedation as needed on ICU
  • Chest X-ray if difficult

Post-op

Complications

  • Bedhead signs, emergency tracheostomy algorithm at the bedside

Analgesia

  • Existing ICU sedation and analgesia
  • Local infiltration by the operator

Destination

  • ICU; bedhead sign completed
