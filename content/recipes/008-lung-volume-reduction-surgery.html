Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line, left double-lumen tube, fibreoptic scope

Induction

  • confirm DLT position after turning lateral

Maintenance

  • GA with relaxant
  • One-lung ventilation: 4–6 ml/kg, PEEP, permissive hypercapnia, avoid gas trapping
  • Restrict fluids
