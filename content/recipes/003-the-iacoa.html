Category 1 caesarean

Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Decision-to-delivery target 30 minutes
  • check for working epidural

Examination (O/E)

  • Airway assessment (difficult airway more likely)
  • Epidural block height and density if in situ

Investigations (Ix)

  • Recent FBC and group and save; don't delay for results

Optimise

  • Left uterine displacement
  • Antacid prophylaxis (ranitidine or omeprazole, sodium citrate if GA)
  • Clear communication of category and plan with the team

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, wedge, suction, difficult airway trolley, antacid

Induction

  • Top-up working epidural (e.g. 2% lidocaine with adrenaline and bicarbonate), or spinal, or RSI GA

Maintenance

  • Regional: phenylephrine infusion to keep BP near baseline
  • GA: volatile 1 MAC until delivery then reduce; opioid after delivery
  • Oxytocin after delivery

Emergence

  • GA: extubate awake, head-up or left lateral
  • Regional: check block and haemodynamics

Post-op

Complications

  • Oxytocin, uterotonics as needed, VTE prophylaxis, debrief

Analgesia

  • Intrathecal or epidural diamorphine
  • Regular paracetamol and NSAID if no contraindication
  • TAP or QL blocks after GA

Destination

  • Obstetric HDU or labour ward recovery
  • Follow-up visit and debrief

Manual removal of placenta

Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Estimated blood loss so far
  • Existing epidural
  • Last oral intake

Examination (O/E)

  • Haemodynamic status
  • Epidural block height if in situ

Investigations (Ix)

  • Blood loss estimate, group and crossmatch

Optimise

  • Resuscitate first; activate major haemorrhage protocol if needed
  • Antacid prophylaxis

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Large-bore IV, monitoring, uterotonics, GTN available

Induction

  • Epidural top-up or spinal if haemodynamically stable

Maintenance

  • GA if major bleeding

Emergence

  • GA: extubate awake
  • Ongoing uterotonic infusion

Post-op

Complications

  • Follow local guidelines. Antibiotics, uterotonics, monitor bleeding

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia; neuraxial opioid if spinal

Destination

  • Labour ward or obstetric HDU depending on blood loss

Perineal tear repair

Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Degree of tear, existing epidural

Examination (O/E)

  • Epidural block level and density

Investigations (Ix)

  • Hb and blood loss estimate

Optimise

  • Top up epidural early if in situ
  • Antacid prophylaxis

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Standard monitoring, IV access

Induction

  • Epidural top-up or spinal in lithotomy

Maintenance

  • Lithotomy; watch BP
  • Supplementary analgesia if block patchy

Emergence

  • Lower legs together slowly from lithotomy

Post-op

Complications

  • Ongoing bleeding
  • Urinary retention
  • Lithotomy nerve injury

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia, NSAID if no contraindication, laxatives

Destination

  • Labour ward or postnatal ward

Trial of instrumental delivery

Pre-op

History (Hx)

  • Done in theatre in case of conversion

Examination (O/E)

  • Epidural block level and density; plan to extend for caesarean

Investigations (Ix)

  • Group and save

Optimise

  • Agree the conversion plan and category with the obstetrician
  • Antacid prophylaxis

Intra-op

Equipment

  • Full caesarean set-up

Induction

  • Dense block to T4, ready to convert immediately

Maintenance

  • Lithotomy; phenylephrine as needed
  • Be ready to convert immediately

Emergence

  • Check block and blood loss
  • Lower legs slowly

Post-op

Complications

  • As for caesarean if converted

Analgesia

  • Simple analgesia; neuraxial opioid if converted

Destination

  • Labour ward
