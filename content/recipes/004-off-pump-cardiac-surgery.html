Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line before induction, central line, TOE, pacing wires available
  • Defib pads, cell salvage, warming

Induction

  • Slow, cardiovascularly stable induction (e.g. fentanyl, low-dose propofol or etomidate, rocuronium)
  • head-down and fluids while the heart is positioned

Maintenance

  • Keep MAP above 70 mmHg
  • Heparin (lower dose than on-pump, per local protocol), check ACT
