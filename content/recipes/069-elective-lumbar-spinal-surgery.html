Intra-op

Equipment

  • Two cannulae (20G and 16G), arterial line in most patients, armoured ETT, prone positioning kit (e.g. Montreal mattress)
  • Follow local guidelines. Cefuroxime 1.5 g IV at induction (check allergies and local policy)
  • Remifentanil infusion, fentanyl (10 ml ampoule), dexamethasone and ondansetron
  • Tranexamic acid and cell salvage for large fusions
  • TIVA if neuromonitoring

Induction

  • IV induction supine
  • neuro-tape the tube securely and tape the eyes closed before turning
  • Log-roll prone as a team
  • recheck tube position, ventilation and eyes
  • ketamine or clonidine and magnesium as opioid-sparing adjuncts

Maintenance

  • Maintain with TIVA or volatile plus remifentanil
  • avoid prolonged hypotension
  • Monitor urine output and correct with IV fluids
  • Dexamethasone and ondansetron for PONV
  • ESP catheters for fusions in some units

Emergence

  • Towards the end, titrate fentanyl as the remifentanil is weaned so the patient wakes comfortable

Post-op

Complications

  • Extubation options: remifentanil wake-up, deep extubation, airway exchange (e.g. to a supraglottic airway), or a usual wake-up with minimal stimulation
  • check leg power and sensation early

Analgesia

  • Multimodal analgesia

Destination

  • Arterial line usually removed after a blood gas in recovery
  • Ward, or HDU after a large fusion
