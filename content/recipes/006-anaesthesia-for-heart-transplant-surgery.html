Intra-op

Equipment

  • Arterial line before induction, central line, PA catheter, TOE
  • Inotropes, pulmonary vasodilators (inhaled nitric oxide), blood products

Induction

  • Slow induction with vasopressors ready

Maintenance

  • treat as full stomach if recently ate
  • Avoid raised PVR: oxygenate, avoid hypercapnia and acidosis
  • Support the denervated heart with isoprenaline or pacing after bypass
