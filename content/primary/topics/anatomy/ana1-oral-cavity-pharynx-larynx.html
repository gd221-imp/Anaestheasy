Hard palate

Palatine process of the maxilla (anteriorly), horizontal plates of the palatine bones (posteriorly).

Soft palate

Levator veli palatini, tensor veli palatini, palatoglossus, palatopharyngeus, musculus uvulae.

Tongue

[diagram showing circumvallate, foliate, fungiform and filiform papillae, lingual tonsil and palatine tonsil] ~32 teeth in an adult.

  • Extrinsic muscles (insert tongue from outside): genioglossus, hyoglossus, styloglossus, palatoglossus
  • Intrinsic muscles: inferior & superior longitudinal, transverse, vertical
  • Innervation: motor = CN XII except palatoglossus (CN X); sensory ant ⅔ = CN V3 (lingual nerve); sensory post ⅓ = CN IX (lingual branch); taste ant ⅔ = chorda tympani (CN VII, parasympathetic); taste post ⅓ = CN IX.

Floor of the mouth

Formed by mylohyoid (the muscular diaphragm), reinforced by geniohyoid; digastric and stylohyoid are related suprahyoid muscles.

The Pharynx

Muscular tube.

  • External circular muscles: superior, middle, inferior constrictors → propel food to the oesophagus
  • Inner longitudinal muscles: stylopharyngeus, palatopharyngeus, salpingopharyngeus → elevate the pharynx
  • choanae ↔ nasopharynx → auditory tube → oropharynx → palatopharyngeal arch/soft palate/base of tongue/tonsil → laryngopharynx → cricoid ↔ laryngeal inlet/piriform recess
  • Innervation: pharyngeal plexus (CN IX, X, XI) → motor = CN X/XI; sensory = CN IX + CN V2. CN IX also mediates the gag reflex.

The Larynx

[diagram: median glosso-epiglottic fold, vallecula, epiglottis, tubercle of epiglottis, vestibular fold, aryepiglottic fold, cuneiform & corniculate cartilages, vestibule, ventricle, vocal fold, infraglottic cavity, trachea]

  • 9 cartilages: cricoid (complete ring), thyroid, epiglottis, 2x arytenoid, 2x corniculate, 2x cuneiform (hyoid bone also labelled on the diagram as a landmark, though not conventionally counted among the 9).
  • Musculature: extrinsic (infrahyoid = ↓hyoid & larynx; suprahyoid = ↑) and intrinsic, altering length/tension of the vocal folds (cricothyroid, arytenoids, thyroarytenoid, cricoarytenoid, aryepiglotticus).
  • Vestibular folds contain the vestibular ligament. Superior = vestibule → epiglottis/piriform fossa/vallecula. In between = ventricle, superior to the vocal folds but inferior to the vestibular folds. Infraglottic = vocal folds to cricoid.
  • Innervation = CN X.
  • Superior laryngeal nerve: internal branch = sensory above the vocal cords; external branch = motor to the inferior constrictor + cricothyroid.
  • Recurrent laryngeal nerve: motor to all intrinsic muscles except cricothyroid; sensory below the vocal cords. Particularly vulnerable during thyroidectomy, carotid endarterectomy & anterior triangle operations.

Nerve lesions (numbered on the diagram)

① Complete nerve injury (SLN + RLN) → complete sensory & motor loss.

② SLN internal branch injury → loss of sensation above the vocal folds.

③ SLN external branch injury → paralysis of cricothyroid only.

④ RLN injury → paralysis of all intrinsic muscles except cricothyroid + loss of sensation below the vocal folds.
