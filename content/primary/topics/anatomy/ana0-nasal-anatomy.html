Key functions

Airway patency, humidification, voice, olfaction, defence.

Anatomy

Nasal cavity axis perpendicular to the plane of the face @ 90°. Nasal cavity = lateral wall → turbinates (inf > middle > sup). Septum in midline, hard + soft palate inferiorly, bony posterior wall.

Nose is susceptible to injury due to

Sharply-altered axis, fragile mucosa, large turbinates, ↑vascularity.

[diagram

External nose showing columella, nostril/alae, philtrum, vermilion notch] Large surface area, ↑vascularity, cilia → 3-6ml mucus swallowed/day (?).

Blood supply

[diagram of the lateral nasal wall]

  • olfactory bulb → CN I → superior aspect
  • anterior/posterior ethmoidal artery → nasal mucosa
  • maxillary nerve → CN V → sensory innervation
  • sphenopalatine artery → most of the nasal mucosa
  • pterygopalatine ganglion → secretomotor fibres
  • greater palatine artery → nasal septum

Nerve supply

CN I → smell; CN V → sensation.

Paranasal sinuses

Frontal, maxillary, ethmoid, sphenoid → 'ostia' link the sinuses to the nasal cavity.

  • Functions: reduce weight, vocal resonance, 'crumple zone'.
