Anatomy

Posterior border = T1 vertebra, anterior = manubrium, lateral = 1st rib. 1st rib is the shortest/broadest/most curved rib.

Cervical rib

An extra rib arising from C7.

Thoracic outlet syndrome

Compression of the nerve/artery/vein.

  • Causes: tumour, trauma, congenital abnormality.
  • 95% neurogenic, 1% arterial, 4% venous.
