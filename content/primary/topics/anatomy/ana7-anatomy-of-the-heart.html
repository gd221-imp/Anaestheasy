Anatomy: pericardium fuses with the adventitia of the great vessels superiorly & the central tendon of the diaphragm inferiorly.

  • Inner layer = parietal + visceral, with 2 sinuses = oblique & transverse.
  • Fibrous skeleton of the heart → 3 U-shaped cords → aortic annulus / L & R trigones → mitral annulus (?).

Valves

  • Mitral valve (bileaflet) → mitral annulus, valve leaflets, chordae tendineae, papillary muscles, adjacent myocardium. Margin notes: 'LV 3-4x RV thickness'; 'ASMI ARMS' (?, mnemonic unclear).
  • Aortic valve (tri-leaflet) → LVOT = below the aortic valve; sinuses of Valsalva → ensure coronary perfusion.
  • Tricuspid valve (tri-leaflet) → trabeculae, papillary muscles.
  • Pulmonary valve → smaller leaflets + sinuses than the left side.
