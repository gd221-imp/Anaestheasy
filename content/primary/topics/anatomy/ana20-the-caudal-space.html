Anatomy

Caudal space = 'most distal portion of the epidural space'. [diagram: epidural space, spinal cord, filum terminale, sacrococcygeal membrane, sub-arachnoid space]

Why caudal blocks are more successful in children

  • Landmarks = the two posterior superior iliac spines and the sacral hiatus form an equilateral triangle; the sacral cornua lie either side of the hiatus - easier to feel in children
  • Less epidural fat = ↑spread
  • ↓capacity of the sacral canal in adults (>30ml)
  • More reliably palpable gap @ the sacral membrane, although present in adults too (?)

Armitage formula (0.25% bupivacaine)

Sacro-lumbar = 0.5ml/kg; lumbar (lower thoracic) = 1ml/kg; mid-thoracic = 1.25ml/kg.
