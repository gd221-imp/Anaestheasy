Anatomy — scalp block indications

Awake craniotomy & pin positioning, scalp laceration, greater occipital nerve block for migraine.

Nerves (with landmark & typical volume, 0.5% levobupivacaine)

  • V1: supraorbital (supraorbital notch, level with the eyebrow) — 1ml
  • V1: supratrochlear (1cm medial to supraorbital) — 1ml
  • V2: zygomaticotemporal (anterior group; ½ way between the lateral orbit & tragus) — 2ml
  • V3: auriculotemporal (anterior group; 1cm anterior to the tragus) — 2ml
  • C2/3: greater auricular (mastoid process) — 2ml
  • C2: greater occipital (½ way between the mastoid process & the occipital protuberance, forming a 'V')
  • C2/3: lesser occipital
