Anatomy

Produced by the choroid plexus (~500ml/day (?)).

Pathway: lateral ventricle → 2x foramen of Monro → 3rd ventricle → cerebral aqueduct → 4th ventricle → 2x foramina of Luschka + foramen of Magendie → around the brain & spinal cord.

Reabsorption

90% via the arachnoid villi of the venous sinuses; 10% via the venous system of the spine.
