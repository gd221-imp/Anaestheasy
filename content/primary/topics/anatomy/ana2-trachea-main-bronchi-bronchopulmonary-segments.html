Anatomy

Cricotracheal ligament connects the cricoid to the 1st tracheal ring. Trachea = 12-20 C-shaped rings, ~12cm long, lined by pseudostratified columnar epithelium w/ mucous glands.

  • Right main bronchus is shorter (~25mm) and wider than the left (~50mm) → higher risk of foreign body aspiration.
  • Carina @ T4/5 or the angle of Louis.
  • Lobar bronchi = 3 on the right, 2 on the left.
  • Segmental bronchi = 10 on the right & 8 (?) on the left.

Blood supply

Bronchial arteries (from the inferior thyroid artery, internal thoracic artery, and the aorta) → cervical (6cm) & thoracic (6cm) components (?).

Pleura

Bronchial arteries → veins and pulmonary parts (?) — i.e. bronchial venous drainage partly anastomoses with the pulmonary venous system.

Alveolus

Single layer of squamous epithelium = type I cells, surrounded by a basal lamina; smaller cuboidal cells = type II & III (?). Alveolus & capillary share a fused basement membrane (?). Elastin present throughout the airway. Alveolar surface area = 140m². 'Surfactant' noted in the margin.
