Anatomy

Sensory root: pons → trigeminal (Gasserian) ganglion, in Meckel's cave, in the middle cranial fossa.

Motor root

Pons → [illegible - a short abbreviation, possibly a ganglion or nucleus name, is written here and not confidently legible] → V3 only.

Parasympathetic fibres are 'relayed' via CN VII and CN IX through trigeminal nerve branches:

  • CN VII via the chorda tympani → submandibular ganglion → carried by the lingual nerve (V3) → submandibular & sublingual glands.
  • CN VII (greater petrosal) → pterygopalatine ganglion → branches of V2 (zygomatic → lacrimal nerve of V1) → lacrimal gland and nasal/palatal glands.
  • CN IX (lesser petrosal) → otic ganglion → auriculotemporal nerve (V3) → parotid gland.
