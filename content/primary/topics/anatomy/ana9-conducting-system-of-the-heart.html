Anatomy

SAN is at the junction of the SVC & RA → fastest inherent discharge rate ∴ 'pacemaker' → contains P-cells.

  • Internodal tracts: anterior (of Bachmann), middle (Wenckebach), posterior (Thorel) → conduct to the AVN, which delays the signal (fibrous ring around it).
  • Innervation: sympathetic from the stellate ganglion; SAN & AVN also innervated by the vagus.

Cardiac action potential

Resting membrane potential ~ -90mV.

  • Phase 0: rapid depolarisation.
  • Phase 1: initial repolarisation.
  • Phase 2: plateau.
  • -60mV to -40mV → K+ leakage.
  • -40mV threshold → Ca2+ influx.
  • +10mV → K+ leaving.

Monophasic (pacemaker) action potential

Reaches ~+10mV, phases 0, 3, 4 labelled (phase 4 = -40mV to -60mV, slow diastolic depolarisation).

Arrhythmias

Altered automaticity → ↓SAN activity or ↑ectopic activity; or re-entry.
