Anatomy

A neuron = an electrically excitable cell that communicates with other cells via synapses.

Nuclei (CNS) & ganglia (peripheral) = groupings of cell bodies.

[diagram of a neuron with an axon myelinated by oligodendrocytes/Schwann cells — 'saltatory conduction']

A peripheral nerve, like muscle, has many axons.

Layers: myelin sheath, endoneurium (around individual fibres) → perineurium (around fascicles) → epineurium (outer layer of the peripheral nerve).
