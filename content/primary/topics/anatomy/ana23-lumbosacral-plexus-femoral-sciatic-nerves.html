The plexuses

Lumbosacral plexus = anterior rami of L1-S4, divided into lumbar (L1-L4), sacral (L4-S4), and pudendal (S2-4) plexuses.

Lumbar plexus

[diagram, L1-L5] branches: iliohypogastric, ilioinguinal, genitofemoral, lateral cutaneous nerve of thigh (LCNofT), femoral, obturator → lumbosacral trunk.

Femoral nerve

Enters the femoral triangle. Boundaries: ASIS, inguinal ligament, pubic tubercle (PT); floor = iliacus, pectineus/psoas major (?); borders = sartorius (lateral), adductor longus (medial). Mnemonic: NAVL (nerve, artery, vein, lymphatics).

Sacral plexus

[diagram, S1-S4] branches: superior & inferior gluteal nerves, sciatic nerve → common peroneal & tibial divisions, pudendal nerve.

Sciatic nerve

Divides into tibial & common peroneal nerves at the apex of the popliteal fossa → sural, deep & superficial peroneal nerves.

  • Can block at: parasacral, anterior, Labat, Beck (?), popliteal-sciatic approaches.

Indications for pudendal nerve block

2nd/3rd stage of labour, episiotomy, 1st/2nd degree tear, or pudendal neuralgia.
