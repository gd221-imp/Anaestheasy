Anatomy

The pharynx = a muscular tube connecting the oral & nasal cavities to the larynx & oesophagus. Functions: air conduction, phonation, swallowing.

  • Nasopharynx = skull base → soft palate.
  • Oropharynx = soft palate → epiglottis.
  • Hypopharynx (laryngopharynx) = epiglottis → cricoid.

The pharyngeal constrictors → inferior, middle, superior (thyropharyngeus & cricopharyngeus).

The elevators → stylopharyngeus, salpingopharyngeus, palatopharyngeus.

Innervation → CN X, except stylopharyngeus (CN IX).

Depressor muscles (from C1/2/3)

Omohyoid, sternohyoid (?), sternothyroid.
