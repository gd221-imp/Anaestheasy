Anatomy

The bones = ethmoid, sphenoid, occipital, frontal, temporal.

The fossae = anterior / middle / posterior.

The foramina

  • 1st line = cribriform plate (CN I) + superior orbital fissure (CN III, IV, VI, V1)
  • 2nd line (medial to lateral) = optic canal (CN II), foramen rotundum (V2), foramen ovale (V3), foramen spinosum (MMA), foramen lacerum (ICA)
  • 3rd line = hypoglossal canal (CN XII), internal acoustic meatus (CN VII, VIII), jugular foramen (CN IX, X, XI)
