Anatomy

7 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar vertebrae, plus the fused sacrum/coccyx.

[diagram of a thoracic vertebra labelled

Spinous process, lamina, transverse process, facet for the superior articular process, facet for the head of rib, body, pedicle]

Vertebra comparison (relative to the thoracic vertebra shown)

  • Cervical: bifid spinous processes, foramen in the transverse process, triangular spinal canal.
  • Lumbar: large kidney-shaped vertebral body, shorter & flatter spinous processes, triangular spinal canal.

Spinal cord

Continuation of the medulla oblongata → ends @ L1/2.

Nerve root levels: 8 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar, 5 sacral, 1 coccygeal.

The paravertebral space

Contents = spinal nerve, white & grey rami communicantes, sympathetic chain, intercostal vessels, fatty tissue.
