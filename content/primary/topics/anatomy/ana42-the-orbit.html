Anatomy: walls of the orbit

  • Lateral: zygoma, sphenoid
  • Medial: ethmoid, maxilla, lacrimal, sphenoid
  • Inferior: maxilla, palatine, zygoma
  • Superior: frontal, sphenoid

Superior orbital fissure (SOF)

CN III, IV, VI, V1, sympathetic fibres, superior ophthalmic vein.

Inferior orbital fissure (IOF): V2.

Optic canal: optic nerve, ophthalmic artery.

Autonomic pathways

  • Parasympathetic: Edinger-Westphal nucleus → CN III (inferior division) → ciliary ganglion → short ciliary nerves → pupil constriction, near-focus (accommodation).
  • Sympathetic: hypothalamus → 1° neuron → T1 → 2° neuron → sympathetic chain → superior cervical ganglion → 3° neuron → nasociliary nerve → superior tarsal muscle (?), dilator pupillae.
