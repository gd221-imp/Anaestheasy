Autonomic innervation

Sympathetic supply from T1→T4 (to T5), via the cardiac plexus. CN X → cardiac branches → SAN, AVN.

Note

N.B. in ~10-15% of the population the posterior interventricular artery originates from the left circumflex artery → 'left dominance'.

[diagram of the heart in RAO/LAO projections showing the coronary arteries — RCA, SAN branch, marginal branch, circumflex, LAD, diagonal branches, coronary sinus, obtuse marginal, mitral valve, great cardiac vein, anterior interventricular branch]

Also noted: anterior cardiac vein (RV → RA); thebesian veins (RV & RA, deoxygenated).

Heart wall layers

Endocardium, myocardium, epicardium.

Cardiac output distribution (approximate, at rest): heart ~250ml/min (5%, O2 extraction ~70%); brain ~650ml/min (10-15%); kidneys ~1.2L/min (25%); liver ~1.1L/min (20% (?)).

Coronary blood flow

Right coronary flow is fairly continuous through the cardiac cycle; left coronary flow is predominantly diastolic (systolic dip due to myocardial compression) — graph plots coronary blood flow & aortic BP against time, showing the systole/diastole phases.
