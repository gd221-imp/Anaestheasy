Airway obstruction/protrusion can be graded A, B, C.

Craniocervical articulation

Occiput-atlas-axis (1st and 2nd cervical vertebrae).

  • 5 ligaments: tectorial membrane, cruciform ligament, alar/apical ligaments, anterior & posterior atlanto-occipital membranes.
  • 2 gaps → atlanto-occipital / atlanto-axial → allow flexion/extension.
