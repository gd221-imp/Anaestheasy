Anatomy

[diagram: lumbar plexus and sacral plexus roots, with anterior and posterior leg nerve-distribution diagrams for both legs]

Lumbar plexus

Subcostal, iliohypogastric, ilioinguinal, genitofemoral, lateral cutaneous nerve of thigh (LCNofT), femoral nerve, obturator nerve, lumbosacral trunk.

Anterior leg diagram labels

Femoral nerve, obturator nerve, branches to vastus medialis/intermedius/lateralis, saphenous nerve → adductor canal, and further labelled branches around the adductor canal that are abbreviated and hard to read with confidence (?).

Sacral plexus

Superior & inferior gluteal nerves, nerve to quadratus femoris (L4/L5/S1), sciatic nerve (L4→S3), pudendal nerve (S2→S4).

Posterior leg diagram labels

Sciatic nerve → tibial nerve & common peroneal nerve → superficial peroneal, deep peroneal, sural nerve, and terminal branches at the foot (calcaneal branch, plantar branches).
