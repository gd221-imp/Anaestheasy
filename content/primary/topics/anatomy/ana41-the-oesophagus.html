Anatomy

A 'fibromuscular tube', 25cm long. Begins at the cricopharyngeus @ C6, ends at the oesophageal hiatus @ T10.

  • UOS @ pharyngo-oesophageal junction → striated muscle from cricopharyngeus, varies with tone (?).
  • LOS @ GOJ → 'physiological sphincter' due to the acute angle (?).

Layers (where smooth muscle)

Adventitia → muscularis externa → submucosa → mucosa.

Four physiological constrictions in the oesophageal lumen: arch of the aorta, left main bronchus, cricoid, diaphragmatic hiatus. (Right side vs left side noted for one of these, e.g. azygos vein crossing on the right (?).)

The azygos vein receives blood from the oesophagus, thoracic & abdominal walls, bronchi, trachea → drains into the SVC.

Lymph

75% via the thoracic duct, 25% via the right lymphatic duct.

The thoracic duct: T12 → C7, arising from the cisterna chyli.

  • Complications: Virchow's node, chylothorax.
