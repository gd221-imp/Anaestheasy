Anatomy

The lower respiratory tract develops from the anterior foregut.

Trachea

  • Mucosa: ciliated pseudostratified columnar epithelium & lamina propria & elastic lamina (ciliated/goblet/brush/basal cells); longitudinal muscle fibres
  • Submucosa: connective tissue with smooth muscle/glands/vessels/nerves/lymphatics
  • Outer/adventitia: vessels/nerves/fat; incomplete (C-shaped) cartilage rings

Bronchi

  • Mucosa: simple columnar epithelium; spiral smooth muscle (bronchoconstriction)
  • Submucosa: connective tissue with vessels/nerves
  • Outer/adventitia: cartilage rings become plates

Bronchioles

  • Mucosa: goblet cells, bundles of smooth muscle, simple cuboidal (?) epithelium; no glands
  • Submucosa: no glands
  • Outer/adventitia: no cartilage

Alveolar surface area

140m².
