Anatomy

4 subdivisions.

  • Superior: superior to the pericardium. Three arteries arise from the aortic arch → innominate/brachiocephalic trunk (→ RT CCA & RT subclavian), L CCA, L subclavian. SVC → innominate (brachiocephalic) vein → R & L subclavian veins/IJ (thoracic duct joins the venous system at the junction of the subclavian vein/IJ on the left side).
  • Anterior: thin segment including fatty tissue, lymph nodes & the thymus gland.
  • Middle: heart & roots of the great vessels within the pericardium.
  • Posterior: behind the pericardium → descending aorta, oesophagus, splanchnic nerves, azygos vein, thoracic duct.

3 'traversers'

  • Phrenic nerve (C3-5) → anterior to the hilum → diaphragm.
  • Thoracic duct → root of the neck (?).
  • Oesophagus → posterior to the trachea → anterior to the aorta → diaphragm @ T10.
