Anatomy

[diagram spanning the whole page tracing the major branches of the aorta from the neck down to the lower limb]

Carotid sheath

ICA, CN X, CCA, IJV.

Upper body arterial branches (from the aortic arch): innominate/brachiocephalic trunk → right common carotid + right subclavian artery; left common carotid artery; left subclavian artery. Further labelled branches include the vertebral artery, external/internal carotid artery, and upper limb vessels (axillary → brachial → radial recurrent & muscular branches, superior ulnar collateral) [several of these upper-limb/neck arterial branch labels are cramped in the original and hard to read with full confidence - worth checking against your source].

Venous drainage

Right & left brachiocephalic veins → SVC → RA (azygos vein joins the SVC).

Thoracic aorta (T4 to T12)

Branches include bronchial, oesophageal, posterior intercostal, subcostal, superior phrenic arteries.

Abdominal aorta (T12 to L4)

Coeliac axis (CA), renal arteries, suprarenal artery, superior mesenteric artery (SMA), gonadal arteries (√ L3-L4), inferior mesenteric artery (IMA), hepatic veins, left renal vein/LSRV, gonadal veins.

Bifurcation @ L4 into common iliac arteries → internal iliac artery (pelvic organs) + external iliac artery (gives the inferior epigastric and deep circumflex iliac arteries) → femoral artery, with branches: superficial epigastric, superficial circumflex iliac, superficial & deep external pudendal, profunda femoris, descending genicular → popliteal artery.

Venous return

Deep: anterior & posterior tibial veins → popliteal vein → femoral vein. Superficial: dorsal venous arch → great saphenous vein (drains into the femoral vein at the saphenofemoral junction) and small saphenous vein (drains into the popliteal vein).
