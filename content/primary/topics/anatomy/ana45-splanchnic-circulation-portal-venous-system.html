Anatomy

Coeliac trunk (foregut) — splenic vein; superior mesenteric vein (midgut); inferior mesenteric vein (hindgut) → all drain into the hepatic portal vein.

Regulation

Myogenic — stretch-responsive Na+ channels.

In times of stress, up to ~1L of blood can be mobilised from the liver/GIT.

Portal hypertension

Portal pressure >5mmHg, but clinically significant if >10mmHg.

  • Causes: pre-hepatic (e.g. portal vein thrombosis); intrahepatic - pre-sinusoidal (schistosomiasis, the commonest cause worldwide), sinusoidal (cirrhosis, the commonest cause in the UK); post-hepatic (Budd-Chiari, right heart failure, constrictive pericarditis).

Hepatic arterial buffer response (HABR)

Adenosine = main mediator.
