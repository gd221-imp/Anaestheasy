Anatomy

[diagram of the larynx from above, showing the epiglottis, hyoid, median thyrohyoid ligament, thyrohyoid membrane, vallecula, tubercle of the epiglottis, arytenoid, cuneiform & corniculate cartilages, cricoid]

Innervation

CN X.

  • Superior laryngeal nerve: external branch → motor to cricothyroid; internal branch → sensory.
  • Recurrent laryngeal nerve → sensory below the vocal cords; motor to all intrinsic muscles except cricothyroid.

Blood supply

Superior laryngeal artery (SLA) & vein (SLV); inferior laryngeal artery (ILA) & vein (ILV).

Vocal cord movement

  • Abduction: posterior cricoarytenoid
  • Adduction: lateral cricoarytenoid
  • Shortening: thyroarytenoid & vocalis
  • Lengthening: cricothyroid
