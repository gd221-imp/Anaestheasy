Anatomy

Anterior boundary = skin/subcutaneous tissue (?); posterior boundary = anterior parietal peritoneum/pleura (?).

Anterior abdominal wall: superior = costal margin, inferior = inguinal ligament/pubic bone, lateral = [illegible - a landmark is named here but not confidently legible], medial = linea alba.

3 muscles laterally

External oblique, internal oblique, transversus abdominis.

Medially: paired rectus sheath muscles separated by the linea alba.

T6-L1 nerves (plus subcostal, iliohypogastric, ilioinguinal) travel between internal oblique & transversus abdominis. They pierce the rectus sheath laterally → run posterior to rectus abdominis → exit superficially, penetrating the muscle.

TAP block

T7-L1, 15-20ml. Complication = spread to the femoral nerve.
