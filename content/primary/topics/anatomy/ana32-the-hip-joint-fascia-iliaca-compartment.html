Anatomy

The hip joint = 'articulation between the femur & pelvis', a ball & socket joint.

  • Supero-anterior capsule → femoral nerve (L2-L4) / obturator nerve (L2-L4).
  • Postero-inferior capsule → sciatic nerve (L4-S3).
  • Overlying skin → lateral cutaneous nerve of thigh, iliohypogastric, genitofemoral, superior cluneal nerves.

Fascia iliaca compartment

  • Anterior = fascia iliaca/fascia lata
  • Posterior = iliacus & psoas major
  • Lateral = sartorius
  • Superior = inguinal ligament
  • Inferior = iliacus, pectineus (?)
  • Medial = adductor longus (?)
