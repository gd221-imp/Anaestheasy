Anatomy

Trachea → C6 to T4.

  • Right main bronchus: 3cm long, 30° from the midline.
  • Left main bronchus: 5cm long, 45° from the midline.

Bronchopulmonary segments

  • Left: UL = apicoposterior, anterior, superior lingular, inferior lingular (one abbreviation unclear in the original (?)) = 4 segments. LL = superior, anteromedial basal, lateral basal, posterior basal = 4 segments.
  • Right: UL = apical, posterior, anterior = 3. ML = lateral, medial = 2. LL = superior, medial basal, anterior basal, lateral basal, posterior basal = 5.
  • Total = 18 bronchopulmonary segments.
