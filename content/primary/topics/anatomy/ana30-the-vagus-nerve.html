Anatomy

Motor, sensory, and autonomic functions.

  • Parasympathetic: dorsal nucleus of the vagus.
  • Medulla: nucleus ambiguus = motor fibres; nucleus tractus solitarius = sensory fibres.

Course

Exits @ jugular foramen → carotid sheath → neck branches (pharyngeal, superior laryngeal, recurrent laryngeal) → thoracic inlet → right vagus → posterior trunk; left vagus → anterior trunk → oesophageal hiatus → branches to the oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine. Also gives cardiac & pulmonary branches in the thorax.
