  • Tibial: between the medial malleolus & Achilles tendon
  • Saphenous: anterior to the medial malleolus
  • Superficial peroneal: anterolateral ankle
  • Sural: between the lateral malleolus & Achilles tendon
  • Deep peroneal: lateral to extensor hallucis tendon

(Saphenous, superficial peroneal & sural nerves are grouped as superficial.)
