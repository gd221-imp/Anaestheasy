Anatomy

Ventral rami C1 → C4, lying on the surface of middle scalene.

[diagram: C1-C5 with branches]

  • Lesser occipital, greater auricular → superficial/sensory branches
  • Nerve to geniohyoid & thyrohyoid (from C1, travelling with CN XII)
  • Transverse cervical nerve
  • Ansa cervicalis (superior & inferior roots, C1-3) → supplies the infrahyoid strap muscles only (sternohyoid, sternothyroid, omohyoid; thyrohyoid via C1 with CN XII) - not the larynx
  • Supraclavicular nerves
  • Phrenic nerve (C3, C4, C5)
