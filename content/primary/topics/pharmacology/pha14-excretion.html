Excretion - Part 2

Routes

Liver (e.g. rocuronium/vecuronium/atracurium), lungs (inhalational agents/ethanol), skin, kidneys

  • Atracurium → excreted by Hoffman degradation

Kidney excretion

  • Glomerular filtration → Bowman's capsule → hydrostatic pressure, free drug (affected by renal blood flow & protein binding)
  • Tubular secretion → active transport → weak acids/bases
  • Tubular re-absorption → lipophilic/non-ionised drugs
  • Remifentanil is metabolised by non-specific plasma and tissue esterases → organ-independent, small Vd (↓CSHT); suxamethonium by plasma cholinesterase (butyrylcholinesterase); esmolol is the one hydrolysed by red cell esterases
  • Acidic compounds → ↑binding to albumin

Note

N.B. paracetamol → glucuronidation ~55%, sulphation ~30%, CYP450 (small amount, ~5-10%) → toxic intermediate NAPQI → conjugated by glutathione → N-acetylcysteine as the hepatic rescue treatment
