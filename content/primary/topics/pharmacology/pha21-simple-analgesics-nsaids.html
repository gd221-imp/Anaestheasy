Simple analgesics

  • Paracetamol → neonate 10mg/kg QDS / child 15mg/kg QDS / adult (<50kg) 500mg-1g QDS
  • Main metabolite = glucuronide conjugates
  • Salicylate levels: <500 mild, 500-700 moderate, >700 severe (units not specified in source)
  • Aspirin → PO: 300-600mg QDS (max ~4g/day) / PR: 480-900mg QDS → affects bone marrow
  • Toxicity → mixed respiratory alkalosis/metabolic acidosis
  • Avoid in under-16s (paediatrics) → risk of Reye's syndrome

Arachidonic acid pathway (paracetamol/aspirin sites of action)

  • Cell membrane → Phospholipase A2 → Arachidonic acid
  • Aspirin inhibits PGH2 synthase (COX site), irreversibly
  • Paracetamol inhibits PGH2 synthase (peroxidase site/COX-3) - centrally acting, less effective peripherally due to local peroxide levels
  • Arachidonic acid → PGG2 → PGH2 → Thromboxane (platelet aggregation) / PGE2, PGF, PGD2 (labour, uterine effects) / Prostacyclin (GI protection, regulates renal blood flow)
  • Arachidonic acid → Lipoxygenase → Leukotrienes → bronchospasm

COX-1

Constitutive; COX-2 = inducible (most beneficial anti-inflammatory effects come from COX-2 inhibition); COX-3 also referenced
