Absorption → site of administration → plasma

  • Depends on: drug formulation, route, physicochemical properties, perfusion

Definition

Bioavailability → fraction of a drug that reaches systemic circulation intact, and is therefore able to act at its site of action

  • Absolute bioavailability = compared to IV
  • Relative bioavailability = compared to another formulation, if IV isn't possible (e.g. capsule vs tablet)
  • BF = AUC(oral)/AUC(IV)
  • Compare using AUC of two [drug] vs time graphs

Crossing the cell membrane - 3 methods

  1. Diffusion (non-polar drugs)
  2. Channels
  3. Carrier-mediated (pinocytosis) → 1° active transport or facilitated transport
