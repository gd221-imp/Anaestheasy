Definition

Bind at binding site → conformational change → recognition and signal amplification/specificity → cascade of intracellular response; spatial amplification - one ligand to receptor → cascade of intracellular response; specificity - desensitisation via downregulation, internalisation, phosphorylation

Classification

RECEPTORS → Cell-surface receptors / Enzyme-linked receptors / Intracellular receptors

Cell-surface receptors

  • GPCR → 7 transmembrane domains, α/β/γ subunits, Gs/Gi/Gq intracellular G-protein → signal amplification (one ligand → cascade of intracellular response), specificity
  • Ligand-gated ion channels
  • Ionotropic (fast) → single protein, non-selective, e.g. nicotinic ACh receptor (2 gated subunits, GABA-A - 5 subunits total)
  • P2X receptor → 2 pore x 3 subunits, extracellular ATP
  • Glutamate receptor (ionotropic) → non-selective, NMDA/AMPA/kainate
  • TRP channels → respond to noxious/irritant drugs (e.g. capsaicin)
  • Voltage-gated ion channels → Na+ subunit = channel pore; Na+/Ca2+/K+ channels; LA → inactivate Na+ ion channels

Enzyme-linked receptors

  • Receptor Tyrosine Kinases → growth factor, cell-surface → cell adhesion/migration
  • Receptor Serine/Threonine kinase → TGF-β
  • Receptor Guanylyl cyclase → ANP/BNP (natriuretic peptide)
  • Cytoplasmic tyrosine-linked → growth hormone, cytokine
  • Receptor phosphatase

Intracellular receptors

  • Plasma membrane-associated → ligand binding induces target transcription of DNA; co-opts intracellular enzymes e.g. cytokine/TNF/IL-1 → important in post-op inflammation
  • Cytoplasmic → soluble guanylate cyclase e.g. cGMP/NO → vessel dilatation
  • Nuclear receptor → ligand-activated transcription factor e.g. steroid, thyroid hormone, vit D
