Uterotonics

  • 1st line: Oxytocin → acts via GPCR (oxytocin/vasopressin receptor family) → IV/IM administration; side effects include flushing, N&V, tachycardia, water retention
  • 2nd line: Ergometrine → non-selective α1/5-HT/dopamine receptor agonist → nausea & vomiting, coronary vasospasm, hypertension
  • 3rd line: Carboprost → PGF2α analogue, given IM, up to a max total dose → can cause bronchospasm (contraindicated in asthma), ↑HR
  • Misoprostol → PGE1 analogue, given PR/oral, oxytocic and gastroprotective effects → can cause HTN, flushing, bronchospasm

Tocolytics

  • Nifedipine → Ca2+ channel blocker → ↓SVR, relaxes uterine smooth muscle
  • Ritodrine/Terbutaline → β2 agonists → cause maternal tachycardia and hyperglycaemia
  • Indomethacin → non-selective COX inhibitor → risk of foetal ductus arteriosus constriction/closure and oligohydramnios
  • Emergency tocolysis → volatile anaesthetic agents, nitrates (e.g. GTN)

Note

N.B. Ergometrine causes sustained myometrial contraction/vasoconstriction; oxytocin causes rhythmic myometrial contraction
