Unwanted Drug Effects

Definition

ADR = any noxious or unintended reaction to a drug given at a standard dose/route

Classification

  1. Reactions that can occur in ANYONE - dose-related, augmented
  2. Reactions that occur only in susceptible individuals - not dose-related, bizarre (includes intolerance, idiosyncrasy)
  3. Allergies - immunologically mediated

Drug Interactions

  1. Pharmaceutical Incompatibility → neutralisation, precipitation, chelation, absorption
  2. Pharmacokinetic Interactions → absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination
  3. Pharmacodynamic Interactions → summation, synergism, potentiation, antagonism
  • e.g. N2O + Sevo (summation), remi + propofol (synergism), NMB & Mg2+ (potentiation), neostigmine & NMB (antagonism)

Electrolyte interactions

  • ↓K+ → ↑cardiac excitability, ↓arrhythmia threshold (caused by diuretics, insulin); ↑K+ effect seen with suxamethonium, ACEI
  • ↓Na+ → potentiates LA (caused by sulphonylureas); relevant to mannitol, NaHCO3
  • ↓Mg2+ → cardiac arrhythmias (caused by laxatives); causes prolonged NMB
