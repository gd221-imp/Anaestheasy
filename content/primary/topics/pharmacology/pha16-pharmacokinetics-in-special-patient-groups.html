Pharmacokinetic principles in different patient groups (Neonates / Elderly / Pregnancy / Obesity / Critically ill)

Absorption

  • Neonates: prolonged gastric emptying = ↑proportion absorbed; ↓gastric acidity
  • Elderly: ↓gastric emptying/motility
  • Pregnancy: ↑absorption in stomach & ↓in intestines
  • Obesity: ↑gut perfusion (?)
  • Critically ill: ↓GI perfusion in favour of vital organs; intestinal atrophy; motility dysfunction

Distribution

  • Neonates: TBW=80%; ↓fat & ↑BBB permeability; ↓protein binding
  • Elderly: ↓TBW; ↑fat, ↓muscle; ↓albumin
  • Pregnancy: ↑TBW & fat = ↑Vd; dilution of albumin & ↑free drug; ↑fatty acid competition
  • Obesity: ↑Vd for lipophilic drugs
  • Critically ill: pH changes; ↑interstitial fluid; ↓oncotic pressure/plasma proteins → ↑Vd for hydrophilic drugs

Metabolism

  • Neonates: immature enzymes; ↓enzyme activity
  • Elderly: ↓hepatic blood flow
  • Pregnancy: ↑cardiac output → ↑hepatic blood flow; ↑enterohepatic circulation; placental enzymes
  • Obesity: ↑phase II reactions
  • Critically ill: ↓hepatic blood flow; ↓enzyme activity (improved by enteral feeding)

Elimination

  • Neonates: GFR 20-40% of adult
  • Elderly: ↓GFR
  • Obesity: eGFR overestimated when using actual weight - use ideal body weight
  • Critically ill: ↓GFR

Interindividual variation in drug response

  • Pseudocholinesterase → variant enzyme → succinylcholine apnoea → tested by dibucaine number & fluoride number (used for testing plasma cholinesterase activity)
  • P-glycoprotein → ABC transporter → prevents drug movement in the direction you want it → inhibited by amiodarone/verapamil/ciclosporin
  • Solute carrier → transports neurotransmitters
  • Receptor variation → GPCR variation e.g. β1, μ-opioid receptor
  • Malignant hyperthermia → RYR1 (ryanodine receptor) variants are found in most (~50-70%) MH-susceptible families (CACNA1S in ~1%) → ↑Ca2+ release; dantrolene = ryanodine receptor inhibitor
