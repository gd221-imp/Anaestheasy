Biological potential

Sine waves → complex waveforms can be analysed using Fourier analysis

(a recording system needs: electrodes, amplifier, ADC, display unit)

  • ECG → measured potential size depends on mass of excitable tissue & extent of mass separating heart from recording electrode. 1-2mV & 3-100Hz
  • EEG → Beta (awake & concentrating, 12-25Hz) / alpha / theta / delta (3Hz, slow wave sleep). 50uV & 3-100Hz
  • EMG → 50uV to 30mV & 5-100Hz

*Electrodes → Ag/AgCl → poor contact reduced by adhesive mount → conductive gel with KCl- → reduces moisture trapping & chemical changes.

Examples

  • peripheral nerve stimulator → 50-80mA & freq 1-10Hz
  • needle nerve stimulator → <2mA; if stimulating @ <0.3mA tip may be intraneural
  • evoked potentials → e.g. visual
  • cardiac pacemaker
  • deep brain stimulator → for movement disorders → placed near the thalamus.
