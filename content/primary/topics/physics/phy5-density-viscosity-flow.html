Density: ρ=m/V ; ρ∝1/V ∝ pressure; specific gravity of gas = ρgas ÷ ρair (1.29kg m-3) >1 = more dense than air.

Viscosity

Tendency of a fluid to resist flow (measured in poise (P)) (η) ∝ 1/T ∝ pressure

Newtonian fluid → viscosity of fluid is unaffected by shear/tangential stresses on it = solely dependent on T C & pressure

Flow

Movement of a volume from A→B per unit time (m3/s)

Hagen-Poiseuille equation: R = 8ηL/πr^4

  • laminar → viscosity = important → @ a critical velocity flow changes from laminar to turbulent. Q = ΔPr^4π/8Lη (resistance is constant) - a tube will produce laminar flow when orifice = diameter > length ⇒ turbulent flow
  • turbulent → density = important ∝r^2 and √ΔP & ∝1/ρ = r^2√ΔP/Lρ
  • measured directly or indirectly by looking @ change in pressure across a fixed orifice.
  • Flowmeter → constant orifice w/ variable pressure = pneumotachograph - known flow rate (calibrated for a specific gas) e.g. oxygen
  • '' pressure '' '' orifice = rotameter - bobbin of fixed size moves up & down in tapered glass e.g. PEFM ⇒ 2-5% accurate

Reynolds number

Density x velocity x diameter / viscosity. <2000 = laminar, 2000-4000 = mixed/transitional, >4000 = turbulent

Gas cylinder colours

O2 = stored as liquid; N2O from heating NH4 to 240C; Entonox - from Poynting effect

Helium = ↓ρ but ↑η than N2 → Heliox used in upper airway obstruction → black cylinder with brown & white shoulders.

95%O2+5%CO2 ⇒ black w/ grey & white quartered shoulders, or just grey if pure CO2
