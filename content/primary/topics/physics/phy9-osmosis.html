• Osmosis → movement of solvent across a semi-permeable membrane, hypotonic to hypertonic
  • Osmotic pressure → hydrostatic pressure within a container separated by a semi-permeable membrane
  • once reached, osmosis stops → irrespective of hypertonicity
  • Van't Hoff equation → pi = iMRT (pi = osmotic pressure, i = Van't Hoff factor, M = molarity, R = universal gas constant, T = temperature (K))
  • Reverse osmosis → pressure pushes solvent against the concentration gradient
  • Osmole → no. of moles contributing to total osmotic pressure (NaCl → Na+/Cl- = 2 osmoles)
  • Osmolality = osmoles per kg of solvent - osmolarity = osmoles per litre (affected by T C)
  • Tonicity is the osmotic property of a solution pertaining to a specific semi-permeable membrane.

e.g. Mannitol → freely filtered & not reabsorbed → ↑osmolality.
