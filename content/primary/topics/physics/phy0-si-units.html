Base SI Units

  • Second = duration of a certain no. of oscillations of a Caesium-133 atom
  • Mole = amount of substance containing same no. of particles as atoms in 0.012kg of Carbon-12
  • Metre = length travelled by light in a vacuum in 1/300 000 000 of a second
  • Ampere = current in two parallel conductors, a metre apart, in a vacuum of infinite length, producing a force of 2x10^-7 N/m
  • Candela = luminous intensity of monochromatic light
  • Kelvin = defined (since 2019) by fixing the Boltzmann constant (formerly 1/273.16 of the triple point of water)
  • Kilogram = defined (since 2019) by fixing the Planck constant (formerly the prototype kg held in Sèvres); mole by the Avogadro constant, ampere by the elementary charge
