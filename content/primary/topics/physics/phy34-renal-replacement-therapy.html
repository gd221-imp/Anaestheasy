Components

'haemofilter' - man-made semi-permeable membrane → made from cellulose, polysulphone or polyamide.

Types

  1. Ultrafiltration - removal of plasma water → corrects volume overload
  2. Haemofiltration - ↑ultrafiltrate volume (due to ↑pore size) with sterile replacement solution administered - works by convection
  3. Haemodialysis - countercurrent system with dialysate solution passing on the non-blood side of the membrane - works by diffusion
  • Continuous - runs 24hr/day → ↓CVS instability, ↑efficiency of solute removal, used in ITU patients
  • Intermittent - high flow → ~4hrs, with a multi-filter & circuit

CVVHF

Double lumen catheter in a major vein, uses convection

CVVHDF - CVVHF + countercurrent dialysate flow, uses convection & diffusion

CAVH - arterial & venous lines, driven by patient's own BP, uses convection

'Dose'

35ml/kg/hr = volume of blood 'cleared'

Blood flow usually 80-240ml/min
