Inductance = production of electrical current when a conductor is in motion within a magnetic field.

Capacitance = two conductors separated by an insulator. Q=VC / V=Q/C

1A = 1 coulomb per second = 6.24x10^18 electrons.

Power = energy dissipated per unit time → product of charge & voltage in time.

Damage to human body by current (mA)

<1 = imperceptible, 5 = painful, 8 = burns, 15 = tetany, 50 = paralysis, 100 = VF

Current density

Current/area → 50-100uA for microshock (↓current but at ↓area)

  • leakage current = small current that flows from equipment → earth.

Equipment Classification

Must have ↑current to earth to break a fuse

  • Class I → green & yellow earth wire → protects with 'live' casing. Microshock = equipment inserted into the body.
  • Class II → double (or reinforced) insulation, no earth wire needed
  • Class III → SELV (<24V to power) - doesn't prevent but ↓shock

Type B

Max patient leakage = 100uA in normal condition, 500uA under a single-fault condition (the limits relate to normal vs single fault, not Class I vs II)

  • Type BF: transformer & floating (isolated) patient circuit = non-earthed; same limits as B (100uA normal / 500uA single fault)
  • Type CF: max leakage 10uA normal / 50uA single fault - safe for direct cardiac connection (e.g. ECG leads, pressure transducers).

Current operated earth leakage circuit breaker → monitors live & neutral wire → relay circuit breaker → faulty circuit → relay trips → breaks the circuit.
