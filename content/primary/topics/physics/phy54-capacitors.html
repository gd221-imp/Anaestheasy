Definition

Capacitor → device that stores charge

C = Q/V (capacitance = charge/voltage; 1 farad = 1 coulomb per volt)

  1. Electron flow onto one plate → -ve charge → drives electrons off the adjacent plate
  2. ↳ when 'full' = no further current → V rises exponentially
  3. ↳ current can only flow if polarity is reversed (plates are separated by a dielectric)

∴ AC > DC, ↑Hz > ↓Hz (due to ↓time available for decay)
