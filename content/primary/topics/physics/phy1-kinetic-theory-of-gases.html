Brownian motion

The observed constant chaotic movement of fluids (particles)

  • Postulates: space between particles > particles themselves; particles move randomly; collisions are elastic; no attraction/repulsion
  • Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution = distribution of probability that a random particle will have a given speed → ↑heat ⇒ ↑energy
  • @ a given temp, average kinetic energy of particles across all gases is the same
  • Kinetic energy = 1/2 mv^2 ∴ ↑molecular wt = ↓ave. speed (m ∝ 1/v)
  • Force = mass x acceleration → pressure = cumulative force/area ∴ pressure↑ by ↑freq of collisions or ↓area

Gas Laws (overview table)

  • Boyle's Law: constant = T C, change = ↓m3, consequence = ↑kPa
  • Charles' Law: constant = kPa, change = ↑↓T C, consequence = ↑↓m3
  • Guy-Lussac's Law: constant = m3, change = ↑↓T C, consequence = ↑↓kPa
