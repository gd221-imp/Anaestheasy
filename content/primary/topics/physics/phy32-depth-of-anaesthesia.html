Depth of anaesthesia components

Hypnosis, Amnesia, Anti-nociception, Reflex suppression.

Non-specific measurement → clinical (PRST score), MAC/Cp50

Specific measurement → EEG-based: BIS, entropy, AER (auditory evoked response)

→ Non-EEG based: isolated forearm technique, lower oesophageal sphincter (LOS) contractility, frontalis muscle activity, HR/ECG variability, or RS (?) [abbreviation unclear]

Bispectral Index (BIS) → limitation

Unknown effect of pre-existing neuropathology.

  • analysis = fast Fourier transformation (converts complex wave → simple waves) → power spectrum → phase spectrum
  • artefact detection → from external sources (e.g. diathermy) or invasive/internal changes; uses spectral edge frequency (95%)

Entropy → measure of the level of disorder of the EEG → ↑=awake & ↓=asleep (RE range > SE range)

  • analysis = Fourier transformation → normalised frequency spectrum → Shannon entropy (0→1) → Entropy value reduces (0-100)

(Limitations: cerebral atrophy = ↑difference between RE/SE; affected by ketamine and nitrous)

  • similar numbers to BIS → RE & SE values are closer together during GA & further apart when awake.

Auditory Evoked Potentials → electrodes (vertex & mastoid), stimulus @ 70dB, 512-1024 sweeps (1-2 mins) @ 6Hz

  • latency & amplitude of peaks & troughs → AER index, with filtering, Pa & Nb waves → interpeak amplitude & latency.
  • waveform components: brainstem, early cortical, late cortical waves.

(Limitation: intracerebral pathway lesions affect interpretation)
