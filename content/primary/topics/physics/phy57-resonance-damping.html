Resonance → condition in which an object is subjected to an oscillating force with a frequency close to its natural frequency ↳ ↑amplitude @ this point

Natural frequency → the frequency of oscillation an object will adopt freely when set in motion or supplied w/ energy

Damping → ↓amplitude of an oscillation as a result of energy loss to friction or other resistive forces

Damping coefficient → 0 (none)

1 (critical)

Zero damping → theoretical situation where a system oscillates in response to a step change in input & amplitude does not diminish with time (but energy is always lost, even in a vacuum)

Under-damped → (damping coefficient 0-1, i.e. anything below critical) → output value oscillates around a baseline, eventually settling @ a new value

Over-damped → >1 → does not oscillate but falls slowly to the new value

Critical damping → allows most rapid attainment of new input value w/ no overshoot (=1)

Optimal damping → most suitable (0.64), combination of rapid response & minimal overshoot
