Decontamination → process of removing contaminants to prevent infection/reaction

↳ Cleaning (removing foreign bodies) - by steam, ultrasound bath, or washing

↳ Disinfection - free of pathogens, except bacterial spores

↳ Sterilisation - free of pathogens, including bacterial spores

Disinfection methods

Glutaraldehyde 2%, alcohol 60-80%, chlorhexidine 0.5-5%, H2O2 (peroxide) - all chemical disinfectants (?)

Sterilisation methods

Ethylene oxide, glutaraldehyde 2%, autoclave, γ radiation, gas plasma
