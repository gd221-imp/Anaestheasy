Operational amplifiers

↑electrical signal by 10^6, ↑resistance (10^6 ohms) so as to not draw current from the upstream circuit (can also integrate a signal)

  • Common mode rejection ratio: inverting & non-inverting differential inputs → neutralise upstream circuit noise
  • CMRR of 10^4 = a signal of 10000mV is needed before 1mV enters the op amp.
  • Inputs → single: capacitance coupling → active transducer (from a Wheatstone bridge) - one input monitors, the other inverts
  • differential: cancels out common noise, a smaller differential will amplify (a good amplifier = ↑CMRR)
  • Gain = ratio of amplifier output to signal output (dB)

Analogue to digital conversion

  • points on the analogue sine wave are sampled @ a chosen frequency
  • each sample has a digital value
  • a low pass filter smooths the output to a facsimile of the original sine wave.

Safety measures

  • floating circuit → no earthing
  • DC
  • opto-isolating circuit → isolated from patient, connected to earthed amplifier
  • screening shelters ECG signal carrying leads from capacitance/inductance coupling from mains currents.
