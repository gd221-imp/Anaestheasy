Invasive BP Measurement

  • normal waveform: systolic pressure, anacrotic slope, dicrotic notch, diastolic pressure (also shows over-damped & under-damped waveform examples)
  • anacrotic slope = myocardial contractility & aortic component; systolic component = stroke volume
  • position of dicrotic notch = peripheral vascular resistance (lower notch = ↓resistance)
  • systolic time ∝ myocardial O2 demand; diastolic time ∝ myocardial O2 supply
  • swing during IPPV can be seen on the trace

(arterial cannulae are parallel-sided but wall flow can affect distal parts of the limb)

Components

  • fluid filled system → NaCl, constant flow 2-4ml/hr @ 300mmHg, ~120cm tubing length
  • pressure transducer → silicone diaphragm, Wheatstone bridge
  • arterial cannula (20-22G): ↓length = ↑natural frequency (?) & ↑length = ↓natural frequency (exact relationship as written is unclear, worth checking against source)

(optimal damping coefficient = 0.67)

  • Square wave test → flush produces a square wave; if optimally damped = ~1-2 oscillations before settling to baseline.
  • Zeroing eliminates offset drift / an aneroid manometer eliminates gradient drift
  • Transducer @ level of the left ventricle (mid-axillary line) → 7.5mmHg difference for every 10cm the transducer is moved up or down.

Non-Invasive BP Measurement

  • phase I - pressure @ return of pulse = systolic (Korotkoff's phases); phase V - pressure @ disappearance of sound = diastolic
  • sphygmomanometer (mercury/aneroid) / oscillometry (von Recklinghausen, auto-MAP) / automated (USS, microphone, oscillet)

Mean BP = (systolic + 2xdiastolic BP)/3, or = diastolic BP + 1/3(systolic-diastolic BP)

  • DINAMAP (oscillometric) - HR displayed = median value; Finapres = Peñáz volume-clamp technique using finger photoplethysmography (not Doppler).
