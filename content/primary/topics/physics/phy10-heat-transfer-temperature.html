Temp dependent enzymatic reactions

T C↑ ∴ kinetic energy↑ ∴ ↑chance of substrate-enzyme collision. T C↑↑↑: enzyme denaturation ∴ ↓reactions. (optimal temp ~37C)

  • Energy = the ability to do work
  • heat energy = total energy of molecular motion, dependent on kinetic energy/state/mass
  • temperature = measure of average kinetic energy of atoms (T C(K) ∝ Ke)
  • Heat capacity = specific heat capacity x mass
  • specific heat capacity = the amount of heat energy to raise the temp of a 1kg mass by 1K

C = ΔQ/mΔT(K); water = 4.1813 J/g/K (1kcal/kg/C)

Heat transfer

CONDUCTION → losses in OT related to ↓skin/surface area contact (also affected by vasodilation from anaesthetic agents (?))

CONVECTION → laminar flow changes to turbulent → 30% of heat loss; exposed body cavities, depends on humidity, usually ~50%

RADIATION → temperature difference between theatre & skin. 40% of heat loss - doesn't require matter, any substance >0K emits radiation. *Stefan-Boltzmann Law: E=σT^4

Evaporation → loss of heat from LHofV, 20%; 10% to respiratory tract losses → managed by humidification/warming

Causes of heat loss & ↓heat production

↓BMR, ↓resp muscle contraction, ↓muscle tone, ↓hypothalamic response, ↓behavioural response, cold fluid & blood administration.

Patient factors: paeds (↑BSA to volume ratio), elderly (↓BMR, ↓muscle mass, ↓adipose insulation), hypothyroid (↓BMR)

Management → minimise losses & actively warm patient → other than the obvious: ↓NMB (allows shivering), 'sport mode' (?) on warming device, head wraps.
