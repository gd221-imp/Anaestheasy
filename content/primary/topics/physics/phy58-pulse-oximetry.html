Beer's Law → absorbance of light through a medium ∝ [medium] (concentration)

Lambert's Law → absorbance of light through a medium ∝ path length

Beer-Lambert Law → I = I₀.e^(-LCβ) → log(I₀/I) = LCβ (absorbance)

Underlying principle

Infrared light is absorbed to different degrees by the oxy/deoxy forms of Hb

  1. 2 wavelengths (λ) = 660nm (red) & 940nm (infrared), shone intermittently
  2. As vessels expand & contract w/ the pulse → alters absorption as per Beer-Lambert law ∴ two waveforms produced
  • excess deoxy(Hb) ⇒ red > infrared absorption ∴ ↓red amplitude
  • excess oxy(Hb) ⇒ infrared > red absorption ∴ ↓infrared amplitude

(combined, this ratio gives the SpO2 estimate)
