Breathing systems have 3 functions

  1. Removal of CO2 2) Supply of O2 3) Supply of anaesthetic. (non-rebreathe vs absorption types)

*Mapleson A (Magill, Lack)

FGF port proximal to the patient = inaccessible during surgery.

Lack modification = a co-axial expiratory limb, scavenging gases & reducing pollution (returns exhaled gas to the reservoir bag end); but ↑work of breathing.

If flow > minute volume → expired alveolar gas is expelled. Minimal rebreathing @ FGF of 70% MV in spontaneous ventilation & 200-300ml/kg in controlled ventilation.

*Mapleson B (obsolete)

*Mapleson C (Cambu bag / Water's circuit) - a shortened Mapleson B

(For B & C: ↑FGF needed (= peak inspiratory flow) to prevent rebreathing → ↑theatre pollution.)

*Mapleson D (Bain circuit)

Needs 2-3x minute volume to prevent re-breathing. Lower resistance than a circle system (no valves or absorber). Used for controlled ventilation.

*Mapleson E (Ayre's T-piece)

T-piece close to the patient.

*Mapleson F (Jackson-Rees)

As E, with a bag added to the expiratory limb to help prevent barotrauma.
