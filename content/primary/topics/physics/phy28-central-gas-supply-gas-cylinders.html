O2

Stored @ 13700kPa. Pin index 2&5. Outlet: black & white. Size E → 680L

  1. N2O: liquid phase w/ vapour pressure @ ~4400kPa → can't gauge contents from pressure alone. Filling ratio = 0.75 (0.67 in hotter climates). Pin index 3&5. Blue outlet. Size E → 1800L
  2. Entonox: stored @ 13700kPa, 50% O2 / 50% N2O. Pin index 7. Two-stage pressure demand regulator → gas flows w/ inspiratory effort. Must be stored horizontally for 24hrs & has a dip tube; @5C or below, Poynting effect can reverse (bubbling O2 through liquid N2O increases vapour pressure to form gas @ a pressure > that of liquefying N2O alone, but separation can occur in the cold).
  3. Air: pin index 1&5. Grey cylinder, black outlet. Does not separate in cylinders (that is Entonox, whose pseudo-critical temperature is -5.5C). 400kPa for medical use, 700kPa for powertool use.
