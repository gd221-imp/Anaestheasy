DC & AC → can be combined.

  • Cell → a power source that provides electricity as DC
  • Resistor → a device that opposes electrical current by producing a voltage drop in proportion to current flow
  • Transducer → a device for conversion from one form of energy to another

(Q) Charge → presence or absence of a quantity of electrons on a conducting surface (Coulombs)

(I) Current → flow of electrons around a circuit (rate of change of charge) (Amps) ∝ rate of change of V

(V) Voltage → driving force for current (Volt) (potential difference) [an ampere = flow of 6.24x10^18 electrons per second past a point]

  • Electromotive force → voltage delivered by a battery or mains supply = EMF associated with electric current.
  • Energy = Voltage x Charge (potential difference that passes a current of 1 ampere when...)
  • Power = Voltage x Current (rate of energy dissipated = 1 watt)
  • Ohm's Law: V=IR

*flow of particles = -ve→+ve; conventional current direction in diagrams = +ve→-ve

Series circuit → I is same throughout, R

Sum of resistors' R, V=I x R of that resistor.

  • Parallel circuit → I = sum of elements, 1/R = 1/R1+1/R2+1/R3, V=I1xR1
  • Voltage divider → Vin=Ix(R1+R2) / Vout=IxR2 ∴ Vin/Vout = (R1+R2)/R2
  • Wheatstone bridge → pair of voltage dividers which divide an unwanted high voltage signal to cancel each other out & allow a much smaller signal to be detectable by a meter in between.

aim = to reject noisy signals - common to both arms → a transducer over one resistor/branch causes a measurable output → ↑transducer number = ↑sensitivity.

Electronic Circuits

  • Capacitance = the ability of an object to store electrical charge (Farad). C=Q/V ; C=εA/d (ε=permittivity, A=area, d=distance)
  • Inductance = tendency of a conductor to induce an EMF that opposes a voltage drop due to resistance (Henry) (coiling of wire → magnetic field)

I=L/R - initial high V drop across an inductor induces a magnetic field. (try to maintain I both ways)

  • In AC current, a capacitor's current waveform leads the voltage & the reverse is true for inductance.

  • RC circuit: Vout/Vin = 1/(2πfRC+1) ⇒ ↑f = ↓Vout ∴ blocks high frequency signals → low pass filter
  • CR circuit: Vout/Vin = 2πfRC/(2πfRC+1) ⇒ ↓f = ↓Vout ∴ blocks low frequency signals → high pass filter
  • LR circuit ⇒ → low pass filter
  • LRC ('tuned') circuit ⇒ [graph of reactance vs frequency showing capacitor & inductor reactance curves crossing]. Capacitive reactance ∝1/f & inductive reactance ∝f.

Frequency dependent resistance = reactance.

Impedance = measure of a circuit's resistance to flow of current, taking into account the effects of inductance & capacitance.
