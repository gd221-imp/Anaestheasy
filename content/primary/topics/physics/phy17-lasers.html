LASER = Light Amplification of Stimulated Emission of Radiation

  • intense, monochromatic, non-divergent, narrow beam

(Fluorescence = spontaneous emission by different wavelength & phase - contrast with laser light)

  • Spontaneous emission → electron absorbs photon → jumps to higher shell → falls to lower shell → emits a photon (not in phase)
  • Stimulated emission → photon from an excited electron stimulates another electron → 2 photons released, in phase (?)
  • Laser light → monochromatic (1 wavelength), coherent (same phase), collimated (highly directional)
  • medium is pumped w/ energy → stimulated emission. Mirrors at each end reflect photons → population inversion = majority of electrons in a higher energy state. A small opening allows the intense light energy out.

Laser classes

  • Class I - no hazard to eye
  • Class II - <1mW
  • Class III - up to 500mW → direct viewing hazardous to eye
  • Class IV - >500mW → hazardous to skin & eye

*light from UV & far infrared penetrates tissue poorly; light from red end of spectrum = ↑penetration

*flammability risk → keep O2 concentration ≤25% near the beam / fill ETT cuff with saline, not air
