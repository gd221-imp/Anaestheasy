Rotameter → does not require an energy source

Rotating-vane flowmeter (Wright respirometer) → under-reads at low flows and over-reads at high flows (inertia); interruption of a light beam by the turbine → voltage

Pneumotachograph → issue of condensation in the capillary tubes → ↑resistance → solved by heating

Pitot tube flowmeter → comparison of pressure in a tube measured & compared. ΔP ∝ V²

Wright peak flowmeter

Benedict-Roth spirometer → stationary base w/ movable 'bell' → displacement ∝ volume changes

↳ usually underestimates due to contraction of exhaled air & condensation
