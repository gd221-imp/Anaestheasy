1. Liquid in glass thermometer → mercury or alcohol-based → cheap, simple [thermometric property: expansion of liquid]
  2. Bourdon → as with a Bourdon pressure gauge [thermometric property: pressure change of gas]
  3. Bimetallic strip [thermometric property: expansion of solid]
  4. Resistance → platinum wire, R ∝ T; not sensitive, linear [thermometric property: resistance of coiled wire]
  5. Thermistor → R ∝ 1/T; -ve thermal coefficient (?); non-linear; small, cheap, quick response [thermometric property: resistance of metal oxide]
  6. Thermocouple → small: ↓heat capacity ∴ responds quickly [thermometric property: Seebeck effect]
  7. Infrared → thermopile, tympanic membrane (?); affected by debris, poor technique, ear wax ↳ needs direct view of the tympanic membrane [thermometric property: thermal radiation]
