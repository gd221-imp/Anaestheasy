Laws of Thermodynamics

  • The Zeroth Law: if 2 thermodynamic systems are in equilibrium with a 3rd (with exchange), then they must be in equilibrium with each other.
  • The 1st Law: energy can't be created or destroyed
  • The 2nd Law: increased entropy → unavailable energy
  • The 3rd Law: as a system approaches absolute zero, all processes cease and the entropy of the system approaches a minimum.

Internal energy: ΔU = TΔS + pΔV ≤ 0 (T=temperature, ΔS=entropy, p=pressure, ΔV=volume)
