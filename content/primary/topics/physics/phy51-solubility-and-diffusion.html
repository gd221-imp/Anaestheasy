Henry's Law

Amount of gas dissolved in a liquid ∝ P.gas in equilibrium w/ the liquid

Graham's Law

Rate of diffusion of a gas is inversely proportional to √molecular weight

Fick's Law

Rate of diffusion of a gas across a membrane ∝ Area × D × (C1-C2) / thickness (D = diffusion constant, ∝ solubility/√MW)

Bunsen solubility coefficient

Volume of gas @ STP that dissolves in one unit volume @ constant T, where P.gas = 1atm
