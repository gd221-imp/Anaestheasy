Capnography can be affected by shunt.

  • usually EtCO2 = 0.3-0.6kPa < PaCO2. Normal EtCO2 4.5-5.5kPa.
  • colorimetric CO2 indicator (?) = changes purple → yellow in the presence of CO2
  • waveform phases:

I = exhalation begins → dead space air first, with no CO2

II = alveolar gas mixes in → CO2 rises

III = alveolar gas predominates (plateau)

IV = inhalation.

  • Should check for a CO2 trace, as some CO2 may be present in the stomach after bag-valve-mask ventilation or from carbonated drinks etc.
  • 'Shark fin' morphology → suggests an obstructive picture.
  • 'm' shaped notch on the plateau → suggests patient is starting to breathe/recovering spontaneous effort.

*In sidestream analysers, a delay in measurement of 3-8 seconds is acceptable; sampling tube should be <2m, internal diameter 1-2mm, with an aspiration rate of ~150-200ml/min (?) [figure partly illegible]

  • PEEP does not affect the measurement of EtCO2.
