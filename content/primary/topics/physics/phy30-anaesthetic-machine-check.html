The check (pre-use anaesthetic machine check)

  1. Connected to mains.
  2. Monitoring → BP cuff → gas monitoring (calibrated) → SpO2 probe
  3. Pipeline → tug test
  4. Reserve gas cylinders → O2/N2O/Air → check contents (15700/4400/13700kPa approx values), turn off, blanking plug
  5. Check pipeline pressures
  6. Check flowmeter
  7. Check anti-hypoxia mechanism
  8. Check O2 analyser & turn off all control valves
  9. Check emergency O2 bypass → pipeline pressure maintained & control valve shuts on release
  10. Check vaporiser & purge; check for leaks (O-rings, fully attached), filling level, and knob moves freely
  11. Check for leaks → 5L/min O2, temporarily occlude common gas outlet, turn on vaporiser (repeat for each), check for bobbin drop
  12. Check tubing → assembly, leaks (occlude & compress bag), obstructions (check inner & outer tubing)
  13. Check unidirectional flow valves.
  14. Ensure HME filter & catheter mount assembled & patent → keep in sterile packaging until use
  15. Check ventilator runs OK & pressure relief valve
  16. Check scavenging system is working (30mm connector)
  17. Check suction.
  18. Check patient bed/trolley.
  19. Check airway equipment & ambu bag
  20. Keep check log book & document in patient record → the check, monitoring in place, patency of system
