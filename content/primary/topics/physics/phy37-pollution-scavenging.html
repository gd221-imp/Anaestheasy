2 types of scavenging, each with 3 components

Components: 1) Collection from the breathing system (between the APL valve & the receiving system) 2) A system to carry the gases away 3) Something to limit +ve/-ve pressure variations.

A) Passive → exhaled gases driven by the patient's own ventilation → 30mm connector → reservoir bag with a spring-loaded valve → wide-bore duct pipe.

B) Active → 30mm connector → receiving system, valveless, with a flow indicator → pump/vacuum.

↑negative pressure = air entrained; ↑positive pressure = opposite risk.
