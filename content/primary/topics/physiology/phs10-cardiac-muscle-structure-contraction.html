• Cardiomyocyte → central nucleus, numerous mitochondria, ↑capillary density, cells grouped by intercalated discs
  • Cell junctions: desmosomes anchor between myocytes → ↑tensile strength & structural integrity; gap junctions = hexameric connexons → allow passage of small molecules/Ca2+ flow
  • Thick filament → myosin → 3x2 globular heavy-meromyosin heads linked to light-meromyosin at the hinge region; adjacent filaments anchored at the M-line; titin → elastic anchor allowing stabilisation of myosin
  • Thin filament → actin → G-actin monomer → F-actin polymer → binding site for myosin heads; tropomyosin → α-helices block myosin-actin binding; troponins → TnI = actin-binding inhibitor, TnC = Ca2+ binding, TnT = tropomyosin binding
  • Z-disc → enclosed by desmin, connects to Z-discs of adjacent sarcomeres; also involved in force transmission & longitudinal load bearing
  • Muscle fibres = 4-6 myocytes thick → arranged in multiple directions allowing the helical ventricular twist model
