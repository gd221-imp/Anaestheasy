Pulmonary arteries

Thinner-walled than systemic, carry blood from RV to the lungs

  • Bronchial arteries = arise from the thoracic aorta, share characteristics of systemic arteries
  • Pulmonary veins return blood to LA; bronchial venous blood returns via azygos veins to RA
  • PVR = driving pressure (PAp - LAp) / CO
  • PVR ↓ with ↑CO through recruitment of previously unperfused capillaries & vessel distension
  • Normal mean pulmonary artery pressure = 8-20mmHg at rest, >20mmHg = pulmonary hypertension (ESC/ERS 2022; older texts >25mmHg); minimum PVR occurs at FRC

West's Lung Zones

The upright lung (gravity-dependent perfusion due to blood flow & vessel distensibility):

  • Zone 1: PA > Pa > Pv, V/Q >1
  • Zone 2: Pa > PA > Pv, V/Q ~1 (@ ~3rd rib, same hydrostatic level as the heart (?))
  • Zone 3: Pa > Pv > PA, V/Q <1

Hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction → ↓pO2 → vasoconstriction → blood diverted away from poorly-ventilated areas → better V/Q matching; chronic hypoxia → ↑vascular smooth muscle → pulmonary hypertension
