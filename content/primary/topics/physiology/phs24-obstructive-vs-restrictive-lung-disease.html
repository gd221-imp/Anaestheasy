Obstructive disease

↑TLC, ↑RV (hyperinflation, gas trapping); ↓flow rates, ↓VC. Upper airway obstruction → ↓flow at otherwise normal lung volumes.

Restrictive disease

↓TLC & ↓RV; ↑flow due to ↑elastic recoil.

Extrathoracic variable obstruction (e.g. unilateral vocal cord palsy) → worse on inspiration.

Intrathoracic variable obstruction (e.g. tracheomalacia) → ↓expiratory flow/trapping.

Alveolar-arterial gradient

PAO2 - PaO2; increased by ↓V/Q areas and, to a lesser extent, ↑V/Q areas.
