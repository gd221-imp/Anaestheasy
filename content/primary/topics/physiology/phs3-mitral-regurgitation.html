Causes

Acute (ruptured chordae tendinae, post-MI, trauma) / Chronic (MVP, rheumatic fever, connective tissue disorder, DCM)

Effects

↑LA volume, ↑LA dimension (up to 4x → AF); ↑LA compliance: ↑blood flow to LV → dilatation & dysfunction → ↑ESV → ↑EDV → ↑P

Symptoms

Fatigue, dyspnoea (on exertion), orthopnoea, palpitations; signs: pan-systolic murmur & 3rd heart sound

Severity grading

NYHA class, regurgitant fraction (regurgitant volume / total LV stroke volume) - <30% mild, 30-49% moderate, >=50% severe; also graded by LV dysfunction

Tailoring anaesthetic ('fast & loose')

(1) avoid bradycardia (2) minimise vasoconstrictor use (3) minimise preload
