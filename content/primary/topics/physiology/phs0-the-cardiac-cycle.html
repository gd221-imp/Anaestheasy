Definition

The cardiac cycle = one systole + one diastole (systole ≈ 1/3, diastole ≈ 2/3 of the cycle).

Phases

  • Atrial systole (1): a-wave = atrial contraction, contributes ~30% of ventricular filling (?) - absent in AF, cannon waves in AV block
  • Isovolumetric contraction (2): c-wave = bulging back of mitral valve into LA (giant in TR (?))
  • Ventricular systole (3): SV ≈ 70mls
  • Isovolumetric relaxation (4): v-wave = venous return to atrium with MV closed = ↑P, exaggerated in TR
  • Ventricular filling (5): y-descent = MV opens & blood released = ↓P; diastasis = slow ventricular filling time

Peak LV pressure ≈ 120mmHg (?), RV pressure ≈ 25mmHg

Heart sounds

3rd HS = ventricular dilatation; 4th HS = stiff ventricle

Pressure-volume relationship (Wiggers diagram)

  • Aortic valve opens ~80mmHg, dicrotic notch from aortic elastic recoil
  • SV = EDV - ESV
  • Work = pressure x volume (area of PV loop)

Coronary blood flow

  • Normal coronary blood flow = 200-250ml/min (~5% of CO)
  • O2 extraction ≈ 60% vs 25% elsewhere
  • Coronary perfusion gradient during diastole = aortic diastolic P - LVEDP
  • Left coronary flow occurs mainly in diastole (impeded in systole by ↑intramural pressure); right coronary flow maintained throughout the cycle
  • Flow ↑ markedly in hypoxia (?)

Ventriculo-arterial coupling

  • Preload = end diastolic stretch (LVEDV)
  • Afterload = LV wall tension required to eject SV; Anrep effect (homeometric autoregulation) → a sudden ↑afterload first ↓SV, then contractility rises over minutes, partly restoring SV; vs ↑SV with ↑LVEDV (Frank-Starling, heterometric)
  • Ees = end-systolic elastance; Ea = arterial elastance; optimal coupling when Ees = 2 x Ea
  • La Place's Law: intraventricular pressure ∝ tension (r/h ∝ tension)
