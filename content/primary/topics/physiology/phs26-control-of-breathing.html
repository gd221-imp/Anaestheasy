Pathway

Sensors → medulla → effectors.

Respiratory centre → dorsal & ventral respiratory groups = central pattern generator (CPG).

  • Above the pons → only input = from stretch receptors & CN X
  • Above mid-pons → inhibitory signals & prevention of apneusis
  • Pons → regularity of breathing
  • Medulla → basic rhythm generator

Dorsal respiratory group → floor of the 4th ventricle, near the nucleus tractus solitarius (sensory input) → inspiratory neurones → upper motor neurones to anterior horn cells → timing of the respiratory cycle.

Ventral respiratory group → 4 nuclei controlling the muscles of respiration (expiratory & inspiratory).

Pontine respiratory group → fine control of respiratory rhythm.

Two phases of expiration

Passive (with ↓inspiratory muscle tone) and active (with ↑expiratory muscle tone).

Central pattern generation = slow membrane depolarisation → spontaneous discharge, exhibiting pacemaker-like behaviour (Na+/Ca2+ driven).

Inhibitory neurones = hyperpolarisation of cells.

Other inputs: reticular formation, sensory stimuli, posture, voluntary control - all converge on anterior horn cells.

Hyperventilation → alkalosis, ↑pH, ↓CBF

Vasoconstriction; ↑CO2 = ↑SNS); ↓ionised Ca2+ → tetany.
