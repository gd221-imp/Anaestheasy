Compliance = gradient of the pressure-volume graph = volume change per unit change of pressure = ΔV/ΔP (approx. 2L/kPa or 200ml/cmH2O).

  • Behaves like resistors in parallel: 1/Ctotal = 1/Cx + 1/Cy; Cstatic > Cdynamic; specific compliance = Ctotal/FRC
  • Bottom of the lung is more compliant than the top; compliance is lower in children than adults
  • ↑Compliance: emphysema, old age (asthma - lung compliance usually near normal)
  • Surfactant deficiency ↓compliance (stiff lungs, alveolar collapse - e.g. neonatal RDS)
  • ↓Compliance: ↑pulmonary venous pressure, alveolar oedema, fibrotic lung disease, pneumonia, extremes of age

Laplace's Law

P = 2T/R

Surfactant

DPPC, protein, carbohydrate; produced by Type II pneumocytes. ↓Surface tension, ↓transudation into the alveolus, ↑compliance/prevents collapse. Hydrophilic end repels, keeping the alveolus open, extending FRC.

Work of breathing

ΔP x ΔV (increased under anaesthesia)

  • Obstructive defect → ↑work of resistance ∴ ↑tidal volume & rate
  • Restrictive defect → ↑work of elastance ∴ ↓tidal volume & ↑RR
