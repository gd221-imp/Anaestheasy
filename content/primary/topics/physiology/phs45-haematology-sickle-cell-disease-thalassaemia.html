Sickle cell disease: point mutation A→T on the β-globin gene (chromosome 11), autosomal recessive inheritance.

  • Aetiology: HbS instability → Fe2+ not adequately protected → reacts with free radicals → oxidant damage → vascular inflammation of RBCs
  • Haem normally binds NO; disrupted NO handling → disrupts anti-thrombogenic/vasodilatory actions
  • Sickling → haemolysis & abnormal attachment to the endothelium
  • Diagnosis: Hb electrophoresis
  • Management: vaccination (pneumococcal/Hib/meningococcus), penicillin prophylaxis; severe disease → hydroxyurea (↑HbF & ↓HbS), allogeneic bone marrow transplant

α-thalassaemia

Gene deletions on chromosome 16. Trait = 1-2 genes affected. HbH disease = 3 genes affected → moderate anaemia. Hydrops fetalis = all 4 genes affected → death in utero.

β-thalassaemia

Minor = carrier state (mild anaemia, iron deficiency-like picture). Major = absent β-chain production → ↑HbA2/HbF from 3-6 months (the time of the HbF→HbA switch); transfusion-dependent; bone marrow hyperplasia/hepatosplenomegaly if untransfused. Thalassaemia intermedia = a milder form of major thalassaemia.
