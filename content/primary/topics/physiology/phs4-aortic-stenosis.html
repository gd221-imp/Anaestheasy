Causes

Congenital, rheumatic, degenerative calcification

Effects

Systolic pressure gradient LV: aorta, ↑LV systolic pressure, ↑LV wall thickness, ↓compliance & diastolic dysfunction, ↑myocardial O2 demand, ↓coronary blood flow

Symptoms

Largely exercise-related - exertional dyspnoea, fatigue, chest pain, syncope. Signs: LVH (voltage criteria), T-wave inversion, features of CCF

  • Voltage criteria: R wave in V5/6 >25mm; Sokolow-Lyon = S in V1 + R in V5 or V6 >35mm

Severity grading (functional capacity or echo; healthy aortic valve area = 2.5-3.5cm²):

  • Mild: <20mmHg / >1.5cm²
  • Moderate: 20-40mmHg / 1-1.5cm²
  • Severe: >40mmHg / <1cm²
  • Critical: >80mmHg / <0.5cm²

Tailoring anaesthetic ('slow & tight')

(1) avoid tachycardia (2) maintain SVR (3) maintain preload (4) maintain sinus rhythm (5) avoid vasodilatation
