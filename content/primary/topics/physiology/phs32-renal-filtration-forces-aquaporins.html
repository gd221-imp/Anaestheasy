Filtration forces along the nephron

  • Renal artery pressure ≈ 100mmHg
  • Afferent arteriole pressure ≈ 80mmHg → glomerular capillary → efferent arteriole → peritubular capillary, where ↓hydrostatic pressure allows reabsorption

NB 1° active transport occurs for

Na+/K+ pump, Ca2+ reabsorption, H+ secretion, H+/K+ exchange.

Three main aquaporins

AQP1 (PCT & thin descending limb, constitutive), AQP2 (collecting duct, apical membrane, ADH-regulated), AQP3 (collecting duct, basal membrane).

Isotonic fluid enters the peritubular capillaries; some Na+ leaks back (pump-leak system) - a gradient-time-limited process.
