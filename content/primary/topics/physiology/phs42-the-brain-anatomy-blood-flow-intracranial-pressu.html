Functional anatomy

  • Frontal lobe → pre-central gyrus, upper motor neurones with the motor homunculus
  • Parietal lobe → post-central gyrus, main sensory area with the sensory homunculus
  • Temporal lobe → auditory & visual processing, language, memory
  • Cerebellum → co-ordination, balance, posture
  • Limbic system → function not purely anatomical - learning, emotion, behaviour, endocrine links
  • Hypothalamus → thirst, hunger, body temperature, fear, sleep, pain, wakefulness, pleasure, arousal, anger, emotions

Cerebral blood flow

~750ml/min (?) total; glucose provides ~90% of brain energy needs (ketones used in starvation); minimal energy reserve ∴ occlusion rapidly causes unconsciousness. Grey matter (cortex) has higher blood flow than white matter.

Cerebral blood flow maintenance/autoregulation depends on: (1) MAP (autoregulated over a wide range, ~60-160mmHg (?)) (2) PaCO2 (3) PaO2.

How autoregulation works:

  • Myogenic theory: stretch of blood vessels → smooth muscle contracts → returns stretch to normal
  • Metabolic theory: extravascular vasoactive substances accumulate with ↑metabolism → ↑CO2 → ↑flow → washes out CO2 → flow returns to normal

Intracranial pressure (ICP)

↑ by volatile anaesthetics, ketamine, suxamethonium; ↓ by opioids, propofol, barbiturates, etomidate.

Monro-Kellie doctrine: the skull is a rigid container - ~75ml blood, ~75ml CSF, ~1400g brain; if one volume rises another must fall to keep the total constant, though there is a critical volume beyond which this compensation fails and ICP rises sharply.

CPP = MAP - ICP (allowing for CVP where relevant).

CSF

Total volume ≈ 150ml, produced at ~550ml/day (?), pH ≈ 7.3, protein content low, glucose ≈ 3.5mmol/L (~2/3 of serum level), osmolality similar to plasma. Produced by the choroid plexus of the lateral & 3rd/4th ventricles. Absorbed through arachnoid villi into the cerebral venous sinuses. Functions: protection, buffering, transport.

TBI management targets

PaCO2 ~4.5-5.5kPa (?), avoid hypoxia, hypotension, and fluid restriction.
