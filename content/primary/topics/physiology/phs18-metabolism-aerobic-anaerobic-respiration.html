Aerobic metabolism

  • FFA → β-oxidation → Acetyl CoA (in mitochondria, in the liver); TG → FFA + glycerol → gluconeogenesis
  • Amino acids → oxidation → Acetyl CoA (+pyruvate + Krebs cycle intermediates)
  • Glucose → glycolysis (cytoplasm) → 2 pyruvate → oxidative decarboxylation (mitochondria) → 2 Acetyl CoA

Phase 1 (glycolysis)

2ATP used, 4 produced ∴ net 2ATP (+2NADH)

Phase 2 (Citric acid/Krebs cycle): 2 Acetyl CoA (2C) + oxaloacetate (4C) → citrate (6C) → 2ATP, 6NADH+, 2FADH2, 4CO2 per glucose molecule (NADH+ yields 3ATP each, FADH2 yields 2ATP each)

Phase 3 (Electron transport chain): oxidation of reduced molecules releases electrons & energy, ADP→ATP (inner mitochondrial membrane) = oxidative phosphorylation. Cyanide blocks the electron transport chain at cytochrome oxidase. O2 needed as the final electron acceptor: O2 + H+ → H2O.

Anaerobic metabolism

Glycolysis → glucose → pyruvate → lactate + net 2ATP → ↓ATP yield & ↓pH.

  • Only ~90 seconds of ATP supply available anaerobically in the body
  • ↓pO2 in mitochondria below 0.4kPa = anaerobic threshold (Pasteur point)
  • O2 cascade: 21kPa in air → PaO2 ~13kPa → ~1kPa at the mitochondria (?)
  • Brain cells: hypoxia for 2 minutes = irreversible damage; 4 minutes = cell death
