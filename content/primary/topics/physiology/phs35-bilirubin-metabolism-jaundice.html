Jaundice occurs when bilirubin >18μmol/L (icterus).

  • Pre-hepatic: haemolysis → ↑unconjugated bilirubin (UCB)
  • Hepatic: hepatocyte damage, ↓glucuronyl transferase activity (e.g. Gilbert's syndrome), Dubin-Johnson syndrome
  • Post-hepatic: dark urine from ↑conjugated bilirubin (CB) due to obstruction; pale stools due to ↓stercobilinogen

Bilirubin pathway

  • 1-2x10⁸ erythrocytes destroyed per hour → ~6g Hb/day; Hb → globin & haem in the reticuloendothelial system
  • Haem → biliverdin & CO (via haem oxygenase) ← Fe2+ removed, ~250-400mg bilirubin produced/day
  • Unconjugated bilirubin delivered bound to albumin, taken up by hepatocytes via OATP (facilitated diffusion), binds glutathione-S-transferase
  • Conjugated with glucuronic acid in the smooth ER by uridine diphosphoglucuronate transferase → bilirubin diglucuronide → excreted into bile against a concentration gradient
  • In the small intestine, bacteria convert bilirubin → urobilinogen → oxidised to urobilin (colours urine) or stercobilin (colours faeces)
  • Plasma UCB: CB ratio ≈ 25:1 (?)
