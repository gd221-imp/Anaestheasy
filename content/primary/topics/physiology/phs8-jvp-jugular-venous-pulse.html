Waveform

A-wave (atrial systole, reflux of blood into IVC → ↑pressure by a few mmHg (?), absent in AF, cannon waves in AV block), c-wave (AV valve cusps bulge back + pulsation from the carotid artery), x-descent (AV ring & base of ventricle pulled down), v-wave (filling of atria, ↑in TR), y-descent (AV valves open → blood flows to ventricle).

JVP pathology

  • Absent → intravascular depletion
  • Elevated, >4cm above sternal angle, normal waveform → RHF / fluid overload
  • Elevated with absent waveform → SVC obstruction
  • Elevated with deep x/y descent → constrictive pericarditis
  • Cannon a-wave → 3rd degree AV block
  • Absent a-wave → AF
  • ↑v-wave → TR
