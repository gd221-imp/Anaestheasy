Hüfner constant

1.34ml O2/g Hb.

Structure

4 polypeptide chains, each containing a haem molecule (porphyrin ring with central Fe2+) - 2x α (chromosome 16) & 2x β (chromosome 11).

  • HbA = 2α & 2β (95% of adult Hb)
  • HbA2 = 2α & 2δ (~3.5% of adult Hb) → ↓O2 carriage
  • HbF = 2α & 2γ (<1% of adult Hb) → ↑O2 affinity

Distal histidine prevents Fe2+→Fe3+ oxidation and irreversible CO binding. Haem forms 6 bonds - 4x nitrogen, 1x proximal histidine, 1x O2. Binding of O2 to Hb increases affinity for further O2 (cooperative binding).

Synthesis

Mitochondria → porphyrin & haem; cytoplasm → globin chains.

Removal: reticuloendothelial system → globin chains → amino acids; haem → not reused → porphyrin ring → bilirubin.

Abnormal Hb

  • Thalassaemia: α = deletion on chromosome 16; β = ↓β chains → ↑HbA2/HbF
  • Sickle cell disease: valine substituted for glutamic acid in the β-globin chain; HbSS = both β-globin chains affected
  • Abnormal ligands: CO (~300x affinity of O2); Fe2+ oxidised to Fe3+ by NO, prilocaine, nitrates → methaemoglobin (MetHb)
