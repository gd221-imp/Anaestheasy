Leads

  • Limb leads = bipolar (Einthoven's triangle)
  • Augmented leads (aVR, aVL, aVF) = unipolar, potential between active electrode and a composite (Wilson's central terminal) reference
  • Chest leads = unipolar; V1-2 = RV, V3-4 = septum, V5-6 = anterolateral aspect of LV
  • Normal cardiac axis = -30° to +90° → >90° = RAD, <-30° = LAD
  • 12-lead = 3-lead + CM5 + rhythm strip
  • Six bipolar leads: I, II, III; six unipolar leads: aVR, aVL, aVF, V1-6 (?)
  • Lead II → RA, LA, +ve = arrhythmia monitoring
  • CM5 → RA on manubrium, LA on 5th ICS anterior axillary line + reference on L shoulder → detects ischaemia
  • CB5 → RA on scapula, LA on 5th ICS anterior axillary line, reference on L shoulder - used for thoracic surgery (?)
