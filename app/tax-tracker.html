Before you take a shift

Projection = latest payslip's taxable pay to date + expected pay × months left. Taxable pay on NHS payslips is already after pension contributions.

Self-employed income up to £1,000 is covered by the trading allowance. Take-home figures assume 40% / 60% / 45% income tax plus 2% NI, and any student loan you select. Thresholds are frozen at £100,000 and £125,140. This is an estimate, not tax advice.
